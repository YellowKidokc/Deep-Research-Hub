## What the corpus says about her growth

- **Early Kait:** stat cascade → "let's get into it" → countdown. Effective, generic.
- **Late Kait:** one turning sentence or small fact → frame (not-doing, can't-judge, map) → two acts → trust moves (convict own side, refused number) → swap test → objection flip → aphorism run → carry-question → dual address → ring.
- **The late gain is almost entirely the `trust` group.** Her openings got sharper, but what changed most is that she started conceding, showing the other phone, and refusing numbers. That's why the late verdicts land harder.
