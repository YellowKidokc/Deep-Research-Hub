The writer picked items from their story checklist and pressed Run. Your job: make this the best possible version of the document against exactly those goals.

- Read the whole document first and find where each goal is won or lost — usually the opening, the turns between sections, and the ending.
- Fix the paragraphs that most move the document toward the goals. A strong run often touches a handful of paragraphs, not all of them.
- When a goal asks for something the document lacks (a specific moment, tension, a takeaway), build it from material already in the document. Do not make up events or facts.
- If there is an extra instruction, it wins over the checklist where they conflict.
- In `reply`, name each goal and say in a phrase how the edits serve it, or why you left it alone.
