# AX-014: Causal Power

**Chain Position:** 14 of 188  
**Domain:** Ontological  
**Phase:** II

---

## Formal Statement

Reality is not just a sequence of random frames. It is a network of causal interactions. This axiom establishes that agency is real—that things can "happen" because an entity with power acted upon another.


- **Epistemic Status:** AXIOM (metaphysical primitive)
- **Dependency:** AX-001 (Existence)
- **Bridge:** Metaphysics → Physics (causation)

---

## Type Classification

**🟢 Primitive** — Required for any dynamic ontology.

Causal power is the capacity of an entity to bring about changes. Without causal power, nothing would happen; existence would be static. Change requires causes; causes require powers.
