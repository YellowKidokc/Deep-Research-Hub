# T7 — Relational Distinctions

**[x9t6v84w-r92y-9t97-svv0-3u7x6t4w8y92] | P7 | Axiom | Relational Distinctions**

> **The Persons are distinguished by relations of origin, not by power, will, or essence.**

---

## 1. Formal Statement

The distinction between Father, Son, and Spirit is purely relational. None is "more God" than the others. They are distinguished by how they relate to the Source:
- **Father:** Unbegotten Source.
- **Son:** Eternally begotten of the Father.
- **Spirit:** Proceeds from the Father (and the Son).
