# T8 — Undivided Works

**[y0u7w95x-s03z-0u08-tww1-4v8y7u5x9z03] | P7 | Axiom | Undivided Works**

> **All external works of God are undivided; the Trinity acts as one.**

---

## 1. Formal Statement

Creation, redemption, and sanctification are works of the one God. While certain works are *appropriated* to one Person (e.g., creation to the Father, structure to the Son, life-giving to the Spirit), all three are involved in every act. God never acts in isolation from Himself.

## 2. Type Classification

**🔴 Constraint** — It ensures the self-consistency of the Logos Field. No operation occurs without the coordination of all three operators.

---

## 3. Justification

Every "Now" event requires the participation of Potential, Structure, and Actualization simultaneously. You cannot have an actual event without a structure, and you cannot have a structure without a source. The Trinity is the **Universal Processor** where all three factors are always present and active.
