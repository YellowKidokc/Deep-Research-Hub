# T6 — Indivisible Essence

**[w8s5u73v-q81x-8s86-ruu9-2t6w5s3v7x81] | P7 | Axiom | Indivisible Essence**

> **The divine essence is indivisible — there are not 'three parts' of God.**

---

## 1. Formal Statement

Each Person of the Trinity fully possesses the whole divine essence. God is not divided. In the context of Theophysics, this means each operator acts upon the entire Logos Field (χ) and utilizes the entire informational resource. 

## 2. Type Classification

**🔴 Constraint** — It forbids **Tritheism** (three separate gods). It ensures the monotheistic core of the framework.

---

## 3. Justification

If the essence were divided, then truth would be fractured. But truth is coherent (G1.4). Therefore, the source of truth must possess its essence wholly in every operation. "1 × 1 × 1 = 1" describes this multiplicative unity.

---

## 4. The Logic Chain

1. **Personal Distinction (T5)**
2. **Indivisible Essence (T6)** (this axiom)
3. The distinctions are relational.
4. **Relational Distinctions (T7)**
