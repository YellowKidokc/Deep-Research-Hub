# Law 1: Universal Gravitation → Sin's Pull

**[L1] | Law | Gravity-Sin Correspondence**

---

## THE LAW

> **Universal Gravitation** ↔ **Sin's Gravitational Pull**
>
> Mass attracts mass with force proportional to mass and inversely proportional to distance squared.
> Sin attracts sin with force proportional to accumulated moral mass and inversely proportional to relational distance.

---

## THE PHYSICS

### Physical Law
Every mass attracts every other mass with a force proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.

### Equation
$$F = G\frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$

| Symbol | Meaning | Units |
|--------|---------|-------|
| F | Gravitational force | Newtons |
| G | Gravitational constant | 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N⋅m²/kg² |
| m₁, m₂ | Masses | kg |
| r | Distance | m |

### Key Properties
- **Always attractive** — gravity never repels
- **Infinite range** — extends to infinity, though weakens
- **Accumulative** — more mass = stronger pull
- **Escape velocity** — v_escape = √(2GM/r) — minimum speed to escape

### Observable In
- Planets orbiting stars
- Falling objects
- Tidal forces
- Black holes (extreme gravity)

## THE THEOLOGY

### Spiritual Parallel
Sin exerts a "gravitational" pull on the soul. Once engaged with, sin accumulates moral mass that pulls harder, making escape progressively more difficult.

### Scripture Anchor
> "The cords of death entangled me; the torrents of destruction overwhelmed me. The cords of the grave coiled around me; the snares of death confronted me." — Psalm 18:4-5

> "I do not do the good I want to do, but the evil I do not want to do—this I keep on doing." — Romans 7:19

### Traditional Doctrine
- **Concupiscence**: The disordered desire that remains after baptism—residual gravitational field
- **Habitual sin**: Repeated sin increases moral mass, strengthening the pull
- **Bondage**: Sin's gravity can become inescapable without external intervention

### Mathematical Form (Spiritual)
$$F_{sin} = G_{moral}\frac{S_1 S_2}{d^2}$$

Where:
- S₁ = Your accumulated moral mass (sin history)
- S₂ = The sin object's moral mass (severity × prevalence)
- d = Relational distance (how close you are to the temptation)

3. **Same boundary conditions** (singularity = inescapable state)

## THE COOL STUFF

### Connection 1: Why "Just Stop" Doesn't Work
Telling an addict to "just stop" is like telling a planet to "just fly away." The gravitational math doesn't allow it. You need external energy input (grace) exceeding escape velocity. Until then, you'll orbit the attractor.

> You can't bootstrap yourself out of a gravity well.

### Connection 2: Black Holes Are Real
A black hole is what happens when escape velocity exceeds the speed of light—nothing can escape. Hell is the spiritual black hole: a state where the accumulated moral mass is so great that not even light (truth) can escape. This is why sin is so dangerous—it can become literally inescapable.

> Some wells have no bottom.

### Connection 3: This Is Why Grace Comes From Outside
Newton's gravity can only be escaped with energy from outside the system. You can't lift yourself by your own bootstraps. Grace must come from outside the gravitational system—from God who stands outside the moral mass entirely.

> The rescue has to come from beyond the well.

### Connection 4: Orbit Is Compromise
A planet in orbit hasn't escaped—it's just falling and missing. Similarly, someone who "manages" their sin is in orbit—not free, just stable. True freedom requires exceeding escape velocity, not finding a sustainable orbit.

> Orbit isn't freedom. It's controlled falling.
