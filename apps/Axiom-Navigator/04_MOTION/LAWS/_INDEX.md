# THE 10 LAWS OF THEOPHYSICS

**The fundamental mappings between physical law and spiritual reality.**

---

## What This Is

Each Law identifies an **isomorphism** (structural identity) between a physical law and a spiritual principle. These aren't metaphors or analogies—they're claims that the *same mathematical structure* governs both domains.

Each law follows this structure:
1. **THE LAW** — Physical ↔ Spiritual mapping
2. **THE PHYSICS** — Physical law, equation, observables
3. **THE THEOLOGY** — Spiritual parallel, scripture, doctrine
4. **THE MAPPING** — Why the correspondence is structural, not arbitrary
5. **THE SOCRATIC PATH** — Why you already believe this
6. **THE COOL STUFF** — Engaging implications
7. **PREDICTIONS** — Testable claims
8. **LINKS** — Connections to axioms, definitions, other laws

## Mapping Principles

### What Makes a Valid Mapping?

1. **Structural Identity**: Same mathematical form in both domains
2. **Behavioral Correspondence**: Same dynamics/effects
3. **Boundary Conditions Match**: Same edge cases
4. **Predictions Transfer**: Physical predictions → spiritual predictions

### What Doesn't Count

- **Loose analogy**: "God is like gravity" (no structural identity)
- **Metaphor only**: "Light is like truth" (no mathematical form)
- **Cherry-picking**: Selecting only matching features
