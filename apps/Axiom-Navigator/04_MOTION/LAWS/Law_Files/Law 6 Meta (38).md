### 📘 **Key Terms & Definitions**   
   
**Physical Concepts:**   
   
   
- **Causality Law:** The principle that every action produces a proportional effect.   
- **Newton’s Third Law:** Every action has an equal and opposite reaction.   
- **Thermodynamic Causality:** Energy transfer in isolated systems, demonstrating inevitable consequences.   
- **Quantum Probability Collapse:** The act of choice finalizes one out of many potential realities.   
   
**Spiritual Concepts:**   
   
   
- **Sowing and Reaping:** The biblical and universal principle that what is planted grows and returns.   
- **Karmic Law:** The cycle of action and consequence across time.   
- **Moral Feedback System:** The self-reinforcing loop where ethical actions yield positive or negative consequences.   
- **Divine Cause:** The theological basis that **God’s law establishes immutable moral structures** in reality.   
   
**Bridge Concepts:**   
   
   
- **Faith-Physics:** Understanding causality in spiritual terms through physical law.   
- **Eternal Consequence Mechanics:** Mapping moral decisions onto **long-term universal impact** models.   
- **Entropy of Sin:** How disorder naturally multiplies without counteracting spiritual input.
