# T5 — Personal Distinction

**[v7r4t62u-p70w-7r75-qtt8-1s5v4r2u6w70] | P7 | Axiom | Personal Distinction**

> **Father, Son, and Spirit are personally distinct (none is the same person).**

---

## 1. Formal Statement

The distinction between the three is real and irreducible. The Father is not the Son; the Son is not the Spirit; the Spirit is not the Father. In the context of Theophysics, this means the three operations (Generation, Structuring, Actualization) are **orthogonal**.

## 2. Type Classification

**🔴 Constraint** — It forbids **Modalism** (the idea that one person wears three masks). It ensures the integrity of the three-operator solution.

---

## 3. Justification

Mathematically, a single operator cannot simultaneously be the Source, the Channel, and the Receiver without creating a degenerate loop. To avoid the infinite regress of the von Neumann chain, the operators must be distinct and co-equal in necessity.

---

## 4. The Logic Chain

1. **Spirit is God (T4)**
2. **Personal Distinction (T5)** (this axiom)
3. The Three share a single essence.
4. **Indivisible Essence (T6)**
