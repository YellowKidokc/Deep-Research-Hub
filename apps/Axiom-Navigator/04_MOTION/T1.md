# T1 — One God

**[r3n0p28q-l36s-3n31-mpp4-7o1r0n8q2s36] | P7 | Axiom | One God**

> **There is exactly one God — one in essence (being, substance).**

---

## 1. Formal Statement

Christianity is strictly monotheistic. God is a single, indivisible essence. This axiom rejects polytheism and tritheism. In the context of Theophysics, this means there is exactly one fundamental Logos Field (χ) that grounds all reality.

## 2. Type Classification

**🔴 Constraint** — It establishes the numerical unity of the Source. 

---

## 3. Justification

The "Master Equation" (χ) describes a single unified field. If there were multiple essences, coherence could not be universal, and the laws of logic would be contingent on which "god" was in power. Universal law requires a single unified Ground.

---

## 4. Scriptural Grounding

"The LORD our God, the LORD is one." (Deuteronomy 6:4)

---

## 5. The Logic Chain

1. **God (G5.2)**
2. **One God (T1)** (this axiom)
3. The one God consists of three distinct Persons.
4. **Father is God (T2)**
5. **Son is God (T3)**
6. **Spirit is God (T4)**
