## THE THREE-TIER SYSTEM

Every equation in Theophysics must be rendered in **three registers**:

1. **Basic (Beginner)** — Everyday language, narrative
2. **Medium (Middle)** — Technical but accessible
3. **Academic** — Full formal mathematical rigor

## USAGE RULES

### Rule 1: First Mention Protocol
**Always use the FULL translation on first mention in each paper tier.**

**Example (Beginner):**
- First: "the Logos Field (χ)"
- After: "χ" or "the Logos Field"

### Rule 2: Context Sensitivity
**Some symbols have dual meanings. Choose based on context.**

**Example:**
- **G in Grace context:** "Grace field" (theological)
- **G in gravity context:** "Newton's constant" (physical)

### Rule 3: Subscript Specification
**Always specify subscripts when relevant.**

**Example:**
- NOT: "lambda"
- YES: "lambda sub C" (λ_C)

### Rule 4: Tier Consistency
**Stay in your tier's register throughout the paper.**

**Beginner paper:**
- Use: "the Logos Field"
- NOT: "chi, the scalar Logos field"

**Academic paper:**
- Use: "chi, the scalar Logos field"
- NOT: "the Logos Field"

---

## CONVERSION WORKFLOW

### From Academic → Middle
1. Replace Greek letters with their English names
2. Keep technical terminology but add explanatory phrases
3. Simplify tensor notation to "field" or "coordinate"

**Example:**
- Academic: "χ, the scalar Logos field"
- Middle: "chi, the information substrate"

### From Middle → Basic
1. Replace technical terms with everyday equivalents
2. Remove mathematical structure language
3. Use narrative phrasing

**Example:**
- Middle: "integrating over the integration domain Omega"
- Basic: "adding up over all of creation"

## QUALITY CONTROL

### Red Flags (DO NOT DO):

❌ Mixing tiers in one paper (e.g., "the Logos Field (χ, the scalar Logos field)")  
❌ Using Greek letters in Beginner papers without full translation  
❌ Using "adding up" language in Academic papers  
❌ Inconsistent symbol usage (switching between G = Grace and G = gravity without context)

### Green Flags (CORRECT):

✅ Consistent tier throughout paper  
✅ First mention uses full translation  
✅ Context-appropriate symbol choices  
✅ Tier-appropriate mathematical depth
