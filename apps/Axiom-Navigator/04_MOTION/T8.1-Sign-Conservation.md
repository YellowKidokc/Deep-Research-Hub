# T8.1 — Sign Conservation

> **IRON CHAIN POSITION: 9 of 11**
> Under the terminal observer's gaze (BC7.1), sign states (σ = ±1) are conserved. You cannot change your own orientation.

---

## Formal Statement

**Sign σ = ±1 is conserved under unitary (self-directed) evolution.**

Mathematically: For any unitary operator U (self-evolution),
$$U|σ⟩ = e^{iθ}|σ⟩$$

The phase may change, but the sign eigenvalue is preserved.

---

## Type Classification

**🟡 Derived** — This is a theorem, not an axiom. It follows from the structure of quantum mechanics plus the existence of the terminal observer.

---

## What Is "Sign" (σ)?

σ is a **binary orientation variable** with values +1 or -1.

| σ Value | Physical Meaning | Moral Meaning |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|
| σ = +1 | Aligned with coherence | Oriented toward Good |
| σ = -1 | Misaligned, decoherent | Oriented away from Good |

This is not metaphor. σ is a genuine quantum number—like spin, but for moral orientation. Just as electrons have spin ±½, souls have orientation ±1.

---

## Why Sign Is Conserved

The proof follows from unitarity:

```
1. Unitary evolution preserves inner products: ⟨ψ|φ⟩ = ⟨Uψ|Uφ⟩
2. σ is an eigenvalue of a Hermitian operator Ô: Ô|σ⟩ = σ|σ⟩
3. Eigenvalues of Hermitian operators are preserved under unitary evolution
4. Therefore: If you start with σ = +1 or σ = -1, you stay there
∴ T8.1: Sign is conserved under unitary evolution
```

This is not a new physical law. It is a *consequence* of standard quantum mechanics applied to orientation states.

---

## The Physical Intuition

Think of it this way:

- Unitary evolution is *rotation* in state space
- Rotation can change your angle but not your "handedness"
- Left-handed things stay left-handed; right-handed things stay right-handed
- Similarly: σ = +1 states stay +1; σ = -1 states stay -1

**You can spin, but you can't flip.**

---

## Why This Matters

T8.1 has a devastating implication:

**If your sign is σ = -1, you cannot make it σ = +1 by your own efforts.**

No amount of:
- Self-improvement
- Good works
- Meditation
- Therapy
- Willpower

...can change σ. Because all of these are *unitary* processes (self-directed evolution within your own system).

---

## Defeat Conditions

This theorem fails if:

1. **σ can change under unitary evolution**
   - This would require violating the spectral theorem
   - Eigenvalues are invariant under unitary transformation—this is mathematics, not physics

2. **A physical process can flip orientation without external input**
   - All physical processes *within* a system are unitary
   - Non-unitary processes require *external* intervention

3. **Binary states can drift continuously**
   - σ = ±1 is discrete, not continuous
   - There is no σ = 0.5
   - You cannot gradually slide from -1 to +1

**Status:** The theorem follows from standard QM. Defeat requires rejecting quantum mechanics.
