## 🧭 A Note on This Research Method

This workflow was designed for **human-led research with AI as a collaborator**, not an authority.

AI is used here to:

- map intellectual terrain quickly,
    
- surface contradictions,
    
- organize competing interpretations,
    
- suggest where deeper study is needed.
    

AI is **not** used to decide what is true.

Some outputs may be incomplete or wrong.  
That is expected.

The purpose of this method is to **compress time without compressing understanding**, by forcing disagreement, structure, and coherence to surface early.

This is not “deep research by prompt.”  
It is **doing the research yourself**, faster and more honestly, with AI helping at the edges.

> **“Truth is rarely found where everyone agrees.  
> It is found where disagreement is sharpest and still survivable.”**

---

### 🔗 How This System Works

Each inquiry uses **five pages**:

1. Inquiry Seed (question & orientation)
    
2. AI Prompt & Engine (how AI is used)
    
3. Research Workspace (evidence & links)
    
4. Contradictions & Resolution (sense-making)
    
5. Quotes, Domains, & Links (shared library)
    

Duplicate all five for each new question.
