# Unified Field Operators & Definitions (UF_Operators)

## 1. Core Fields & Domains

### The Logos Field ($\chi$)
*   **Symbol:** $\chi$ (Chi)
*   **Definition:** The fundamental substrate of possibility and information from which physical reality emerges.
*   **Domain:** The set of all possible states $\Omega$.
*   **Codomain:** Complex probability amplitude $\mathbb{C}$ (in quantum analog) or Information Content (in semantic analog).
*   **Interpretation:** $\chi$ represents the "Word" or "Logos" that underpins reality.
*   **Master Equation Form:**
    $$ \chi(x,t) = \int [G \cdot M \cdot E \cdot S \cdot T \cdot K \cdot R \cdot Q \cdot F \cdot C] \, dV $$
    *   **Factors:**
        *   $G$: Grace (Negentropy)
        *   $M$: Mass-Energy
        *   $E$: Electromagnetism/Light
        *   $S$: Entropy State
        *   $T$: Time/metric
        *   $K$: Knowledge/Information
        *   $R$: Relational Coupling
        *   $Q$: Quality/Coherence Measure
        *   $F$: Faith (Observer Coupling)
        *   $C$: Consciousness

### Coherence Divergence ($\nabla \cdot \chi$)
*   **Symbol:** $\nabla \cdot \chi$
*   **Definition:** The measure of internal inconsistency or "sin" within a system.
*   **Condition:**
    *   $\nabla \cdot \chi = 0$: **Perfect Coherence** (Sinless/Christ-nature). The system is fully integrated and non-contradictory.
    *   $\nabla \cdot \chi \neq 0$: **Decoherence** (Sin/Entropy). The system contains internal contradictions or "leaks."
*   **Interpretation:** A non-zero divergence represents a loss of structural integrity in the Logos Field.

## 2. The Trinity Operators (Process Dynamics)

### The Father: Possibility Generator ($S_{max}$)
*   **Role:** The Source / Potentiality.
*   **Mathematical Identity:** Maximum Entropy ($S_{max}$) in the sense of "all possibilities are equally present" (Flat Prior).
*   **Operator $G$ (Grace):** An external injection of negentropy or constraint that raises coherence without satisfying local conservation laws.
    *   **Equation:** $G: S \to S - \Delta S$ (Grace reduces entropy freely).

### The Son: Structure Selector ($\Lambda$)
*   **Role:** The Pattern / Logos.
*   **Mathematical Identity:** The Coherence Operator ($\Lambda$).
*   **Constraint:** Enforces $\nabla \cdot \chi = 0$.
*   **Function:** Defines the "manifold of allowed states" $M = \{a : \nabla \cdot \chi(a) = 0\}$.

### The Spirit: Actualizer ($P$)
*   **Role:** The Breath / Motion.
*   **Mathematical Identity:** The Projection/Collapse Operator ($P$).
*   **Function:** Selects a specific state $s \in M$ from the possibility space to become "actual" in time.
*   **Mechanism:** Samples from the manifold $M$ based on the Born Rule analog: $P(s) \propto |\langle s | \Psi \rangle|^2$.

## 3. The Coherence Triad (Civilizational Scale)

### Variables
*   **$\Lambda$ (Logos):** The informational/structural core. The "Truth" bandwidth.
*   **$\Pi$ (Polis):** The social/political structure.
*   **$A$ (Anthropos):** The individual human agent.

### Dynamics
*   **Lead-Lag Relationship:** $\Lambda \to \Pi \to A$
    *   *Interpretation:* Information collapse (Logos) precedes social structure (Polis), which precedes individual formation (Anthropos).
*   **Composite Coherence ($C_{total}$):** Modeled as a geometric mean.
    *   **Equation:** $C_{total} = \sqrt[3]{\Lambda \cdot \Pi \cdot A}$
    *   **Implication:** If any single domain approaches zero, the total coherence of the civilization approaches zero.

## 4. Thermodynamic & Information Constants

### Entropy ($S$)
*   **Definition:** Measure of disorder or "Misalignment" within the system.
*   **Units:** Bits (Information Theory) or J/K (Thermodynamics).
*   **Context:** "Sin" is quantified as specific entropy (bits of misalignment per agent).

### The Sin Parameter ($b$)
*   **Definition:** The average bit-count of "sin" (decoherence) generated per agent per unit time.
*   **Usage:** Used for sensitivity analysis rather than absolute calculation.

### Resurrection Factor ($R_J$)
*   **Symbol:** $R_J$
*   **Definition:** The "Jesus Factor" or Resurrection term. A specific phase-transition parameter.
*   **Condition:** $R_J > \Sigma$ (Where $\Sigma$ is the total accumulated entropy/debt).
*   **Dynamics:** When $R_J$ activates, the system undergoes an inverse-entropy phase transition:
    *   **Equation:** $\frac{dS}{dt} = \alpha S - \beta R_J G$
    *   *Where:* $\alpha \ge 0$ is natural decay, $\beta$ is coupling efficiency, and $G$ is Grace input.
