## The Physics

### Quantum Zeno Effect
- Discovered by Sudarshan and Misra (1977)
- Named after Zeno's paradox (motion impossible if divided infinitely)
- Each measurement "resets" the wave function
- Frequent resets prevent evolution to other states
- Experimentally verified in atoms, ions, superconducting qubits

### Mathematical Statement
P(survival) → 1 as Δt → 0

Where Δt is the interval between observations. In the limit of continuous observation, the state cannot change.
