# P1_CONSCIOUSNESS — Constrains

> What P1 rules out.

## Eliminated Positions

| Position | Why Excluded |
|----------|--------------|
| Eliminative Materialism | Denies the existence of the denier |
| Epiphenomenalism | Consciousness as mere byproduct fails |
| Pure Objectivity | Observer is always present in observation |
| P-zombies as real | Philosophical zombies are incoherent |

## Boundary Conditions

- Cannot deny first-person experience without using it
- Cannot explain consciousness reductively (hard problem)
- Cannot remove observer from physics (measurement problem)

## Implication

The one who denies consciousness uses consciousness to deny.
Self-refutation is immediate.
