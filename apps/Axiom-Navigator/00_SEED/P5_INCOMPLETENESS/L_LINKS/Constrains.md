# P5_INCOMPLETENESS — Constrains

> What P5 rules out.

## Eliminated Positions

| Position | Why Excluded |
|----------|--------------|
| Complete Formalism | Godel refutes |
| Self-Grounding Metaphysics | Systems cannot ground themselves |
| Explanation Closure | "Why" cannot be eliminated |
| Logical Positivism | Verification principle is unverifiable |

## Boundary Conditions

- No system proves its own consistency
- No language defines its own truth
- No algorithm decides all algorithms
- No explanation explains itself

## Implication

The universe cannot be a closed formal system.
Something must ground it from outside.

P5 does not say what that something is.
P5 only says it must exist.

This is where Christ fits — structurally, logically, inevitably.
The shape of the gap IS the shape of the Logos.
