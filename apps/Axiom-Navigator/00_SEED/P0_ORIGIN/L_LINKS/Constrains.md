# P0_ORIGIN — Constrains

> What P0 rules out.

## Eliminated Positions

| Position | Why Excluded |
|----------|--------------|
| Nihilism | Self-refuting (nihilist exists to claim nothing exists) |
| Radical Skepticism | Uses knowledge to deny knowledge |
| Monism (strict) | Distinction is primitive — unity without difference is empty |
| Chaos-first cosmology | Information precedes randomness |

## Boundary Conditions

- Cannot coherently deny existence
- Cannot coherently deny distinction
- Cannot coherently deny intelligibility

## Implication

Anyone who argues against P0 uses P0 to do so.
The objection validates what it attempts to deny.
