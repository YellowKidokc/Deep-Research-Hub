# P0.1 — Existence

**[e4a1c29d-847f-4a32-9c5f-8b2e1a9d3f47] | P1 | Axiom | Existence**

> **Something exists rather than nothing.**

---

## 1. Formal Statement

Existence is the irreducible foundation of all subsequent claims. To deny it is self-refuting, as the denial itself requires an existent denier.

## 2. Type Classification

**🟢 Primitive** — Cannot be derived from anything prior. It is the precondition for any proof or investigation.

---

## 3. Mathematical Form
```
∃x (The set of all things is not empty)
```

**Negation:** Nothing exists (Ontological nullity).

**Falsification:** Demonstrate that non-existence is logically coherent as a stable state without presupposing an observer or a thinker.

---

## 4. Defense Grid

| Attack Vector | Response | Status |
|--------------|----------|--------|
| "Why is there something rather than nothing?" | The question presupposes the existence of the questioner. It cannot be asked from non-existence. | ✅ Blocked |
| "Existence is a brute fact" | Correct. That is the definition of a Primitive Axiom. | ✅ Accepted |
| "Quantum vacuum is nothing" | The vacuum is a high-energy field state with mathematical structure. It is emphatically something. | ✅ Blocked |

---

| Domain | Expression |

---

## 6. Theological Reflection

The divine self-identification in Scripture ("I AM") is not merely a name, but a statement of necessary existence. God is Being as such, the ground upon which the possibility of any "something" rests. 

*Note on Cain and Abel:* The presence of other "people" outside the garden in Genesis (implied by Cain's fear of being killed) suggests that existence and human development may have different thresholds or stages, but the fundamental fact of existence remains the starting point for all narratives.

---

## 7. The Logic Chain

1. **Existence (P0.1)** (this axiom)
2. If something exists, it must be distinguishable from "nothing."
3. **Distinction (P0.2)**
4. Distinguishability is the definition of a bit.
5. **Information Primacy (P0.3)**
