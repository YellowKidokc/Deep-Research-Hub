# P0.3 — Information Primacy

**[g6c3e51f-a69h-6c54-bee7-0d4g3c1f5h69] | P1 | Axiom | Information Primacy**

> **Distinguishability IS information; therefore information is ontologically primitive.**

---

## 1. Formal Statement

"Information" is defined as the capacity to distinguish states. Since existence requires distinction (P0.2), information is not an emergent property of matter, but the fundamental substrate from which matter and energy emerge.

## 2. Type Classification

**🟢 Primitive** — Definitional identity. We are not hypothesizing about information; we are identifying it as the source of all structure.

---

## 3. Mathematical Form
```
H(X) = -Σ p(x) log p(x) (Shannon entropy as the fundamental measure of reality)
```

**Justification:** Matter and energy reduce to informational patterns (spin, charge, position); information cannot be reduced further without losing existence (violating P0.2).

---

## 4. The "It from Bit" Connection

John Archibald Wheeler:
> "Every 'it'—every particle, every field of force, even the space-time continuum itself—derives its function, its meaning, its very existence entirely from binary choices, bits. 'It from bit.'"

In the Theophysics framework, this is not a metaphor. It is the physics:
- Particles are distinguished by **quantum bits**.
- Spacetime is distinguished by **coordinate bits**.
- Matter does not *carry* information; matter **IS** information.

---

## 6. The Trap

To deny P0.3, you must claim that matter can exist without any distinguishing properties. But a "thing" without properties is indistinguishable from "nothing," which violates the first law of existence (P0.1). 

If you have properties, you have distinctions. If you have distinctions, you have information.

**The chain is locked.**

---

## 7. The Logic Chain

1. **Existence (P0.1)**
2. **Distinction (P0.2)**
3. **Information Primacy (P0.3)** (this axiom)
4. If information is primary, it requires a medium or field to exist.
5. **Information Substrate (O1.1)**
