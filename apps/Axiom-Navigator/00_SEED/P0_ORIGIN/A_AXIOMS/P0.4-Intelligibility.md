# P0.4 — Intelligibility

**[h7d4f62g-b70i-7d65-cff8-1e5h4d2g6i70] | P1 | Axiom | Intelligibility**

> **Whatever exists is, in principle, intelligible.**

---

## 1. Formal Statement

Existence is not a chaotic, opaque static. Because it is built on distinctions (P0.2) and information (P0.3), it is fundamentally structured and therefore knowable. Intelligibility is the property that allows a mind to map the structure of the world.

## 2. Type Classification

**🟢 Primitive** — It assumes that the "message" of reality is readable. Without this axiom, science and logic are impossible.

---

## 3. Justification

If existence were inherently unintelligible, no law of physics could be written, and no prediction could be made. The fact that the universe follows mathematical laws is the physical proof of its intelligibility. This axiom prevents the objection that "distinction doesn't imply meaning." It bridges the gap between raw data and the Logos.

---

## 4. Domain Manifestations

| Domain | Expression |
|--------|-----------|
| **Physics** | The laws of nature are discoverable through observation. |
| **Mathematics** | Truths are derivable from axioms. |
| **Theology** | God is a rational Creator, not a source of chaos. |

---

## 5. Falsification

This axiom is falsified if a "truly unintelligible existent" could be demonstrated—an entity or state that exists but has zero internal or external structure, relationships, or distinguishable properties.

---

## 6. The Logic Chain

1. **Existence (P0.1)**
2. **Distinction (P0.2)**
3. **Information Primacy (P0.3)**
4. **Intelligibility (P0.4)** (this axiom)
5. If existence is intelligible, it implies an orderer.
6. **Order Requirement (O2.1)**
