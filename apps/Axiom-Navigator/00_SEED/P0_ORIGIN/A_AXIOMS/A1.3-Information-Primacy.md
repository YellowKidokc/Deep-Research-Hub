# A1.3 — Information Primacy

> **IRON CHAIN POSITION: 3 of 11**
> Distinguishability (A1.2) IS information. Therefore information is ontologically primitive.

---

## Formal Statement

**Distinguishability IS information; therefore information is ontologically primitive.**

---

## Type Classification

**🟢 Primitive** — This is not a hypothesis. It is a definitional identity.

"Information" *means* "the capacity to distinguish states." If you have distinguishability (A1.2), you already have information. They are the same thing described from different angles.

---

## The Definitional Move

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Information | That which distinguishes one state from another |
| Distinguishability | The property of being different from something else |
| **Identity** | Information ≡ Distinguishability |

This is not a claim about what information "really is" in some deep metaphysical sense. It is a recognition that the two concepts are co-extensive. Wherever you have one, you have the other.

---

## Derivation from A1.2

```
1. Existence requires distinction (A1.2)
2. Information ≡ that which distinguishes states (definition)
3. Therefore: Existence requires information
4. Therefore: Information is not derived from existence—it is co-primordial with it
∴ A1.3
```

---

## The "It from Bit" Connection

John Archibald Wheeler:

> "Every 'it'—every particle, every field of force, even the space-time continuum itself—derives its function, its meaning, its very existence entirely from binary choices, bits. 'It from bit.'"

Wheeler was not being poetic. He was stating the physics:
- Particles are distinguished by quantum numbers (bits)
- Fields are distinguished by values at points (bits)
- Spacetime is distinguished by coordinates (bits)

**Matter does not carry information. Matter IS information.**

---

## Defeat Conditions

This axiom fails if and only if:

1. **Matter/energy can be shown as ontologically prior to information**
   - This requires showing matter without any distinguishing properties
   - But "without distinguishing properties" = nothing
   - Self-refuting

2. **Distinguishability can exist without information**
   - But "distinguishability" and "information" are definitionally identical
   - Denying this is denying the meaning of words

3. **Information is merely epistemic (in the mind) not ontic (in reality)**
   - But distinctions exist whether or not anyone observes them
   - The electron's spin is ±½ whether or not we measure it

**Status:** All defeat conditions reduce to definitional contradictions or empirical falsehoods.

## Why This Matters for the Chain

A1.3 establishes that information is not emergent, not derived, not secondary. It is *there from the beginning*.

This is critical because:
- If information is derived from matter, then χ (Logos Field) is just a description
- If information is primary, then χ is the *substrate* of reality
- The entire framework depends on which direction the arrow points

```

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
    [Chain continues...]
```

**The chain holds.**
