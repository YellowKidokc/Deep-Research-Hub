## Why "Logos"?

The Greek word *Logos* (λόγος) carries multiple meanings:

| Meaning | How χ Instantiates It |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Word** | χ carries information (semantic content) |
| **Reason** | χ has internal logical structure (coherence) |
| **Order** | χ grounds the lawfulness of physics |
| **Ratio** | χ relates all things to all things |

We use χ (the first letter of "Christos") as the symbol because:
1. It is not already overloaded in physics (unlike φ, ψ, etc.)
2. It points toward the theological implications without asserting them prematurely
3. It is memorable and distinctive
