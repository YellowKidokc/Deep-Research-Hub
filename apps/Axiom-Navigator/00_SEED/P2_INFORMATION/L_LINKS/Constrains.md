# P2_INFORMATION — Constrains

> What P2 rules out.

## Eliminated Positions

| Position | Why Excluded |
|----------|--------------|
| Naive Materialism | Matter is information carrier, not foundation |
| Substance-first Ontology | Pattern precedes stuff |
| Information Nihilism | "No meaning" is itself meaningful |
| Pure Randomness | Random is defined against pattern |

## Boundary Conditions

- Information cannot be created from nothing
- Information cannot be destroyed (only transformed)
- Structure precedes material instantiation

## Implication

The universe is not made of stuff with accidental patterns.
The universe is made of pattern that manifests as stuff.

Wheeler: "It from bit."
