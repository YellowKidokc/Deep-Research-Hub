# P3_COHERENCE — Constrains

> What P3 rules out.

## Eliminated Positions

| Position | Why Excluded |
|----------|--------------|
| Pure Chaos Cosmology | Order is primitive, not accidental |
| Entropy-only Universe | Negentropy is real and unexplained |
| Random-first Ontology | Pattern precedes randomness |
| Meaning Nihilism | Coherence vs noise is objective |

## Boundary Conditions

- Order requires explanation (not just description)
- Entropy gradient requires source
- Fine-tuning is real, not anthropic selection

## Implication

The second law describes decay, not origin.
Where does the order come from that decays?

P3 forces this question without answering it.
That's the point.
