# P4_AGENCY — Constrains

> What P4 rules out.

## Eliminated Positions

| Position | Why Excluded |
|----------|--------------|
| Hard Determinism | Self-refuting (determinist "chose" to believe) |
| Epiphenomenalism | Consciousness without causal power fails |
| Moral Relativism | Ought requires real choice |
| Mechanism as Complete | Agents are not just physics |

## Boundary Conditions

- Cannot deny choice without choosing to deny
- Cannot ground morality without agency
- Cannot have responsibility without alternatives

## Implication

The determinist had no choice but to be a determinist.
This is not an argument. It is a demonstration.

P4 is not defended. P4 is recognized.
