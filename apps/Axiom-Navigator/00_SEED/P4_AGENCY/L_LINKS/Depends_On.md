| Stage | Why Required |

## Logical Chain

```
P0 (Existence) → Something exists
P1 (Consciousness) → Observation occurs
P3 (Coherence) → Stable structure exists
       ↓
P4 (Agency) → Choice is real
```

## Notes

- Agency without consciousness is mechanism, not choice
- Agency requires coherent substrate (can't choose in pure chaos)
- P4 does not strictly require P2 (agency is not information-theoretic)
