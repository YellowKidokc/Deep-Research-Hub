# David's case map (from "Key arguments for defending Christ's resurrection", 2026-09-26)

Thesis: if God made the world, He made the rules (physics, math, logic, morality), and all of it reflects Him.
The 18 arguments are entry points into that one thesis.

## Key arguments (K01-K18)

K01 Mathematics is not neutral: math's intrinsic properties map onto divine attributes, not onto naturalistic ground.
K02 Logic presupposes Logos: the laws of logic need a personal, rational ground to be binding.
K03 Consciousness is not reducible: measurement needs an observer outside the system (Watcher model).
K04 Moral conservation is non-optional (Law 9 / Noether): the Cross as the mechanism that satisfies the conservation constraint.
K05 Entropy is the default (Law 5): coherence needs an external sustainer; decay, not atheism, is the default.
K06 The framework itself is the argument: ten independent laws solved at equilibrium produce the Christian moral vocabulary.
K07 The universe is moral, not just physical: consequences are built into the structure.
K08 Free will is structurally real (the W variable): accountability is coherent only if will is a real degree of freedom.
K09 Beauty is signal, not noise: aesthetic structure is data pointing to a displayer.
K10 Love is not reducible to chemistry (Law 4 / strong force): agape is a binding force.
K11 The problem of good: goodness in excess of survival needs a ground.
K12 Language presupposes meaning: reality is sayable, and meaning is objective.
K13 The universe had a beginning (Kalam, extended): the cause needs capabilities that are divine attributes.
K14 Suffering is pedagogical: moral growth has a thermodynamic cost; the Cross is the proof case.
K15 Prophecy is pattern, not coincidence: the compound probability of convergent prophecy.
K16 The resurrection is a phase transition, not a violation: regime change in the Master Equation (chi crossing a threshold).
K17 The Trinity is mathematically necessary: unity, distinction and relation as the minimal self-consistent basis.
K18 Forgiveness is thermodynamically unique: external input that restores order without the expected cost.
