# Sūra I.: Fātiḥa, or the Opening Chapter.
### Section 1
![](https://sacred-texts.com/isl/gif/001/001_001.gif)
1. Bismi All _a_ hi a**l** rra _h_ m _a_ ni a**l** rra _h_ eem**i**
1. In the name of God, Most Gracious,  
Most Merciful.
* * *
![](https://sacred-texts.com/isl/gif/001/001_002.gif)
2. Al _h_ amdu lill _a_ hi rabbi alAA _a_ lameen**a**
2. Praise be to God,  
The Cherisher and Sustainer of the Worlds;
* * *
![](https://sacred-texts.com/isl/gif/001/001_003.gif)
3. A**l** rra _h_ m _a_ ni a**l** rra _h_ eem**i**
3. Most Gracious, Most Merciful;
* * *
![](https://sacred-texts.com/isl/gif/001/001_004.gif)
4. M _a_ liki yawmi a**l** ddeen**i**
4. Master of the Day of Judgment.
* * *
![](https://sacred-texts.com/isl/gif/001/001_005.gif)
5. Iyy _a_ ka naAAbudu wa-iyy _a_ ka nastaAAeen**u**
5. Thee do we worship,  
And Thine aid we seek.
* * *
![](https://sacred-texts.com/isl/gif/001/001_006.gif)
6. Ihdin _a_ a**l** _ss_ ir _at_ a almustaqeem**a**
6. Show us the straight way,
* * *
![](https://sacred-texts.com/isl/gif/001/001_007.gif)
7. _S_ ir _at_ a alla _th_ eena anAAamta AAalayhim ghayri almagh _d_ oobi AAalayhim wal _a_ a**l** _dda_ lleen**a**
7. The way of those on whom  
Thou hast bestowed Thy Grace,  
Those whose (portion)  
Is not wrath,  
And who go not astray.
