# What does the Bible say about long-suffering?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 15, 2015 
7 min read 
Longsuffering is the patient endurance of hardship through various kinds. It is perseverance ([Luke 8:15](https://ref.ly/Luke%208.15;nasb95?t=biblia)), endurance, and the ability to withstand attacks, insults, even physical suffering for long periods of time ([Romans 5:3-4](https://ref.ly/Rom%205.3-4;nasb95?t=biblia)). We are promised trials and tribulations in Scripture ([Matthew 24:9](https://ref.ly/Matt%2024.9;nasb95?t=biblia); [Mark 13:19](https://ref.ly/Mark%2013.19;nasb95?t=biblia); [John 16:33](https://ref.ly/John%2016.33;nasb95?t=biblia), etc.) and so we must learn longsuffering.
The word “longsuffering” occurs in the King James Bible in several places.
