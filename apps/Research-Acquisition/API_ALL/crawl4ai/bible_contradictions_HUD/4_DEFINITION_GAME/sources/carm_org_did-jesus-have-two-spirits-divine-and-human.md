# Did Jesus have two spirits, divine and human?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
August 11, 2025 
3 min read 
No, Jesus did not have two spirits. He had one, which is related to His personhood. Jesus is one person, not two persons. Therefore, Jesus has one Spirit. Let me explain.
In the context of the question, the term ‘spirit’ means the center of consciousness where the will, desire, and self-awareness are located. Jesus did not have two centers of consciousness. This is because Jesus is one person, not two.
Theologically speaking, the single person of Jesus has two distinct natures. This is called the hypostatic union. Within the single person of Jesus, there is a divine nature and a human nature (John 1:1, 14; Col. 2:9; 1 Tim. 2:5). Also, the attributes of both natures are described in the single person. This is called the communication of the properties (communicatio idiomatum). So Jesus is one person who has the attributes of both natures. After all, Jesus said, “I am thirsty” (John 19:28), demonstrating his human nature. And he also said, “…I am with you always, even to the end of the age” (Matt. 28:20), demonstrating His divine nature. By way of clarification, Jesus will eternally remain as a man who exists in a glorified body (1 Cor. 15:35-45).
