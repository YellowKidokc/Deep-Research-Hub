# John 8:58 and 10:30-33, “I am.”
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 05, 2008 
8 min read 
Jesus said in [John 8:58](https://ref.ly/John%208.58;nasb95?t=biblia), “Before Abraham was, I am.” This is a very important verse to [Trinitarians](https://carm.org/about-doctrine/what-is-trinitarianism/) because it is one of the places we use to show that Jesus is God. We maintain that Jesus attributed t![i am john](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/i-am-400x400.jpg)he divine name of God (“I AM” from [Exodus 3:14](https://ref.ly/Exod%203.14;nasb95?t=biblia)), to Himself, but this verse alone may not be sufficient to prove His deity. There is a combination of other verses that contribute to the doctrine. Nevertheless, many non-Christian groups deny that Jesus is God. Therefore, when they come to this verse, it must be dealt with. The reason is simple. If Jesus did say, “I am,” it would give strong evidence that Jesus was claiming to be God.
This paper will not attempt to analyze the Greek translation principles that have lead various Bibles to render [John 8:58](https://ref.ly/John%208.58;nasb95?t=biblia) as “I have been” or “I was in existence,” etc. Suffice it to say that the best-recognized translations which have sought literal renderings of the text have translated the verse as “I am”: NASB, NIV, KJV, RSV, etc.
The Jehovah’s Witness Watchtower organization claims that the best translation of [John 8:58](https://ref.ly/John%208.58;nasb95?t=biblia) is “Before Abraham was, I have been.” Notice that they do not have it say, “I am.” Is it legitimate for the Watchtower organization to **insist** that [John 8:58](https://ref.ly/John%208.58;nasb95?t=biblia) is best translated as “I have been”? Let’s take a look.
