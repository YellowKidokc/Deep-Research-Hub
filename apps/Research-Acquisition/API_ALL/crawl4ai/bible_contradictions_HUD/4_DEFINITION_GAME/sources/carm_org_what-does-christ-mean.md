# What does “Christ” mean?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 04, 2014 
7 min read 
![Christ \(in English and in Greek\)](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/christos01-min.PNG) The word “Christ” means anointed. It comes from the Greek Χριστός, _cristos_ , and is a translation of the Hebrew word for Messiah, מָשִׁיחַ, _meshiyach_ , which means anointed or chosen one. “He found first his own brother Simon and said to him, ‘We have found the Messiah’ (which translated means Christ),” ([John 1:41](https://ref.ly/John%201.41;nasb95?t=biblia)).
The term Christ is not the last name of Jesus. His only known name is Jesus, but he is said to be the Christ. The word is more of a title signifying the office of Christ as well as his purpose; that is, Jesus is the Christ, the anointed one, the Messiah who came to save his people ([Matt. 15:24](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2015.24;nasb95?t=biblia); [18:11](https://ref.ly/Matt%2018.11;nasb95?t=biblia)). He is referred to as “Jesus Christ” numerous times in the Bible: [Mark 1:1](https://ref.ly/Mark%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia); [John 1:17](https://ref.ly/John%201.17;nasb95?t=biblia); [17:3](https://ref.ly/John%2017.3;nasb95?t=biblia); [Acts 2:38](https://ref.ly/Acts%202.38;nasb95?t=biblia); [8:12](https://ref.ly/Acts%208.12;nasb95?t=biblia); [Rom. 1:4](https://ref.ly/Rom.%201.4;nasb95?t=biblia); [5:1](https://ref.ly/Rom%205.1;nasb95?t=biblia); [1 Cor. 1:1](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia); [2 Cor. 1:2](https://ref.ly/2%20Cor.%201.2;nasb95?t=biblia); [Gal. 1:1](https://ref.ly/Gal.%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia); [Eph. 1:2](https://ref.ly/Eph.%201.2;nasb95?t=biblia), etc.
Jesus is the Christ as is claimed in the New Testament.
