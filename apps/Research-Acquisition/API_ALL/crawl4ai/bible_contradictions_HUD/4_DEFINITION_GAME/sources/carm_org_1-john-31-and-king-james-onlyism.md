# 1 John 3:1 and King James Onlyism
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
October 31, 2018 
5 min read 
King James Onlyists often make a big deal of any instance where the KJV contains a clause that is not present in a [modern translation](https://carm.org/king-james-onlyism/the-missing-verses-in-modern-translations-an-overview/). Any place where a verse in the King James is longer than the same verse in another translation, the King James Onlyist will accuse the translator of “deleting” or “removing” something. They do this because they assume that the KJV is the perfect standard to which all other translations must be compared. Yet, there are also places where valuable clauses are present in modern translations but are lacking in the KJV. One notable example is in [1 John 3:1](https://ref.ly/1%20John%203.1;nasb95?t=biblia).
In the King James Version, we find this verse as:
“Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God: therefore the world knoweth us not, because it knew him not,” ([1 John 3:1, KJV](https://ref.ly/1%20John%203.1;kjv1900?t=biblia)).
