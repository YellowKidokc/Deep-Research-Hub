# How many supervisors were there? 3,300 or 3,600?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 28, 2008 
3 min read 
How many supervisors were there? 3,300 or 3,600? [1 Kings 5:16](https://ref.ly/1%20Kings%205.16;nasb95?t=biblia) and [2 Chronicles 2:2](https://ref.ly/2%20Chron%202.2;nasb95?t=biblia)
Either this is a copyist error or the people are being counted differently. If the people are being counted differently, it is interesting to note that the total number of supervisors is the same when we add the numbers from [1 Kings 5:16](https://ref.ly/1%20Kings%205.16;nasb95?t=biblia) and [1 Kings 9:23](https://ref.ly/1%20Kings%209.23;nasb95?t=biblia) together and also add [2 Chronicles 2:2](https://ref.ly/2%20Chron%202.2;nasb95?t=biblia) and [2 Chronicles 8:10](https://ref.ly/2%20Chron%208.10;nasb95?t=biblia) together.
However, the interesting problem is that in both places, the numbers are different, yet the totals are the same.
