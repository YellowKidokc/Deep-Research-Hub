# How many women were at Jesus’ tomb?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
May 04, 2023 
5 min read 
How many women were at the tomb of Jesus? The four different gospels mention different numbers. [John 20:1](https://ref.ly/John%2020.1;nasb95?t=biblia) says one. [Matt. 28:1](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2028.1;nasb95?t=biblia) says two. [Mark 16:1](https://ref.ly/Mark%2016.1;nasb95?t=biblia) says three. Luke also says three, but is a different three than [Mark 16:1](https://ref.ly/Mark%2016.1;nasb95?t=biblia). So, which is it, one, two, three, four, or more? Does it mean there is a contradiction, or is it just different views from different perspectives? Let’s take a look. ![women at Jesus' tomb](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/empty-tomb-03.jpg)
So, this brings the total to four, but possibly more since [Luke 24:10](https://ref.ly/Luke%2024.10;nasb95?t=biblia) says that “other women” were reporting, too. The verse does not necessitate the ‘other women’ were at the tomb. It could be that Mary Magdalen and Mary, the mother of James, told other women who later went to the apostles as a larger group.
