# Annihilationism and Daniel 12:2, people wake to everlasting contempt
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 11, 2018 
2 min read 
“Many of those who sleep in the dust of the ground will awake, these to everlasting life, but the others to disgrace and everlasting contempt” ([Daniel 12:2](https://ref.ly/Dan%2012.2;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Conditionalists interpret [Daniel 12:2](https://ref.ly/Dan%2012.2;nasb95?t=biblia) to mean that ‘eternal contempt’ is not experienced by the wicked, but is what the righteous experience for the wicked. Since both conditionalist and traditionalists could argue for their perspective using [Daniel 12:2](https://ref.ly/Dan%2012.2;nasb95?t=biblia), and the text does not seem to be explicitly clear on the issue, I don’t think this is a good verse for either side to use to support their position. In my opinion, it is too ambiguous and can be interpreted either way.
