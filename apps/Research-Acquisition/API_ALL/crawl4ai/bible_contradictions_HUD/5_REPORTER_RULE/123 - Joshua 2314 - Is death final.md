## Is death final?
### Yes, death is final.
> This day I [Joshua] am going the way of all the earth. [Joshua 23:14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/23.html#14)
> As the cloud is consumed and vanishes away, so he that goeth down to the grave shall come up no more. [Job 7:9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/7.html#9)
> Before I go whence I shall not return, even to the land of darkness and the shadow of death; A land of darkness, as darkness itself; and of the shadow of death, without any order, and where the light is as darkness. [Job 10:20-21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/10.html#20)
> But man dieth, and wasteth away: yea, man giveth up the ghost, and where is he? ... So man lieth down, and riseth not: till the heavens be no more, they shall not awake, nor be raised out of their sleep ... If a man die, shall he live again? [Job 14:10-14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/14.html#10)
> Yet he shall perish for ever like his own dung. [Job 20:7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/20.html#7)
> For in death there is no remembrance of thee: in the grave who shall give thee thanks? [Psalm 6:5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/6.html#5)
> Let the wicked be ashamed, and let them be silent in the grave. [Psalm 31:17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/31.html#17)
> O spare me, that I may recover strength, before I go hence, and be no more. [Psalm 39:13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/39.html#13)
> The dead ... whom thou rememberest no more. [Psalm 88:5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/88.html#5)
> The dead praise not the LORD, neither any that go down into silence. [Psalm 115:17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/115.html#17)
> For that which befalleth the sons of men befalleth beasts; even one thing befalleth them: as the one dieth, so dieth the other; yea, they have all one breath; so that a man hath no preeminence above a beast. [Ecclesiastes 3:19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/3.html#19)
> The dead know not anything, neither have they any more a reward. [Ecclesiastes 9:5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/9.html#5)
> For there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest. [Ecclesiastes 9:10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/9.html#10)
> They are dead, they shall not live; they are deceased, they shall not rise. [Isaiah 26:14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/26.html#14)
> For the grave cannot praise thee, death can not celebrate thee: they that go down into the pit cannot hope for thy truth. [Isaiah 38:18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/38.html#18)
> A sinful man ... his glory is dung, and worms ... tomorrow he shall not be found, because he is returned into his earth; and his thought is come to nothing. [1 Machabees 2:62-63](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/apocrypha/1mac/2.html#62)
### No, there will be a resurrection from the dead.
> Thy dead men shall live, together with my dead body shall they arise. Awake and sing, ye that dwell in dust: for thy dew is as the dew of herbs, and the earth shall cast out the dead. [Isaiah 26:19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/26.html#19)
> Thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, O my people, I will open your graves, and cause you to come up out of your graves, and bring you into the land of Israel. [Ezekiel 37:12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ezek/37.html#12)
> And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt. [Daniel 12:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dan/12.html#2)
> And these shall go away into everlasting punishment: but the righteous into life eternal. [Matthew 25:46](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/25.html#46)
> Thou shalt be recompensed at the resurrection of the just. [Luke 14:14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/14.html#14)
> Marvel not at this: for the hour is coming, in the which all that are in the graves shall hear his voice, And shall come forth; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation. [John 5:28-29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/5.html#28)
> That Christ should suffer, and that he should be the first that should rise from the dead. [Acts 26:23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/26.html#23)
> For if the dead rise not, then is not Christ raised. [1 Corinthians 15:16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/15.html#16)
> The trumpet shall sound and the dead shall be raised. [1 Corinthians 15:52](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/15.html#52)
> This is the promise that he hath promised us, even eternal life. [1 John 2:25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1jn/2.html#25)
> And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; ... and they were judged, every man according to their works. [Revelation 20:12-13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rev/20.html#12)
