# Did water baptism stop after John the Baptist?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
June 13, 2016 
3 min read 
Occasionally there are those who for some unknown reasons will try and maintain that water baptism stopped after John the Baptist. Sometimes it is asserted that the true baptism is [Spirit baptism](https://carm.org/about-the-holy-spirit/what-is-baptism-in-the-holy-spirit/) which replaces water baptism. Sometimes [Ephesians 4:5](https://ref.ly/Eph%204.5;nasb95?t=biblia) is cited which says there is “one faith, one Lord, one baptism.” However, this verse does not negate the fact that in the New Testament there are accounts of water baptism well after the time of John the Baptist. Let’s consider the following verses.
In [Acts 8:37](https://ref.ly/Acts%208.37;nasb95?t=biblia), [39](https://ref.ly/Acts%208.39;nasb95?t=biblia), the Ethiopian eunuch is traveling and Philip meets him. He had been reading through Isaiah 53 which says…
[Isaiah 53:7](https://ref.ly/Isa%2053.7;nasb95?t=biblia), “He was led as a sheep to slaughter and as a lamb before its shearer is silent, so he does not open his mouth.”
