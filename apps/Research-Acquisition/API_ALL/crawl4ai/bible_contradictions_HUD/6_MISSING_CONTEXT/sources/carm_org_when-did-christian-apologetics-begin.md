# When did Christian apologetics begin?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
March 21, 2022 
3 min read 
Christian Apologetics is the branch of Christian theology that deals with the defense and establishment of the Christian faith. Christian Apologetics began in the first century after Christianity and its claims came under attack by people who denied the truth of Christ’s work, miracles, resurrection, and teachings. As a result, historical documents (the Gospels and Acts), along with the epistles from Paul, Peter, Jude, and John, were written to correct false beliefs and erroneous accusations against the faith. The New Testament scriptures are the first apologetic work. ![Christian Apologetics](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/word-cloud-christian-apologetics02.png)
But this apologetic practice was not just against unbelievers. It was also to correct believers who were confused. Please consider this.
“Now we request you, brethren, with regard to the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering together to Him, 2 that you not be quickly shaken from your composure or be disturbed either by a spirit or a message or a letter as if from us to the effect that the day of the Lord has come. 3 Let no one in any way deceive you, for it will not come unless the apostasy comes first, and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of destruction, 4 who opposes and exalts himself above every so-called god or object of worship, so that he takes his seat in the temple of God, displaying himself as being God. 5 Do you not remember that while I was still with you, I was telling you these things?” ([2 Thess. 2:1-5](https://ref.ly/2%20Thess.%202.1-5;nasb95?t=biblia)).
