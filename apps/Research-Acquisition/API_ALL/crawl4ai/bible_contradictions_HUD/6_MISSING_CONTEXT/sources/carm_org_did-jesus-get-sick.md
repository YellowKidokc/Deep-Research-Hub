# Did Jesus ever get sick?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
March 29, 2024 
4 min read 
The Bible does not say that Jesus did or did not get sick. So, we can’t say for sure either way. However, we can explore the two possibilities: yes, He got sick, and no, He did not get sick. We know that Jesus was subject to the human condition. He got tired ([John 4:6](https://ref.ly/John%204.6;nasb95?t=biblia)), became hungry ([Luke 4:2](https://ref.ly/Luke%204.2;nasb95?t=biblia)), and slept ([Mark 4:38](https://ref.ly/Mark%204.38;nasb95?t=biblia)). Furthermore, He was tempted in all things that we are tempted ([Heb. 4:15](https://ref.ly/Heb.%204.15;nasb95?t=biblia)). He was, and still is, very much human as well as God (see Hypostatic Union). So, if He was subject to the physical conditions of a human, such as being tired, getting hungry, and sleeping, then it seems logical to say that He got sick. However, we see He came in contact with many sick people but was never affected by their sickness. Jesus touched the leprous man and healed him ([Matt. 8:2-4](https://ref.ly/Matt.%208.2-4;nasb95?t=biblia); [Luke 5:12-13](https://ref.ly/Luke%205.12-13;nasb95?t=biblia)). Jesus was at the home of Simon, the leper, and never got sick ([Matthew 26:6](https://ref.ly/Matt%2026.6;nasb95?t=biblia)). He was touched by the woman with the hemorrhage for 12 years, and she was healed, but He was not defiled ([Mark 5:25-29](https://ref.ly/Mark%205.25-29;nasb95?t=biblia)). So, it seems as though He was not subject to sickness. But, let’s take a look at this a bit more.[![Did Jesus get sick](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/Jesus-19.jpg)](https://carm.org/evidence-and-answers/it-is-improbable-that-jesus-rose-from-the-dead/attachment/jesus-19/)
When Adam sinned, the world was affected by his transgression. This is found in [Rom. 5:19](https://ref.ly/Rom.%205.19;nasb95?t=biblia), which says that by his sin, “the many were made sinners.” Also, sin entered the world through Adam ([Rom. 5:12](https://ref.ly/Rom.%205.12;nasb95?t=biblia)). This means that the physical world was also affected by sin, which is why we have disease, plagues, earthquakes, bad storms, etc. But if Jesus did not inherit the sinful nature from Adam, then it makes sense to say He was not subject to the curse, which included sickness. Now, this is not a definitive argument. But it makes sense to say He probably did not get sick.
However, if Jesus got physically sick, it would be due to the sin in the world around Him. After all, Jesus was affected by the sin of others in that they struck Him ([John 18:22](https://ref.ly/John%2018.22;nasb95?t=biblia)) and crucified Him ([Matt. 27:35](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2027.35;nasb95?t=biblia)). We can see that He could be affected by the physical world around Him. But was it to the extent that He got sick? We don’t know.
