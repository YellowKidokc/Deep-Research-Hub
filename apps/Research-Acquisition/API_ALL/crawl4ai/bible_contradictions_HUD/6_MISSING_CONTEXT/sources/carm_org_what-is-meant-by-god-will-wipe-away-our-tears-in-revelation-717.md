# What is meant by God will wipe away our tears in Revelation 7:17?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
January 14, 2026 
3 min read 
Revelation 7:17, "for the Lamb in the center of the throne will be their shepherd, and will guide them to springs of the water of life; and God will wipe every tear from their eyes.” This is a statement of the divine tenderness of God, who will be our comfort and who will deliver us from trial and tribulation in heaven.
The place of ultimate rest is with God. But in this life, we have so many difficulties, failures, heartaches, and losses. But when we get to be with the Lord in His glorious and wonderful presence, our sadness, worries, and fears will be gone. This is why God uses the metaphor of wiping away our tears to describe our future state. It means we will be without sorrow, regret, and suffering.
