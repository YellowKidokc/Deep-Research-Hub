# Did Jesus ever say anything mean to anyone?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 05, 2016 
5 min read 
Jesus said some pretty mean things to people. Following is a list of comments that Jesus said to religious leaders that were quite harsh. He condemned the hypocrites and false leaders who were putting road-blocks in the way of peoples’ salvation.
It is obvious that Jesus, who is God in flesh, the great loving teacher, also said some pretty harsh things to people. He called them hypocrites, fools, blind people, blind guides, whitewashed tombs, lawless, of their father the devil, and liars.
Why would Jesus be so harsh with people? He hated their [sin](https://carm.org/answers-for-seekers/what-is-sin/) and despised those who hindered others from coming to a saving relationship with him. Remember, saying harsh things to people is not automatically wrong. But it needs to be tempered with wisdom. We need to be careful when we speak harshly and we need to attempt to be loving and kind whenever possible. But it is not wrong, in and of itself, to speak the truth when dealing with peoples’ rebellion against God.
