# What does the Eastern Orthodox Church say about itself?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 10, 2025 
5 min read 
The Eastern Orthodox Church claims to be the only true church that can trace its origins back to Jesus. It claims to have a holy tradition passed down through the church fathers and cannot be corrupted. Though Scripture is honored in the EO, it is not supreme. In fact, Scripture is of equal authority to the Eastern Orthodox Church. So, instead of submitting to God’s word, the Orthodox Church submits Scripture to itself.
