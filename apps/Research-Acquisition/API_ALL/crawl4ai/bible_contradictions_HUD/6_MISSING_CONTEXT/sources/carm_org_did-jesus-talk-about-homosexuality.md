# Did Jesus talk about homosexuality?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
January 01, 2014 
3 min read 
There is nothing recorded in the four Gospels where Jesus specifically mentions homosexuality the way Paul did in [1 Cor. 6:9](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%206.9;nasb95?t=biblia). But, he does condemn it indirectly by affirming the Old Testament standard of heterosexual marriage as well as citing Old Testament references of condemnation where homosexuality was involved. Let’s take a look at some of the things he said.
We see Jesus’ condemnation of Sodom and Gomorrah ([Matthew 10:15](https://ref.ly/Matt%2010.15;nasb95?t=biblia)), and we know that homosexuality was involved in those two cities. We see Jesus confirming the heterosexual marriage pattern by citing where God made Adam and Eve, male and female ([Matthew 19:4-6](https://ref.ly/Matt%2019.4-6;nasb95?t=biblia)). Jesus mentions divorce in the context of male and female ([Matthew 19:9](https://ref.ly/Matt%2019.9;nasb95?t=biblia); [Mark 10:11-12](https://ref.ly/Mark%2010.11-12;nasb95?t=biblia)) – not male and male or female and female. And, Jesus supported the teachings of Moses ([John 5:46-47](https://ref.ly/John%205.46-47;nasb95?t=biblia)) in which a clear condemnation of homosexuality is taught in such verses as [Leviticus 18:22](https://ref.ly/Lev%2018.22;nasb95?t=biblia); [20:13](https://ref.ly/Leviticus%2020.13;nasb95?t=biblia).
So, even though Jesus did not specifically mention the act of [homosexuality,](https://carm.org/homosexuality-secular-movements/where-does-the-bible-say-that-homosexuality-is-a-sin/) he supported the Old Testament, including its condemnation of homosexuality and reiterated that marriage is between a man and a woman. So, did he condemn homosexuality? Yes, he did, by affirming heterosexuality as the norm and the God-ordained definition of marriage.
