# I am skeptical about God
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 19, 2009 
2 min read 
When people tell me they are skeptical about God, I want to find out what those reasons are for their skepticism. Is it because they believe there is no evidence for His existence? Well, there _is_ evidence for God, and it is recorded in the Bible. Are they skeptical because they don’t like how God does things in the Old Testament? If that is the case, then it’s up to that person to demonstrate a proper standard with which he could judge the God of the Bible. Or, are they being skeptical because they don’t want to believe in God, along with the responsibilities of what it means to be a follower of Him? But that is not a good reason. If God exists, we ought to follow Him. Or are they looking for an excuse to deny Him? Whatever the case, different people have different reasons for being skeptical about God’s existence. ![Skeptical about God](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/person-doubt-01.jpg)
A lot of times, those who are skeptical about God choose to remain skeptical even when various answers to their objections are provided. There’s a lot of subjectivity with people. What satisfies one person may not satisfy another. Oftentimes, when someone is skeptical about God, I ask what would make them not skeptical. If they don’t know, then why are they skeptical at all? If they do know, then perhaps we can address those issues if they were to be revealed.
Sometimes, skepticism is a way of hiding from the facts and God Himself. It is certainly possible for someone to suppress the truth of God’s existence ([Rom. 1:18-20](https://ref.ly/Rom.%201.18-20;nasb95?t=biblia)) because they don’t want to face Him or their own sinful behaviors, and so they look for excuses to deny Him.
