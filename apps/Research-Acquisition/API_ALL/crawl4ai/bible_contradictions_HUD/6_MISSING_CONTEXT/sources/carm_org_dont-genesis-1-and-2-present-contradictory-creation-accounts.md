# Don’t Genesis 1 and 2 present contradictory creation accounts?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 27, 2008 
2 min read 
Don’t Genesis 1 and 2 present contradictory creation accounts? No, they do not because they are intended to describe different things. God created day by day in Genesis 1, but Genesis 2 is a recap in a shorter version.
![Don't Genesis 1 and 2 present contradictory creation accounts?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/creation.jpg)
There is no contradiction between Genesis 1 and 2. Genesis 1 is a detailed explanation of the six days of creation, day by day. Genesis two is a recap and a more detailed explanation of the sixth day, the day that Adam and Eve were made. The recap is stated in [Gen. 2:4](https://ref.ly/Gen.%202.4;nasb95?t=biblia), “This is the account of the heavens and the earth when they were created, in the day that the Lord God made earth and heaven.” Then, Moses goes on to detail the creation of Adam and Eve as is seen in verses 7 through 24 of [Gen. 2](https://ref.ly/Gen.%202;nasb95?t=biblia). Proof that it is not a creative account is found in the fact that animals aren’t even mentioned until after the creation of Adam. Why? Probably because Adam designated their purpose. They didn’t need to be mentioned until after Adam was created.
