# The Great Banquet, Luke 14:15-24
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 04, 2008 
15 min read 
The Old Testament background for the parable of the Great Banquet is found in [Isaiah 25:6-9](https://ref.ly/Isa%2025.6-9;nasb95?t=biblia),
6 “And the Lord of hosts will prepare a lavish banquet for all peoples on this mountain; a banquet of aged wine, choice pieces with marrow, and refined, aged wine.  
7 And on this mountain He will swallow up the covering which is over all peoples, even the veil which is stretched over all nations.  
8 He will swallow up death for all time, and the Lord God will wipe tears away from all faces, and He will remove the reproach of His people from all the earth; for the Lord has spoken.  
9 And it will be said in that day, “Behold, this is our God for whom we have waited that He might save us. This is the Lord for whom we have waited; let us rejoice and be glad in His salvation.”
“A ritual banquet is one that marks some personal or interpersonal transition or transformation, held to give honor to those undergoing the important social change. As a ritual feature of hospitality, banquets indicate the transformation of a stranger into a guest ([Gen. 19:3-14](https://ref.ly/Gen.%2019.3-14;nasb95?t=biblia); [Luke 5:29](https://ref.ly/Luke%205.29;nasb95?t=biblia)) or of an enemy into a covenant partner ([Gen. 26:26-31](https://ref.ly/Gen.%2026.26-31;nasb95?t=biblia); [2 Sam. 3:20](https://ref.ly/2%20Sam.%203.20;nasb95?t=biblia)). Banquets mark important transitional points in a person’s life, e.g., Isaac’s weaning day ([Gen. 21:8](https://ref.ly/Gen.%2021.8;nasb95?t=biblia)); the weddings of Jacob ([Gen. 29:22](https://ref.ly/Gen.%2029.22;nasb95?t=biblia)), Samson ([Judg. 14:10](https://ref.ly/Judg.%2014.10;nasb95?t=biblia)), the Lamb ([Rev. 19:9](https://ref.ly/Rev.%2019.9;nasb95?t=biblia)), and in the parable of [Matt. 22:2-10](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2022.2-10;nasb95?t=biblia); the birthdays of Pharaoh ([Gen. 40:20](https://ref.ly/Gen.%2040.20;nasb95?t=biblia)), of Herod ([Mark 6:21](https://ref.ly/Mark%206.21;nasb95?t=biblia)); or the victory banquet hosted by God in [Rev. 19:17](https://ref.ly/Rev.%2019.17;nasb95?t=biblia). At the Last Supper Jesus changes the ceremonial banquet of the Jewish Passover into a ritual banquet effectively symbolizing the meaning of his impending death ([Mark 14:12-25](https://ref.ly/Mark%2014.12-25;nasb95?t=biblia) and parallels).”(Achtemeier, Paul J., _Harper’s Bible Dictionary_ , (San Francisco: Harper and Row, Publishers, Inc.), 1985.)
