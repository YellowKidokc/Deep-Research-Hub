# What does it mean to take communion in an unworthy manner?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
June 26, 2009 
4 min read 
This is an important question. The language of taking communion in an unworthy manner is derived from the text in 1 Corinthians. Let’s take a look.
[1 Cor. 11:26-29](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2011.26-29;nasb95?t=biblia), “For as often as you eat this bread and drink the cup, you proclaim the Lord’s death until He comes. 27 Therefore whoever eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in an unworthy manner, shall be guilty of the body and the blood of the Lord. 28 But let a man examine himself, and so let him eat of the bread and drink of the cup. 29 For he who eats and drinks, eats and drinks judgment to himself, if he does not judge the body rightly.”
There is much discussion about what it means to take the bread and cup “in an unworthy manner.” There are different interpretations.
