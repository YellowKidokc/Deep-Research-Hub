# An examination of atheism’s truth claims
by [Rob Shumacher](https://carm.org/rob-shumacher)
January 02, 2012 
10 min read 
Atheism is a faith/worldview that denies the existence of any supernatural deity. The Oxford English Dictionary provides the following definition: “To believe nothing of a designing Principle or Mind, nor any Cause, Measure, or Rule of things, but Chance . . . is to be a perfect atheist.”
Broken down, _A_(no) and _theism_ (god) simply means “no god.” Although the word itself and its meaning are certainly straightforward, there are many philosophical complications and issues that must be addressed and clarifications that need to be made where atheism is concerned.
The first and foremost problem that arises from the word “atheist” is the type of truth claim being made. Dr. Mortimer Adler describes the difficulty in this manner: “An affirmative existential proposition can be proved, but a negative existential proposition–one that denies the existence of some thing – cannot be proved.”[ ![examination atheism truth claims](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/atheism-01.jpg) ](https://carm.org/secular-issues/i-lack-belief-in-a-god/attachment/atheism-01-2/)
