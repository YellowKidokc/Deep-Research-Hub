# Who was King Abijah’s mother?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 01, 2008 
1 min read 
Who was King Abijah’s mother? King Abijah’s mother was Micaiah, a different spelling of Maacah.
This is a very simple answer. Maacah was simply another spelling for Micaiah. They are one and the same. (Haley, Alleged Discrepancies of the Bible, p. 317, and Paul J. Achtemeier, Harper’s Bible Dictionary, San Francisco: Harper and Row, 1985.)
