# What does it mean to be a Christian?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 22, 2008 
3 min read 
What does it mean to be a Christian? It means to trust in Christ’s sacrifice on the cross, be forgiven of your sins, and live a life according to His teachings. However, some people think that to be a Christian means you can’t see movies, that you can’t dance, can’t have a drink, or that you can’t have any more fun. That isn’t true. Christianity isn’t about rules and regulations to follow. It is about a relationship with the Lord Jesus Christ.
In the Old Testament, God gave the Law, which had to be followed precisely. There were instructions about clothes, food, what you could or could not do on the Sabbath, what you had to do in the temple, how priests were to carry out their functions, how strangers were to be treated, how people were punished, etc. All these things were to be observed properly and precisely since they all affected their standing before God. That was in Old Testament times, but now we have Jesus, and the requirements of the Law are no longer necessary for us in order to please God. ￼ ![to be a Christian](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/christian-being-a.jpg)
Being a Christian means you are changed on the inside–not controlled from the outside. It means that your heart has been changed by the presence of God. It does not mean that you are required to go to church, required to pay tithes, required to be good, required to do anything in order to stay a Christian. It means you desire to do those things because you’ve been changed.
