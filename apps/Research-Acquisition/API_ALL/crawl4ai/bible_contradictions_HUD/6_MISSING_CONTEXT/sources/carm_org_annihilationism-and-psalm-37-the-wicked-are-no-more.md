# Annihilationism and Psalm 37, the wicked are no more
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 11, 2018 
35 min read 
[Psalm 37:1-40](https://ref.ly/Ps%2037.1-40;nasb95?t=biblia) is about security for those who trust in God and how the wicked will be destroyed. It is not speaking about soul-sleep or some future Day of Judgment annihilation. Following are extractions from the Psalm.
Is this Psalm referring only to present destruction or does it include future annihilation?
“It may be that no passage of Scripture declares annihilationism (the ultimate destruction of wicked unbelievers) with clearer language than Psalm 37.”(rethinkinghell.com/2018/05/psalm-37-a-song-of-annihilation/)
