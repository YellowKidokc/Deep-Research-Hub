# What does the Shepherd’s Chapel teach?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
January 04, 2002 
3 min read 
The Shepherd’s Chapel is, at the very least, a controversial church with many unorthodox doctrines. The following list of teachings from the Shepherd’s Chapel is derived from scouring the Internet, chat rooms, bulletin boards, asking the followers of [Arnold Murray](https://carm.org/shepherds-chapel/who-is-arnold-murray/), and looking at the Shepherd’s Chapel web site. The Shepherd’s Chapel is considered to be a non-Christian cult. Nevertheless, we recommend that you avoid this group due to its aberrant teachings and hostile approach that many of its adherents display to those who do not agree with them.
