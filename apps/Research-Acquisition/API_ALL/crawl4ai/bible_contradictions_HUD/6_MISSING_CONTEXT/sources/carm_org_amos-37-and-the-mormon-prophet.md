# Amos 3:7 and the Mormon prophet
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
October 26, 2016 
5 min read 
“Surely the Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets,” ([Amos 3:7, KJV](https://ref.ly/Amos%203.7;kjv1900?t=biblia)).
The Mormon Church argues that this verse proves their belief in a modern-day prophet and continued revelation. There are several reasons, however, to reject this interpretation.
The context of the passage is specifically addressing God’s judgment of cities in the time of ancient Israel. The verse is obviously not asserting that God is sending a constant stream of unending revelation to update the prophets on everything He is doing everywhere in the universe as He guides and sustains all of creation.
