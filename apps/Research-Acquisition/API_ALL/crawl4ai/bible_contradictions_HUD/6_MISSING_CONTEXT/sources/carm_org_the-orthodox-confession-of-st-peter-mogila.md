# The Orthodox Confession of St. Peter Mogila
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 08, 2025 
264 min read 
The _Confession of St. Peter Mogila_ was formulated in Kiev and officially approved by the Synod of Jassy in 1642 as a doctrinal statement of the Eastern Orthodox Church. It was written in response to the Protestant Reformation and Roman Catholic influence. The document is in the public domain. The text is sourced from maksimologija.org, with the full synod record available at https://archive.org/stream/actsdecreesofsyn00orth/actsdecreesofsyn00orth_djvu.txt. CARM has reproduced the document for reference, with minor corrections to spelling and punctuation. I have underlined and provided color highlights for emphasis.  ![The Orthodox Confession of St. Peter Mogila](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/documents-confession-01.jpg)
