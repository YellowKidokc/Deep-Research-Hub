# What if Mormon missionaries told the truth about their religion?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
June 10, 2016 
8 min read 
If Mormon missionaries came to your door and told the absolute truth about their religion (the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints), how many people would accept what they really teach? After all, Mormonism teaches that God is an exalted man from another planet who has a goddess wife. It teaches that you can become a god if you keep the laws and ordinances necessary for what they call exaltation. Furthermore, when you pay a full 10% tithe of your income to the church, you have the potential of going to the temple, where you go through a secret ritual, learn secret handshakes, and get a new name. This is so you have the potential of becoming a God of your own world after you die. Such things are clearly not biblical.
