# Does the Bible tell us if there is life on other planets?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 22, 2008 
6 min read 
The Bible does not say if there is life on other planets. It simply says that God created the heavens and earth and put man on it. But this silence on the subject does not require that earth is the only place with life on it. But then again, this doesn’t mean there is life out there, either. ![life on other planets](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/ufo.gif)
Let me propose some reasons why I believe there is no life on other planets. Now, I should tell you that these reasons are simply exercises in biblical theology.
The Bible says there is one Trinitarian God. Jesus was God in flesh ([John 1:1](https://ref.ly/John%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia), [14](https://ref.ly/John%201.14;nasb95?t=biblia); [Col. 2:9](https://ref.ly/Col.%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia); [Phil. 2:5-8](https://ref.ly/Phil.%202.5-8;nasb95?t=biblia)), the second person of the Trinity, and died for our sins ([Rom. 5:8](https://ref.ly/Rom.%205.8;nasb95?t=biblia); [1 Pet. 2:24](https://ref.ly/1%20Pet.%202.24;nasb95?t=biblia)). If there were other beings on other planets that have sinned, then they would need a redeemer. God would have to die for them as well. (Actually God doesn’t have to die for anyone. He saved us because of His grace, not because of our worth.)
