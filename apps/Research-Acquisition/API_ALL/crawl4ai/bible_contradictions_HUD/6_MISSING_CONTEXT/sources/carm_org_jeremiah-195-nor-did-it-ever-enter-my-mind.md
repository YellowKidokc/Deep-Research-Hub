# Jeremiah 19:5, “Nor did it ever enter My mind.”
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 09, 2008 
1 min read 
“and have built the high places of Baal to burn their sons in the fire as burnt offerings to Baal, a thing which I never commanded or spoke of, nor did it ever enter My mind,” ([Jer. 19:5](https://ref.ly/Jer.%2019.5;nasb95?t=biblia)).
This verse is answered the same way as [Jer. 7:31](https://ref.ly/Jer.%207.31;nasb95?t=biblia).ex
Is the Lord actually saying He did not think of something? Even in [open theism](https://carm.org/dictionary/open-theism/), God knows all things actual as well as potential. That means God can know all things in the present tense as well as all possibilities of things that could exist. Certainly, God who knew the past sins of Israel would have thought about them doing such sin–as horrible as it was. So, it doesn’t make sense to interpret this as God admitting that He never thought of something.
