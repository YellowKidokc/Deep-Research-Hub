# Did Jesus meet the demoniacs in Gerasene or Gaderenes?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
3 min read 
Did Jesus meet the demoniacs in Gerasene or Gaderenes? [Matt. 8:28](https://ref.ly/Matt.%208.28;nasb95?t=biblia), [Mark 5:1-2](https://ref.ly/Mark%205.1-2;nasb95?t=biblia), and [Luke 8:26-27](https://ref.ly/Luke%208.26-27;nasb95?t=biblia). ![Gerasene or Gaderenes](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2009/03/person-man-tomb-02.jpg)
In Matthew, it says “Gadarenes,” while in Mark and Luke, it says “Gerasenes.” There are two possible explanations for the difference in spelling. First, there could be a minor scribal copying error in the spelling. This sometimes happened as scribes would occasionally make mistakes. The overall reliability of the Scriptures is still above 99% in their copying accuracy. This fact does not invalidate the inspiration and reliability of the Bible. Second, Gadara may have been the capital of the region in the Gerasenes, and Matthew may have simply referred to it by a more local term: the Gaderenes. This is a reasonable explanation.
Remember, a contradiction occurs when one account makes another impossible. Since there are reasonable explanations for the Bible Difficulties, it does not necessitate a contradiction. A difference in reference, spelling, and local knowledge does not mean God’s word cannot be trusted.
