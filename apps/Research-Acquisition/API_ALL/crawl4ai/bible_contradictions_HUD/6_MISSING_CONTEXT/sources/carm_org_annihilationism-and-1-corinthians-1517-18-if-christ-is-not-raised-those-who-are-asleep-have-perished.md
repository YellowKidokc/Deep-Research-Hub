# Annihilationism and 1 Corinthians 15:17-18, if Christ is not raised, those who are asleep have perished
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 11, 2018 
6 min read 
**Annihilationism and[1 Corinthians 15:17-18](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor%2015.17-18;nasb95?t=biblia), if Christ is not raised, those who are asleep have perished?**
Within the conditionalist (annihilationist) camp, some hold to a doctrine called soul sleep. I’ve found that conditionalists don’t seem to know what it actually means. I get a variety of definitions and this has resulted in a lot of questions they cannot answer (See [Soul sleep problems and questions](https://carm.org/annihilationism/soul-sleep-problems-and-questions/)). Nevertheless, soul sleep is the basic position that a person who dies physically still exists in his spirit, but is unconscious and unaware until he is reunited with his body at the Day of Judgment. The good people are then resurrected in glorified bodies, and the wicked are resurrected to be judged and annihilated.
[1 Corinthians 15:17-18](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor%2015.17-18;nasb95?t=biblia), “and if Christ has not been raised, your faith is worthless; you are still in your sins. 18 Then those also who have fallen asleep in Christ have perished.”
