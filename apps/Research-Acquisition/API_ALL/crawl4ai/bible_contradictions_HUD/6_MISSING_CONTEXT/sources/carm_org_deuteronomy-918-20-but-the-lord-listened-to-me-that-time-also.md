# Deuteronomy 9:18-20, “…but the Lord listened to me that time also…”
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 08, 2008 
7 min read 
When Moses intercedes for Israel and God spares them, does that prove that God doesn’t know the future? Open Theists seem to things so. Note how they handle the following verse:
“And I fell down before the Lord, as at the first, forty days and nights; I neither ate bread nor drank water, because of all your sin which you had committed in doing what was evil in the sight of the Lord to provoke Him to anger. 19For I was afraid of the anger and hot displeasure with which the Lord was wrathful against you in order to destroy you, but the Lord listened to me that time also. 20And the Lord was angry enough with Aaron to destroy him; so I also prayed for Aaron at the same time,” ([Deuteronomy 9:18-20](https://ref.ly/Deut%209.18-20;nasb95?t=biblia)).
[Deut. 9:18-20](https://ref.ly/Deut.%209.18-20;nasb95?t=biblia) is a recap of the Exodus account. Moses is simply reminding the people of Israel about their past sin and how the Lord was merciful to them. The account where Moses intercedes for Israel first appears in Exodus. For convenience sake, I have reproduced the comments on [Exodus 32:12-14](https://ref.ly/Exod%2032.12-14;nasb95?t=biblia) here which cover the same subject.
