# What does Jesus save us from?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 21, 2008 
6 min read 
Jesus is the most important figure in all of human history. He is God in flesh ([John 1:1](https://ref.ly/John%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia),[14](https://ref.ly/John%201.14;nasb95?t=biblia); [Col. 2:9](https://ref.ly/Col.%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia)), physically risen from the dead ([John 2:19-21](https://ref.ly/John%202.19-21;nasb95?t=biblia)), Lord ([Luke 24:34](https://ref.ly/Luke%2024.34;nasb95?t=biblia)), and Savior ([Acts 5:30-32](https://ref.ly/Acts%205.30-32;nasb95?t=biblia)). He came to die for sinners ([Rom. 5:8](https://ref.ly/Rom.%205.8;nasb95?t=biblia)) and to deliver people from the righteous wrath of God upon us.
I ask you, are you a sinner? Have you ever lied, stolen, lusted, coveted, or been angry with someone unjustly? If so, then you have broken the Law of God. God has said, “Y![Jesus saves from the wrath of God ](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/jesus-saves-400x400.jpg)ou shall not steal; You shall not lie; You shall have no other gods before Me; You shall not murder, etc.,” (Exodus 20). He has given the standard of righteousness and if you have broken any of God’s commandments then you have fallen short of that standard and are under the inevitable judgment of God. When you die, you will face Him and on the Day of Judgment, He will punish all sinners.
If you do not like this teaching, then throw away your Bible and turn from Christ, for this is the message of God’s word — that Jesus came to die for sinners and to save them from the wrath to come.
