# What does “died to sin” mean?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
May 20, 2010 
4 min read 
When the Bible says that someone has died to sin, it means that the Christian has died “with Christ.” It is not that we have actually died physically, but that we are, by faith, joined to the death of Christ on the cross. It was there, after fulfilling the law ([Matt. 5:17](https://ref.ly/Matt.%205.17;nasb95?t=biblia); [Gal. 4:4](https://ref.ly/Gal.%204.4;nasb95?t=biblia)), that he suffered death, which is the wages of sin ([Rom. 6:23](https://ref.ly/Rom.%206.23;nasb95?t=biblia)) though he never sinned ([1 Pet. 2:22](https://ref.ly/1%20Pet.%202.22;nasb95?t=biblia); [Heb. 4:15](https://ref.ly/Heb.%204.15;nasb95?t=biblia)). By bearing our sins in his body ([1 Pet. 2:24](https://ref.ly/1%20Pet.%202.24;nasb95?t=biblia)), becoming sin on our behalf ([2 Cor. 5:21](https://ref.ly/2%20Cor.%205.21;nasb95?t=biblia)), and because Jesus represented us on the cross ([1 Cor. 15:22](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2015.22;nasb95?t=biblia); [Isa. 53:4-6](https://ref.ly/Isa.%2053.4-6;nasb95?t=biblia); [1 Pet. 2:24](https://ref.ly/1%20Pet.%202.24;nasb95?t=biblia)) and died there, when we trust, by faith, in the sacrifice of Christ we are then declared to be dead to sin, dead to the law ([Rom. 7:4](https://ref.ly/Rom.%207.4;nasb95?t=biblia)), and justified ([Rom. 5:1](https://ref.ly/Rom.%205.1;nasb95?t=biblia)). This death that Jesus underwent, and our identification with it, is symbolized by baptism. ![died to sin](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2010/05/died-to-sin-168x300.jpg)
[Rom. 6:3-4](https://ref.ly/Rom.%206.3-4;nasb95?t=biblia), “Or do you not know that all of us who have been baptized into Christ Jesus have been baptized into His death? 4 Therefore we have been buried with Him through baptism into death, in order that as Christ was raised from the dead through the glory of the Father, so we too might walk in newness of life.”
