# Jeremiah 11:11 and the film “Us”
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
March 27, 2019 
2 min read 
The film Us, by Jordan Peele, is a horror movie about a family who goes on a beach vacation and encounters another family that is exactly like them. The doppelgangers are, of course, nefarious and the movie descends into a tale of fear, fleeing, and fights. But, early in the film, a man is holding a sign that says “[Jeremiah 11:11](https://ref.ly/Jer%2011.11;nasb95?t=biblia)” which has caused a surge in Google searches for that verse.
The scripture, which I will examine below, is appropriate in the context of the grim future of the family’s plight where disaster, in its varying, suspenseful forms, overtakes them and they desperately attempt to survive. So, there you go. For those who love horror movies, suspense, drama, and the like, this film is for you.
But, what of the verse? What does it say and what does it mean?
