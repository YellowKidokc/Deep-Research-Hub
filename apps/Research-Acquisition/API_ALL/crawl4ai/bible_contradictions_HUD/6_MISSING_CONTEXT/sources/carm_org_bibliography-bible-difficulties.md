# Bibliography
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 27, 2008 
3 min read 
When researchers examine a particular topic, it is important to go to original and/or authoritative sources to examine what these sources say about their own beliefs, doctrines, and teachings. Primary source documentation is key to doing proper research. From there, the researcher can conclude the intended teachings and doctrines. The researchers at CARM strive, whenever possible, to operate this way as part of their criteria. One of the main issues that researchers have with any active religious organization is keeping up with changing writings, doctrines, and teachings. This means that an article written today may be considered “out of date” within a short time. ![Bible Bibliography](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/bibliography-2-300x200.jpg)
But this should not be construed as putting forth erroneous material. Many times, organizations make changes, with few notifications made public. To that end, when recommending bibliographies or resources to our readers for research, this should be understood within the context of a possible limited timeframe or date restriction. If our readers find information out of date in any of our recommendations, we would seek and appreciate your help advising us by contacting us at info@carm.org and telling us which page (URL) and which exact issue is the problem. We have included a short bibliography of resources for researching the Bible Difficulties section.
