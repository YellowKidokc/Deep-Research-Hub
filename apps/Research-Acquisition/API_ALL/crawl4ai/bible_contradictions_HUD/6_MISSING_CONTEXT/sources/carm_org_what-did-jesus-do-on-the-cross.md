# What did Jesus do on the cross?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 24, 2013 
5 min read 
What Jesus did on the cross was to die for our sins so that we might be forgiven when we trust in what Christ has done there. When we, by faith, receive the sacrifice he offered, then we can have eternal life and escape the righteous judgment of God the Father. On the cross, Jesus voluntarily bore our sins. He allowed people to lie about him and kill him. He used the evil done to him to bring good to others. He sacrificed himself and demonstrated the greatest love of all. On the cross is where we are redeemed, and it is where our sin debt to God is canceled.
