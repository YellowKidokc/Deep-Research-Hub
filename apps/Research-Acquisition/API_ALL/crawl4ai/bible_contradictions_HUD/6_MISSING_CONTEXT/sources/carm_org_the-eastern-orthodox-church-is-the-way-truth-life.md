# The Eastern Orthodox Church is the way, the truth, and the life
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 12, 2025 
7 min read 
One of the things I’ve noticed with Eastern Orthodoxy is how it seems to attribute the qualities of Jesus to itself. Take, for example, [John 14:6](https://ref.ly/John%2014.6;nasb95?t=biblia) where Jesus said to him, “I am the way, and the truth, and the life; no one comes to the Father but through Me.” Does the EO church claim it is the way, the truth, and the life, and that no one can get to God, but through it? Essentially, yes, it does. Take a look.
![The Eastern Orthodox Church is the way, the truth, and the life](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/icon-church-01.jpg)
The Eastern Orthodox Church says it is the way to God. It functions as the mediator between God and people through its priesthood. It claims to be the medium of the priestly power of Christ. Therefore, it is the way to God from whom the graces of God can be obtained.
To have the truth of God and His revelation, you must go through the Eastern Orthodox Church because it is the pillar and ground of truth, the one true Church, that is equal to scripture, that cannot err, and whose doctrines are divine.
