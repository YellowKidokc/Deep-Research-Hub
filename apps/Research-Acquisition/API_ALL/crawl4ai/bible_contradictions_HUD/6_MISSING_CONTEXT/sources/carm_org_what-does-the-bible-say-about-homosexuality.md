# What does the Bible say about homosexuality?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 10, 2008 
14 min read 
The Bible doesn’t speak of homosexuality very often; but when it does, it condemns it as sin. Let’s take a look.
Homosexuality is clearly condemned in the Bible. It undermines God’s created order where He made Adam and Eve, a man and a woman, to carry out his command to fill and subdue the earth ([Genesis 1:28](https://ref.ly/Gen%201.28;nasb95?t=biblia)). Homosexuality cannot fulfill that mandate. Also, homosexuality undermines the basic family unit of husband and wife which is the God-ordained means of procreation. Again, homosexuality cannot do that. And, believe it or not, homosexuality is also dangerous to society.
Homosexuality has a heavy judgment administered by God Himself upon those who commit it and support it.
