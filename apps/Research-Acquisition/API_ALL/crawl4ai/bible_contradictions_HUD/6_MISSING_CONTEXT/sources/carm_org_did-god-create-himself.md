# Did God Create Himself?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
March 23, 2019 
2 min read 
Did God create Himself? No, He did not. The Bible tells us that He has always been God (Psalm 90:2). God is not created. He is eternal. Furthermore, for God to create anything He had to already exist in order to perform the action. But He could not do anything if He did not exist. Therefore, it is not possible for God to create Himself.
Someone might ask how it is that God has always existed. This is a difficult question to answer because we do not relate to God in His time frame and how He exists. We know that things have beginnings and that events have caused. But, this does not apply to God. He exists outside of our time reference in a way that we cannot comprehend since He is not restricted to time the way we are. He exists outside the universe and before He created it. Therefore, the issue of eternity, which deals with time, is difficult to apply to God because God does not relate to time the same way we do. Yet, we know that God is without beginning and, therefore, we say that God has existed forever. Nevertheless, some say that God is outside of time, but this statement is problematic since we do not know what that means. We cannot relate to it being outside of time.
In fact, I’ve recently modified my use of the phrase “outside of time” as it relates to God. I have written an article about it: [Is God outside of time?](https://carm.org/about-god/is-god-outside-of-time/) In that article, I explain that I no longer use that phrase as it relates to God.
