# Where did Jesus go after He died on the cross?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 03, 2014 
4 min read 
The Bible does not specifically state what happened to Jesus after He died on the cross. Because of this, there is debate over where He went and what He did. So, I will present differing views so you might know the scope of the answer and decide for yourself which position is preferable. Perhaps the best-known scripture that appears to deal with this issue is found in 1 Pet. 3:18-20. 
"For Christ also died for sins once for all, the just for the unjust, in order that He might bring us to God, having been put to death in the flesh, but made alive in the spirit; 19 in which also He went and made proclamation to the spirits now in prison, 20 who once were disobedient, when the patience of God kept waiting in the days of Noah, during the construction of the ark, in which a few, that is, eight persons, were brought safely through the water.”
When Jesus was made alive in the spirit, it does not mean that His spirit died, and then it became alive again. “Made alive in the spirit” is contrasted with “put to death in the flesh.” He first lived as a mortal man, but “…He began to live a spiritual ‘resurrection’ life, whereby He has the power to bring us to God.”(Jamieson, Robert, A.R. Fausset, and David Brown, Commentary Critical and Explanatory on the Whole Bible, (Oak Harbor, WA: Logos Research Systems, Inc.), 1998.) Furthermore, some Bibles (NIV, KJV, and NKJV) render the verse as “made alive by the Spirit,” which refers to the Holy Spirit's work with Christ." 'By the Spirit' translates the single word pneumati, which could refer to the third Person of the Trinity as the agent of Christ’s resurrection. (Walvoord, John F., and Roy B. Zuck, The Bible Knowledge Commentary, (Wheaton, Illinois: Scripture Press Publications, Inc.), 1983, 1985.)
