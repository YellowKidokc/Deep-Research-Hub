# Colossians Chapter 1
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 23, 2008 
26 min read 
_1 “Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, and Timothy our brother,”_
Paul presents himself as an official representative of the Savior. His appointment as apostle is not by the will of men, but by the Lord Jesus Himself.
“By the will of God” means that he had attained his high office neither through aspiration, nomination, nor usurpation. Paul’s call was by divine preparation: “But when God, who set me apart from birth and called me by his grace, was pleased to reveal his Son in me so that I might preach him among the Gentiles, I did not consult any man,” ([Gal. 1:15-16](https://ref.ly/Gal.%201.15-16;nasb95?t=biblia)). (For further references of this type see [1 Cor. 1:1](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia); [2 Cor. 1:1](https://ref.ly/2%20Cor.%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia); [Gal. 1:1](https://ref.ly/Gal.%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia); [Eph. 1:1](https://ref.ly/Eph.%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia); [2 Tim. 1:1](https://ref.ly/2%20Tim.%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia)).
