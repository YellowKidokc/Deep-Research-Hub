# Did Jesus ever claim to be God?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
August 29, 2014 
4 min read 
Yes, Jesus claimed to be God. He claimed to be “I AM,” which is God’s name ([Exodus 3:14](https://ref.ly/Exod%203.14;nasb95?t=biblia) with [John 8:58](https://ref.ly/John%208.58;nasb95?t=biblia)). For us to understand his claim in its cultural and biblical context, we need to read something in the Old Testament and then in the New Testament. Before God freed the people of Israel from bondage in Egypt, he appeared to Moses in a burning bush. Let’s take a look.
We can see that God said his name is “I AM WHO I AM.” He told Moses to say to the sons of Israel that “I AM has sent me to you.” So, God’s name is “I AM.” Let’s take a look at the Hebrew.
God said to Moses, “I AM WHO I AM”; and He said, “Thus you shall say to the sons of Israel, ‘I AM has sent me to you.’ ”
