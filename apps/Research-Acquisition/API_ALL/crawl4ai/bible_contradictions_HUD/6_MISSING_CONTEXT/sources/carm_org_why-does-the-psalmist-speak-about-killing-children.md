# Why does the Psalmist speak about killing children?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 01, 2008 
3 min read 
Why does the Psalmist speak about killing children? Critics often bring up this verse as an attack on the validity of the Bible, but does the Bible teach that it is okay to kill children?  ![killing children](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/skeleton-240x300.jpg) The answer, of course, is no it doesn’t. We must ask what the Psalmist was saying and why he was saying it.
“How blessed will be the one who seizes and dashes your little ones against the rock,” ([Psalm 137:9](https://ref.ly/Ps%20137.9;nasb95?t=biblia)).
The context of Psalm 137 is the Babylonian captivity. The Psalmist speaks of the captors tormenting the people of God (vv. 1-3), a promise to remember Jerusalem (vv. 5-6), and a curse against the captors (vv. 7-9).
