# Did Jesus stop being God when He was forsaken on the cross?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 16, 2013 
2 min read 
When Jesus was on the cross, and our sin was imputed to Him, and He said, “My God, My God, why have you forsaken Me?” ([Matthew 27:46](https://ref.ly/Matt%2027.46;nasb95?t=biblia)), did he stop being divine? Did He stop being the Son of God? The answer is simple and extremely important. No, He did not stop being divine. He did not stop being the Son of God. To say that He lost His divine nature and stopped being the Son of God is false doctrine and very dangerous. Please consider the following points . . .
I hope that it is clear that to say Jesus lost His divine nature on the cross when our sins were reckoned to Him is a dangerous and false teaching. Such a doctrine should be rejected by the Christian church as being heretical.
