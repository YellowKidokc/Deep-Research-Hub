# What is solipsism and is it true?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 24, 2014 
6 min read 
Solipsism is the philosophical idea that the only thing that can be known to exist is one’s own mind, and everything outside of one’s own mind cannot be verified. It means that a person can not know anything exists except for himself. Therefore, solipsism is a form of skepticism since it doubts the existence of everything except one’s own existence. Solipsism also holds that everything a person perceives to exist is a projection of their own mind.
It is difficult to argue against solipsism because whatever argument is raised in refutation to the position needs only to say that the other person and his arguments are a projection of his own mind. Though various proofs might be offered against solipsism, the solipsist needs only to deny the arguments as being a projection of one’s own mind.[ ![What is solipsism?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/solipsism.jpg) ](https://carm.org/about-philosophy/what-is-solipsism-and-is-it-true/attachment/solipsism/)
