# Where did the devil take Jesus first, the pinnacle or somewhere else?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
6 min read 
There is no contradiction or difficulty here between [Matthew 4:5](https://ref.ly/Matt%204.5;nasb95?t=biblia), [8](https://ref.ly/Matthew%204.8;nasb95?t=biblia), and [Luke 4:5](https://ref.ly/Luke%204.5;nasb95?t=biblia), [9](https://ref.ly/Luke%204.9;nasb95?t=biblia) and when the devil ![Where did the devil take Jesus](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/devil-03.jpg) took Jesus to the Temple. All we need to do is put the verses from each gospel side by side to see the order of events. When we do that, we see that Jesus was led to high places three times. Take a look at the chart below.
We quickly see that [Luke 4:5](https://ref.ly/Luke%204.5;nasb95?t=biblia) is the first mention of Jesus being taken anywhere and it simply says, “And he [the devil] led Him [Jesus] up and showed Him all the kingdoms of the world in a moment of time.” It isn’t until later in [Luke 4:9](https://ref.ly/Luke%204.9;nasb95?t=biblia) that Jesus is lead to the pinnacle on top of the temple. Therefore, the answer is simple: Jesus was “led up” and was shown the kingdoms of the world. Later He was led to the pinnacle.
