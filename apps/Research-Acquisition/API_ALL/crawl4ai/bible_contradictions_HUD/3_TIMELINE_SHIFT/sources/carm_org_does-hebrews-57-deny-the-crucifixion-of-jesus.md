# Does Hebrews 5:7 deny the Crucifixion of Jesus?
by [Ryan Turner](https://carm.org/ryan-turner)
July 27, 2010 
5 min read 
“In the days of His flesh, He offered up both prayers and supplications with loud crying and tears to the One able to save Him from death, and He was heard because of His piety,” ([Hebrews 5:7, NASB](https://ref.ly/Heb%205.7;nasb95?t=biblia)).
There are a number of Muslims who state that [Hebrews 5:7](https://ref.ly/Heb%205.7;nasb95?t=biblia) teaches that Jesus was not crucified. _(See: http://www.missionislam.com/comprel/crucified.htm and http://www.answering-christianity.com/hebrews5_7.htm for just two examples.)_ They believe that the statement “He was heard because of His piety,” indicates that God answered Jesus’ prayer not to be crucified.
First, the context of Hebrews 5 is how Jesus is our High Priest and Mediator. High priests would often offer prayers and petitions on behalf of the people of Israel. Therefore, [Jesus](https://carm.org/about-jesus/who-was-jesus/) as the great High Priest, did the same.
