# The Good Samaritan Luke 10:25-37
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 04, 2008 
11 min read 
A verse-by-verse explanation of the parable of the good Samaritan in [Luke 10:25-37](https://ref.ly/Luke%2010.25-37;nasb95?t=biblia). It is a great story with a lot of culturally rich context. Once you see it, you’ll probably never see the parable the same again.
Theme: What must I do to inherit eternal life?
![good Samaritan](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/good-samaritan-01-150x124.jpg) 25. And behold, a certain lawyer stood up and put Him to the test, saying, “Teacher, what shall I do to inherit eternal life?” Lawyer: One who is an expert in the Law of Moses. Often this individual was called upon to settle legal issues. “He stood up.” This is a social courtesy and a greeting of respect. Yet, in his heart he sought to test Jesus. This is a contradiction between his actions and his words. 26. And He said to him, “What is written in the Law? How does it read to you?”
