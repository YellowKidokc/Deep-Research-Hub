# Can a person lose their salvation as Hebrews 6:4-6 seems to teach?
by [Alex Carmichael](https://carm.org/alex-carmichael)
October 19, 2011 
14 min read 
There are many people who have taught (and others still teach today) that this passage clearly shows that a Christian can lose their salvation. And in a cursory reading, it does seem that this interpretation is correct.
But, as it is with many Scriptures, we should be aware of the “heresy of the first glance.”
So let’s take a close look at this passage and see what it is really teaching.
