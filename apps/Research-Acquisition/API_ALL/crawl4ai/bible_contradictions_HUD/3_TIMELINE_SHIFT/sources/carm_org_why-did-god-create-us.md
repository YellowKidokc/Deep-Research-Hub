# Why did God create us?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
3 min read 
Someone asked, “If God had to create us to demonstrate love fully, would that be saying that God didn’t have love fulfilled in the Trinity?” The answer is simple. God created us for His own glory. ![Why did God create us?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/image-of-god.jpg)
“Everyone who is called by My name, And whom I have created for My glory, Whom I have formed, even whom I have made,” ([Isaiah 43:7](https://ref.ly/Isa%2043.7;nasb95?t=biblia)).
If we say that God is completely sufficient in the Trinity and that complete sufficiency precludes any creative work, then we would not understand what complete sufficiency would mean – since God, who is completely self-sufficient and self-fulfilling, decided to create.
