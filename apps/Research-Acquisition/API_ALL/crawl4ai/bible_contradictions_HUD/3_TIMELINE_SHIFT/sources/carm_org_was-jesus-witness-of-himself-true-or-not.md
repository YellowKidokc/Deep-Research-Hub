# Was Jesus’ witness of Himself true or not?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 03, 2008 
4 min read 
Was it right for Jesus to bear witness  ![Witness](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/col1_16-300x211.jpg) of Himself? Does that invalidate His testimony? The gospel of John seems to say both yes and no. Does it contradict itself on this matter? Let’s take a look:
In [John 5:31](https://ref.ly/John%205.31;nasb95?t=biblia), the context is Jesus speaking about how He depends upon the Father and how He is seeking the will of the Father. [John 5:30-32](https://ref.ly/John%205.30-32;nasb95?t=biblia) says, “I can do nothing on My own initiative. As I hear, I judge; and My judgment is just, because I do not seek My own will, but the will of Him who sent Me. 31If I alone bear witness of Myself, My testimony is not true. 32There is another who bears witness of Me, and I know that the testimony which He bears of Me is true.”
The word “alone” is not in Greek but is included in the NASB translation, though not the NIV or KJV. Contextually, Jesus is not speaking as one alone, but as one dependent on the Father and that His judgments are true because He does the will of the Father.
