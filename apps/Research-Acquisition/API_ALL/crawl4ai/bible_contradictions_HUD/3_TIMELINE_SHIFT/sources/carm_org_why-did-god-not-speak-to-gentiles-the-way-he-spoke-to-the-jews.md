# Why did God not speak to Gentiles the way He spoke to the Jews?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
August 05, 2013 
3 min read 
We don’t know the exact reason why God did not speak to the Gentiles in the same way He spoke to the Jews because Scripture does not provide a direct explanation. However, this doesn’t mean we can’t explore some possibilities based on what is revealed in the Bible.
Scripture clearly shows that God chose Israel as His special people. They were the only nation to receive the Law and the revelation given through the prophets ([Rom. 2:17-20](https://ref.ly/Rom.%202.17-20;nasb95?t=biblia)). This special relationship is emphasized in verses such as [Deuteronomy 7:6](https://ref.ly/Deut%207.6;nasb95?t=biblia) and [14:2](https://ref.ly/Deuteronomy%2014.2;nasb95?t=biblia), which declare that the Israelites were set apart as God’s chosen people. [Psalm 33:12](https://ref.ly/Ps%2033.12;nasb95?t=biblia) reinforces this idea, proclaiming, “Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord, the people He chose for His inheritance.” ![Why did God not speak to Gentiles the way He spoke to the Jews?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/God-01.jpg)
God’s choice of Israel had a specific purpose (which was not based on any good thing in them). He called them to be separate from the Gentile nations ([Deut. 7:3](https://ref.ly/Deut.%207.3;nasb95?t=biblia); [Neh. 10:30](https://ref.ly/Neh.%2010.30;nasb95?t=biblia); [13:23-31](https://ref.ly/Neh%2013.23-31;nasb95?t=biblia)) and to live as a holy people ([Lev. 20:24](https://ref.ly/Lev.%2020.24;nasb95?t=biblia); [Ezra 9:1](https://ref.ly/Ezra%209.1;nasb95?t=biblia)). The reason for this separation becomes clearer when we consider God’s overarching plan for redemption. Israel was chosen to be the nation through whom the Messiah would come. The Israelite’s separation represented God’s holiness and work through which the Messiah would come for the people of the world ([John 3:16](https://ref.ly/John%203.16;nasb95?t=biblia)). [Genesis 12:3](https://ref.ly/Gen%2012.3;nasb95?t=biblia) and [Galatians 3:8](https://ref.ly/Gal%203.8;nasb95?t=biblia) highlight this, with the promise that through Abraham’s seed, all nations would be blessed.
