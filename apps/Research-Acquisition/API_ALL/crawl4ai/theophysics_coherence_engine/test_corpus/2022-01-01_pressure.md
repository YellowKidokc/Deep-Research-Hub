# Increasing Pressure (2022)

Challenges emerge as resources become scarce. Some members grow impatient with decisions, and conflicts arise more frequently. Trust wavers as inconsistencies appear in leadership statements.

While we try to maintain our values, tensions increase. Hope fades as problems multiply. Unity fractures as factions form. Some abandon their commitments, and peaceful resolution becomes harder.

**Concerns**:
- Resource scarcity causing stress
- Impatience growing
- Inconsistent messaging eroding trust
- Hope diminishing
- Faithfulness questioned
- Peace disrupted by arguments
- Unity threatened by divisions
- Pride emerging in leadership
- Selfish behaviors increasing
- Blame replacing grace
- Discipline weakening
- Joy replaced by anxiety
