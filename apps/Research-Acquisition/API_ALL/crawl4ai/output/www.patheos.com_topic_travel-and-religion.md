About Patheos Travel and Religion 
Many religious locations have unique history that help people build faith, learn something new, and appreciate diverse cultures. Explore what the world has to offer the faithful with us. Patheos writers include some of the top faith leaders and scholars in the world exploring travel and religion. Our hope is by providing religious travel information, stories and conversation, it leads to understanding, empathy and acceptance of all faiths.
