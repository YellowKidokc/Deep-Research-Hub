About Patheos Family, Parenting and Religion 
Parenting and family responsibilities can be challenging for people across different faiths. Learn how to best navigate the challenges of parenting. Families across religions all have a deep desire to keep the family unity strong and happy in this life. Patheos writers include some of the top faith leaders and scholars in the world exploring the family, parenting and religion. Our hope is by providing religious family and parenting information, stories and conversation, it leads to understanding, empathy and acceptance of all faiths.
