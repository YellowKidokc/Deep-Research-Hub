About Patheos Home and Religion 
From interior design to family home activities, explore ways to create a refuge from the world at home. Patheos writers include some of the top faith leaders and scholars in the world exploring the intersection of home and religion. Our hope is by providing religious home information, stories and conversation, it leads to understanding, empathy and acceptance of all faiths.
