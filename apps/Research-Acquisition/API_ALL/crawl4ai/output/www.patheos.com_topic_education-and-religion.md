About Patheos Education and Religion 
Education and religion can go hand in hand to help students find a faith-centered intellectual environment. Many top universities across the globe are affiliated with a religious institution. Learn about what is happening in the intersection of the secular and religious worlds. Patheos writers include some of the top faith leaders and scholars in the world exploring the interaction of education and religion. Our hope is by providing religious education information, stories and conversation, it leads to understanding, empathy and acceptance of all faiths.
