## Has there ever been a righteous person?
* * *
### No, there has never been a righteous person.
> Since childbirth is a dirty process that involves women, how could any of us be clean or righteous? We can't and we aren't. We are all unclean, unrighteous, filthy rags.
>> What is man, that he should be clean? and he which is born of a woman, that he should be righteous? [Job 15:14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/15.html#14)
>> We are all as an unclean thing, and all our righteousnesses are as filthy rags. [Isaiah 64:6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/64.html#6)
>> Who hath declared from the beginning, that we may know? and beforetime, that we may say, He is righteous? yea, there is none that sheweth, yea, there is none that declareth, yea, there is none that heareth your words. [Isaiah 41:26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/41.html#26)
>> There is none righteous, no, not one. [Romans 3:10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/3.html#10)
