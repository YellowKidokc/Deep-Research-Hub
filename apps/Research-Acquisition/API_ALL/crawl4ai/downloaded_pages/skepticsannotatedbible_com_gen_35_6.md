1 And God said unto Jacob, Arise, go up to Bethel, and dwell there: and make there an altar unto God, that appeared unto thee when thou fleddest from the face of Esau thy brother.
God said to Jacob, 
> Go to Bethel and make an altar to the God who appeared to you when you were fleeing from Esau. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#1n)]
2 Then Jacob said unto his household, and to all that were with him, Put away the strange gods that are among you, and be clean, and change your garments:
Jacob said to his household 
> Put away all your strange gods, be clean, and change your clothes.
3 And let us arise, and go up to Bethel; and I will make there an altar unto God, who answered me in the day of my distress, and was with me in the way which I went.
> Get up. We're going to Bethel to build an altar to God.
4 And they gave unto Jacob all the strange gods which were in their hand, and all their earrings which were in their ears; and Jacob hid them under the oak which was by Shechem.
So everyone gave Jacob all their strange gods (which were in their hands) and earrings (which were in their ears).
Jacob hid them under the oak tree near Shechem.
5 And they journeyed: and **the terror of God was upon the cities that were round about them** , and they did not pursue after the sons of Jacob.
As they traveled toward Bethel, the terror of God [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#2n)] on the surrounding cities, so no one bothered them as they passed by.
6 So Jacob came to Luz, which is in the land of Canaan, that is, Bethel, he and all the people that were with him.
_So Jacob came to Luz._ [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#3n)]
7 And he built there an altar, and called the place Elbethel: because there God appeared unto him, when he fled from the face of his brother.
8 But Deborah Rebekah's nurse died, and she was buried beneath Bethel under an oak: and the name of it was called Allonbachuth.
Jacob built an altar and called the place "Elbethel".
9 And God appeared unto Jacob again, when he came out of Padanaram, and blessed him.
10 And God said unto him, Thy name is Jacob: thy name shall not be called any more Jacob, but Israel shall be thy name: and he called his name Israel.
God appeared to Jacob again [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#4n)] and blessed him, saying,
> Your name is Jacob, but it won't be Jacob anymore. Israel will be your name from now on. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#5n)]
11 And God said unto him, I am God Almighty: be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall be of thee, and kings shall come out of thy loins;
12 And the land which I gave Abraham and Isaac, to thee I will give it, and to thy seed after thee will I give the land.
> I am God almighty. Be fruitful and multiply.
> A nation, a company of nations, and kings will come out of your loins.
> The land that I gave to Abraham and Isaac, I'll give to you and your seed.
13 And God went up from him in the place where he talked with him.
14 And Jacob set up a pillar in the place where he talked with him, even a pillar of stone: and he poured a drink offering thereon, and he poured oil thereon.
Jacob set up a pillar in the place where he talked to God. And he poured wine and oil on it.
15 And Jacob called the name of the place where God spake with him, Bethel.
Jacob names Bethel again. [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#6n)]

### Notes
  1. _God said unto Jacob, Arise, go up to Bethel ... and make there an altar unto God._ [[v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#1)] 
Presumably God is referring to himself here in the third person. The God who appeared to Jacob when he was fleeing Esau was [the man/god that Jacob beat in a wrestling match](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/32.html#wrestling).
  2. _The terror or God was upon the cities._ [[v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#5)] 
[What the Bible says about terrorism](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/terrorism.html)
  3. _So Jacob came to Luz._ [[v.6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#6)] 
[When did Jacob rename Luz to Bethel?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/bethel.html)
  4. _God appeared unto Jacob again._ [[v9.](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#9)] 
God appears to Jacob so often that he's becoming a bit of a pest.
[Can God be seen?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/seen.html)
  5. _Thy name shall not be called any more Jacob, but Israel shall be thy name._ [[v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#10)] 
God names Jacob "Israel" for the second time. (See [32:28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/32.html#28) for the first naming.) He says that Jacob will no longer be called Jacob. Yet Jacob is still called Jacob in the Bible, and even God calls him Jacob in [46:2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#2).
  6. _Jacob called the name of the place where God spake with him, Bethel._ [[v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#15)] 
The first time Jacob named Bethel ([28:19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/28.html#19)) the name didn't stick.
  7. _The midwife said unto her, Fear not; thou shalt have this son._ [[v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#17)] 
In the Bible, a woman is expected to die happily as long as she has a son.
  8. _The sons of Leah; Reuben, Jacob's firstborn, and Simeon, and Levi...._ [[v.23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#23)] 
[How many generations from Abraham to Moses?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/abe-moses.html)
  9. _The days of Isaac were an hundred and fourscore years._ [[v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#28)] 
Remember back [in chapter 27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/27.html#deceive), when Rebekah and Jacob conspire to rob Esau of the blessing of his dying father? Yeah, well, that was when Isaac was only 100 years old - 80 years before he finally dies here in chapter 35..
