# Does John 14:16 predict the coming of Muhammad?
by [Ryan Turner](https://carm.org/ryan-turner)
December 24, 2009 
4 min read 
Muslims argue that John 14:16 is a prophecy about Muhammad. They argue this from a verse in the Quran, “O Children of Israel! I am the apostle of God . . . giving glad tidings of an Apostle to come after me. Whose name shall be Ahmad” (Surah 61:6, Yusuf Ali’s translation, emphasis added).[ ![John 14:16 Muhammad](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/islamquran1.gif) ](https://carm.org/uncategorized/o-alcorao/attachment//)
The alleged equivalent for _Ahmad_ (“praised one”) in Arabic is _pericytes_ (“praised one”) in Greek. However, _pericytes_ does not even occur in John 14:16. Instead, the Greek word _paraclete_ (“helper”) occurs. Therefore, in order to support their argument, Muslims have to argue that the text of John has been corrupted. (I obtained many of these ten arguments from: Norman Geisler and Abdul Saleeb, _Answering Islam: The Crescent in Light of the Cross_ , Grand Rapids: Baker, 2002, pp. 157-59.)
First, there is absolutely no ancient textual evidence among all of the over 5,600 Greek manuscripts to place the word _pericytes_ (“praised one”) in place of _paraclete_ (“helper”). _(For reasons why we can trust the accuracy of the Bible, please see:[Has the New Testament been corrupted?](https://carm.org/evidence-and-answers/has-the-new-testament-been-corrupted/) Interestingly, there are over 5,600 Greek New Testament manuscripts, but if you include all of the translations from Greek-like Latin, Syriac, Coptic, etc., there are over 20,000 ancient New Testament manuscripts.)_ For a Muslim to argue that the correct reading should be _pericytes_ instead of _paraclete_ , shows his lack of understanding of the actual Greek text and the reliability of the copying of the New Testament.
