# Are we justified by faith (Romans) or by works (James)?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 25, 2008 
9 min read 
The Bible speaks of being justified by faith without works ([Rom. 3:28](https://ref.ly/Rom.%203.28;nasb95?t=biblia)) and by faith and works ([James 2:24](https://ref.ly/James%202.24;nasb95?t=biblia)). Is this a contradiction? No, it isn’t if you read the context of both sets of scripture. The answer is simple. In Romans, we are justified by faith before God. In James, we are justified by works before people ([James 2:18](https://ref.ly/James%202.18;nasb95?t=biblia)).
