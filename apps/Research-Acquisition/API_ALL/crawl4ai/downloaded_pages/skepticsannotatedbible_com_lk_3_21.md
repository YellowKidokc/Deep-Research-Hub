### Notes
  1. _In the fifteen year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar_ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#1)) 
This would be about 29 CE or so, since [Tiberius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiberius#Emperor_\(14%E2%80%9337_AD\)) was emperor from 14 to 37 CE. 
  2. _O generation of vipers_ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#7)) 
John the Baptist calls all of his contemporaries "a generation of vipers." 
  3. _The soldiers ... demanded of him, saying,what shall we do? And he said ... Do violence to no man._ ([v.14a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#14)) 
Good advice, except maybe to soldiers (which is who he was talking to). Soldiers are trained to "do violence" to people. 
  4. _Be content with your wages._ ([v.14b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#14)) 
No matter how unjust they may be? 
  5. _He shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost and with fire._ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#16)) 
[Did Jesus baptize anyone?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/baptize.html)
  6. _Thou art my beloved Son._ ([v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#22)) 
[How did God address Jesus at his baptism?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/this_or_thou.html)
  7. _Joseph, which was the son of Heli_ ([v.23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#23)) 
[Who was Jesus' grandfather on his father's side?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/joseph.html)
[Genealogy of Jesus (Matthew vs. Luke)](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gen_ml.html)
[How many generations were there from David to Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/generations.html)
  8. _Zorobabel, which was the son of Salathiel_ ([v.27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#27)) 
[Who was Zerubbabel's father?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/zerub.html)
  9. _Nathan, which was the son of David_ ([v.31](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#31)) 
[From which of David's sons was Jesus descended?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/davids_sons.html)
  10. _Pharez, which was the son of Judah_ ([v.33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#33)) 
Judah "went in unto" his daughter-in-law, Tamar, who was disguised as a prostitute. She conceived and bore Pharez, an ancestor of Jesus. ([Genesis 38:27-29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/38.html#27)) 
[How many generations must a bastard wait until his offspring can enter the congregation of the Lord?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/bastard.html)
  11. _Salah which was the son of Cainan_ ([v.35-36](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#35)) 
[Who was Salah's father](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/salah.html)? 
  12. _Enoch ... the son of Jared ... the son of Mahaleleel ... the son of Cainan ... the son of Enos ... the son of Seth ... the son of Adam._ ([vv.37-38](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#37)) 
[Was Enoch the seventh from Adam?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/enoch7.html)
  13. _Adam, which was the son of God_ ([v.38](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#38)) 
[How many sons does God have?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sons.html)
