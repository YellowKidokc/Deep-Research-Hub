## Did Jesus know everything?
* * *
### Yes, he knew everything.
> Now are we sure that thou knowest all things. [John 16:30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/16.html#30)
> Lord, thou knowest all things. [John 21:17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/21.html#17)
> Of Christ; In whom are hid all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge. [Colossians 2:2-3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/col/2.html#2)
### No, he did not know everything.
He didn't know who had touched him. 
> Jesus, immediately knowing in himself that virtue had gone out of him, turned him about in the press, and said, Who touched my clothes? [Mark 5:30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/5.html#30)
> And Jesus said, Who touched me? [Luke 8:45](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/8.html#45)
He was surprised at the centurion's faith. (People who know everything are never surprised.) 
> When Jesus heard it, he marvelled, and said to them that followed, Verily I say unto you, I have not found so great faith, no, not in Israel. [Matthew 8:10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#10), [Luke 7:9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/7.html#9)
And he didn't know when the end of the world would come. 
> Of that day and that hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels which are in heaven, neither the Son, but the Father. [Mark 13:32](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/13.html#32)
