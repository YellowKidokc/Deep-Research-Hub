# The Church Fathers on John 6:39
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
March 14, 2023 
5 min read 
The church fathers are not always in unanimous consensus. They differ on many topics. Here, we’re looking at [John 6:39](https://ref.ly/John%206.39;nasb95?t=biblia), and you will notice that they disagree with what it means. They say that God gives perseverance and that none perish (Augustine), that people can lose their salvation (Chrysostom), and that all given by God to Jesus are eunuchs for the kigndom of heaven (Jerome). Let’s take a look.
[John 6:39](https://ref.ly/John%206.39;nasb95?t=biblia), “This is the will of Him who sent Me, that of all that He has given Me I lose nothing, but raise it up on the last day.”
