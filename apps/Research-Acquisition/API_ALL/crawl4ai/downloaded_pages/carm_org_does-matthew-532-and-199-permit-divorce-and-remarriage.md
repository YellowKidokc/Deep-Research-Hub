# Does Matthew 5:32 and 19:9 permit divorce and remarriage?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
February 22, 2019 
15 min read 
Does [Matthew 5:32](https://ref.ly/Matt%205.32;nasb95?t=biblia) and [19:9](https://ref.ly/Matthew%2019.9;nasb95?t=biblia) permit divorce and remarriage? Yes, they do. Some Christians say that anytime a person is divorced, for whatever reason, he or she cannot remarry. Bu ![Does Matthew 5:32 and 19:9 permit divorce and remarriage?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/iStock_000023841294XXXLarge-300x300.jpg) t is there an exception, according to Jesus? Yes, there is, and it occurs in [Matt. 5:32](https://ref.ly/Matt.%205.32;nasb95?t=biblia) and [19:9](https://ref.ly/Matt%2019.9;nasb95?t=biblia), as listed above. Jesus tells us that a person who divorces his wife (spouse) and marries another commits adultery – except for when the offender has committed sexual immorality. In other words, a person would not be committing adultery by divorcing his spouse and marrying another person if the (ex) spouse had committed adultery. That is the exception laid out by Jesus. If it were not the case, he would not have said: “except for immorality.”
But, if adultery is the reason, why does it say “immorality” and not adultery?
