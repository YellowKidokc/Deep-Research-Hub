### Notes
  1. _I know that thou canst do every thing and that no thought can be withholden from thee._ ([v.2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/42.html#2)) 
[Can God do anything?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/cando.html)
[Does God know everything?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/knows.html)
  2. _I have heard of thee by the hearing of the ear: but now mine eye seeth thee._ ([v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/42.html#5)) 
[Can God be seen?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/seen.html)
  3. _The LORD said to Elipha ... My wrath is kindled against thee._ ([v.](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/42.html#)) 
Eliphaz and his friends spent many chapters defending God and blaming Job. Job defended himself, while saying nothing good about God, except that he could do everying and read people's minds. So what was God mad at them for?
  4. _All the evil that the LORD had brought upon him_ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/42.html#11)) 
[Did God or Satan bring evil to Job?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/job_evil.html)
[What the Bible says about Evil](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/evil.html)
  5. _He had also seven sons and three daughters._ ([v.13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/42.html#13)) 
God and Satan killed Job's first set of kids ([DWB](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/est.html#job)). He replaced them with ten more, seven sons and three daughters, just like the originals. The Bible doesn't explain why God didn't give Job twice as many children, like he did with his animals. And it says nothing about his wife (who in [2:9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/2.html#wife) said he should curse God and die). Was she still around? Or did God replace her with a newer, prettier wife? And, lastly, were Job's new children made in the usual way? And if so, who was their mom?
  6. _In all the land were no women found so fair as the daughters of Job_ ([v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/job/42.html#15)) 
Job's three new daughters were even prettier that his original ones! The new ones even had names: Jemima, Kezia, Kerenhappuch.
(This is where Job's Daughters International comes from.)
