# Should 1 Thessalonians 5:22 say “form” or “appearance”?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
October 31, 2018 
8 min read 
Often, discussions about Bible translations sidetrack away from the topic of “translation” and instead focus on manuscript differences in the underlying Greek and Hebrew texts. Yet, if the question is one of translation, the more interesting cases are the instances where everyone agrees what the original text says in Greek or Hebrew yet they seem to disagree on what it _means_ or how it should be interpreted in English. A classic example of this is [1 Thessalonians 5:22](https://ref.ly/1%20Thess%205.22;nasb95?t=biblia).
In the King James Version, the verse reads:
“Abstain from all appearance of evil,” ([1 Thessalonians 5:22, KJV](https://ref.ly/1%20Thess%205.22;kjv1900?t=biblia)).
