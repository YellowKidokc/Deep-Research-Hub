# The Genesis statement of Adam ruling over Eve is not God’s perfect will
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
2 min read 
Adam ruling over Eve is part of the curse. The egalitarians sometimes say that the judgment of God upon Eve, where God says “your desire shall be for your husband and he will rule over you” ([Gen. 3:16](https://ref.ly/Gen.%203.16;nasb95?t=biblia)) is not part of God’s perfect plan. Instead, it is a result of the fall. They then say that since Christians are not under condemnation and having to keep Old Testament Law, then the curse upon Eve is no longer valid and does not apply to women today.[ ![Adam and Eve, Adam ruling over Eve](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/man-woman-01.jpg) ](https://carm.org/women-in-ministry/the-genesis-statement-of-adam-ruling-over-eve-is-not-gods-perfect-will/attachment/man-woman-01/)
In the article Genesis 2, Adam and Eve, and Authority, I established that before the fall, Adam named the animals. Eve did not. Naming them was his first act of dominion, and it is a sign of authority. Also, Adam named Eve ([Gen. 2:22-23](https://ref.ly/Gen.%202.22-23;nasb95?t=biblia); [3:20](https://ref.ly/Gen%203.20;nasb95?t=biblia)) and exercised his authority over her–before The Fall. Thus, “he will rule over you” is not inherently contrary to the pre-fall authority structure.
If the curse of God is no longer valid, then what about the rest of that same verse that says a woman will have pain in childbirth? “To the woman He said, “I will greatly multiply Your pain in childbirth, In pain you shall bring forth children; Yet your desire shall be for your husband, And he shall rule over you” ([Gen. 3:16](https://ref.ly/Gen.%203.16;nasb95?t=biblia)). Obviously, women still have pain in childbirth; so, apparently, the curse is still valid. Even if “he will rule over you” were part of the curse, the curse is presently still in effect.
