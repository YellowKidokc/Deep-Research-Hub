# Where was Jesus born?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
November 12, 2021 
13 min read 
Jesus was born in Bethlehem, a small town a few miles from Jerusalem where King David was born ([1 Samuel 17:12](https://ref.ly/1%20Sam%2017.12;nasb95?t=biblia), etc.) and where the prophet Micah had foretold the Messiah would come ([Micah 5:2](https://ref.ly/Micah%205.2;nasb95?t=biblia)). Evidence from historical sources, archaeology, and long-standing tradition place the exact location of his birth in a cave just outside the town proper, beneath where the Church of the Nativity now stands. ![The nativity in Bethlehem](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/nativity-300x223.jpg)
Our primary sources for Jesus’ birth are the gospels of Matthew and Luke. Both place the event in Bethlehem. As Matthew explains:
“Now after Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of Herod the king, magi from the east arrived in Jerusalem, saying, ‘Where is He who has been born King of the Jews? For we saw His star in the east and have come to worship Him,'” ([Matthew 2:1-2](https://ref.ly/Matt%202.1-2;nasb95?t=biblia)).
