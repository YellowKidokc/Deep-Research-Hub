# Does Matthew 5:48 teach that we can become gods?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
June 10, 2016 
4 min read 
Does [Matthew 5:48](https://ref.ly/Matt%205.48;nasb95?t=biblia) teach that we can become gods? In [Matthew 5:48](https://ref.ly/Matt%205.48;nasb95?t=biblia), in the midst of His famous “[Sermon on the Mount](https://carm.org/other-questions/what-is-the-sermon-on-the-mount/),” Jesus uttered the words:
“Therefore you are to be perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect.”
[Mormons](https://carm.org/mormonism/mormonism-in-a-nutshell/) have found in these words the promise of [their own exaltation](https://carm.org/mormonism/the-mormon-law-of-eternal-progression/). They believe this verse means that they can one day become gods just as the Father in heaven is now a God, by obedience to the “laws and ordinances of the gospel.” A careful, examination of the context, however, gives us a very different picture of Jesus’ meaning.
