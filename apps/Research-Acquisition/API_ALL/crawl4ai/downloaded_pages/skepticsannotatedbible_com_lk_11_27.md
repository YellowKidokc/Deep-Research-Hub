### CHAPTER 11
The Our Father (or The Lord's Prayer)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 11:1-4, For the Lord's Prayer see [Mt 6:9-13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/6.html#S3)
1 And it came to pass, that, as he was praying in a certain place, when he ceased, one of his disciples said unto him, Lord, teach us to pray, as John also taught his disciples.
2 And he said unto them, When ye pray, say, Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done, as in heaven, so in earth.
3 Give us day by day our daily bread.
4 And forgive us our sins; for we also forgive every one that is indebted to us. And lead us not into temptation; but deliver us from evil.
As Jesus was praying, one of his disciples said to him,
> Teach us to pray, like John (the Baptist) taught his disciples.
Jesus said,
> When you pray, say:
>> Our Father, who art in heaven, [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#1n)]  
>  hallowed by thy name;  
>  thy kingdom come; thy will be done  
>  on earth, as it is in heaven. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#2n)]  
>  Give us this day our daily bread;  
>  and forgive us our trespasses  
>  as we forgive those who trespass against us.  
>  And lead us not into temptation, [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#3n)]  
>  but deliver us from evil.
Just ask God (and he'll give it to you)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 11:5-13, Cf. [Mt 7:7-11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/7.html#S3)
5 And he said unto them, Which of you shall have a friend, and shall go unto him at midnight, and say unto him, Friend, lend me three loaves;
6 For a friend of mine in his journey is come to me, and I have nothing to set before him?
7 And he from within shall answer and say, Trouble me not: the door is now shut, and my children are with me in bed; I cannot rise and give thee.
8 I say unto you, Though he will not rise and give him, because he is his friend, yet because of his importunity he will rise and give him as many as he needeth.
9 And I say unto you, Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.
> God will give you whatever your ask him for.
10 For every one that asketh receiveth; and he that seeketh findeth; and to him that knocketh it shall be opened.
> If you ask for something, you'll get it.
> If you look for something, you'll find it. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#4n)]
> And if you knock on a door, it'll open.
11 If a son shall ask bread of any of you that is a father, will he give him a stone? or if he ask a fish, will he for a fish give him a serpent?
> If your son asks for bread, do you give him a stone?
> If he asks for a fish, do you give him a serpent?
12 Or if he shall ask an egg, will he offer him a scorpion?
> Or if he asks for an egg, do you give him a scorpion?
13 If ye then, being evil, know how to give good gifts unto your children: how much more shall your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to them that ask him?
> If an evil person like you gives good things to your children,
> then God and the Holy Spirit will give good things to you.

15 But some of them said, He casteth out devils through Beelzebub the chief of the devils. 
Some said,
> He casts out devils through the power of Beelzebub, the chief of the devils. [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#6n)]
16 And others, tempting him, sought of him a sign from heaven. 
And others wanted Jesus to show them a sign from heaven.
17 But he, knowing their thoughts, said unto them, Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation; and a house divided against a house falleth.
Jesus knew what they were thinking, and said,
> A divided kingdom will be destroyed.
18 If Satan also be divided against himself, how shall his kingdom stand? because ye say that I cast out devils through Beelzebub.
> Can Satan's kingdom stand if it's divided against itself?
19 And if I by Beelzebub cast out devils, by whom do your sons cast them out? therefore shall they be your judges.
> If I'm casting out devils by the power of Beelzebub, then by whom do you cast them out? [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#7n)]
20 But if I with the finger of God cast out devils, no doubt the kingdom of God is come upon you.
21 When a strong man armed keepeth his palace, his goods are in peace:
22 But when a stronger than he shall come upon him, and overcome him, he taketh from him all his armour wherein he trusted, and divideth his spoils.
> But if I'm casting them out with the finger of God, [[8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#8n)]
> then the kingdom of God has come upon you.
23 He that is not with me is against me: and he that gathereth not with me scattereth.
> If you're not with me, you're against me. [[9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#9n)]

Jesus said,
> When an unclean spirit goes out of a person, he looks for another place to stay. If he doesn't find any, he says to himself, "I'll go back to my old house." [[10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#10n)]
25 And when he cometh, he findeth it swept and garnished.
26 Then goeth he, and taketh to him seven other spirits more wicked than himself; and they enter in, and dwell there: and the last state of that man is worse than the first.
> If he finds it swept and clean, he takes seven other spirits more wicked than himself and brings them back with him. And that person is even worse off than before.

### Notes
  1. _Our Father_ ([v.2a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#2)) 
This prayer (or slightly different versions of it) is found in two places in the gospels. Here and in [Matthew 6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/6.html#9), where it's included in the sermon on the mount.
Matthew's gospel has the ending used in the Protestant version (For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.), which they call "The Lord's Prayer".
Catholics use Luke's version, which omits the ending the Protestants use, and they call it "The Our Father."
[Are we all God's children?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gods_children.html)
  2. _Thy will be done, as in heaven, so in earth._ ([v.2b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#2)) 
[Is God's will always done in heaven?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/will.html)
  3. _And lead us not into temptation._ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#4)) 
[James 1:13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jas/1.html#13) says that God doesn't tempt people. If so then why must we ask him not to tempt us?
[Pope Francis is changing the Catholic "Our Father"](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2017/12/08/569385769/pope-francis-suggests-changing-the-words-to-lord-s-prayer) from "And lead us not into temptation" to "Do not let us enter into temptation."
[Does God tempt people?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/tempted_by_God.html)
  4. _Seek, and ye shall find ... For ... he that seeketh findeth_ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#10)) 
[Can God be found?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/found.html)
  5. _He was casting out a devil, and it was dumb. And ... when the devil was gone out, the dumb spake._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#14)) 
People who cannot speak are possessed with devils.
  6. _Some of them said, He casteth out devils through Beelzebub the chief of the devils ... and others ... sought of him a sign from heaven._ ([vv.15-16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#15)) 
A reasonable hypothesis (assuming devils exist) and a fair request (assuming a god exists).
  7. _If I by Beelzebub cast out devils, by whom do your sons cast them out?_ ([v.19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#19)) 
Jesus believed the Pharisees could cast out demons, too!
  8. _I with the finger of God cast out devils._ ([v.20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#20)) 
[What the Bible says about God's finger](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/Finger-of-God.html)
  9. _He that is not with me is against me._ ([v.23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#23)) 
[Who is for or against Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/withme.html)
  10. _When the unclean spirit is gone out of a man...._ ([v.24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#24)) 
Jesus says it is impossible to get rid of unclean spirits. If you manage to evict one, he'll soon return with seven others "more wicked than himself" and you'll be worse off than you were before. So just learn to live with whatever unclean spirits that are currently possessing you.
  11. _Rather, blessed are they that hear the word of God, and keep it._ ([v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#28)) 
Jesus insults his mother (the Most Holy Blessed Virgin Mary)
[Was Mary blessed?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/mary.html)
[Did Jesus sin?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/interp/jesus_sin.html)
  12. _There shall no sign be given it, but the sign of Jonah the prophet._ ([v.29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#29)) 
[Did Jesus perform any signs and wonders?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/signs.html)
  13. _Behold, a greater than Solomon is here._ ([v.31](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#31)) 
[Who was the greatest: Jesus or Solomon?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/greatest.html)
  14. _When thine eye is evil_ ([v.34](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#34)) 
There's nothing worse than an evil eye.
  15. _When the Pharisee saw it, he marvelled that he had not first washed before dinner._ ([v.38](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#38)) 
Jesus is criticized for not washing his hands before eating, so he calls his critics fools, thus making himself, by his own standards ([Matthew 5:22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/5.html#22)), worthy of "hell fire."
[Is it OK to call someone a fool?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/fool.html)
  16. From the blood of Abel unto the blood of Zachariah ... It shall be required of this generation. ([v.51](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#51)) 
Jesus blames all the deaths of the prophets (from Abel to Zacharias) on his generation.
