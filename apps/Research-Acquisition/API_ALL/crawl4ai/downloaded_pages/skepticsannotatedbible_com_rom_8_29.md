### CHAPTER 8
If Christ is in you, your body is dead
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 8:1-21
1 There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.
2 For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free from the law of sin and death.
3 For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh, God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh:
4 That the righteousness of the law might be fulfilled in us, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.
5 For they that are after the flesh do mind the things of the flesh; but they that are after the Spirit the things of the Spirit.
6 For to be carnally minded is death; but to be spiritually minded is life and peace.
7 Because the carnal mind is enmity against God: for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be.
8 So then they that are in the flesh cannot please God.
9 But ye are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit, if so be that the Spirit of God dwell in you. Now if any man have not the Spirit of Christ, he is none of his.
If you're in the flesh you can't please God. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#1n)]
10 And **if Christ be in you, the body is dead** because of sin; but the Spirit is life because of righteousness.
11 But if the Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead dwell in you, he that raised up Christ from the dead shall also quicken your mortal bodies by his Spirit that dwelleth in you.
12 Therefore, brethren, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live after the flesh.
13 For if ye live after the flesh, ye shall die: but if ye through the Spirit do mortify the deeds of the body, ye shall live.
If Christ is in you, your body is dead.
14 For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.
15 For ye have not received the spirit of bondage again to fear; but ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father.
16 The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God:
17 And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together.
18 For I reckon that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which shall be revealed in us.
19 For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God.
20 For the creature was made subject to vanity, not willingly, but by reason of him who hath subjected the same in hope,
21 Because the creature itself also shall be delivered from the bondage of corruption into the glorious liberty of the children of God.
Those who are led by the Spirit of God,
are the sons of God. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#2n)]
He that spared not his own son (shouldn't be trusted by anyone)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/fv.gif) 8:22-39
22 For we know that the whole creation groaneth and travaileth in pain together until now.
23 And not only they, but ourselves also, which have the firstfruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for the adoption, to wit, the redemption of our body.
The whole creation groans together until now. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#3n)]
24 For we are saved by hope: but hope that is seen is not hope: for what a man seeth, why doth he yet hope for?
25 But if we hope for that we see not, then do we with patience wait for it.
We are saved by hope. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#4n)]
If you can see hope, it isn't hope.
26 Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered.
We don't know how to pray. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#5n)]
But the Spirit intercedes for us by groaning.
27 And he that searcheth the hearts knoweth what is the mind of the Spirit, because he maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God.
The Spirit intercedes for the saint to the will of God. [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#6n)]
28 And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.
Everything works out well for those who love God. [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#7n)]
29 For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren.
God predestined who would be saved [[8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#8n)]
by his son, the firstborn among many siblings. [[9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#9n)]
30 Moreover whom he did predestinate, them he also called: and whom he called, them he also justified: and whom he justified, them he also glorified.
God called who he predestined,
he justified who he called,
and glorified who he justified.
31 What shall we then say to these things? If God be for us, who can be against us?
If God is for us, who can be against us? [[10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#10n)]
32 **He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us** all, how shall he not with him also freely give us all things?
He didn't spare his own son, but killed him for us. [[11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#11n)]
33 Who shall lay any thing to the charge of God's elect? It is God that justifieth.
34 Who is he that condemneth? It is Christ that died, yea rather, that is risen again, who is even at the right hand of God, who also maketh intercession for us.
35 Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?
36 As it is written, For thy sake we are killed all the day long; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter.
37 Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us.
Who can accuse any of God's elect of anything? [[12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#12n)]
38 For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come,
39 Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord. 
Nothing can separate us from God's love. [[13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#13n)]

### Notes
  1. _They that are in the flesh cannot please God ... If Christ be in you, the body is dead_ ([vv.8-10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#8)) 
If you have a body, you can't please God and Christ isn't in you.
  2. _As many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#14)) 
[Are we all God's children?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gods_children.html)
[How many sons does God have?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sons.html)
  3. _The whole creation groaneth and travaileth in pain together until now._ ([v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#22)) 
Some Christians use this verse to show that we live in a [sin-cursed world](http://www.answersingenesis.org/home/area/overheads/pages/oh20010831_71.asp).
(But [others disagree](http://www.godandscience.org/youngearth/romans8.html).)
  4. _We are saved by hope._ ([v.24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#24)) 
[Should we believe in unseen things?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/unseen.html)
  5. _We know not what we should pray for as we ought._ ([v.26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#26)) 
[Do Christians know how to pray?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/pray.html)
  6. _The Spirit ... maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God._ ([v.27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#27)) 
God intercedes with God by talking to, and sometimes even arguing with, himself.
  7. _All things work together for good to them that love God._ ([v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#28)) 
One of the greatest and most absurd lies in all of scripture.
  8. _Whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate._ ([v.29a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#29)) 
Everyone is predestined by God to be either saved or damned.
[Do humans have free will?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/free.html)
What the Bible says about [determinism](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/determinism.html) and [free will](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/free_will.html)
  9. _His son ... the firstborn_ ([v.29b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#29)) 
[Who was God's firstborn son?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/firstborn.html)
  10. _If God be for us, who can be against us?_ ([v.31](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#31)) 
No one can oppose Christians since God is on their side.
  11. _He ... spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all._ ([v.32](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#32)) 
"He that spared not his own Son" shouldn't be trusted by anyone.
[DWB](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html): [Jesus](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/nt.html#Jesus)
  12. _Who shall lay any thing to the charge of God's elect?_ ([v.33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#33)) 
A Christian cannot be accused of any wrongdoing.
  13. _Neither death ... nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord._ ([vv.38-39](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#38)) 
[Is it possible to fall from grace?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/fall_from.html)
