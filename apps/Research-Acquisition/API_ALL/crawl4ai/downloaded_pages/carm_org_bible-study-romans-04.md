# Romans Bible Study, Chapter 4
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
February 09, 2023 
25 min read 
The Jews put a lot of confidence in their heritage. In fact, at one point in a conversation with Jesus, the Jewish leaders boasted that they had Abraham as their father ([Matt. 3:9](https://ref.ly/Matt.%203.9;nasb95?t=biblia); [Luke 3:8](https://ref.ly/Luke%203.8;nasb95?t=biblia)). To a Jew, Abraham was the beginning of their race, the father of them all with whom God chose to establish His covenant – through which the Jews sought their own justification.
