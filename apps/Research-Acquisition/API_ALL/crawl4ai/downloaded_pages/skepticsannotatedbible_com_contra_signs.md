## Did Jesus perform many signs and wonders?
* * *
### Yes, Jesus performed many signs and wonders.
Jesus performed many signs, wonders, and miracles everywhere he went. He was famous for them. (See [here](http://www.godisreal.today/miracles-of-jesus/) for a list of all of Jesus's miracles.) 
> And they went forth, and preached every where, the Lord working with them, and confirming the word with signs following. [Mark 16:20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/16.html#20)
> The same came to Jesus by night, and said unto him, Rabbi, we know that thou art a teacher come from God: for no man can do these miracles that thou doest, except God be with him. [John 3:2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/3.html#2)
> But though he had done so many miracles before them, yet they believed not on him. [John 12:37](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/12.html#37)
> And many other signs truly did Jesus in the presence of his disciples, which are not written in this book. [John 20:30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/20.html#30)
> Ye men of Israel, hear these words; Jesus of Nazareth, a man approved of God among you by miracles and wonders and signs, which God did by him in the midst of you, as ye yourselves also know. [Acts 2:22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#22)
### No, Jesus refused to perform any sign.
> And he sighed deeply in his spirit, and saith, Why doth this generation seek after a sign? verily I say unto you, There shall no sign be given unto this generation. [Mark 8:12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#12)
> This is an evil generation: they seek a sign; and there shall no sign be given it, but the sign of Jonas the prophet. [Luke 11:29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/11.html#29)
### Except for the sign of Jonah.
> An evil and adulterous generation seeketh after a sign; and there shall no sign be given to it, but the sign of the prophet Jonas. [Matthew 12:39](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/12.html#39), [16:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/16.html#4)
