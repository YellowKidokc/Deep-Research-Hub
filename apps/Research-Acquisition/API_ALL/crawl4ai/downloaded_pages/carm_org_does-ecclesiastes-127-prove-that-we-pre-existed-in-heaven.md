# Does Ecclesiastes 12:7 prove that we pre-existed in heaven?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
September 07, 2016 
10 min read 
Groups like the LDS (Mormons), the World Mission Society Church of God, and the Swedenborgians argue that we existed in heaven as spirits prior to our physical birth on Earth. One of the most popular arguments among proponents of this view is to point to [Ecclesiastes 12:7](https://ref.ly/Eccles%2012.7;nasb95?t=biblia), particularly where it says “the spirit returns to God who gave it.” If our spirits _return_ to God, so the argument goes, they must have been with God at some point before. They conclude, therefore, that we were once spirits living with God in heaven and we can return to our heavenly home when we die.
[Ecclesiastes 12:7](https://ref.ly/Eccles%2012.7;nasb95?t=biblia) “and the dust returns to the earth as it was, and the spirit returns to God who gave it.”
The Bible clearly teaches that our physical life comes first and spiritual life comes afterward ([1 Corinthians 15:46-47](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor%2015.46-47;nasb95?t=biblia)) and that we are firstborn of flesh and then afterward of spirit ([John 3:3-6](https://ref.ly/John%203.3-6;nasb95?t=biblia)). The Gospel of John repeatedly emphasizes the importance of the fact that Jesus is from heaven whereas we are not ([John 3:31](https://ref.ly/John%203.31;nasb95?t=biblia), [8:23](https://ref.ly/John%208.23;nasb95?t=biblia), [6:23-51](https://ref.ly/John%206.23-51;nasb95?t=biblia)).
