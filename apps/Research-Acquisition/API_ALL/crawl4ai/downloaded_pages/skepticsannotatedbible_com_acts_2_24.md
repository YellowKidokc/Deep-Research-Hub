### Notes
  1. _When the day of Pentecost was fully come_ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#1)) 
Whoopie! It's Pentecost: A mighty wind comes, cloven tongues of fire sit on top of heads, everyone starts speaking in languages they don't understand and act like they're drunk.
  2. _They were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance._ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#4)) 
[When was the Holy Ghost given?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/ghost.html)
  3. _These men are full of new wine._ ([v.13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#13)) 
Those who heard the apostles speaking in tongues thought they were drunk.
  4. _These are not drunken, as ye suppose, seeing it is but the third hour of the day._ ([v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#15)) 
Peter assures the crowd that the apostles aren't drunk, saying, "Heck, it's only nine o'clock in the morning. That's way too early for us to get drunk."
He claims that their strange behavior was predicted by the prophet Joel. [[Joel 2:28-32](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jl/2.html#28)]
  5. _Blood, and fire, and vapour of smoke_ ([v.19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#19)) 
Peter says that strange behavior (speaking in tongues, etc.), along with great signs and wonders, was to be expected since they were living in "the last days."
  6. _The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood._ ([v.20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#20)) 
It happens with every solar or lunar eclipse.
  7. _Whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be saved._ ([v.21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#21)) 
[Will those who call on the Lord be saved?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/call_on.html)
  8. _Jesus of Nazareth, a man approved of God among you by miracles and wonders and signs_ ([v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#22)) 
[Did Jesus perform many signs?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/signs.html)
  9. _Whom God hath raised up_ ([v.24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#24)) 
_This Jesus hath God raised up._ ([v.32](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#32))
[Who raised Jesus from the dead?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/raised.html)
  10. _His [Jesus'] soul was not left in hell._ ([v.31](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#31)) 
Jesus did a little time in hell. I'm not sure what for.
  11. _Be baptized ... in the name of Jesus Christ._ ([v.38](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#38)) 
[In whose name is baptism to be performed?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/baptism.html)
  12. _The same day there were added unto them about three thousand souls._ ([v.41](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#41)) 
The number of believers went from 120 to 3120 in a single day.
  13. _All that believed were together, and had all things common._ ([v.44](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/2.html#44)) 
The early Christians were the first communists.
