1 And Jacob called unto his sons, and said, Gather yourselves together, that I may tell you that which shall befall you in the last days.
2 Gather yourselves together, and hear, ye sons of Jacob; and hearken unto Israel your father.
When Jacob was preparing to die, he gathered his sons around him and addressed each of them individually with a prophecy about them.
3 Reuben, thou art my firstborn, my might, and the beginning of my strength, the excellency of dignity, and the excellency of power:
4 Unstable as water, thou shalt not excel; because thou wentest up to thy father's bed; then defiledst thou it: he went up to my couch.
He began with his oldest son, Reuben, saying, 
> Reuben will not excel because he had sex with his father's wife. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/49.html#1n)]
5 Simeon and Levi are brethren; instruments of cruelty are in their habitations.
6 O my soul, come not thou into their secret; unto their assembly, mine honour, be not thou united: for in their anger they slew a man, and in their selfwill they digged down a wall.
7 Cursed be their anger, for it was fierce; and their wrath, for it was cruel: I will divide them in Jacob, and scatter them in Israel.
8 Judah, thou art he whom thy brethren shall praise: thy hand shall be in the neck of thine enemies; thy father's children shall bow down before thee.
9 Judah is a lion's whelp: from the prey, my son, thou art gone up: he stooped down, he couched as a lion, and as an old lion; who shall rouse him up?
Jacob continued addressing his sons, saying, 
> Simeon and Levi are cruel. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/49.html#2n)]
10 The sceptre shall not depart from Judah, nor a lawgiver from between his feet, until Shiloh come; and unto him shall the gathering of the people be.
11 Binding his foal unto the vine, and his ass's colt unto the choice vine; he washed his garments in wine, and his clothes in the blood of grapes:
12 His eyes shall be red with wine, and his teeth white with milk.
> Judah is like a lion. His descendants will be kings until Shiloh comes. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/49.html#3n)]
> He washed his garments in wine, and his eyes will be red with wine. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/49.html#4n)]
13 Zebulun shall dwell at the haven of the sea; and he shall be for an haven of ships; and his border shall be unto Zidon.
> Zebulun will live by the sea.
14 Issachar is a strong ass couching down between two burdens:
15 And he saw that rest was good, and the land that it was pleasant; and bowed his shoulder to bear, and became a servant unto tribute.
> Issachar is a strong ass crouching down between two burdens.
16 Dan shall judge his people, as one of the tribes of Israel.
17 Dan shall be a serpent by the way, an adder in the path, that biteth the horse heels, so that his rider shall fall backward.

> Dan will be a snake that bites horse heels.

> Gad will overcome at last.

> Asher will have fat bread and royal dainties.

> Naphtali is a wild female deer that gives goodly words.

25 Even by the God of thy father, who shall help thee; and by the Almighty, who shall bless thee with blessings of heaven above, blessings of the deep that lieth under, blessings of the breasts, and of the womb:

> Archers shoot at him and hate him.

### Notes
  1. _Thou wentest up to thy father's bed._ [[v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/49.html#4)] 
The very brief story (1 verse) is told in [Genesis 35:22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#sex), where Reuben has sex with Jacob's wife, Bilhah.
  2. _Simeon and Levi are ... instruments of cruelty._ [[v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/49.html#5)] 
Simeon and Levi [killed every male in a city while they were recovering from circumcision](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/34.html#dinah). It was about as close to a Romeo and Juliet type love story as you're going to find in the Bible.
  3. _Until Shiloh comes_ [[v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/49.html#10)] 
The tribe of Judah will reign "until Shiloh," but Israel's first king (Saul) was from the tribe of Benjamin ([Acts 13:21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/13.html#21)), and most of the time after this prophecy there was no king at all.
  4. _His eyes shall be red with wine._ [[v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/49.html#12)] 
Did Judah really wash his clothes in wine? Were his eyes bloodshot from drinking too much? Or is this [ a prophecy of Jesus](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rev/1.html#14)? (I didn't know Jesus had a drinking problem.)
  5. _These are the twelve tribes of Israel._ [[v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/49.html#28)] 
[What were the 12 tribes of Israel?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/tribes.html)
  6. _Jacob ... yielded up the ghost._ [[v.33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/49.html#33)] 
Contrary to the prophecy ([Genesis 46:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#4)), Jacob died in Egypt, not Israel.
