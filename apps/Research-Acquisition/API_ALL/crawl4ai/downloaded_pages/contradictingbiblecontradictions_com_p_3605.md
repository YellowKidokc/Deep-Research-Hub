# Is Jesus peaceful?
Published November 17, 2015
**Yes.**  
_Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called sons of God._  
**Matthew 5:9** NASB
_And behold, one of those who were with Jesus reached and drew out his sword, and struck the slave of the high priest and cut off his ear._  
_52 Then Jesus said to him, “ Put your sword back into its place; for all those who take up the sword shall perish by the sword.”_  
**Matthew 26:51-52** NASB
_Glory to God in the highest,  
And on earth peace among men with whom He is pleased._  
**Luke 2:14** NASB
_Peace I leave with you; My peace I give to you; not as the world gives do I give to you. Do not let your heart be troubled, nor let it be fearful._  
**John 14:27** NASB
_These things I have spoken to you, so that in Me you may have peace. In the world you have tribulation, but take courage; I have overcome the world._  
**John 16:33** NASB
_The word which He sent to the sons of Israel, preaching peace through Jesus Christ (He is Lord of all)_  
**Acts 10:36** NASB
**No.**  
_Do not think that I came to bring peace on the earth; I did not come to bring peace, but a sword._  
**Matthew 10:34** NASB
_Do you suppose that I came to grant peace on earth? I tell you, no, but rather division;_  
_52 for from now on five members in one household will be divided, three against two and two against three._  
**Luke 12:51-52** NASB
_And He said to them,__“But now, whoever has a money belt is to take it along, likewise also a bag, and whoever has no sword is to sell his coat and buy one.”_  
**Luke 22:36** NASB
_And I saw heaven opened, and behold, a white horse, and He who sat on it is called Faithful and True, and in righteousness He judges and wages war._  
**Revelation 19:11** NASB  

[**SAB Contradiction 438**](http://skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sword.html)
**The Problem**  
Is Jesus peaceful? The trigger in this question is that we should reply a priori: Yes, of course He is. However reading all the texts we discover that it is not as easy as that. 
**List 1, Yes.**  
The first list of the critic “Yes, Jesus is peaceful” makes plain that it is a certain peace Jesus brings: spiritual peace. He even prohibits Peter to defend him with a sword: “Put your sword back …” Jesus accepts being arrested by soldiers.
**List 2, No.**  
The second list is remarkably smaller, only 4 texts to prove that Jesus is not peaceful. However, _none of them is to the point_.  

Matthew 10:34  |  Jesus is not bringing peace on earth, but in hearts. Many will ignore and even oppose this and live a warlike life on earth. Jesus’ determination for his peace will not end. He remains peaceful and this will indirectly bring war through his opponents.  
---|---  
Luke 12:51-52  |  Idem  
Luke 22:36  |  Jesus’ disciples are ordered to take swords with them to fulfil a prophecy of Isaiah that the Messiah will be counted among criminals. They took two knives from the Passover meal with them which was enough. Jesus _didn’t have a knife_. He remained peaceful.  
Revelation 19:11  |  Final confrontation of the war Jesus announced is still in progress, since his Ascension into heaven until the last Day of Judgement (armies assembled _to make war against Him_ who sat on the horse, 19:19). Jesus remains peaceful, not making war, only being forced to wage war.  
**Conclusion**
