### Notes
  1. _These are the children of the province that went up out of the captivity_ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ezra/2.html#1)) 
The second chapter of Ezra provides a list of the Jewish people returning to Judah after their captivity in Babylon. It makes for rather dull reading: just a list of men's names and the number of offspring that accompanied each of them. The same list is given in the [seventh chapter of Nehemiah](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/neh/7.html) (as though once were not enough), but the two lists contradict each other in 19 places.

[How many children of Arah returned from Babylon?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/arah.html)

  18. _Urim and with Thummim_ ([v.63](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ezra/2.html#63)) 
The Urim and Thummim were like the two sides a magic coin that could be flipped to to give a yes or no answer to any question. They were also what [Joseph Smith used to translate the Book of Mormon.](http://www.lightplanet.com/mormons/daily/prayer/seer_stones_eom.htm)

Yet if we count up all of the numbers in the list just given in verses 3-62 we obtain a different number: 29,818.
