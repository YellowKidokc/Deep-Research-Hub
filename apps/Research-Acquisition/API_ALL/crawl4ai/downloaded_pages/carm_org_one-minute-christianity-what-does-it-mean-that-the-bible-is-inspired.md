# What does it mean that the Bible is inspired?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 04, 2023 
2 min read 
When we say that the Bible is inspired, we mean that when the authors wrote the Bible, God spoke through them. Their writing was not a mechanical dictation where the people wrote down what they heard directly spoken from God. Nor did they go into a trance-like state where they were unaware of what they were doing. Instead, the writers of the Bible were free to express what they wanted, the way they wanted, and when they wanted. Yet, God was also moving through them to ensure the integrity and accuracy of what they said. Therefore, the Christian church did not give us the Bible. Instead, it recognizes what the Bible already was, God’s inspired word. After all, Jesus said in [John 10:27](https://ref.ly/John%2010.27;nasb95?t=biblia), “My sheep hear My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me.”
