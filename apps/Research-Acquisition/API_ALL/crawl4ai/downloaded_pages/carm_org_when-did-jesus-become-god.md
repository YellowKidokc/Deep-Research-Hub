# When did Jesus become God?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 14, 2024 
2 min read 
Answering the question of when Jesus became God is not as easy as it might appear. This is because there are two different ways we can respond. First, the person of Jesus came into existence 2000 years ago. In other words, Jesus is one person with two distinct natures: God and man. We call this the [hypostatic union](https://carm.org/doctrine-and-theology/what-is-the-hypostatic-union/). It means that Jesus has two distinct and simultaneous natures – unlike us. So, 2000 years ago, the union of the divine nature and the human nature occurred ([John 1:1](https://ref.ly/John%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia), [14](https://ref.ly/John%201.14;nasb95?t=biblia)). That is why we could say that Jesus came into existence then, and that’s when He (as a person) became God. But still, the wording is tricky because we don’t want to imply that Jesus, who has the qualities of divinity, became divine – if He already _was_ divine.[ ![When did Jesus become God?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/person-jesus-single-line.png) ](https://carm.org/oneness-pentecostal/personhood-the-father-and-jesus/attachment/person-jesus-single-line/)
On the other hand, because Jesus has two natures, divine and human, and since God has always existed, then we can say that Jesus never became God because He always was God. This is due to the doctrine signified by the Latin term [communicatio idiomatum](https://carm.org/doctrine-and-theology/communicatio-idiomatum-the-communication-of-properties/), or the communication of the properties. It teaches that the two natures of Christ each have their properties: divine properties and human properties. So, we would say that the attributes of both natures are ascribed to one person of Jesus. Therefore, Jesus has both the properties of being divine and human.
It is worth noting that Jesus, right now, still has and always will have two natures. That is, by definition, who He is. He will never stop being God in the flesh. And yes, right now in heaven, Jesus is a man. That is, He is a man in a glorified body ([1 Cor. 15:35-45](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2015.35-45;nasb95?t=biblia)).
