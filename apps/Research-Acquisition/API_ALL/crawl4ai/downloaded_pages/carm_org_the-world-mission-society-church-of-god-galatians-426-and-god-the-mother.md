# The World Mission Society Church of God, Galatians 4:26, and “God the Mother”
by [Ryan Turner](https://carm.org/ryan-turner)
August 05, 2016 
4 min read 
According to the World Mission Society Church of God (WMSCOG), the Bible teaches that there is a heavenly Mother. Their primary verse for this claim is in Paul’s letter to the Galatians.
“But the Jerusalem that is above is free, and she is our mother” (Galatians 4:26).
They take this to mean that the “Jerusalem that is above” refers to a literal divine being who created us alongside God the Father and is literally our heavenly mother.
