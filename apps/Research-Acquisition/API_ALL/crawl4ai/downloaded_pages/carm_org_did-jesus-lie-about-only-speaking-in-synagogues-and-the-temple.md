# Did Jesus lie about only speaking in synagogues and the temple?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 07, 2009 
5 min read 
Did Jesus knowingly, purposefully lie to the Jews?
In [John 18:20](https://ref.ly/John%2018.20;nasb95?t=biblia) He said that He always taught in synagogues and the Temple,  ![Did Jesus lie about only speaking in synagogues and the temple?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/temple-01.jpg) but we know that He also spoke on a mountain ([Matt. 5:1-2](https://ref.ly/Matt.%205.1-2;nasb95?t=biblia)), a boat ([Matt. 13:1-3](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2013.1-3;nasb95?t=biblia)), and other places. So, was Jesus lying? No, He wasn’t lying.
The supposed problem rests on the definition of “always.” Does it mean “without exception” since this is the only sense in which the supposed contradiction can stand?
