4 And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the host: and Zadok and Abiathar were the priests:
5 And Azariah the son of Nathan was over the officers: and Zabud the son of Nathan was principal officer, and the king's friend:
6 And Ahishar was over the household: and Adoniram the son of Abda was over the tribute.

8 And these are their names: The son of Hur, in mount Ephraim:
9 The son of Dekar, in Makaz, and in Shaalbim, and Bethshemesh, and Elonbethhanan:
10 The son of Hesed, in Aruboth; to him pertained Sochoh, and all the land of Hepher:
Here's a list of all of Solomon's men: princes, priests, scribes, officers, and cooks.

13 The son of Geber, in Ramothgilead; to him pertained the towns of Jair the son of Manasseh, which are in Gilead; to him also pertained the region of Argob, which is in Bashan, threescore great cities with walls and brasen bars:

Solomon was wiser than Heman
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 4:20-34
20 Judah and Israel were many, as the sand which is by the sea in multitude, eating and drinking, and making merry.
21 And Solomon reigned over all kingdoms from the river unto the land of the Philistines, and unto the border of Egypt: they brought presents, and served Solomon all the days of his life.
There were as many people in Judah and Israel as there are grains of sand on all of shores of the sea.
And the people were happy, too. Eating, drinking, and making merry.
Everyone brought Solomon lots of presents.
22 And Solomon's provision for one day was thirty measures of fine flour, and threescore measures of meal,
23 Ten fat oxen, and twenty oxen out of the pastures, and an hundred sheep, beside harts, and roebucks, and fallowdeer, and fatted fowl.
Every day Solomon got 30 measures of flour, 60 measures of meal, 10 fat oxen, 100 sheep, and a partridge in a pear tree.
24 For he had dominion over all the region on this side the river, from Tiphsah even to Azzah, over all the kings on this side the river: and he had peace on all sides round about him.
25 And Judah and Israel dwelt safely, every man under his vine and under his fig tree, from Dan even to Beersheba, all the days of Solomon.
Everyone lived in peace under his vine and fig tree.
26 And Solomon had forty thousand stalls of horses for his chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen.
27 And those officers provided victual for king Solomon, and for all that came unto king Solomon's table, every man in his month: they lacked nothing.
28 Barley also and straw for the horses and dromedaries brought they unto the place where the officers were, every man according to his charge.
And Solomon had 40,000 stalls of horses and 12,000 horsemen. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#2n)]
29 And God gave Solomon wisdom and understanding exceeding much, and largeness of heart, even as the sand that is on the sea shore.
God gave Solomon wisdom and understanding, and a heart that was like the sand on the sea shore.
30 And Solomon's wisdom excelled the wisdom of all the children of the east country, and all the wisdom of Egypt.
He was wiser than anyone in Egypt or anywhere to the east.
31 For **he was wiser than** all men; than Ethan the Ezrahite, and **Heman** , and Chalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol: and his fame was in all nations round about.
He was, in fact, wiser than any man who has ever lived. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#3n)]
He was even wiser than Heman!
_Heman, and Chalcol, and Darda the sons of Mahol_ [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#4n)]
32 And **he spake three thousand proverbs: and his songs were a thousand and five.**
He spoke 3,000 proverbs and wrote 1005 songs. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#5n)]
33 And he spake of trees, from the cedar tree that is in Lebanon even unto the hyssop that springeth out of the wall: he spake also of beasts, and of fowl, and of creeping things, and of fishes.
He talked about trees, beasts, creeping things, and fishes.
34 And there came of all people to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all kings of the earth, which had heard of his wisdom.
Everyone came from all over the earth just to hear him speak.

### Notes
  1. _Taphath the daughter of Solomon_ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#11)) 
_Basmath the daughter of Solomon_ ([v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#15))
Solomon had [700 wives and 300 concubines](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/11.html#3), but only three children: two daughters ([Taphath](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#11) and [Basmath](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#15)) and one son ([Rehoboam](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/11.html#43)).
  2. _Solomon had forty thousand stalls of horses for his chariots._ ([v.26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#26)) 
[How many stalls did Solomon have?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/stalls.html)
  3. _He was wiser than all men._ ([v.31a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#31)) 
How could Solomon be "wiser than all men" and yet have his heart "turned away ... after other gods?" ([1 Kings 11:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/11.html#4))
  4. _Heman, and Chalcol, and Darda the sons of Mahol_ ([v.31b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#31)) 
[Who was Heman's father?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/heman.html)
  5. _His songs were a thousand and five._ ([v.31](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/4.html#31)) 
I think that beats [Willie Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_songs_written_by_Willie_Nelson).
