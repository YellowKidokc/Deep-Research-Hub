# Romans – Philemon
by [CARM](https://carm.org/carm)
5 min read 
These Bible Difficulties and contradictions from Romans to Philemon are listed in order of their appearance in the Bible. But, we must ask. Does the Bible have contradictions? Or are the difficulties cleared up when the context is examined? This list of supposed contradictions is answered by context, logic, and cross-referencing for clarity. There are no Bible difficulties/contradictions from Romans to Philemon. You can see this more clearly when you understand what a contradiction is. The article on this is in the introduction below. Also, there will be textual copyist errors that have occurred through the centuries. These are very few and don't affect doctrine. In some cases, there are a number of differences that result from a fine point of writing that may or may not exist in a few places. In other instances, difficulties are cleared up by reading context or other scriptures that clarify. Enjoy this list of difficulties/contradictions from Romans to Philemon!
