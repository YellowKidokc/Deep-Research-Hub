# Does Roman 9:27-28 Misquote Isaiah 10:22-23?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
April 06, 2022 
10 min read 
![Does Paul misquote Isaiah?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/misquote.jpg) Critics of the New Testament often point to alleged problems in how its authors utilized the Old Testament. In some cases, the New Testament is said to have ignorantly misquoted or even deceitfully altered the words of the Law and the Prophets, misleading their readers to suit their own ends. Such skeptics most often point to well-known examples like the [ Book of Hebrews’ use of Psalm 40](https://carm.org/about-bible-verses/does-hebrews-105-misquote-psalm-406/) or [ Jesus’ citation of Isaiah 61 in Luke](https://carm.org/about-the-bible/does-luke-418-misquote-isaiah-611/), but Paul doesn’t escape their crosshairs either. Did he bungle a quote from Isaiah in Romans 9?
Paul relies heavily on the Old Covenant Scriptures throughout his letters, and the Book of Romans is no exception. In the ninth chapter, he explicitly draws on the words of Isaiah, and it is here that we are told he is guilty of a “misquote”:
“Isaiah cries out concerning Israel, ‘Though the number of the sons of Israel be like the sand of the sea, it is the remnant that will be saved; for the Lord will execute His word on the earth, thoroughly and quickly,” (Romans 9:27-28).
