# What do Islam and the Quran say about Jesus?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
June 23, 2013 
10 min read 
When talking to Muslims, it can be helpful to be aware of the teachings about Jesus in the Quran. The following is a chart that lists important verses in the Quran that talk about Jesus. Many other verses reference Jesus in the Quran but are largely repetitive. The English version used is the Usef Ali Translation of 1985. [The Noble Quran](http://quran.com)
And (remember) her who guarded her chastity: We breathed into her of Our spirit, and We made her and her son a sign for all peoples. (21:91, Yusif Ali)
