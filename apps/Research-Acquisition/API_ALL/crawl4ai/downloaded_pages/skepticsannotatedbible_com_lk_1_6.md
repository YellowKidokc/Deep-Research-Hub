### Notes
  1. _Zechariah ... and his wife ... Elisabeth ... were both righteous before God walking in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord blameless._ ([vv.5-6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/1.html#5)) 
[Has there ever been a righteous person?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/righteous.html)
[Does righteousness come from following the Law?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/righteousness.html)
  2. _They had no child, because that Elisabeth was barren._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/1.html#7)) 
Oh God, [another barren woman](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/topics/barren.html)! It's always the woman's fault in the Bible.
Zechariah and Elisabeth were both getting old, too old to have kids. But Zechariah prayed, so an angel appeared to tell him that God would take care of things for him. God would somehow get Liz pregnant and she'd have a son named John, who would be filled with the Holy Ghost from the moment God, the angel, the Holy Ghost, or Zechariah (or maybe all of them working together) got his mom pregnant.
  3. _The angel said unto him, Fear not, Zechariah: for thy prayer is heard; and thy wife Elisabeth shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name John._ ([v.13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/1.html#13)) 
Whoopie! Another miraculous birth! Did Zechariah have sex with Elisabeth or did the angel take care of things for him?
  4. _He shall be great in the sight of the Lord, and shall drink neither wine nor strong drink._ ([v.15a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/1.html#15)) 
[Is it OK to drink alcohol?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/alcohol.html)
  5. _He shall be filled with the Holy Ghost, even from his mother's womb._ ([v.15b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/1.html#15)) 
John the Baptist was "filled with the Holy Ghost" even as a fetus!
[When was the Holy Ghost given?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/ghost.html)
  6. _Thou shalt be dumb, and not able to speak ... because thou believest not my words._ ([v.20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/1.html#20)) 
Zechariah asks the angel Gabriel how his wife Elisabeth could become pregnant, since she is "stricken with years." Gabriel makes him "dumb" just for asking.
  7. _Hail Mary_ ([v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/1.html#28)) 
The angel Gabriel stopped by Nazareth to visit the Most Blessed Virgin Mary and say the rosary with her. OK, not really. He stopped by to tell her that the Holy Ghost would soon be getting her pregnant the way he did her cousin Elisabeth. But during his visit he did manage to come up with the first half of the "Hail Mary."
