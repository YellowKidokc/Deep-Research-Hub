### CHAPTER 5
Adam's family
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/wom1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 5:1-5
1 This is the book of the generations of Adam. In the day that God created man, in the likeness of God made he him;
Get ready for some exciting reading. "The book of the generations of Adam" is about to start!
2 Male and female created **he** them; and blessed them, and **called their name Adam** , in the day when they were created.
God created a man and a woman, and he "called _their_ name Adam." So the woman's name was Adam too!
3 And Adam lived an hundred and thirty years, and begat a son in his own likeness, and after his image; and called his name Seth:
When Adam was 130 years old he had a son that looked a lot like him. He called him Seth. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#1n)]
4 And the days of Adam after he had begotten Seth were eight hundred years: and he begat sons and daughters:
Sometime in the next 800 years, Adam begat some daughters. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#2n)]
5 **And all the days that Adam lived were nine hundred and thirty years:** **and he died.**
Adam finally dies -- 930 years after eating from the tree of knowledge, contrary to God's false prophecy that Adam would die the day that he ate the forbidden fruit ([2:17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/2.html#17)).
From Seth to Noah

6 And Seth lived an hundred and five years, and begat Enos:
7 And Seth lived after he begat Enos eight hundred and seven years, and begat sons and daughters:
It took Seth 105 years to produce his first son. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#3n)]

Seth lived 912 years.

Enos lived 905 years.
14 And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years: and he died. 
15 And Mahalaleel lived sixty and five years, and begat Jared:
16 And Mahalaleel lived after he begat Jared eight hundred and thirty years, and begat sons and daughters:
Cainan lived 910 years.

Mahalaleel lived 895 years.
18 And Jared lived an hundred sixty and two years, and he begat Enoch:

_Jared ... begat Enoch._ [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#4n)]
20 And all the days of Jared were nine hundred sixty and two years: and he died.
21 And Enoch lived sixty and five years, and begat Methuselah:

Jared lived 962 years.

Enoch lived 365 years before he was taken (alive?) by God. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#5n)]
25 And Methuselah lived an hundred eighty and seven years, and begat Lamech.

When Methuseslah was 187 years old, he begat Lamech. [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#6n)]
27 And all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred sixty and nine years: and he died.
28 And Lamech lived an hundred eighty and two years, and begat a son:
29 And he called his name Noah, saying, This same shall comfort us concerning our work and toil of our hands, because of the ground which the LORD hath cursed.
30 And Lamech lived after he begat Noah five hundred ninety and five years, and begat sons and daughters:
Methuselah lived 969 years. (World record holder.)

Lamech lived 777 years.

When Noah was 500 years old, he had three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth. [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#7n)]

### Notes
  1. _Adam ... called his name Seth._ [[v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#3)] 
This is Adam's third son, and the first since Cain killed Abel. God told Adam and Eve to [be fruitful and multiply](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#28), but they had no children for 130 years.
  2. _Daughters_ [[v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#4)] 
These nameless ones are the first girls born in the Bible.
  3. _Seth lived an hundred and five years, and begat Enos._ [[v.6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#6)] 
Adam's descendants were an infertile bunch. It took them an average of 158 years to have a first son.
So much for [being fruitful and multiplying](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#28)!
Here's a list of the number of years it took them to get the job done: Seth 105, Enosh 90, Kenan 70, Malhalalel 65, Jared 162, Enoch 65, Methuselah 187, Lamech 182, Noah 500.
  4. _Jared ... begat Enoch._ [[v.18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#18)] 
[Was Enoch the seventh from Adam?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/enoch7.html)
  5. _God took him._ [[v.24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#24)] 
[Must everyone die?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/die.html)
[Did Enoch die?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/enoch_die.html)
[Has anyone ever ascended into heaven?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/enoch.html)
  6. _Methuselah lived an hundred eighty and seven years, and begat Lamech._ [[v.25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#25)] 
When Lamech was born, nine generations were alive at once.
Adam, Seth, Enos, Cainan, Mahalaleel, Jared, Enoch, Methuselah, and Lamech were all alive at the time of Lamech's birth.
Adam lived to see his great-great-great-great-great-great-grandson.
  7. _Noah was five hundred years old: and Noah begat Shem, Ham, and Japheth._ [[v.32](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/5.html#32)] 
When Noah was 500 years old, he had three sons in one year!
Was that with one (nameless) wife or several? Or were they triplets?
[Who was Noah's youngest son?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/ham.html)
