# Transubstantiation and 1 Corinthians 10:16
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
January 25, 2017 
6 min read 
According to Roman Catholicism, the bread and wine of the communion meal become the actual flesh and blood of Jesus’ human body at the moment the priest blesses them during the Mass. This doctrine is known as “transubstantiation.” Roman Catholics sometimes defend this doctrine by appealing to [1 Corinthians 10:16](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor%2010.16;nasb95?t=biblia):
“Is not the cup of blessing which we bless a sharing in the blood of Christ? Is not the bread which we break a sharing in the body of Christ?”
