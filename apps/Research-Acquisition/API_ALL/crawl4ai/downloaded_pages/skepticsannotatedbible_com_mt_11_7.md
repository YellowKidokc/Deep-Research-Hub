John the Baptist: Should we look for another?
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 11:1-6, Cf. [Lk 7:18-23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/7.html#S3)
1 And it came to pass, when Jesus had made an end of commanding his twelve disciples, he departed thence to teach and to preach in their cities.
When Jesus finished instructing his disciples, he left to teach and preach in the cities.
2 Now when John had heard in the prison the works of Christ, he sent two of his disciples,
3 And said unto him, **Art thou he that should come, or do we look for another?**
While John the Baptist was in prison, he heard about the works of Jesus, and he sent two of his disciples to ask him, 
> Are you the one that is supposed to come, or should we look for another? [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/11.html#1n)]
4 Jesus answered and said unto them, Go and shew John again those things which ye do hear and see:
Jesus said to them, 
> Go and show John the things that you hear and see.
5 The blind receive their sight, and the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have the gospel preached to them.
> The blind see, the lame walk, lepers are clean, the deaf hear, the dead live, and the poor have the gospel preached to them.
6 And blessed is he, whosoever shall not be offended in me.
> And blessed is whoever is not offended by me.

### Notes
  1. _Art thou he that should come, or do we look for another?_ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/11.html#3)) 
John the Baptist is still not sure about Jesus. This is rather odd, since he was the one to "prepare the way of the Lord." ([Mt 3:3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/3.html#3)). Heck, he even baptized Jesus and heard the voice of God say, "This is my beloved son, in whom I am well pleased" ([Mt 3:17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/3.html#17)).
But still, while in prison and about to die, he sends his disciples to ask, "Art thou he that should come, or do we look for another?" Well, if _he_ isn't sure after seeing and hearing the events at Jesus's baptism, then how can anyone else be?
[Did John the Baptist recognize Jesus as the Son of God?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/john-recognize.html)
  2. _Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, which shall prepare thy way before thee._ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/11.html#10)) 
Jesus is claiming here that John the Baptist is the person referred to in [Malachi 3:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mal/3.html#1).
  3. _Among them that are born of women there hath not risen a greater than John the Baptist._ ([v11.](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/11.html#11)) 
John the Baptist was the greatest man ever to live (even greater than Jesus, who was also [born of a woman](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gal/4.html#4)), but "he that is least in the kingdom of heaven is greater than he."
[Who was the greatest prophet?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/interp/greatest.html)
[Who was the greatest?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/greatest.html)
  4. _From the days of John the Baptist until now the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/11.html#12)) 
Until when? (John the Baptist was still alive when Jesus supposedly said these words.)
This verse was used by the January 6th insurrectionists to justify their attack on the capital.
  5. _John [the Baptist] ... is Elijah,_ ([vv.13-14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/11.html#13)) 
[Was John the Baptist Elijah?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/elijah.html)
  6. _The Son of man came eating and drinking, and they say, Behold a man gluttonous, and a winebibber, a friend of publicans and sinners._ ([v.19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/11.html#19)) 
[How should publicans be treated?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/publicans.html)
  7. _Woe unto thee!_ ([vv.21-24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/11.html#21)) 
Jesus condemns entire cities (including [his hometown of Capernaum](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/9.html#1)) to dreadful deaths and the eternal torment of hell because they didn't care for his preaching.
  8. _Jesus ... said, I thank thee, O Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because thou hast hid these things from the wise and prudent, and hast revealed them unto babes._ ([v.25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/11.html#25)) 
[Who is the Lord of the earth?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/lord.html)
