# All Bible verses that are at location 20:20
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
January 13, 2020 
5 min read 
The year 2020 is here. There’s 2020 vision. There is a news show called 2020. So, I wondered what verses in the Bible had the address of 20:20. In other words, how many verses in the NASB95 Bible have a chapter 20 and a verse 20? After a quick search, I discovered there were 17. Now, there’s nothing special about those verses, but since I did the research, I thought I would list out all the scriptures in the Bible that have an address of 20:20.
