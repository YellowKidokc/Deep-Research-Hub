### Notes
  1. _He saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees come to his baptism._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/3.html#7)) 
[Were the Pharisees baptized by John?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/pharisees.html)
  2. _He said to them, O generation of vipers._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/3.html#7)) 
While insulting the Pharisees and Sadducees, John the Baptist calls an entire generation a "generation of vipers." (Jesus also liked to use this term. See [Mt 12:34](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/12.html#34))
  3. _He that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear: he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire._ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/3.html#11)) 
[Who is the greatest?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/greatest.html)
[Did Jesus baptize anyone?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/baptize.html)
  4. _Then cometh Jesus from Galilee to Jordan unto John, to be baptized of him._ ([v.13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/3.html#13)) 
John has a good point in [v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/3.html#14). If Jesus is the sinless Son of God, then shouldn't Jesus be baptizing John instead of the reverse? Isn't baptism supposed to forgive sins and be a sign of repentance? If so, then why would Jesus need to be baptized? And what the heck is "it becometh us to fulfil all righteousness" supposed to mean?
[Did Jesus sin?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/interp/jesus_sin.html)
  5. _This is my beloved son._ ([v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/3.html#17)) 
[How did God address Jesus at his baptism? ](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/this_or_thou.html)
