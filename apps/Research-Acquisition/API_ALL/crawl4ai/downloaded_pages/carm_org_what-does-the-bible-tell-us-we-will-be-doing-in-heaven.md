# What does the Bible tell us we will be doing in heaven?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
May 09, 2025 
4 min read 
The Bible does not give a very detailed account of what we will be doing in heaven. But it does say that we can’t even imagine what God has prepared for us ([1 Cor. 2:9](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia)). This means that though we can understand _some_ things we will be doing in heaven, it will be greater than we can imagine. Nevertheless, here are some of the things the Bible tells us it will be like in heaven. ![What does the Bible tell us we will be doing in heaven, heaven what will we do](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/heaven-02-300x297.jpg)
In summary, we will be reigning with Christ and enjoying the presence of God. We won’t have to work for salvation because we can have breast. But I think the greatest treasure there will be our fellowship with the Lord. He is the wonderful King whose presence we will enjoy for eternity.
