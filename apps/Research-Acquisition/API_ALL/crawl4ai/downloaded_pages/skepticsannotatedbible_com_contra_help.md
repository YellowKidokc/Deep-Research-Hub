## Does God help in times of need?
* * *
### No.
> And ye shall cry out in that day because of your king which ye shall have chosen you; and the LORD will not hear you in that day. [1 Samuel 8:18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1sam/8.html#18)
> Why standest thou afar off, O Lord? why hidest thou thyself in times of trouble?. [Psalm 10:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/10.html#1)
> My God, my god, why hast thou forsaken me? why art thou so far from helping me, and from the words of my roaring? O my God, I cry in the daytime, but thou hearest not. [Psalm 22:1-2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/22.html#1)
> LORD, why castest thou off my soul? why hidest thou thy face from me? [Psalm 88:14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/88.html#14)
> And when ye spread forth your hands, I will hide mine eyes from you: yea, when ye make many prayers, I will not hear. [Isaiah 1:15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/1.html#15)
> Verily thou art a God that hidest thyself. [Isaiah 45:15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/45.html#15)
> Thus saith the Lord GOD; Are ye come to enquire of me? As I live, saith the Lord GOD, I will not be enquired of by you. [Ezekiel 20:3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ezek/20.html#3)
> O LORD, how long shall I cry, and thou wilt not hear! even cry out unto thee of violence, and thou wilt not save! [Habakkuk 1:2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/hab/1.html#2)
> They shall go with their flocks and with their herds to seek the LORD; but they shall not find him; he hath withdrawn himself from them. [Hosea 5:6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/hos/5.html#6)
> Then shall they cry unto the LORD, but he will not hear them: he will even hide his face from them. [Micah 3:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mic/3.html#4)
### Yes.
> For he hath not despised nor abhorred the affliction of the afflicted; neither hath he hid his face from him; but when he cried unto him, he heard. [Psalm 22:24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/22.html#24)
> God is our refuge and our strength, a very present help in trouble. [Psalm 46:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/46.html#1)
> The Lord is near to all who call upon him. [Psalm 145:18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/145.html#18)
> The LORD is good, a strong hold in the day of trouble. [Nahum 1:7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/nah/1.html#7)
