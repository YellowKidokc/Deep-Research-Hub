### Notes
  1. _God spake unto Israel ... and said, Jacob, Jacob._ [[v.2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#2)] 
God calls Jacob "Jacob", though he said in [Genesis 32:28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/32.html#28) and [35:10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#10) that he would no longer be called Jacob but Israel.
  2. _I will also surely bring thee up again._ [[v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#4)] 
God promises to bring Jacob safely back from Egypt, but Jacob dies in Egypt ([Genesis 49:33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/49.html#33)).
  3. _The sons of Levi; Gershon, Kohath, and Merari_ [[v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#11)] 
[Was Mahli the son of Levi?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/mahli.html)
  4. _Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan._ [[v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#12)] 
God killed his first two sons in [chapter 38](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/38.html#er).
  5. _Thirty and three_ [[v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#15)] 
The list includes six sons, 23 living grandsons ([two were killed by God in Canaan](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/38.html#34)), two great-grandsons, and one granddaughter, for a grand total of 32.
And yet verse 15 says there were 33.
Notice that this verse includes "sons and daughters". So there Jacob and Leah had 31 male descendants, but only one female descendant (Dinah).
It's also interesting that the Jacob's wives and daughters-in-law are not included in the list.
[How many descendants of Jacob and Leah went to Egypt with Jacob?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jacob-leah.html)
  6. _Even sixteen souls_ [[v.18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#18)] 
The list of the descendants of Jacob and Zilpah includes fifteen males and one female, continuing the amazing male/female ratios in the Bible.
  7. _Asenath the daughter of Potipherah priest of On_ [[v.20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#20)] 
[Will God destroy those that intermarry?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/intermarriage.html)
  8. _The sons of Benjamin_ [[v.21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#21)] 
There are four lists of Benjamin's sons in the Bible, and none of them agree. This one lists ten sons, [Num 26:38-40](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/26.html#40), [1 Chr 7:6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/7.html#6) lists three, and [1 Chr 8:1-2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/8.html#1) lists five. Only one son (Bela) is found in all four lists.
[Who were the sons of Benjamin?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/bens_sons.html)
[Were Naaman and Ard the sons or grandsons of Benjamin?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/bens_sons_grandsons.html)
[How old was Benjamin when his clan migrated to Egypt?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/bens_age.html)
  9. _All the souls were fourteen._ [[v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#22)] 
Twelve sons, no daughters.
  10. _All the souls were seven_ [[v.25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#25)] 
Five sons, no daughters.
  11. _All the souls of the house of Jacob, which came into Egypt, were threescore and ten._ [[v.27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/46.html#27)] 
[How many were in Jacob's family when they came into Egypt?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jacobs_clan.html)
