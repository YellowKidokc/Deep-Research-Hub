I find Mary's story in this chapter to be incredibly sad.   
  
She's espoused (an archaic word for "married") to Joseph, but before the two of them can consummate the bond, the Holy Ghost swoops in and beats him to the punch. Joseph seemingly presumes she was either raped to pregnancy or cheated on him with another man. However, being a decent man, rather than announce it publicly to shame her, he keeps what he's found out between he and Mary.  
  
...but then he has a dream soon afterward. Therein, an angel tells him to marry his poor defiled Mary (though he was already espoused to her...) because the Holy Ghost was the one who got her pregnant. Further humiliating Joseph, the angel reveals that her child will be a boy. 'One more thing, call the kid "Jesus" because he's going to be the greatest example of sacrificial atonement for the rest of eternity.'  
  
'Oh, and Joseph, this was the plan all along... sorry we didn't confer with you sooner, but we figured you wouldn't support the rape of your wife. Have fun raising someone else's son!'  
  
Joseph, despite the news, marries his wife (?) when he wakes up. He avoids pregnancy sex with her, probably because that's a bit creepy. Mary says nothing during this entire situation.   
  
A great portion of the Christian faith relies on the virgin birth of Jesus to be factually accurate. If this is so, then it's a despicable thing to hang one's religious beliefs on. The resurrection is already suspect enough, but the plight of Mary here is sickening.   
  
(So much for the apologists' claims that the New Testament is 'better' than the Old.)

### Notes
  1. _The book of the generation of Jesus Christ, the son of David._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#1)) 
The gospel of Matthew begins with a genealogy that claims to be Jesus's but is actually that of Mary's husband, Joseph. (See [v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#16)) This is strange since its purpose (if it has a purpose) is to show that Jesus is a descendant of David, and therefore meets one of the qualifications of being the Messiah. ([2 Sam 7:12-13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2sam/7.html#12)) But if the Holy Ghost is the one who got Mary pregnant ([v.25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#25), [Luke 1:35](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/1.html#35)), Jesus isn't David's "seed" and didn't "proceed out of his bowels."
It's a boring genealogy like we are told to avoid in [1 Tim 1:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1tim/1.html#4) ("Neither give heed to fables and endless genealogies") and [Titus 3:9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/tit/3.html#9) ("Avoid foolish questions and genealogies").
One of the interesting features of Matthew's gospel is the inclusion of four women in the genealogy. Women are rarely mentioned in biblical genealogies, and yet Matthew includes four -- all of which have sexual activities that would have been considered scandalous at the time. Tamar (v.[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#3)) disguised herself as a prostitute in order to have sex with her father-in-law, Judah; Rahab (v.[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#5)) was a prostitute who took in Joshua's spies; Ruth (v. [5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#5)) seduced her relative Boaz; and Bathsheba (v.[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#6)) committed adultery with David.
[Was Jesus the son of David?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/Son_of_David.html)
  2. _Judas begat Phares ... of Thamar._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#3)) 
Judah (Judas) "went in unto" his daughter-in-law, Tamar (Thamar), who was disguised as a prostitute. She conceived and bore Pharez (Phares), an ancestor of Jesus (or actually Joseph). See [Genesis 38:13-30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/38.html#S3)
[How many generations must a bastard wait until his offspring can enter the congregation of the Lord?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/bastard.html)
  3. _Salmon begat Booz of Rachab._ ([v.5a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#5)) 
Rachab (Rahab) is probably the prostitute who was visited by the spies in Joshua ([2:1-21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/2.html#S1), [6:22-25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/6.html#22)). She is not mentioned anywhere else in the Bible as being the mother of Booz.
  4. _Booz begat Obed of Ruth._ ([v.5b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#5)) 
See [Ruth 2-4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ru/2.html) where she seduces Boaz (Booz).
  5. _David the king begat Solomon._ ([v.6a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#6)) 
There are 28 generations listed from David to Jesus in Matthew's genealogy, while Luke's ([3:23-31](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/3.html#23)) has 43. Except for David at one end and Jesus at the other, only three names in the two lists are the same.
[The genealogy of Jesus](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gen_ml.html)
[From which of David's sons was Jesus descended?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/davids_sons.html)
[How many generations were there from David to Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/generations.html)
  6. _Of her that had been the wife of Urias_ ([v.6b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#6)) 
Bathsheba (See the story about the adulterous affair between David and Bathsheba that resulted in the birth of Solomon in [2 Samuel 11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2sam/11.html#S2).)
  7. _Joram begat Ozias._ ([v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#8)) 
[Who was Uzziah's father?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/uzziah.html)
  8. _Ozias begat Joatham._ ([v.9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#9)) 
[1 Chr 3:11-12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/3.html#11) lists three generations between Osiah and Jotham (Joash, Amaziah, and Azariah); Matthew omits all three.
[Matthew vs. 1 Chronicles genealogies](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gen_mc.html)
  9. _Josias begat Jechonias._ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#11)) 
[Was Jeconiah the son or the grandson of Josiah?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jeconiahs_dad.html)
  10. _Jechonias begat Salathiel._ ([v.12a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#12)) 
[Did Jeconiah have any children?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jeconiah.html)
  11. _Salathiel begat Zorobabel_ ([v.12b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#12)) 
[Who was Zerubbabel's father?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/zerub.html)
  12. _Jacob begat Joseph._ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#16)) 
[Matthew vs Luke genealogies of Jesus](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gen_ml.html)
[Who was Jesus's paternal grandfather?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/joseph.html)
  13. _From David until the carrying away into Babylon are fourteen generations._ ([v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#17)) 
[How many generations were between the Babylonian captivity and Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/fourteen.html)
[How many generations from Jesus to Abraham?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/42.html)
  14. _When ... Mary was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost._ ([v.18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#18)) 
After listing the genealogy of Jesus, Matthew tells us that Joseph was not Jesus's father after all, which of course makes the entire genealogy meaningless. The Holy Ghost, not Joseph, was the one who impregnated Mary, contradicting many scriptures which clearly state that Joseph was the father of Jesus.
[Was Joseph the father of Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jesus_dad.html)
  15. _Joseph ... being a just man._ ([v.19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#19)) 
[Has there ever been a just person?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/just.html)
  16. _That which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost._ ([v.20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#20)) 
According to [Luke 1:35](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/1.html#35), Mary knew before she became pregnant that the Holy Ghost would "come upon" her. If so, then she must not have told Joseph, since in this verse he is told by an angel after Mary is already pregnant.
[Dreams in the Bible](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/topics/dreams.html)
  17. _Thou shalt call his name JESUS. ... They shall call his name Emmanuel._ ([v.21a, 23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#21)) 
[What was Jesus H. Christ's real name?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/JesusHChrist.html)
  18. _He shall save his people from their sins._ ([v.21b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#21)) 
[What will happen to Jews when they die?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jews.html)
  19. _A virgin shall be with child, and shall bring forth a son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel._ ([v.23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#23)) 
The prophecy given in [Is 7:14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/7.html#14) referred not to a virgin but to a young woman, living at the time of the prophecy. And Jesus, of course, was called Jesus -- and is not called Emmanuel in any other verse in the New Testament.
  20. _Joseph ... knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son._ ([v.24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#24)) 
Many Christians believe that Mary was always a virgin, but these verses imply that she and Joseph "came together" after the birth of Jesus, their "firstborn son."
