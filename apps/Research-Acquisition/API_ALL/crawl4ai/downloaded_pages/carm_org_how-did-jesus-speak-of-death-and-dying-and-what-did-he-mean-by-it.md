# How did Jesus speak of death and dying and what did he mean by it?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 11, 2018 
66 min read 
Generally speaking, conditionalists define death as something that occurs with both body and soul. Though not all conditionalists hold this exact position, it is still worth our examining what Jesus said about death and killing. There are nine words he used.
At the end of this article is a table containing every instance in the New Testament where Jesus spoke of death, dying, killing, etc., in one form or another. By reading each one, I was able to determine that Jesus is speaking of three main categories: 1) physical death, 2) spiritual death and separation from God, 3) being spiritually dead but physically alive. This is what I found.
This means that 86% of the time Jesus spoke of physical death. 9% of the time he spoke of spiritual death and separation from God. 4% at the time Jesus spoke of being spiritually dead while physically alive. There is only one instance of a figurative usage which makes it 1%.
