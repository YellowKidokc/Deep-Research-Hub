### CHAPTER 58
He shall wash his feet in the blood of the wicked 
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/int.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/fv.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/wom1.gif) 58:1-11 
To the chief Musician, Altaschith, Michtam of David. 
1 Do ye indeed speak righteousness, O congregation? do ye judge uprightly, O ye sons of men? 
2 Yea, in heart ye work wickedness; ye weigh the violence of your hands in the earth. 
3 The wicked are estranged from the womb: they go astray **as soon as they be born, speaking lies.**
4 Their poison is like the poison of a serpent: they are like the deaf adder that stoppeth her ear; 
5 Which will not hearken to the voice of charmers, charming never so wisely. 
Wicked people are wicked before they are born. 
They are born speaking lies. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/58.html#1n)] 
6 **Break their teeth, O God, in their mouth** : break out the great teeth of the young lions, O LORD. 
Break their teeth, Oh God, in their mouth. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/58.html#2n)] 
7 Let them melt away as waters which run continually: when he bendeth his bow to shoot his arrows, **let them be as cut in pieces.**
Let them be cut into pieces. 
8 As a snail which melteth, **let every one of them pass away: like the untimely birth of a woman** , that they may not see the sun.
9 Before your pots can feel the thorns, he shall take them away as with a whirlwind, both living, and in his wrath. 
Let them die, like a snail melts. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/58.html#3n)] 
Let them be aborted before they are born. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/58.html#4n)] 
10 **The righteous shall rejoice when he seeth the vengeance: he shall wash his feet in the blood of the wicked.**
11 So that a man shall say, Verily there is a reward for the righteous: verily he is a God that judgeth in the earth. 
Righteous people will rejoice when they see the vengeance. 
They will wash their feet in the blood of the wicked. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/58.html#5n)]

### Notes
  1. _The wicked are estranged from the womb: they go astray as soon as they be born, speaking lies._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/58.html#3)) 
Wicked people are wicked from birth -- God made them that way. 
They tell lies immediately after birth (before they can even talk!). 
  2. _Break their teeth, O God, in their mouth ... Let them be as cut in pieces._ ([vv.6-7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/58.html#6)) 
[How should we treat our enemies?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/enemies.html)  

  3. _As a snail which melteth_ ([v.8a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/58.html#8)) 
Snails don't melt; they simply leave a slimy trail as they move along. 
  4. _Let every one of them pass away: like the untimely birth of a woman, that they may not see the sun._ ([v.8b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/58.html#8)) 
A prayer that your enemies will die like an aborted fetus. 
[What the Bible says about abortion](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/abortion.html)
  5. _The righteous shall rejoice when he seeth the vengeance: he shall wash his feet in the blood of the wicked._ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/58.html#10)) 
"The righteous" will rejoice when he sees "the wicked" being dismembered by God. He'll even get a chance to wash his feet in their blood. Now _that's_ entertainment! 
[Should we rejoice to see our enemies suffer?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/rejoice.html)
