## [Exodus](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/outline.html "Exodus Outline")
### CHAPTER 20
God spoke all these words.  
(Which are often called "The Ten Commandments")
  
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/interp.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/int.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/good1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/wom1.gif) 20:1-17  
Cf. [Dt 5:1-21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#ten)
1 And **God spake all these words** , saying,
2 I am the LORD thy God, which have brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.
God spoke all these words [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#1n)]:
> I am your God, who brought you out of Egypt.
3 Thou shalt have no other gods before me.
> Don't have any gods before me. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#2n)]
4 Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth. 
> Don't make any graven images. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#3n)] 
> Don't make a likeness of anything. (No drawing, painting, photography, sculptures, etc.)
5 Thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them: for **I the LORD thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation** of them that hate me;
6 And shewing mercy unto thousands of them that love me, and keep my commandments.
> I am a jealous God, punishing children for what their fathers did, to the third and fourth generation. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#4n)]
7 Thou shalt not take the name of the LORD thy God in vain; for the LORD will not hold him guiltless that taketh his name in vain.
> Don't take my name in vain. (I'll punish you if you do.)
8 Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy.
> Remember the sabbath day. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#5n)]
9 Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work:
> Do all your work on the six non-sabbath days.
10 But the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates:
> The seventh day is the sabbath. Don't do any work on that day. And don't let your children, slaves, strangers, or cattle do any work on that day either.
11 For **in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them** is, and rested the seventh day: wherefore the LORD blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it.
> I made heaven and earth in six days [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#6n)] and rested on the seventh. So I blessed the seventh day and made it holy.
12 Honour thy father and thy mother: that thy days may be long upon the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.
> Honor your father and thy mother. [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#7n)]
13 Thou shalt not kill.
> Don't kill. [[8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#8n)]
14 Thou shalt not commit adultery.
> Don't commit adultery. [[9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#9n)]
15 Thou shalt not steal.
> Don't steal. [[10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#10n)]
16 Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour.
> Don't lie. [[11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#11n)]
17 **Thou shalt not covet** thy neighbour's house, thou shalt not covet **thy neighbour's wife** , **nor his manservant, nor his maidservant** , **nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that is thy neighbour's.**
> Don't covet your neighbor's possessions [[12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#12n)] -- including his wife, slaves, ox, or ass.
A few more commandments
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sex1.gif) 20:18-26
18 And all the people saw the thunderings, and the lightnings, and the noise of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking: and when the people saw it, they removed, and stood afar off.
The people saw thunder and lightning, the noise of the trumpet, and the smoking mountain.
19 And they said unto Moses, Speak thou with us, and we will hear: but let not God speak with us, lest we die.
They said to Moses, 
> Speak to us and we will hear. 
> But don't let God speak to us or we will die.
20 And Moses said unto the people, Fear not: for God is come to prove you, and that his fear may be before your faces, that ye sin not.
Moses said, 
> Don't be afraid.
> Or rather, fear God, so you won't sin.
21 And the people stood afar off, and Moses drew near unto the thick darkness where God was.
22 And the LORD said unto Moses, Thus thou shalt say unto the children of Israel, Ye have seen that I have talked with you from heaven.
Moses came near the thick darkness where God was.
God said to Moses,
> Tell the people you talked to me from heaven.
23 Ye shall not make with me gods of silver, neither shall ye make unto you gods of gold.
> Don't make gods out of silver or gold. [[13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#13n)]
24 An altar of earth **thou shalt** make unto me, and shalt **sacrifice** thereon thy **burnt offerings** , and thy peace offerings, thy sheep, and thine oxen: in all places where I record my name I will come unto thee, and I will bless thee.
25 And if thou wilt make me an altar of stone, thou shalt not build it of hewn stone: for if thou lift up thy tool upon it, thou hast polluted it.
> Build an altar and sacrifice sheep and oxen to me as burnt offerings and peace offerings. [[14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#14n)]
> Do this everywhere you go and I'll bless you for it.
26 **Neither shalt thou go up by steps unto mine altar, that thy nakedness be not discovered thereon.**
> Don't go up steps to the altar, so no one will discover your nakedness. [[15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#15n)]

### Notes
  1. _God spake all these words._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#1)) 
"These words" are generally considered to be _The_ Ten Commandments.
But the text doesn't call this un-numbered list "The Ten Commandments"; it only says that "these words" were spoken by God.
(See [Ex 34:28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/34.html#28); [Dt 4:13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/4.html#13), [10:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/10.html#4) where the phrase "Ten Commandments" is actually used.)
  2. _Thou shalt have no other gods before me._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#3)) 
[How many gods are there?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gods.html)
  3. _Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image._ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#4)) 
[Is it OK to make images?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/images.html)
  4. _I the LORD thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation._ ([v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#5)) 
[Are we punished for the sins of others?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/iniquity.html)
  5. _Remember the sabbath day._ ([v8.](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#8)) 
[Is it necessary to keep the Sabbath?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sabbath.html)
  6. _In six days the LORD made heaven and earth._ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#11)) 
Believers often say that the "days" of creation should be taken allegorically, but this verse is quite clear.
God created the universe in six 24 hour days.
  7. _Honour thy father and thy mother._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#12)) 
Verses 12-16 list the (more or less) good commandments.
([The only ones Jesus seemed to know or care about.](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/19.html#17 "SAB: Matthew 19:17-19"))
[How should parents be treated?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/parents.html)
[Is it OK to call your father (or anyone else) father?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/father.html)
  8. _Thou shalt not kill._ ([v.13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#13)) 
[To kill or not to kill](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/kill.html)
  9. _Thou shalt not commit adultery._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#14)) 
[Is it wrong to commit adultery?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/adultery.html)
  10. _Thou shalt not steal._ ([v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#15)) 
[Is it wrong to steal?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/steal.html)
  11. _Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour._ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#16)) 
[Is it wrong to lie?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/lie.html)
  12. _Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's house, ... wife, ... manservant, ... maidservant,... ox, ... ass, nor any thing that is thy neighbour's._ ([v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#17)) 
To the God of the Bible, women and slaves (servants in the KJV) are the property of men; they are his possessions -- like an ox or an ass.
[Is it OK to covet?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/covet.html)
[Does God approve of slavery?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/slavery.html)
  13. _Ye shall not make with me gods of silver, neither shall ye make unto you gods of gold._ ([v.23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#23)) 
God adds a few more rules in verses 23-26.
  14. _Thou shalt make unto me, and shalt sacrifice thereon thy burnt offerings._ ([v.24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#24)) 
[Does God desire animal sacrifices?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/desire.html)
[Did God command the Israelites to make burnt offerings?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/burnt.html)
  15. _Neither shalt thou go up by steps unto mine altar, that thy nakedness be not discovered thereon._ ([v.26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#26)) 
God tells the priests not to go up the steps to the altar "that thy nakedness not be discovered thereon."
(Skirts on stairs _are_ a problem.)
