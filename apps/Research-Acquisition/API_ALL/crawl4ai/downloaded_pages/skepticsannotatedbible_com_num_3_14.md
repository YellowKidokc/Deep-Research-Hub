### CHAPTER 3
God kills Aaron's sons, Nadab and Abihu
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/int.gif) 3:1-4, Cf. [Lev 10:1-3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/10.html#1)
1 These also are the generations of Aaron and Moses in the day that the LORD spake with Moses in mount Sinai.
These are the names of the sons of Aaron and Moses.
2 And these are the names of the sons of Aaron; Nadab the firstborn, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
The sons of Aaron: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
3 These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the priests which were anointed, whom he consecrated to minister in the priest's office.
Aaron's sons were anointed priests.
4 And **Nadab and Abihu died before the LORD, when they offered strange fire before the LORD,** in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no children: and Eleazar and Ithamar ministered in the priest's office in the sight of Aaron their father.
God killed Nadab and Abihu for offering him strange fire. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#1n)]
Instructions for priests
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/int.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 3:5-13
5 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,
6 Bring the tribe of Levi near, and present them before Aaron the priest, that they may minister unto him.
God said to Moses, 
> Bring the tribe of Levi to Aaron.
7 And they shall keep his charge, and the charge of the whole congregation before the tabernacle of the congregation, to do the service of the tabernacle.
8 And they shall keep all the instruments of the tabernacle of the congregation, and the charge of the children of Israel, to do the service of the tabernacle.
9 And thou shalt give the Levites unto Aaron and to his sons: they are wholly given unto him out of the children of Israel.
> They will serve in the tabernacle.
10 And thou shalt appoint Aaron and his sons, and they shall wait on their priest's office: and ** the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.**
> Kill any strangers that come near the tabernacle. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#2n)]
11 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,
12 And I, behold, I have taken the Levites from among the children of Israel instead of all the firstborn that openeth the matrix among the children of Israel: therefore the Levites shall be mine;
> I have separated the Levites from the rest of the Israelites.
> The Levites belong to me.
13 Because **all the firstborn are mine; for on the day that I smote all the firstborn in the land of Egypt I hallowed unto me all the firstborn in Israel, both man and beast: mine shall they be: I am the LORD.**
> All firstborn men and beasts belong to me.
> They became mine when I killed all the firstborn of Egypt.
> I am the Lord.
A census of the Levites
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/boring.63b5b35a30388.jpg) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/wom1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/int.gif) 3:14-51,

14 And the LORD spake unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,
15 Number the children of Levi after the house of their fathers, by their families: every male from a month old and upward shalt thou number them.
God said to Moses, 
> Count the male Levites that are at least one month old. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#3n)]
16 And Moses numbered them according to the word of the LORD, as he was commanded.
So Moses numbered the Levites according the the word of the Lord.
17 And these were the sons of Levi by their names; Gershon, and Kohath, and Merari.
The sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.
18 And these are the names of the sons of Gershon by their families; Libni, and Shimei.
The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei.
19 And the sons of Kohath by their families; Amram, and Izehar, Hebron, and Uzziel.
The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izehar, Hebron, and Uzziel.
20 And the sons of Merari by their families; Mahli, and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites according to the house of their fathers.
The sons of Merari: Mahli, and Mushi. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#4n)]
21 Of Gershon was the family of the Libnites, and the family of the Shimites: these are the families of the Gershonites.
22 Those that were numbered of them, according to the number of all the males, from a month old and upward, even those that were numbered of them were seven thousand and five hundred.
23 The families of the Gershonites shall pitch behind the tabernacle westward.
24 And the chief of the house of the father of the Gershonites shall be Eliasaph the son of Lael.
25 And the charge of the sons of Gershon in the tabernacle of the congregation shall be the tabernacle, and the tent, the covering thereof, and the hanging for the door of the tabernacle of the congregation,
26 And the hangings of the court, and the curtain for the door of the court, which is by the tabernacle, and by the altar round about, and the cords of it for all the service thereof.
There were 7,500 male Gershonites older than one month.
They camped on the west side of the tabernacle.
The were in charge of the tent, tent covering, court hangings, door curtains, and cords. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#5n)]
27 And of Kohath was the family of the Amramites, and the family of the Izeharites, and the family of the Hebronites, and the family of the Uzzielites: these are the families of the Kohathites.
28 In the number of all the males, from a month old and upward, were eight thousand and six hundred, keeping the charge of the sanctuary.
29 The families of the sons of Kohath shall pitch on the side of the tabernacle southward.
30 And the chief of the house of the father of the families of the Kohathites shall be Elizaphan the son of Uzziel.
31 And their charge shall be the ark, and the table, and the candlestick, and the altars, and the vessels of the sanctuary wherewith they minister, and the hanging, and all the service thereof.
There were 8,600 male Kohathites older than one month.
They camped on the south side of the tabernacle.
They were in charge of the ark, table, candlestick, altars, vessels, and hanging.
32 And Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest shall be chief over the chief of the Levites, and have the oversight of them that keep the charge of the sanctuary.
Aaron's son, Eleazar, will be chief over the Levites. He will oversee the sanctuary.
33 Of Merari was the family of the Mahlites, and the family of the Mushites: these are the families of Merari.
34 And those that were numbered of them, according to the number of all the males, from a month old and upward, were six thousand and two hundred.
35 And the chief of the house of the father of the families of Merari was Zuriel the son of Abihail: these shall pitch on the side of the tabernacle northward.
36 And under the custody and charge of the sons of Merari shall be the boards of the tabernacle, and the bars thereof, and the pillars thereof, and the sockets thereof, and all the vessels thereof, and all that serveth thereto,
37 And the pillars of the court round about, and their sockets, and their pins, and their cords.
There were 6,200 male Merariites older than one month.
They camped on the north side of the tabernacle.
They were in charge of the tabernacle boards, bars, pillars, sockets, vessels, and cords.
38 But those that encamp before the tabernacle toward the east, even before the tabernacle of the congregation eastward, shall be Moses, and Aaron and his sons, keeping the charge of the sanctuary for the charge of the children of Israel; and ****the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.****
Moses, Aaron, and his sons camped on the east side of the tabernacle.
They took care of the sanctuary.
(Kill any stranger that comes near the sanctuary.) [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#6n)]
39 All that were numbered of the Levites, which Moses and Aaron numbered at the commandment of the LORD, throughout their families, all the males from a month old and upward, were twenty and two thousand.
Moses and Aaron counted a total of 22,000 [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#7n)] Levite males that were at least one month old, as they were commanded by God.
40 And the LORD said unto Moses, Number all the firstborn of the males of the children of Israel from a month old and upward, and take the number of their names.
41 And thou shalt take the Levites for me (I am the LORD) instead of all the firstborn among the children of Israel; and the cattle of the Levites instead of all the firstlings among the cattle of the children of Israel.
God said to Moses, 
> Count the firstborn Israelite males that are at least one year old. [[8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#8n)]
> Give the firstborn male Levites to me (instead of the non-Levite Isrealite firstborn children and animals).
> I am God.
42 And Moses numbered, as the LORD commanded him, all the firstborn among the children of Israel.
Moses numbered all the firstborn Israelite children, as God commanded.
43 And all the firstborn males by the number of names, from a month old and upward, of those that were numbered of them, were twenty and two thousand two hundred and threescore and thirteen.
And Moses counted 22,273 firstborn male Israelites. [[9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#9n)]
44 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,
45 Take the Levites instead of all the firstborn among the children of Israel, and the cattle of the Levites instead of their cattle; and the Levites shall be mine: I am the LORD.
God said to Moses, 
> Take the Levites and give them to me (instead of the firstborn Israelites and cattle)
> I am God.
46 And for those that are to be redeemed of the two hundred and threescore and thirteen of the firstborn of the children of Israel, which are more than the Levites;
> Those that are to be redeemed are the 273 firstborn Israelites, which are more than the Levites.
47 Thou shalt even take five shekels apiece by the poll, after the shekel of the sanctuary shalt thou take them: (the shekel is twenty gerahs:)
48 And thou shalt give the money, wherewith the odd number of them is to be redeemed, unto Aaron and to his sons.
> Take five shekels for each, and give the money to Aaron and his sons.
49 And Moses took the redemption money of them that were over and above them that were redeemed by the Levites:
50 Of the firstborn of the children of Israel took he the money; a thousand three hundred and threescore and five shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary:
51 And Moses gave the money of them that were redeemed unto Aaron and to his sons, according to the word of the LORD, as the LORD commanded Moses.
Moses took the redemption money, 1375 shekels, and gave it to Aaron and his sons, according to the word of the Lord.

### Notes
  1. _Nadab and Abihu died before the LORD, when they offered strange fire before the LORD._ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#4)) 
[DWB](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html): [God burned Aaron's sons to death for offering "strange fire before the Lord"](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/lev.html#strange-fire)
  2. _The stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death._ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#10)) 
[How should strangers be treated?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/strangers.html)
  3. _Every male from a month old and upward shalt thou number them._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#14)) 
When "Moses numbered them according to the word of the Lord" he was told to count "every male from a month old and upward."
Women and girls didn't count as persons. Neither did babies (or fetuses) under 1 month old. (See [Lev 27:6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/27.html#6))
[Did God tell Moses to number the Levites?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/number-levites.html)
[What the Bible says about abortion](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/abortion.html)
  4. _The sons of Merari by their families; Mahli...._ ([v.20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#20)) 
[Was Mahli the son of Levi?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/mahli.html)
  5. _Seven thousand and five hundred_ ([v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#22)) 
It was a tough job. It took 7,500 guys to take care of tent, tent covering, court hangings, door curtains, and cords.
(Similar numbers and jobs were given to the other Levite clans.)
  6. _The stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death._ ([v.38](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#38)) 
[How should strangers be treated?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/strangers.html)
  7. _All that were numbered of the Levites ... twenty and two thousand._ ([v.39](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#39)) 
[How many male Levites more than one month old did Moses and Aaron count?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/levites.html)
  8. _The LORD said unto Moses, Number all the firstborn of the males of the children of Israel from a month old and upward._ ([v.40](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#40)) 
Once again, God told Moses to count "every male from a month old and upward."
Women and girls didn't count as persons. Neither did babies (or fetuses) under 1 month old.
[Is it OK to take a census?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/census.html)
[What the Bible says about abortion](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/abortion.html)
  9. _All the firstborn among the children of Israel. ... were twenty and two thousand two hundred and threescore and thirteen._ ([vv.42-43](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#42)) 
Moses counted 22,273 firstborn male Israelites more than one month old, and we learned [in the last chapter](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/2.html#32) that there were 603,550 male Israelites over 20 years old.
So there must have been at least a million or so male Israelites over a month old -- and yet only 22,273 of these were firstborn.
This means that only about 2% of the male Israelites were firstborn, with an average of fifty sons in each family!
[Is it OK to take a census?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/census.html)
[What the Bible says about abortion](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/abortion.html)
