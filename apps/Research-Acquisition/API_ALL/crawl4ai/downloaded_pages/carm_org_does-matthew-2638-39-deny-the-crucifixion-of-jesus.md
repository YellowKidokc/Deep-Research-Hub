# Does Matthew 26:38-39 deny the Crucifixion of Jesus?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 27, 2010 
8 min read 
“Then He said to them, ‘My soul is deeply grieved, to the point of death; remain here and keep watch with Me.’ 39 And He went a little beyond them, and fell on His face and prayed, saying, ‘My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass from Me; yet not as I will, but as You will ([Matthew 26:38-39, NASB](https://ref.ly/Matt%2026.38-39;nasb95?t=biblia)).'”
There are a number of Muslims who state that [Matthew 26:38-39](https://ref.ly/Matt%2026.38-39;nasb95?t=biblia) teaches that Jesus was not crucified. (See: <http://www.missionislam.com/comprel/crucified.htm> for an example.) They base their viewpoint by combining [Matthew 26:38-39](https://ref.ly/Matt%2026.38-39;nasb95?t=biblia) and [Hebrews 5:7](https://ref.ly/Heb%205.7;nasb95?t=biblia). Some Muslims believe that Jesus prayed to be delivered from death with the words “let this cup pass” and God answered that prayer by delivering Jesus from the crucifixion.
I have already dealt with [Hebrews 5:7](https://ref.ly/Heb%205.7;nasb95?t=biblia) in a separate article (see: [Does Hebrews 5:7 deny the Crucifixion of Jesus?)](https://carm.org/islam/does-hebrews-57-deny-the-crucifixion-of-jesus/) where I pointed out that **(1)** the text never says that God spared Jesus from dying on the cross, only that Jesus prayed to be delivered; **(2)** the context of [Hebrews 5:7](https://ref.ly/Heb%205.7;nasb95?t=biblia) is where the author quotes a Messianic Psalm ([Psalm 22:24](https://ref.ly/Ps%2022.24;nasb95?t=biblia)) which prophecies to the death of Jesus by crucifixion ([Psalm 22:16](https://ref.ly/Ps%2022.16;nasb95?t=biblia)) and His subsequent triumph; **(3)** Jesus was delivered from death in the greatest sense by triumphing over it through God raising Him from the dead, which was an answer to His prayer for deliverance; and **(4)** there are numerous passages in the book of Hebrews which teach that Jesus died by crucifixion (cf. [Hebrews 2:9-10](https://ref.ly/Heb%202.9-10;nasb95?t=biblia), [14](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%202.14;nasb95?t=biblia); [6:6](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%206.6;nasb95?t=biblia); [7:27](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%207.27;nasb95?t=biblia); [9:11-28](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%209.11-28;nasb95?t=biblia); [10:1-39](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%2010.1-39;nasb95?t=biblia); [10:10](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%2010.10;nasb95?t=biblia); [11:17-19](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%2011.17-19;nasb95?t=biblia); [12:2](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%2012.2;nasb95?t=biblia); [12:24](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%2012.24;nasb95?t=biblia); [13:12](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%2013.12;nasb95?t=biblia); [13:20-21](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%2013.20-21;nasb95?t=biblia)). Therefore, [Hebrews 5:7](https://ref.ly/Heb%205.7;nasb95?t=biblia) does not teach that God spared Jesus from being crucified. Rather, God delivered Jesus from death by raising Him from the dead.
