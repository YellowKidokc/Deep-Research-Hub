## [1 Kings](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/outline.html "1 Kings Outline")
### CHAPTER 19
Jezebel threatens to kill Elijah
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 19:1-4
1 And Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done, and withal how he had slain all the prophets with the sword.
Ahab told his wife, Jezebel, that Elijah had [killed the prophets of Baal. ](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/18.html#S3)
2 Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time.
Jezebel sent a message to Elijah, saying, 
> I'm going to kill you tomorrow at about this time.
3 And when he saw that, he arose, and went for his life, and came to Beersheba, which belongeth to Judah, and left his servant there.
4 But he himself went a day's journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down under a juniper tree: and he requested for himself that he might die; and said, It is enough; now, O LORD, take away my life; for I am not better than my fathers.
When Elijah heard that, he went into the wilderness and sat under a juniper tree, and said to God, 
> Just kill me now.
An angel visits Elijah (and feeds him angel food cake)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 19:5-8
5 And as he lay and slept under a juniper tree, behold, then an angel touched him, and said unto him, Arise and eat.
Then he went to sleep and was touched by an angel, who said to him, 
> Get up and eat.
6 And he looked, and, behold, there was a cake baken on the coals, and a cruse of water at his head. And he did eat and drink, and laid him down again.
Elijah looked up and saw a cake and some water. 
So he ate the angel food cake, drank the water, and went back to sleep.
7 And the angel of the LORD came again the second time, and touched him, and said, Arise and eat; because the journey is too great for thee.
The angel came back, touched him again, and said, 
> Get up and eat. It's time to get going.
8 And he arose, and did eat and drink, and went in the strength of that meat [forty days and forty nights](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/topics/forty-days.html) unto Horeb the mount of God.
So Elijah got up and climbed God's mountain (Mount Horeb).
He stayed there [forty days](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/topics/forty-days.html) without eating or drinking. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/19.html#1n)]
God visits Elijah
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 19:9-18
9 And he came thither unto a cave, and lodged there; and, behold, the word of the LORD came to him, and he said unto him, What doest thou here, Elijah?
He went into a cave where the word of God came to him and said, 
> What are you doing here, Elijah?
10 And he said, I have been very jealous for the LORD God of hosts: for the children of Israel have forsaken thy covenant, thrown down thine altars, and slain thy prophets with the sword; and I, even I only, am left; and they seek my life, to take it away.
Elijah said to the word of God, 
> The Israelites have destroyed your altars and killed your prophets. 
> Now they want to kill me.
11 And he said, Go forth, and stand upon the mount before the LORD. And, behold, the LORD passed by, and a great and strong wind rent the mountains, and brake in pieces the rocks before the LORD; but the LORD was not in the wind: and after the wind an earthquake; but the LORD was not in the earthquake:
The word of God said to Elijah, 
> Go stand on the mountain in front of God.
So Elijah stood on the mountain, and a strong wind blew that tore the mountain apart. But God wasn't in the wind.
Then an earthquake came. But God wasn't in the earthquake.
12 And after the earthquake a fire; but the LORD was not in the fire: and after the fire a still small voice.
Then there was a fire. But God wasn't in the fire. 
Finally, there was a little voice.
13 And it was so, when Elijah heard it, that he wrapped his face in his mantle, and went out, and stood in the entering in of the cave. And, behold, there came a voice unto him, and said, What doest thou here, Elijah?
A voice said to him, 
> What are you doing here, Elijah?
14 And he said, I have been very jealous for the LORD God of hosts: because the children of Israel have forsaken thy covenant, thrown down thine altars, and slain thy prophets with the sword; and I, even I only, am left; and they seek my life, to take it away.
Elijah said to the voice, 
> The Israelites have destroyed your altars and killed your prophets. 
> Now they want to kill me.
15 And the LORD said unto him, Go, return on thy way to the wilderness of Damascus: and when thou comest, anoint Hazael to be king over Syria:
16 And Jehu the son of Nimshi shalt thou anoint to be king over Israel: and Elisha the son of Shaphat of Abelmeholah shalt thou anoint to be prophet in thy room.
God said to him, 
> Go make Hazael the king of Syria, 
> Jehu the son of Nimshi [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/19.html#2n)] the king of Israel,
> and Elisha the prophet.
17 And it shall come to pass, that him that escapeth the sword of Hazael shall Jehu slay: and him that escapeth from the sword of Jehu shall Elisha slay.
> Jehu will kill whoever isn't killed by Hazael. 
> And whoever isn't killed by Jehu will be killed by Elisha.
18 Yet I have left me seven thousand in Israel, all the knees which have not bowed unto Baal, and every mouth which hath not kissed him.
> But there will be 7000 in Israel who haven't bowed or kissed Baal who will survive.
Elisha joins Elijah
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 19:19-21
19 So he departed thence, and found Elisha the son of Shaphat, who was plowing with twelve yoke of oxen before him, and he with the twelfth: and Elijah passed by him, and cast his mantle upon him.
So Elijah found Elisha, who was plowing with 12 oxen, and put his mantle on him. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/19.html#3n)]
20 And he left the oxen, and ran after Elijah, and said, Let me, I pray thee, kiss my father and my mother, and then I will follow thee. And he said unto him, Go back again: for what have I done to thee?
Elisha said to Elijah, 
> Let me kiss my mom and dad, then I'll follow you.
Elijah said, "OK".
21 And he returned back from him, and took a yoke of oxen, and slew them, and boiled their flesh with the instruments of the oxen, and gave unto the people, and they did eat. Then he arose, and went after Elijah, and ministered unto him.
Elisha killed his oxen, boiled their flesh, and gave it to the people. 
Then he followed after Elijah.

### Notes
  1. _And he arose, and did eat and drink, and went in the strength of that meat forty days and forty nights._ ([v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/19.html#8)) 
Elijah went forty days on the strength of two (angel food cake) meals.
  2. _Jehu the son of Nimshi_ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/19.html#16)) 
[Was Jehu the son or grandson of Nimshi?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jehu.html)
  3. _Elijah passed by him, and cast his mantle upon him._ ([v.19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/19.html#19)) 
[When did Elisha receive Elijah's mantle?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/mantle.html)
