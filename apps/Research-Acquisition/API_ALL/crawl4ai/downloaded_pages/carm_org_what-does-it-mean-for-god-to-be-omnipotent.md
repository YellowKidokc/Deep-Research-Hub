# What does it mean for God to be omnipotent?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 13, 2021 
3 min read 
What it means for God to be omnipotent is that he can do whatever he desires to do. God has all power, but this power is consistent with his nature. He cannot do the logically impossible (which would conflict with his nature). God cannot make a round square. He cannot stop being God ([Mal. 3:6](https://ref.ly/Mal.%203.6;nasb95?t=biblia)). He cannot lie ([Titus 1:2](https://ref.ly/Titus%201.2;nasb95?t=biblia)). So God’s omnipotence means he can do whatever he desires to do because he has that power to create an entire universe and hold all the atoms together by the exertion of his will ([Col. 1:17](https://ref.ly/Col.%201.17;nasb95?t=biblia); [Heb. 1:3](https://ref.ly/Heb.%201.3;nasb95?t=biblia)). ￼![What does it mean for God to be omnipotent? God is omnipotent](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/word-cloud-omnipotent-god.png)
Omnipotence “God’s ability to do all things that do not conflict with the divine will or knowledge. God’s power is limited only by God’s own nature and not by any external force ([Job 42:2](https://ref.ly/Job%2042.2;nasb95?t=biblia); [Matt. 19:26](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2019.26;nasb95?t=biblia); [Luke 1:37](https://ref.ly/Luke%201.37;nasb95?t=biblia)).” (McKim, Donald K. The Westminster Dictionary of Theological Terms, Second Edition: Revised and Expanded. Westminster John Knox Press. Kindle Edition.)
Omnipotence “having unlimited power and able to do anything” (https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/omnipotent)
