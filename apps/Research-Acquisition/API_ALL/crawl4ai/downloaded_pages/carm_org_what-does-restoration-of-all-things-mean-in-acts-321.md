# What does “restoration of all things” mean in Acts 3:21?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 24, 2009 
2 min read 
![restoration of all things](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/beauty-400x400.jpg) [Acts 3:21](https://ref.ly/Acts%203.21;nasb95?t=biblia) says, “whom heaven must receive until the period of restoration of all things about which God spoke by the mouth of His holy prophets from ancient time.”
This is most probably a reference to the future period when God will restore the earth to its original condition which existed before[ the fall ](https://carm.org/dictionary/fall/)of Adam and Eve. We see this reflected in Paul’s comments as well.
[Rom. 8:20-21](https://ref.ly/Rom.%208.20-21;nasb95?t=biblia), “For the creation was subjected to futility, not of its own will, but because of Him who subjected it, in hope 21 that the creation itself also will be set free from its slavery to corruption into the freedom of the glory of the children of God.”
