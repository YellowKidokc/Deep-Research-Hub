# Did Jesus sin by telling the disciples to take a donkey?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
February 20, 2024 
5 min read 
Did Jesus sin by telling the disciples to take a donkey in [Matt. 21:1-3](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2021.1-3;nasb95?t=biblia), [Mark 11:2-3](https://ref.ly/Mark%2011.2-3;nasb95?t=biblia); [Luke 19:30–32](https://ref.ly/Luke%2019.30%E2%80%9332;nasb95?t=biblia)? There are two main reasons why He did not. First, since Jesus is God in the flesh ([John 1:1](https://ref.ly/John%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia), [14](https://ref.ly/John%201.14;nasb95?t=biblia); [Col. 2:9](https://ref.ly/Col.%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia); [Heb. 1:8](https://ref.ly/Heb.%201.8;nasb95?t=biblia)), the Scriptures about God owning everything applies to Him. Therefore, he could not steal because, ultimately, the donkey and colt belonged to Him. Please consider the following verses.
So very quickly, we see that God owns everything, even the donkey and colt. Therefore, He could not have stolen them, which was not a sin.
