# Was the tax collector named Matthew or Levi?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
1 min read 
Was the tax collector named Matthew or Levi? While all three synoptic gospels describe Jesus calling a tax collector to come and follow him, they seem to disagree on the name, offering both Matthew and Levi. Is this a contradiction? Let’s take a look. ![tax collector Matthew Levi](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/tax-collector-01.jpg)
The answer is very simple. Both are true because Matthew and Levi are two names for the same person. Matthew is the Greek name and Levi was the Hebrew name. As a tax collector, Matthew worked for Greek-speaking Romans. He gathered taxes from Hebrew-speaking Jews. As an example, we see Peter also being called Simon (Matt. 16:16).
