# What are some problems with King James Onlyism?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 19, 2024 
7 min read 
The King James Bible was originally translated in 1611 and has been of great value to the English speaking world ever since. However, there are those who say that it is better than all other English Bibles and should be the only Bible we use. This is called King James Onlyism – and it has problems. As good as the KJV is, it has its areas of concern. And, of course, there are also problems with the King-James-Only attitude.[ ![problems king james only](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/manuscript-psalm-22.jpg) ](https://carm.org/king-james-onlyism/what-are-some-problems-with-king-james-onlyism/attachment/manuscript-psalm-22/)
As you can see, there are problems with a King James-only ideology. But that does not mean the KJV isn’t a good Bible. It is. If you want a more up-to-date Bible without the archaic phrases and outdated words, then go with something like the NASB or the ESV.
