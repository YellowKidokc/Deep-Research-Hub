### Notes
  1. _Herod the tetrach_ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/14.html#1)) 
The Herod referred to here is [Herod Antipas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herod_Antipas), the son of Herod the Great, who ruled Galilee and Perea from 4 BCE to 39 CE.
  2. _Herod ... said unto his servants, This is John the Baptist; he is risen from the dead._ ([vv.1-2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/14.html#1)) 
Herod thought Jesus was a resurrected John the Baptist.
Apparently, it was a common opinion at the time (See [Mt.16.13-14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/16.html#13), [Mk.6:14-15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/6.html#14), [8:27-28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#27), [Lk.9:7-8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#7), [18-19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#18)).
If so many of Jesus's contemporaries could be so easily fooled regarding John the Baptist, what does this do to the credibility of the resurrection of Jesus?
This is an especially strange thing for Herod to say, since John the Baptist was still alive and in his prison! How could he be risen from the dead while still alive?
[Did Herod think Jesus was John the Baptist?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/herod.html)
  3. _His brother Philip's wife._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/14.html#3)) 
Herodias was the wife of Herod Antipas's half-brother Philip I (aka [Herod II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herod_II "Wikipedia: Herod II")), and therefore the marriage was unlawful according to [Lev 18:16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/18.html#16).
  4. _Herod's birthday was kept._ ([v.6a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/14.html#6)) 
This verse and [Gen 40:21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/40.html#20) are the two verses used by Jehovah's Witnesses to condemn birthdays. After all, there are only two birthday parties in the Bible -- the Pharaoh's and Herod's. And since they both were bad guys, birthdays must be bad too.
[Birthdays and Jehovah's Witnesses](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/interp/birthday.html)
  5. _The daughter of Herodias danced before them, and pleased Herod._ ([v.6b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/14.html#6)) 
[Is dancing a sin?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/dancing.html)
  6. _When the disciples saw him walking on the sea, they were troubled, saying, It is a spirit; and they cried out for fear._ ([v.26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/14.html#26)) 
Even after seeing all of Jesus's miracles, the feeding of the five thousand, etc., Jesus's disciples still aren't sure what to make of Jesus. When they see him walking on the water, they think they are seeing a ghost.
  7. _Thou art the Son of God._ ([v.33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/14.html#33)) 
Finally, after seeing Jesus calm the storm, Jesus's disciples believe he is the Son of God.
Notice, though, that in Mark's gospel ([6:51](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/6.html#51)), the disciples aren't nearly so impressed with Jesus, after seeing him calm the storm. They were "amazed" and "wondered", but they didn't declare him to be the Son of God (because they didn't understand what had happened with the loaves and their hearts were hardened).
