### CHAPTER 20
Molech, familiar spirits, and wizards
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/fv.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/lang1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 20:1-5
1 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,
2 Again, thou shalt say to the children of Israel, **Whosoever** he be of the children of Israel, or of the strangers that sojourn in Israel, that **giveth any of his seed unto Molech; he shall surely be put to death: the people of the land shall stone him with stones.**
3 And I will set my face against that man, and will cut him off from among his people; because he hath given of his seed unto Molech, to defile my sanctuary, and to profane my holy name.
God said to Moses, 
> Say this to all of the Israelites, 
>> Whoever gives his seed to Molech shall be killed.
>> The people of the land will stone him with stones [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#1n)]
>> because he has profaned my holy name.
4 And **if the people** of the land do any ways **hide their eyes from the man** , when he giveth of his seed unto Molech, **and kill him not** :
5 **Then I will set my face against that man, and against his family, and will cut him off,** and all that go a whoring after him, to commit whoredom with Molech, from among their people.
> > I will cut off whoever refuses to stone to him to death, along with their families and those who go whoring after, and commit whoredom with, Molech.
6 And the soul that turneth after such as have **familiar spirits** , and after **wizards** , to go a whoring after them, I will even set my face against that soul, and will cut him off from among his people.
> > I will cut off those who go a whoring after familiar spirits and wizards.
7 Sanctify yourselves therefore, and be ye holy: for I am the LORD your God.
> > Be holy, because I am God. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#2n)]
8 And ye shall keep my statutes, and do them: I am the LORD which sanctify you.
> > Keep my statutes.
>> I am the Lord.
People who must be stoned (or burned) to death
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/int.gif)![Homosexuality](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/gay1.gif) 20:9-16
9 For **every one that curseth his father or his mother shall be surely put to death** : he hath cursed his father or his mother; his blood shall be upon him.
> > Whoever curses his father or mother must be put to death. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#3n)]
>> Their blood shall be upon them.
10 And the man that committeth adultery with another man's wife, even he that committeth adultery with his neighbour's wife, **the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.**
> > Adulterers and adulteresses must be put to death. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#4n)]
11 And **the man that lieth with his father's wife** hath uncovered his father's nakedness:**both of them shall surely be put to death** ; their blood shall be upon them.
> > If a man has sex with his father's wife, kill them both. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#5n)]
>> Their blood shall be upon them.
12 And **if a man lie with his daughter in law, both of them shall surely be put to death** : they have wrought confusion; their blood shall be upon them.
> > If a man has sex with his daughter-in-law, kill them both. [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#6n)]
>> Their blood shall be upon them.
13 **If a man also lie with mankind, as he lieth with a woman** , both of them have committed an abomination: **they shall surely be put to death** ; their blood shall be upon them.
> > If a man has sex with another man, kill them both. [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#7n)]
>> They have committed an abomination.
>> Their blood shall be upon them.
14 And **if a man take a wife and her mother, it is wickedness: they shall be burnt with fire, both he and they** ; that there be no wickedness among you.
> > If a man has sex with his wife and her mother [[8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#8n)], burn to death all three.
15 And **if a man lie with a beast,****he shall surely be put to death: and ye shall slay the beast.**
> > If a man has sex with an animal, kill them both.
16 And **if a woman approach unto any beast, and lie down thereto** , **thou shalt kill the woman, and the beast** : they shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.
> > If a woman has sex with an animal, kill them both.
>> Their blood shall be upon them.
People whose nakedness you shouldn't uncover
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sex1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/fv.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/wom1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 20:17-21
17 And **if a man shall take his sister** , his father's daughter, or his mother's daughter, **and see her nakedness, and she see his nakedness** ; it is a wicked thing; and **they shall be cut off** in the sight of their people: he hath uncovered his sister's nakedness;he shall bear his iniquity.
> > If a man takes his sister and sees her naked, and she sees him naked, it is a wicked thing. [[9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#9n)]
>> They shall be cut off.
18 And **if a man shall lie with a woman having her sickness, and shall uncover her nakedness** ; he hath discovered her fountain, and she hath uncovered the fountain of her blood: and **both of them shall be cut off** from among their people.
> > If a man lies with a menstruating woman and uncovers her nakedness, he has discovered her fountain, and she has uncovered the fountain of her blood.
>> Both of them shall be cut off from their people. [[10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#10n)]
19 And **thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy mother's sister, nor of thy father's sister** : for he uncovereth his near kin: they shall bear their iniquity.
> > A man shall not uncover the nakedness of his aunt. Those who do so will bear their iniquity.
20 And **if a man shall lie with his uncle's wife** , he hath uncovered his uncle's nakedness: they shall bear their sin; **they shall die childless**.
> > A man shall not lie with his uncle's wife. That would uncover his uncle's nakedness.
>> They shall die childless.
21 And **if a man shall take his brother's wife** , it is an unclean thing: he hath uncovered his brother's nakedness; **they shall be childless.**
> > A man shall not lie with his brother's wife. It is an unclean thing.
>> They shall die childless.
Do what God says (or he'll hate you)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 20:22-26
22 Ye shall therefore keep all my statutes, and all my judgments, and do them: that the land, whither I bring you to dwell therein, spue you not out.
> > You shall keep all my statutes and do them  
>  (or the land will spue you out).
23 And ye shall not walk in the manners of the nation, which I cast out before you: for they committed all these things, and therefore I abhorred them.
> > Don't behave like the nations that I cast out before you. Because they did all these things. Therefore I abhorred them. [[11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#11n)]
24 But I have said unto you, Ye shall inherit their land, and I will give it unto you to possess it, a land that floweth with milk and honey: I am the LORD your God, which have separated you from other people.
> > But as I've said to you, you'll inherit their land, and I'll give it to you, a land flowing with milk and honey.
>> I am the Lord your God, who has separated you from other people.
25 Ye shall therefore put difference between clean beasts and unclean, and between unclean fowls and clean: and ye shall not make your souls abominable by beast, or by fowl, or by any manner of living thing that creepeth on the ground, which I have separated from you as unclean.
> > So you will discriminate between clean and unclean beasts and fowls. You won't make yourselves abominable with beasts, fowl, or creeping things.
26 And ye shall be holy unto me: for I the LORD am holy, and have severed you from other people, that ye should be mine.
> > You will be holy to me, for I am holy, and I have separated you from other people so you could be mine.
