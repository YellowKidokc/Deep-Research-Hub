# What does Acts 2:44-45 mean when it says that Christians “held all things in common”?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
April 21, 2016 
8 min read 
cts 2 describes the coming of the Holy Spirit upon the early disciples, their preaching of the gospel, and the beginnings of the Christian church. Amidst the description of the character and practice of this early church in Jerusalem, we come to the often discussed and sometimes controversial statement, namely that the disciples distributed their property and held all things in common:[ ![all things in common](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/people-01.jpg) ](https://carm.org/about-bible-verses/what-does-acts-244-45-mean-when-it-says-that-christians-held-all-things-in-common/attachment/people-01-2/)
“Now all the believers were together and held all things in common. They sold their possessions and property and distributed the proceeds to all, as anyone had a need” (Acts 2:44-45)
This pattern is repeated in Acts 4.
