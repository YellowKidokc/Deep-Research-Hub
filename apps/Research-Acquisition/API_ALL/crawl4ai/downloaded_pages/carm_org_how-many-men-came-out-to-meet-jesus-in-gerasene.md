# How many men came out to meet Jesus in Gerasene?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
March 05, 2009 
3 min read 
How many men came out to meet Jesus in Gerasene? The number varies depending on which gospel you look at. Matthew says two men while Mark and Luke say one man. The verse is to look at our Matt. 8:28, Mark 5:1-2, Luke 8:26-27. So, which was it, one or two?
![How many men came out to meet Jesus in Gerasene?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2009/03/person-man-tomb-03.jpg)
* **One man** : Mark 5:1-2, “And they came to the other side of the sea, into the country of the Gerasenes. 2 And when He had come out of the boat, immediately a man from the tombs with an unclean spirit met Him,”
* **One man** : Luke 8:26-27, “And they sailed to the country of the Gerasenes, which is opposite Galilee. 27 And when He had come out onto the land, He was met by a certain man from the city who was possessed with demons; and who had not put on any clothing for a long time, and was not living in a house, but in the tombs.”
A contradiction occurs only when one statement makes the other impossible. If Mark or Luke said that **only one** demoniac came to Jesus while Matthew says that two came out, that would be a contradiction. If there are two demoniacs, there is certainly at least one; therefore, there is no contradiction.
This is a reflection of the emphasis that each author presents. Matthew reveals the two who were exceedingly violent. But Mark and Luke don’t. They present the single person as being possessed, but not violent. We do not know why they chose to mention only one. But again, Mark and Luke do not say there was only one man – which would have presented a contradiction.
