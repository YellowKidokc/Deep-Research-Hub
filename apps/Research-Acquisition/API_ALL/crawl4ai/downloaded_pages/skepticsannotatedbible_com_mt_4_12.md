The devil came said to him, 
> If you are the Son of God, jump off, because it is written, "God will make his angels protect you from falling, lest you smash your foot against a stone." ([Ps 91:11-12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/91.html#11))
7 Jesus said unto him, It is written again, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.
Jesus said, "Don't tempt the Lord your God." ([Dt 6:16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/6.html#16)) [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/4.html#2n)]

Then the devil took Jesus to a very high mountain, and showed him all the kingdoms of the world. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/4.html#3n)]
9 And saith unto him, All these things will I give thee, if thou wilt fall down and worship me.
He then said to Jesus, "All these things will I give thee, if thou wilt fall down and worship me." [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/4.html#4n)]
10 Then saith Jesus unto him, Get thee hence, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.
Jesus said to the devil, 
> Go away Satan, for it is written, "Worship the Lord your God, and only serve him." ([Dt 6:13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/6.html#13)) [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/4.html#5n)]

Then the devil left him, and the angels came to help him.

### Notes
  1. _Then was Jesus led up of the spirit into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/4.html#1)) 
Jesus is led by the Spirit of God to be tempted by the devil. Which, at least according to [James 1:13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jas/1.html#13) ("God cannot be tempted with evil.") means that Jesus is not God.
[Can God be tempted?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/tempt_God.html)
[Is Jesus God?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jesus_god.html)
[When Jesus was tempted by the devil, what was the sequence of temptations?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sequence.html)
  2. _Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God._ ([v7.](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/4.html#7)) 
[Can God be tempted?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/tempt_God.html)
[Is it OK to test (or tempt) God?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/test.html)
  3. _The devil taketh him up into an exceeding high mountain, and sheweth him all the kingdoms of the world._ ([v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/4.html#8)) 
The devil kidnaps Jesus and takes him up to the top of the temple, and then to the top of "an exceeding high mountain, high enough to see "all the kingdoms of the world." I guess the earth was flat in those days.
  4. _All these things will I give thee._ ([v.9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/4.html#9)) 
[Who is the Lord of the earth?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/lord.html)
[Who owns the earth?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/owns.html)
  5. _Him only shalt thou serve._ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/4.html#10)) 
The devil correctly quotes scripture ([Psalm 91:11-12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/91.html#11)), while Jesus misquotes Deuteronomy by adding "only" to [Deuteronomy 6:13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/6.html#13).
[Should we serve God alone?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/serve.html)
  6. _When Jesus had heard that John was cast into prison, he departed into Galilee ... And Jesus, walking by the sea of Galilee, saw two brethren, Simon called Peter, and Andrew his brother ... And he saith unto them, Follow me. ([vv.12-19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/4.html#12)) _
[Which came first: the calling of Peter and Andrew or the imprisonment of John the Baptist?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/imprisonment.html)
[Did Jesus heal Peter's mother-in-law before or after calling Peter?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/peters-mil.html)
[How did Peter asnd Andrew become Jesus's disciples?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/calling.html)
