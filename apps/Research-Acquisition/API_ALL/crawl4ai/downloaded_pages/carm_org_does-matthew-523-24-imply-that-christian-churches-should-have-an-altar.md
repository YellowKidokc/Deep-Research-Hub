# Does Matthew 5:23-24 imply that Christian churches should have an altar?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
January 08, 2020 
6 min read 
No, neither Jesus nor the New Testament authors implied that Christian worship requires an altar. Jesus’ life and ministry obviously occurred before His death and resurrection. He was a Jew living among Jews who worshiped God at the temple through [animal sacrifices](https://carm.org/about-god/why-did-god-require-animal-sacrifices-in-the-old-testament/), and Jesus sometimes illustrated His points to His immediate hearers by drawing on these experiences. Such illustrations do not imply that Christians today need animal sacrifices, temples, or altars. Indeed, to make [Matthew 5:23-24](https://ref.ly/Matt%205.23-24;nasb95?t=biblia) about the importance of altars would actually be to _reverse_ Jesus’ point!
During the famous “Sermon on the Mount,” Jesus said:
“Therefore if you are presenting your offering at the altar, and there remember that your brother has something against you, leave your offering there before the altar and go; first be reconciled to your brother, and then come and present your offering,” ([Matthew 5:23-24](https://ref.ly/Matt%205.23-24;nasb95?t=biblia)).
