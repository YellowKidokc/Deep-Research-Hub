## [Jonah](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jon/outline.html "Jonah Outline")
### CHAPTER 1
Jonah runs from God (and is swallowed by a fish) 
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 1:1-17 
1 Now the word of the LORD came unto Jonah the son of Amittai, saying, 
2 Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and cry against it; for their wickedness is come up before me. 
The word of God came to Jonah and said, 
> Go to Nineveh and cry against it, 
> because I've heard about its wickedness. 
3 But **Jonah rose up to flee** unto Tarshish **from the presence of the LORD** , and went down to Joppa; and he found a ship going to Tarshish: so he paid the fare thereof, and went down into it, to go with them unto Tarshish from the presence of the LORD. 
So Jonah fled from the presence of God. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jon/1.html#1n)] 
He went to Joppa and boarded a ship bound for Tarshish. 
4 But the LORD sent out a great wind into the sea, and there was a mighty tempest in the sea, so that the ship was like to be broken. 
But God sent a storm on the sea, 
which threatened to sink the ship. 
5 Then the mariners were afraid, and cried every man unto his god, and cast forth the wares that were in the ship into the sea, to lighten it of them. But Jonah was gone down into the sides of the ship; and he lay, and was fast asleep. 
The crew threw things overboard to lighten the ship, 
while Jonah slept below deck. 
6 So the shipmaster came to him, and said unto him, What meanest thou, O sleeper? arise, call upon thy God, if so be that God will think upon us, that we perish not. 
The captain came to Jonah and said, 
> Get up and ask your God to save us. 
7 And they said every one to his fellow, Come, and **let us cast lots, that we may know for whose cause this evil is upon us. So they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah.**
Meanwhile the crew cast lots to find the source of the evil. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jon/1.html#2n)] 
And the lot fell upon Jonah. 
8 Then said they unto him, Tell us, we pray thee, for whose cause this evil is upon us; What is thine occupation? and whence comest thou? what is thy country? and of what people art thou? 
So they asked Jonah, 
> Why is the storm coming from you? 
9 And he said unto them, I am an Hebrew; and I fear the LORD, the God of heaven, which hath made the sea and the dry land. 
Jonah said, 
> I'm a Hebrew. I think God is after me. 
10 Then were the men exceedingly afraid, and said unto him. Why hast thou done this? For the men knew that he fled from the presence of the LORD, because he had told them.
11 Then said they unto him, What shall we do unto thee, that the sea may be calm unto us? for the sea wrought, and was tempestuous. 
The sailors said to Jonah, 
> Why have you done this? 
(They knew he was fleeing from God, because he told them.) 
> What should we do to you, so the sea will be calm? 
12 And he said unto them, Take me up, and **cast me forth into the sea** ; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you.
13 Nevertheless the men rowed hard to bring it to the land; but they could not: for the sea wrought, and was tempestuous against them. 
Jonah said, 
> Throw me overboard. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jon/1.html#3n)] 
14 Wherefore they cried unto the LORD, and said, We beseech thee, O LORD, we beseech thee, let us not perish for this man's life, and lay not upon us innocent blood: for thou, O LORD, hast done as it pleased thee. 
The sailors cried to God, saying 
> We know you want to kill this man. 
> Please don't kill us too. 
15 So they look up Jonah, and cast him forth into the sea: and the sea ceased from her raging.
16 Then the men feared the LORD exceedingly, and offered a sacrifice unto the LORD, and made vows. 
So they threw Jonah overboard. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jon/1.html#4n)] 
And the sea became calm. 
17 Now **the LORD had prepared a****great fish** **to swallow up Jonah. And Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights.**
A great fish swallowed Jonah, 
and he was in the fish's belly for three days and three nights.

### Notes
  1. _Jonah rose up to flee ... from the presence of the LORD._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jon/1.html#3)) 
(There are some places where God can't find you.) 
[Does God know and see everything? ](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/knows.html)
  2. [ ](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/knows.html)_Let us cast lots, that we may know for whose cause this evil is upon us._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jon/1.html#7)) 
The sailors cast lots to see who was causing the storm. 
And it worked, too (casting lots always does in the Bible), since the lot fell on Jonah. 
  3. _Cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jon/1.html#12)) 
Jonah believes that by throwing himself into the sea, the storm will die down. And it worked! 
  4. _So they look up Jonah, and cast him forth into the sea: and the sea ceased from her raging._ ([v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jon/1.html#15)) 
[Who cast Jonah into the sea?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jonah.html)
  5. _The LORD had prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonah._ ([v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jon/1.html#17)) 
God makes "a great fish" to swallow Jonah. And Jonah stayed in the fish's belly for three days and three nights. 
[Was Jonah swallowed by a fish or a whale?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/whale.html)
