# Was Jesus crucified on a cross or a tree?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 03, 2010 
6 min read 
Jesus was crucified on a cross, not a literal tree that had leaves on it. The Greek words for cross and tree are different. Let’s take a look. The Greek word for cross is σταυρός “stauros”:
“literally cross, an instrument of capital punishment, an upright pointed stake, often with a crossbeam above it, or intersected by a crossbeam (MT 27.32); (2) by metonymy, as the means of atonement punishment of the cross, crucifixion (PH 2.8); as a religious technical term representing the significance of the atoning death of Jesus in the Christian religion cross (1C 1.18); metaphorically, the dedication of life and the self-denial that a believer must be prepared to take on himself in following Christ (LU 14.27)” _(Friberg, T., Friberg, B., & Miller, N. F. (2000). Vol. 4: Analytical lexicon of the Greek New Testament. Baker’s Greek New Testament Library (355). Grand Rapids, Mich.: Baker Books.)_
The word (σταυρός, stauros) “cross” occurs 27 times in the New Testament in 27 verses: [Matthew 10:38](https://ref.ly/Matt%2010.38;nasb95?t=biblia); [16:24](https://ref.ly/Matthew%2016.24;nasb95?t=biblia); [27:32](https://ref.ly/Matthew%2027.32;nasb95?t=biblia); [27:40](https://ref.ly/Matthew%2027.40;nasb95?t=biblia); [27:42](https://ref.ly/Matthew%2027.42;nasb95?t=biblia); [Mark 8:34](https://ref.ly/Mark%208.34;nasb95?t=biblia); [15:21](https://ref.ly/Mark%2015.21;nasb95?t=biblia); [15:30](https://ref.ly/Mark%2015.30;nasb95?t=biblia); [Mark 15:32](https://ref.ly/Mark%2015.32;nasb95?t=biblia); [Luke 9:23](https://ref.ly/Luke%209.23;nasb95?t=biblia); [14:27](https://ref.ly/Luke%2014.27;nasb95?t=biblia); [23:26](https://ref.ly/Luke%2023.26;nasb95?t=biblia); [John 19:17](https://ref.ly/John%2019.17;nasb95?t=biblia); [19:19](https://ref.ly/John%2019.19;nasb95?t=biblia); [19:25](https://ref.ly/John%2019.25;nasb95?t=biblia); [19:31](https://ref.ly/John%2019.31;nasb95?t=biblia); [1 Cor. 1:17](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%201.17;nasb95?t=biblia); [1:18](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor%201.18;nasb95?t=biblia); [Gal. 5:11](https://ref.ly/Gal.%205.11;nasb95?t=biblia); [6:12](https://ref.ly/Gal%206.12;nasb95?t=biblia); [6:14](https://ref.ly/Gal%206.14;nasb95?t=biblia); [Eph. 2:16](https://ref.ly/Eph.%202.16;nasb95?t=biblia); [Phil. 2:8](https://ref.ly/Phil.%202.8;nasb95?t=biblia); [3:18](https://ref.ly/Phil%203.18;nasb95?t=biblia); [Col. 1:20](https://ref.ly/Col.%201.20;nasb95?t=biblia); [2:14](https://ref.ly/Col%202.14;nasb95?t=biblia); [Heb. 12:2](https://ref.ly/Heb.%2012.2;nasb95?t=biblia). Never once does the word mean a literal tree growing in the ground. It means cross.
