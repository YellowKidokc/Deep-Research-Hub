The mission of the apostles
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 9:1-6, Cf. [Mt 10:5-15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/10.html#S2), [Mk 6:6-13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/6.html#S2)
1 Then **he** called his twelve disciples together, and **gave them power and authority over all devils** , and to cure diseases.
Jesus gave his twelve disciples power over all devils and diseases.
2 And he sent them to preach the kingdom of God, and to heal the sick. 
He sent them out to preach and heal the sick.
3 And he said unto them, Take nothing for your journey, neither staves, nor scrip, neither bread, neither money; neither have two coats apiece.
He said to them,
> Take nothing with you, not a staff, bag, bread, or money. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#1n)]
> And take only one coat.
4 And whatsoever house ye enter into, there abide, and thence depart.
Stay with whoever will let you stay with them.
5 And whosoever will not receive you, when ye go out of that city, shake off the very dust from your feet for a testimony against them.
If no one will let you stay, leave and go somewhere else.
6 And they departed, and went through the towns, preaching the gospel, and healing every where.
And the disciples went preaching and healing everywhere.
Herod: Is Jesus the risen John the Baptist?
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 9:7-9, Cf. [Mt 14:1-12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/14.html#S1), [Mk 6:14-29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/6.html#S5)

8 And of some, that Elijah had appeared; and of others, that one of the old prophets was risen again.

Jesus and the apostles went to Bethsaida, where Jesus healed those who needed healing.

In the late afternoon, the twelve apostles said to Jesus,
> Send the people away so they can get something to eat.

Jesus said, "Give them something to eat."
They said, "We only have five loaves and two fishes."
14 For they were about five thousand men. And he said to his disciples, Make them sit down by fifties in a company.
There were about five thousand men there.
Jesus said, "Make them sit down in groups of fifties."
15 And they did so, and made them all sit down.
So everyone sat down.
16 Then he took the five loaves and the two fishes, and looking up to heaven, he blessed them, and brake, and gave to the disciples to set before the multitude.
Jesus gave the loaves and fishes to his disciples to distribute to the people.
17 And they did eat, and were all filled: and there was taken up of fragments that remained to them twelve baskets.
And everyone ate until they were filled, leaving twelve baskets leftover.

They answered,

Jesus said, "Who do you say I am?"
Peter said, "The Christ of God."

### Notes
  1. _Take nothing for your journey, neither staves._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#3)) 
[Did Jesus tell his apostles to go barefoot and without a staff?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/barefoot.html)
  2. _John have I beheaded: but who is this?_ ([v.9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#9)) 
The death of John the Baptist is not recorded in Luke. (See [Mt 14:6-12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/14.html#S2) and [Mk 6:14-29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/6.html#S5).)
There were various opinions about the identity of Jesus. Some thought he was Elijah or one of the prophets; others that he was the risen John the Baptist. With such a credulous populace, is it surprising that some would later claim, and probably even believe, that they had seen the risen Christ?
[Did Herod think Jesus was John the Baptist?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/herod.html)
  3. _As he was alone praying, his disciples were with him_ ([v.18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#18)) 
How was Jesus "alone" if "his disciples were with him?"
  4. _And he straitly charged them, and commanded them to tell no man that thing._ ([v.21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#21)) 
[Did Jesus have any secret teachings?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/secret.html)
  5. _There be some standing here, which shall not taste of death, till they see the kingdom of God._ ([v.27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#27)) 
[What the Bible says about the end of the world](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/end.html)
  6. _Eight days after these sayings, he took Peter and John and James, and went up into a mountain to pray._ ([v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#28)) 
[When was the transfiguration?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/transfig.html)
  7. _A spirit taketh him, and he suddenly crieth out; and it teareth him that he foameth again, and bruising him._ ([v.39](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#39)) 
Epilepsy is caused by devils.
  8. _O faithless and perverse generation!_ ([v.41](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#41)) 
Jesus speaks harshly to his disciples because they couldn't cure epilepsy by casting out devils.
  9. _But they understood not this saying, and it was hid from them, that they perceived it not: and they feared to ask him of that saying._ ([v.45](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#45)) 
[Did the disciples understand when Jesus told them about his coming death?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/understand.html)
  10. _We saw one casting out devils in thy name; and we forbad him, because he followeth not with us._ ([v.49](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#49)) 
[Who can cast out devils in the name of Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/devils.html)
[Is casting out devils a sign of a true Christian?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/cast_out.html)
  11. _He that is not against us is for us._ ([v.50](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#50)) 
[Who is for or against Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/withme.html)
  12. _They went, and entered into a village of the Samaritans ... And they did not receive him._ ([v.53](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#53)) 
[Did the Samaritans receive Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sam_received.html)
  13. _Wilt thou that we command fire to come down from heaven, and consume them, even as Elijah did?_ ([v.54](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#54)) 
James and John ask Jesus if they can call down fire from heaven to consume the Samaritans who didn't much care for Jesus' teaching. They'd like to kill people the way Elijah did. But shucks, Jesus won't let them.
[DWB](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html): [God burned 102 men to death for asking Elijah to come down from his hill](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/2kg.html#102)
  14. _The Son of man is not come to destroy men's lives, but to save them._ ([v.56](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#56)) 
Well, it's a nice thought, but see [the next chapter](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/10.html#13), where Jesus says whoever doesn't receive his disciples will be slaughtered like the people of Sodom and Gommorah.
[DWB](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html): [Sodom and Gomorrah](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/gen.html#sodom)
  15. _Foxes have holes, and birds of the air have nests; but the Son of man hath not where to lay his head._ ([v.58](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#58)) 
[Was Jesus homeless?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/homeless.html)
  16. _Jesus said unto him, Let the dead bury their dead._ ([v.60](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/9.html#60)) 
Jesus won't even let his followers bury their dead parents or say farewell to their families before abandoning them.
[How should parents be treated?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/parents.html)
