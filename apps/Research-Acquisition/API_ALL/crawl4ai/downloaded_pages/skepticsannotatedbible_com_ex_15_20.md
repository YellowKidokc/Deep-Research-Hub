### CHAPTER 15
The song of Moses
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif)![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 15:1-21
1 Then sang Moses and the children of Israel this song unto the LORD, and spake, saying, I will sing unto the LORD, for he hath triumphed gloriously: the horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea.
After God drowned the Egyptians, Moses and the Israelites sang a song. Here are some of the lyrics:
> God has thrown the Egyptian horses and riders into the sea.
2 The LORD is my strength and song, and he is become my salvation: he is my God, and I will prepare him an habitation; my father's God, and I will exalt him.
3 **The LORD is a man of war** : the LORD is his name.
> The Lord is a man of war. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/15.html#1n)]
> The Lord is his name.
4 Pharaoh's chariots and his host hath he cast into the sea: his chosen captains also are drowned in the Red sea.
5 The depths have covered them: they sank into the bottom as a stone.
> He threw the Pharaoh's chariots into the sea, and drowned his captains in the Red Sea.
6 Thy right hand, O LORD, is become glorious in power: **thy right hand, O LORD, hath dashed in pieces the enemy.**
> Your right hand, O Lord, has become glorious in power; it has dashed the enemy in pieces.
7 And in the greatness of thine excellency thou hast overthrown them that rose up against thee: thou sentest forth thy wrath, which consumed them as stubble.
> You send forth your wrath and burn them as stubble.
8 And **with the blast of thy nostrils the waters were gathered together** , the floods stood upright as an heap, and the depths were congealed in the heart of the sea.
9 The enemy said, I will pursue, I will overtake, I will divide the spoil; my lust shall be satisfied upon them; I will draw my sword, my hand shall destroy them.
> You make great floods with a blast from your nostrils.
10 Thou didst blow with thy wind, the sea covered them: they sank as lead in the mighty waters.
> You drown people by blowing with your wind.
11 Who is like unto thee, O LORD, among the gods? who is like thee, glorious in holiness, fearful in praises, doing wonders?
> Who is like you among the gods? [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/15.html#2n)]
12 **Thou stretchedst out thy right hand, the earth swallowed them.**
13 Thou in thy mercy hast led forth the people which thou hast redeemed: thou hast guided them in thy strength unto thy holy habitation.
14 The people shall hear, and be afraid: sorrow shall take hold on the inhabitants of Palestina.
15 Then the dukes of Edom shall be amazed; the mighty men of Moab, trembling shall take hold upon them; all the inhabitants of Canaan shall melt away.
16 Fear and dread shall fall upon them; by the greatness of thine arm they shall be as still as a stone; till thy people pass over, O LORD, till the people pass over, which thou hast purchased.
17 Thou shalt bring them in, and plant them in the mountain of thine inheritance, in the place, O LORD, which thou hast made for thee to dwell in, in the Sanctuary, O LORD, which thy hands have established.
18 The LORD shall reign for ever and ever.
> You stretch out your right hand and make the earth swallow people.
19 For the horse of Pharaoh went in with his chariots and with his horsemen into the sea, and the LORD brought again the waters of the sea upon them; but the children of Israel went on dry land in the midst of the sea.
> God drowned Pharaoh's horse and horsemen in the sea, but the Israelites passed through on dry land.
20 And Miriam the prophetess, the sister of Aaron, took a timbrel in her hand; and all the women went out after her with timbrels and with dances.
Aaron's sister, Miriam the prophetess, took a timbrel in her hand and danced with all the Israelite women. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/15.html#3n)]
21 And Miriam answered them, Sing ye to the LORD, for he hath triumphed gloriously; the horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea.
They sang,
> God has triumphed gloriously by throwing horse and his rider into the sea.
The waters of Marah
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) 15:22-27
22 So Moses brought Israel from the Red sea, and they went out into the wilderness of Shur; and they went three days in the wilderness, and found no water.
23 And when they came to Marah, they could not drink of **the waters of Marah** , for they were bitter: therefore the name of it was called Marah.
The Israelites resume their journey, but find no water.
24 And the people murmured against Moses, saying, What shall we drink?
The people murmured against Moses, saying, 
> What shall we drink?
25 And he cried unto the LORD; and the LORD shewed him a tree, which when he had cast into the waters, the waters were made sweet: there he made for them a statute and an ordinance, and there he proved them,
Moses cried to God and God showed him a tree that when thrown in water made the water sweet.
26 And said, If thou wilt diligently hearken to the voice of the LORD thy God, and wilt do that which is right in his sight, and wilt give ear to his commandments, and keep all his statutes, I will put none of these diseases upon thee, which I have brought upon the Egyptians: for I am the LORD that healeth thee.
27 And they came to Elim, where were twelve wells of water, and threescore and ten palm trees: and they encamped there by the waters.
God said,
> If you obey all of my commandments, I won't make you sick (like I did the Egyptians). [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/15.html#4n)]

### Notes
  1. _The Lord is a man of war._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/15.html#3)) 
Indeed, judging from his acts in the Old Testament, he is a vicious warlike monster.
But how can the same God be both a "man of war" and a "God of peace"?
[Is God warlike or peaceful?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/peace.html)
  2. _Who is like you among the gods?_ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/15.html#11)) 
(Who can kill so many people?)
[How many gods are there?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gods.html)
  3. _With timbrels and with dances_ ([v.20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/15.html#20)) 
[Is dancing a sin?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/dancing.html)
  4. _I will put none of these diseases upon thee._ ([v.26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/15.html#26)) 
[What the Bible says about medical science](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/medical-science.html)
