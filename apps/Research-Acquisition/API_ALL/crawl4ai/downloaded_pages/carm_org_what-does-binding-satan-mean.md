# What does “binding Satan” mean?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 12, 2017 
4 min read 
The concept of “binding Satan,” means to restrict the work of the devil. It means to cause him to have less ability, to decrease the power of, to prevent, his success. The phrase is used mostly in charismatic circles dealing with spiritual warfare where Christians, in prayer, ask the Lord to bind Satan in order to accomplish a spiritual good.
Unfortunately, some Christians mistakenly pray _to_ Satan when they are undertaking spiritual battle. Sometimes they will address the evil one in their prayers and say something like “I bind you Satan in the name of Jesus.” Where the sentiment of relying on the authority of the name of Christ ([Acts 4:7](https://ref.ly/Acts%204.7;nasb95?t=biblia)) is certainly biblical, we should be careful not to address our prayers to the devil. We should address our prayers to God and ask him, in the name of Jesus, to bind the devil. Our prayers should always be addressed to God and God alone.
Would there ever be a time when it is appropriate to address the devil directly? Yes, if he were to manifest in person. Then it is not a prayer _to_ him but a direct address and even if that were to occur, the proper response is found in Scripture. Consider where Michael the Archangel had an encounter with the devil.
