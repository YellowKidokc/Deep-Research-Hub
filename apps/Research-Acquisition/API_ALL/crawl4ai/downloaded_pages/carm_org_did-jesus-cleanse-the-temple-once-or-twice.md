# Did Jesus cleanse the Temple once or twice?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
3 min read 
Did Jesus cleanse the Temple once or twice? Matthew, Mark, and Luke report Jesus driving merchants and moneychangers out of the temple, but some critics claim that the three gospels are not consistent and don’t match. Did Jesus cleanse the temple more than once? Do the gospels disagree? How do we reconcile these passages?
![Did Jesus cleanse the Temple once or twice?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/Jesus-23.jpg)
There are two possible explanations. The first is that Matthew and Mark purposely arranged their material in different order. Matthew is more topical in his presentation of the material than Mark. Therefore, it may be that Matthew condensed the information into a thematic arrangement. This was common in ancient writing since parchment and vellum were expensive and time consuming to produce. Also, when a mistake was made in writing, there was no “backspace” to correct it. So, the brevity that represented the whole was acceptable.
The other explanation is that Jesus drove moneychangers out of the temple twice. This is possible, but it is disputed. For more information on this, go to the Chronology of the Temple Cleansing and see a possible arrangement of the gospels.
