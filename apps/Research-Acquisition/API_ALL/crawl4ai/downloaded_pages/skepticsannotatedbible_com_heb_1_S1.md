God gave his Son a better name than the angels 
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/pR1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 1:1-7 
1 God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, 
2 Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds; 
In these last days, [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/heb/1.html#1n)] 
God's son 
3 Who being the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person, and upholding all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high: 
sat on the right hand of the Majesty on high. 
4 Being made so much better than the angels, as he hath by inheritance obtained a more excellent name than they.
5 For unto which of the angels said he at any time, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee? And again, I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to me a Son? 
6 And again, when he bringeth in the firstbegotten into the world, he saith, And let all the angels of God worship him. 
7 And of the angels he saith, Who maketh his angels spirits, and his ministers a flame of fire. 
He was much better than the angels, 
so he got a better name than theirs.

### Notes
  1. _God ... hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son._ ([vv.1-2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/heb/1.html#1)) 
The author of Hebrews believed that he was living in the "last days." 
[What the Bible says about the end of the world](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/end.html)
  2. _Unto the Son he saith, Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever._ ([v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/heb/1.html#8)) 
The JWs deny the divinity of Christ so they just changed the words in the [New World Translation](http://www.watchtower.org/bible/heb/chapter_001.htm) to read: "But with reference to the Son: 'God is your throne forever and ever.'" 
[Is Jesus God?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/interp/Jesus_God.html)
  3. _They shall perish._ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/heb/1.html#11)) 
[Will the earth last forever?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/earth.html)
