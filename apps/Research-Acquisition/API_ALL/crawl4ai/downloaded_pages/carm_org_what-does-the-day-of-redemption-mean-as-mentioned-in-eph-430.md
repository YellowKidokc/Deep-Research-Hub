# What does the Day of Redemption mean as mentioned in Eph. 4:30?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 24, 2009 
2 min read 
The “Day of Redemption” is a reference to the future time when the Christians receive their [resurrected bodies](https://carm.org/dictionary/resurrection/). The term comes from Paul’s words to the Ephesians:
[Eph. 4:30](https://ref.ly/Eph.%204.30;nasb95?t=biblia), “Do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.”
Our salvation includes the future glorification of our fleshly abodes.
