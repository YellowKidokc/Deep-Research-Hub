# What did Jesus say about hell and the final judgment?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 11, 2018 
10 min read 
What did Jesus say about hell and the final judgment? To find out, I went to my Bible program which allows me to limit my search to the words of Christ. It was easy to enter certain words and find every verse that Jesus spoke that contained those words. The words I searched for were agony, fire, hades, hell, judge, perish, and torment. Also, I used the New American Standard Bible because of its literal translation from Greek. Sometimes, I’ll take the Greek words and do searches with them, but since the NASB is such a good literal translation, I decided to use the English to see what I might discover. ￼ ![hell and final judgment](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/hell-05.jpg)
This is what I found.
Jesus considers hell to be a place of judgment ([John 5:24](https://ref.ly/John%205.24;nasb95?t=biblia), [29](https://ref.ly/John%205.29;nasb95?t=biblia)), of fire ([Matt. 5:22](https://ref.ly/Matt.%205.22;nasb95?t=biblia); [13:40](https://ref.ly/Matt%2013.40;nasb95?t=biblia), [42](https://ref.ly/Matt%2013.42;nasb95?t=biblia); [18:9](https://ref.ly/Matt%2018.9;nasb95?t=biblia); [John 15:6](https://ref.ly/John%2015.6;nasb95?t=biblia)) that was originally prepared for the devil and his angels ([Matthew 25:41](https://ref.ly/Matt%2025.41;nasb95?t=biblia)). That fire is eternal ([Matt. 18:8](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2018.8;nasb95?t=biblia); [25:41](https://ref.ly/Matt%2025.41;nasb95?t=biblia)). Jesus references the wicked descending into Hades ([Matt. 11:23](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2011.23;nasb95?t=biblia); [Luke 10:15](https://ref.ly/Luke%2010.15;nasb95?t=biblia)) who are in flame ([Luke 16:24](https://ref.ly/Luke%2016.24;nasb95?t=biblia)). This punishment occurs at the end of the age ([Matt. 13:40](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2013.40;nasb95?t=biblia)). They are thrown into this place bodily ([Matt. 5:29-30](https://ref.ly/Matt.%205.29-30;nasb95?t=biblia); [Mark 9:45](https://ref.ly/Mark%209.45;nasb95?t=biblia), [47](https://ref.ly/Mark%209.47;nasb95?t=biblia)). He says that people are destroyed there ([Matt. 10:28](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2010.28;nasb95?t=biblia)) and perish there ([John 3:16](https://ref.ly/John%203.16;nasb95?t=biblia)). They will experience torment ([Luke 16:23-24](https://ref.ly/Luke%2016.23-24;nasb95?t=biblia), [28](https://ref.ly/Luke%2016.28;nasb95?t=biblia)), agony ([Luke 16:24-25](https://ref.ly/Luke%2016.24-25;nasb95?t=biblia)), with weeping and gnashing of teeth ([Matt. 13:42](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2013.42;nasb95?t=biblia), [50](https://ref.ly/Matt%2013.50;nasb95?t=biblia)). It is eternal punishment ([Matt. 25:46](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2025.46;nasb95?t=biblia)) in a fire that is unquenchable ([Mark 9:43](https://ref.ly/Mark%209.43;nasb95?t=biblia), [47-48](https://ref.ly/Mark%209.47-48;nasb95?t=biblia)). Finally, there are also sons of hell ([Matt. 23:15](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2023.15;nasb95?t=biblia)). Hell is a sentence ([Matt. 23:33](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2023.33;nasb95?t=biblia)) and God ought to be feared since he sends people there ([Luke 12:5](https://ref.ly/Luke%2012.5;nasb95?t=biblia)). It would appear in the service that Jesus’ position on how and eternal conscious torment is not consistent with annihilationism.
