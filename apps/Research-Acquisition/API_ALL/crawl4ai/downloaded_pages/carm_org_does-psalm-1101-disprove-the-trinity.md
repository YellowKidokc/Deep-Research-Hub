# Does Psalm 110:1 disprove the Trinity?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
May 19, 2017 
6 min read 
[Jehovah’s Witnesses](https://carm.org/2008/12/06/jehovahs-witnesses-in-a-nutshell/) and other [Unitarian](https://carm.org/2008/12/15/what-is-unitarianism/) groups who deny the biblical doctrine of [the Trinity](https://carm.org/doctrine-and-theology/trinity-chart/) often turn to [Psalm 110:1](https://ref.ly/Ps%20110.1;nasb95?t=biblia) as an argument against the Trinity. Such an argument is based on several misconceptions about what Christians believe and what the doctrine of the Trinity is. The text reads:
![psalm 110](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/psalms-300x300.jpg)
“The LORD says to my Lord: ‘Sit at My right hand Until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet,'” ([Psalm 110:1](https://ref.ly/Ps%20110.1;nasb95?t=biblia)).
