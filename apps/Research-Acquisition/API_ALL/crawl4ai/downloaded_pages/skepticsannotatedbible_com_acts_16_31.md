1 Then came he to Derbe and Lystra: and, behold, a certain disciple was there, named Timothy, the son of a certain woman, which was a Jewess, and believed; but his father was a Greek: 
2 Which was well reported of by the brethren that were at Lystra and Iconium.
When Paul and Silas came to Derbe and Lystra, there was a disciple named Timothy, whose mother was a Jewish Christian and whose father was a Greek.
3 Him would Paul have to go forth with him; and took and circumcised him because of the Jews which were in those quarters: for they knew all that his father was a Greek.
4 And as they went through the cities, they delivered them the decrees for to keep, that were ordained of the apostles and elders which were at Jerusalem.

### Notes
  1. _Paul ... took and circumcised him._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/16.html#3)) 
Paul circumcised Timothy, thereby making it impossible for him to be saved, according to his own words in [Galatians 5:2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gal/5.html#2).
[Is circumcision required?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/circum.html)
  2. _They ... were forbidden of the Holy Ghost to preach the word in Asia._ ([v.6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/16.html#6)) 
Paul, Silas, and the newly circumcised Timothy were forbidden by the Holy Ghost to preach in Asia.
(God just doesn't like Asians, I guess.)
[Should the gospel be preached to everyone?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/samaritans.html)
  3. _They assayed to go into Bithynia: but the Spirit suffered them not._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/16.html#7)) 
Then they tried to go to Bithynia, but the Spirit wouldn't let them do that either. Shucks!
  4. _A vision appeared to Paul in the night; There stood a man of Macedonia ... saying, Come over into Macedonia, and help us._ ([v.9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/16.html#9)) 
A man appears to Paul in a dream telling him to go to Macedonia. So Paul, who was a big believer in dreams, did as the dream-man requested.
  5. _We endeavoured to go into Macedonia_ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/16.html#10)) 
All of a sudden, the tense changes from third person to first person plural. It's unclear who the "we" refers to.
For other "we passages" see [20:5-15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/20.html#5), [21:1-18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/21.html#1), and [27:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/27.html#1)-[28:16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/28.html#16).
  6. _A certain woman named Lydia, a seller of purple_ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/16.html#14)) 
[Strange Flesh](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/sf/intro.html): [Lydia, a seller of purple](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/sf/lesbians.html#lydia)
  7. _Suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken: and immediately all the doors were opened, and every one's bands were loosed._ ([v.26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/16.html#26)) 
Another Holy Prison Break! (See [12:6-19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/12.html#S2) for the first one.)
Paul and Silas were rescued by an earthquake that opened all the doors of the prison.
  8. _What must I do to be saved?_ ([v.30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/16.html#30)) 
If you "believe on the Lord Jesus Christ," then you and your whole family will be saved; otherwise, God will send you all to hell. But in Matthew ([19:17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/19.html#17)), when Jesus was asked the same question, he gave an entirely different answer.
[Is Salvation by faith alone?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/faithalone.html)
  9. _Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house._ ([v.31](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/16.html#31)) 
If a husband believes, his whole family is automatically saved.
[If a husband believes, is his wife saved also?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/husband.html)
  10. _He ... was baptized, he and all his, straightway._ ([v.33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/16.html#33)) 
The jailer was so impressed with the holy jailbreak that he converted on the spot. He and his entire family were baptized that night. It didn't matter to Paul (or God) what the jailer's wife and kids thought about it. They were "his" so he could do whatever the hell he wanted with (or to) them. (This stupid story supports the idea of infant baptism, which has divided Christians for centuries.)
