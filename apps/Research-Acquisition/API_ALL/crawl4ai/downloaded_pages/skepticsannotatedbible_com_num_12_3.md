### Notes
  1. _Miriam and Aaron spake against Moses because of the Ethiopian woman whom he had married._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/12.html#1)) 
This is the first (and only) time Moses's Ethiopian wife is mentioned. The only wife we've heard of so far has been Zipporah, and she was from Midian, not Ethiopia. So did Moses have two wives - Zipporah and an Ethiopian woman? And what were Aaron and Miriam complaining about? That he married a non-Hebrew woman? That he married a black woman? Or that he already had a wife (which he apparently abandoned) and was marrying another?
[Will God destroy those that intermarry?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/intermarriage.html)
  2. _Moses was very meek, above all the men which were upon the face of the earth._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/12.html#3)) 
That's a strange way to describe one of the cruelest men to have ever lived. (If he ever _did_ live, which he probably didn't.)
Moses, as he is described in the Bible, is anything but meek (See [Num 31:14-18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/31.html#14) for an example of his "meekness").
The NRSV translates this as: "Now the man Moses was very humble, more so than anyone else on the face of the earth." But if Moses wrote the entire Pentateuch, then he must have written this verse, too. Which seems like a strange thing for the humblest man on earth to say!
[Was Moses meek?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/meek.html)
[Who wrote the Pentateuch?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/pentateuch.html)
  3. _If there be a prophet among you, I the LORD ... will speak unto him in a dream._ ([v.6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/12.html#6)) 
Now _there's_ a reliable way to communicate with someone!
  4. _With him will I speak mouth to mouth, even apparently, and not in dark speeches; and the similitude of the LORD shall he behold._ ([v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/12.html#8)) 
[Has anyone seen God?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/seen.html)
  5. _Miriam became leprous, white as snow._ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/12.html#10)) 
In the Bible, leprosy is caused by the wrath of God or the malice of Satan.
Notice that only Miriam is punished, though both she and Aaron complained.
  6. _Aaron said ... Let her not be as one dead._ ([vv.11-12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/12.html#11)) 
And Moses cried unto the LORD, saying, Heal her now, O God, I beseech thee. ([v.13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/12.html#13))
Although both Aaron and Moses beg God to remove the leprosy with which he cursed Miriam, there is nothing in the text that says that he did.
  7. _The LORD said unto Moses, If her father had but spit in her face, should she not be ashamed seven days?_ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/12.html#14)) 
God says the darndest things! (Cf. [Dt 25:9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/25.html#9))
