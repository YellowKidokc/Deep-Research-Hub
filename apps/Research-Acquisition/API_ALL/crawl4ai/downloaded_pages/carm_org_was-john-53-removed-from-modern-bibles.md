# Was John 5:3 removed from modern Bibles?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
October 31, 2018 
11 min read 
Many modern translations of the Bible do not have John 5:4 in the main body of their text, relegating it instead to a footnote. King James Only advocates accuse these modern translators of deleting or suppressing the verse and tampering with the word of God. Modern translators, however, claim that the verse isn’t in the earliest and most reliable manuscripts and that it was a later scribal addition. Thus, they say that they are not _removing_ anything from the Bible. Rather, others _added_ these words in. They were not part of what John originally wrote. To settle the matter, we must look to the manuscript evidence.
The verses under discussion here traditionally read in the KJV:
“In these lay a great multitude of impotent folk, of blind, halt, withered, waiting for the moving of the water. For an angel went down at a certain season into the pool, and troubled the water: whosoever then first after the troubling of the water stepped in was made whole of whatsoever disease he had,” (John 5:3-4, KJV).
