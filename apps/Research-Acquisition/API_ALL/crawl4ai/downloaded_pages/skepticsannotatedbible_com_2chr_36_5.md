### CHAPTER 36
King Jehoahaz 
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/boring.63b5b35a30388.jpg) 36:1-4, Cf. [2 Kg 23:30-35](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/23.html#30)
1 Then the people of the land took Jehoahaz the son of Josiah, and made him king in his father's stead in Jerusalem. 
2 Jehoahaz was twenty and three years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. 
3 And the king of Egypt put him down at Jerusalem, and condemned the land in an hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold. 
After Josiah died, his son, Jehoahaz, became king of Judah. 
4 And the king of Egypt made Eliakim his brother king over Judah and Jerusalem, and turned his name to Jehoiakim. And Necho took Jehoahaz his brother, and carried him to Egypt. 
Jehoahaz was imprisoned by Necho, the king of Egypt, who made Jehoahaz's brother, Jehoiakim, king. 
King Jehoiakim 
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 36:5-8, Cf. [2 Kg 23:36-24:7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/23.html#30)
5 Jehoiakim was twenty and five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and he did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD his God. 
Jehoiakim did evil things in the sight of God. 
6 Against him came up Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and bound him in fetters, to carry him to Babylon.
7 Nebuchadnezzar also carried of the vessels of the house of the LORD to Babylon, and put them in his temple at Babylon. 
King Nebuchadnezzar carried Jehoakim to Babylon, where he (presumably) died. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2chr/36.html#1n)] 
8 Now the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and his abominations which he did, and that which was found in him, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah: and Jehoiachin his son reigned in his stead. 
You can read about the rest of Jehoiakim's abominations in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah. 
Jehoiakim's son, Jehoiachin, became king after his father was taken to Babylon. 
King Jehoiachin 
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 36:9, Cf. [2 Kg 24:8-17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/24.html#8)
9 Jehoiachin was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem: and he did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD.
Jehoiachin became king when he was eight years old and reigned for three months and ten days. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2chr/36.html#2n)] 
He did evil things in God's sight. 
King Zedekiah 
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) 36:10-16, Cf. [2 Kg 24:18-20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/24.html#18), [Jer 52:1-3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jer/52.html#1)
10 And when the year was expired, king Nebuchadnezzar sent, and brought him to Babylon, with the goodly vessels of the house of the LORD, and made Zedekiah his brother king over Judah and Jerusalem. 
11 Zedekiah was one and twenty years old when he began to reign, and reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. 
At the end of the year, Nebuchadnezzar brought Jehoiachin to Babylon and made Zedekiah, Jehoiachin's younger brother, king. 
12 And he did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD his God, and humbled not himself before Jeremiah the prophet speaking from the mouth of the LORD. 
Zedekiah did evil things in God's sight, and didn't humble himself in front of Jeremiah, who spoke from the mouth of God. 
13 And he also rebelled against king Nebuchadnezzar, who had made him swear by God: but he stiffened his neck, and hardened his heart from turning unto the LORD God of Israel. 
Zedekiah rebelled against Nebuchadnezzar and hardened his heart against God. 
14 Moreover all the chief of the priests, and the people, transgressed very much after all the abominations of the heathen; and polluted the house of the LORD which he had hallowed in Jerusalem. 
The priests and people committed abominations and polluted God's house in Jerusalem. 
15 And the LORD God of their fathers sent to them by his messengers, rising up betimes, and sending; because he had compassion on his people, and on his dwelling place: 
16 But they mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words, and misused his prophets, until **the wrath of the LORD arose against his people** , till there was no remedy.
God sent prophets, but the peopled mocked them. 
So God's wrath rose up against them until there was no remedy. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2chr/36.html#3n)] 
The Fall of Jerusalem

17 **Therefore he brought upon them the king of the Chaldees, who slew their young men with the sword** in the house of their sanctuary, **and had no compassion upon young man or maiden, old man, or him that stooped for age: he gave them all into his hand.**
God sent the king of the Chaldees to kill their young men with a sword, and had no compassion on anyone - women, children, or old people. 
18 And all the vessels of the house of God, great and small, and the treasures of the house of the LORD, and the treasures of the king, and of his princes; all these he brought to Babylon. 
19 And they burnt the house of God, and brake down the wall of Jerusalem, and burnt all the palaces thereof with fire, and destroyed all the goodly vessels thereof. 
They burned down God's house and destroyed everything in Jerusalem. 
20 And them that had escaped from the sword carried he away to Babylon; where they were servants to him and his sons until the reign of the kingdom of Persia: 
Those who weren't killed by the sword were carried away to Babylon. 
21 To fulfil the word of the LORD by the mouth of Jeremiah, until the land had enjoyed her sabbaths: for as long as she lay desolate she kept sabbath, to fulfil threescore and ten years. 
All this was done to fulfil God's word from the mouth of Jeremiah. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2chr/36.html#4n)]

### Notes
  1. _Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and bound him in fetters, to carry him to Babylon._ ([v.6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2chr/36.html#6)) 
[Did Jehoiakim die in Babylon or near Jerusalem?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jehoiakim_death.html)
  2. _Jehoiachin was eight years old when he began to reign ... and he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord._ ([v.9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2chr/36.html#9)) 
What did an eight year old do that was "evil in the sight of the Lord"? 
[How old was Jehoachin when he began to reign?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jehoiachin.html)
  3. _The wrath of the LORD arose against his people, till there was no remedy._ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2chr/36.html#16)) 
God gets angry with his people, so he sends the king of the Chaldees to kill all the "young men with the sword." He has compassion for no one, not even old men that are "stooped for age." In his tender mercy and loving kindness he has them all slaughtered. 
[DWB](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html): [The Fall of Jerusalem](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/chr.html#jerusalem)
  4. _The word of the LORD spoken by the mouth of Jeremiah_ ([v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2chr/36.html#22)) 
According to this verse, Jeremiah prophesied that King Cyrus would rebuild the temple in Jerusalem. But there is no such prophecy in Jeremiah. Maybe the author was thinking of Isaiah ([44:28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/44.html#28), [45:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/45.html#1)) or Ezra ([1:1-2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ezra/1.html#1), [4:3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ezra/4.html#3), [5:13-17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ezra/5.html#13), [6:3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ezra/6.html#3)). 
  5. _All the kingdoms of the earth hath the LORD God of heaven given me._ ([23a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2chr/36.html#23)) 
God gave "all the kingdoms of the earth" to King Cyrus. (OK, that might be a bit of an exaggeration.) 
  6. _The LORD his God be with him, and let him go up._ ([23b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2chr/36.html#23)) 
Now how's that for a strange ending? 
Actually, the last two verses from 2 Chronicles are taken from the first few verses of [Ezra](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ezra/1.html). It just happens that whoever decided to tack these verses on (for whatever reason) forgot to finish the sentence!
