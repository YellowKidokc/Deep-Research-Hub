![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) 8:1-9, Cf. [Mt 15:32-39](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/15.html#S5)
1 In those days the multitude being very great, and having nothing to eat, Jesus called his disciples unto him, and saith unto them,
2 I have compassion on the multitude, because they have now been with me three days, and have nothing to eat:
3 And if I send them away fasting to their own houses, they will faint by the way; for divers of them came from far.
Jesus said to his disciples,
> I feel sorry for the people.
> They haven't eaten anything in three days. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#1n)]
4 And his disciples answered him, From whence can a man satisfy these men with bread here in the wilderness?
His disciples said to him,
> Where can we find food for them in the wilderness?
5 And he asked them, How many loaves have ye? And they said, Seven.
Jesus said, "How many loaves do you have?"
They answered, "Seven."
6 And he commanded the people to sit down on the ground: and he took the seven loaves, and gave thanks, and brake, and gave to his disciples to set before them; and they did set them before the people.
Jesus told the people to sit down on the ground.
Then he took the seven loaves, gave thanks, broke them, and gave the bread to his disciples, who distributed it to the people.
7 And they had a few small fishes: and he blessed, and commanded to set them also before them.
There were also a few small fishes that they distributed among the people.
8 So they did eat, and were filled: and they took up of the broken meat that was left seven baskets.
Everyone ate as much as they wanted.
Then the disciples gathered seven baskets of leftovers.
9 And they that had eaten were about four thousand: and he sent them away.
About four thousand people were fed.
Then Jesus sent them away.

10 And straightway he entered into a ship with his disciples, and came into the parts of Dalmanutha.
Jesus and his disciples left on a boat to Dalmanutha.
11 And the Pharisees came forth, and began to question with him, seeking of him a sign from heaven, tempting him.
The Pharisees tempted Jesus by asking him for a sign from heaven.
12 And he sighed deeply in his spirit, and saith, Why doth this generation seek after a sign? verily I say unto you, There shall no sign be given unto this generation.
Jesus sighed and said,

Then Jesus got back on the boat and went to the other side.

Jesus said,
> Beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and of Herod.

### Notes
  1. _Jesus called his disciples unto him, and saith unto them, I have compassion on the multitude, because they have now been with me three days, and have nothing to eat._ ([vv.1-2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#1)) 
The disciples wonder where they'll get food for thousands of people. But they ought to know by now, since Jesus did that trick in [Mark 6:34-44](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/6.html#34). This "doublet" was probably the result of two oral traditions of the same story.
  2. _There shall no sign be given unto this generation._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#12)) 
[Did Jesus perform any signs and wonders?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/signs.html)
  3. _He cometh to Bethsaida; and they bring a blind man unto him._ ([v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#22)) 
[Where did Jesus cure the blind man?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/bethsaida.html)
  4. _He ... spit on his eyes, and ... asked him if he saw ought._ ([v.23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#23)) 
[How did Jesus cure the blind man?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/spit.html)
  5. _I see men as trees, walking_ ([v.24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#24)) 
Jesus' spit did not completely cure the blind man. So Jesus tried again. He put his hands on the man's eyes and, this time, the blind man "saw every man clearly."
  6. _Whom do men say that I am?_ ([v.27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#27)) 
There were various opinions about the identity of Jesus. Some thought he was Elijah or one of the prophets. And many thought he was a risen John the Baptist. With credulity like that just about anyone could later be passed off as the risen Christ.
  7. _He charged them that they should tell no man of him._ ([v.30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#30)) 
[Did Jesus have any secret teachings?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/secret.html)
  8. _He began to teach them, that the Son of man must ... be killed, and after three days rise again._ ([v.31](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#31)) 
[Did Jesus forewarn the apostles of his death and resurrection?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/comingdeath.html)
  9. _He rebuked Peter, saying, Get thee behind me, Satan._ ([v.33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/8.html#33)) 
When Peter expressed his dismay about Jesus' coming death, Jesus said to him "Get thee behind me, Satan." -- a fine way to address his holiness, the first pope!
