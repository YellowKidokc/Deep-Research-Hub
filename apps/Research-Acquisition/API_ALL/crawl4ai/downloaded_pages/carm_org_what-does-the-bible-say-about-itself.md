# What does the Bible say about itself?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
May 25, 2024 
7 min read 
The Bible describes the qualities about itself as they relate to the Word of God, the words of the Lord, etc. The word of God is God’s speech, written accounts through the prophets and apostles. It is the standard by which Christians learn what the faith is about, who God is, and what He wants for us. I produced an outline of what the Bible (God’s words) says about itself. It is below. Ultimately, they describe what Scripture, God’s word, the word of God, God’s speech, etc., says about itself.[ ![What does the Bible say about itself?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/bible-06s.jpg) ](https://carm.org/about-the-bible/how-many-books-are-there-in-the-bible/attachment/bible-06s/)
God’s word is what we live by ([Deut. 8:3](https://ref.ly/Deut.%208.3;nasb95?t=biblia)). It is pure ([Psalm 12:6](https://ref.ly/Ps%2012.6;nasb95?t=biblia)). We should treasure it in our hearts ([Psalm 119:11](https://ref.ly/Ps%20119.11;nasb95?t=biblia)). It illuminates ([Psalm 119:105](https://ref.ly/Ps%20119.105;nasb95?t=biblia)), is powerful ([Jer. 23:29](https://ref.ly/Jer.%2023.29;nasb95?t=biblia)), and accomplishes what God desires ([Isa. 55:11](https://ref.ly/Isa.%2055.11;nasb95?t=biblia)). God’s word is about Jesus ([John 5:39](https://ref.ly/John%205.39;nasb95?t=biblia)), that are spirit and life ([John 6:63](https://ref.ly/John%206.63;nasb95?t=biblia)). We cannot break God’s word ([John 10:35](https://ref.ly/John%2010.35;nasb95?t=biblia)) because it is true ([John 17:17](https://ref.ly/John%2017.17;nasb95?t=biblia)) and a standard of what is right([Acts 17:11](https://ref.ly/Acts%2017.11;nasb95?t=biblia)). God’s word is instructive ([Rom. 15:4](https://ref.ly/Rom.%2015.4;nasb95?t=biblia)), not to be exceeded ([1 Cor. 4:6](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%204.6;nasb95?t=biblia)), pierces the heart ([Heb. 4:12](https://ref.ly/Heb.%204.12;nasb95?t=biblia)), comes from God ([1 Thess. 2:13](https://ref.ly/1%20Thess.%202.13;nasb95?t=biblia)), works in believers ([1 Thess. 2:13](https://ref.ly/1%20Thess.%202.13;nasb95?t=biblia)), is inspired by God ([2 Tim. 3:16](https://ref.ly/2%20Tim.%203.16;nasb95?t=biblia)), God’s prophetic word comes from the Holy Spirit ([2 Pet. 1:21](https://ref.ly/2%20Pet.%201.21;nasb95?t=biblia)). It has the power to save our souls ([James 1:21](https://ref.ly/James%201.21;nasb95?t=biblia)).
