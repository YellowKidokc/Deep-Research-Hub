# Did Jesus or Simon of Cyrene carry the cross?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
4 min read 
Did Jesus or Simon of Cyrene carry the cross? ![Nailed to cross](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/handcrossnail.gif) [Matthew 27:31-32](https://ref.ly/Matt%2027.31-32;nasb95?t=biblia); [Mark 15:20-21](https://ref.ly/Mark%2015.20-21;nasb95?t=biblia); [Luke 23:26](https://ref.ly/Luke%2023.26;nasb95?t=biblia) and [John 19:17](https://ref.ly/John%2019.17;nasb95?t=biblia). Actually, both did.
Both carried the cross. [John 19:17](https://ref.ly/John%2019.17;nasb95?t=biblia) does not say that Jesus carried the cross alone. It says he bore his own cross. He carried the cross until he couldn’t carry any more than Simon assisted. A contradiction occurs when one statement makes another statement impossible but both are supposed to be true. There is no contradiction.
