# Contradictions in Watchtower Literature
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 30, 2008 
12 min read 
There are many contradictions in Watchtower Literature. Yet, the Watchtower says it is God’s only true organization: “Only this organization functions for Jehovah’s purpose and to his praise. To it alone God’s Sacred Word, the Bible, is not a sealed book,” (_Watchtower_ , July 1, 1973, p. 402). It claims to speak for God, yet it has produced numerous contradictory statements. Can you trust an organization that contradicts itself about so many things? No, you cannot. It is not God’s organization.
