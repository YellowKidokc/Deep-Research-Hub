# The King James anointing and the fat translations: Isaiah 10:27 and King James Onlyism
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
October 08, 2021 
7 min read 
King James Onlyists almost always find ways to read major theological significance into minor translational differences. One example of this is [Isaiah 10:27](https://ref.ly/Isa%2010.27;nasb95?t=biblia), where the KJV translates a Hebrew word as “anointing” that most modern translations render as “fat.” By reading “anointing” as a messianic reference, King James Onlyists accuse modern translators of trying to remove the prophecy about Jesus and undermine the reader’s faith. This accusation is misplaced on multiple levels.
The verse around which this argument centers reads: ![The King James anointing and the fat translations: Isaiah 10:27 and King James Onlyism](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/kjvo-02.jpg)
“And it shall come to pass in that day, that his burden shall be taken away from off thy shoulder, and his yoke from off thy neck, and the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing” ([Isaiah 10:27, KJV](https://ref.ly/Isa%2010.27;kjv1900?t=biblia)).
