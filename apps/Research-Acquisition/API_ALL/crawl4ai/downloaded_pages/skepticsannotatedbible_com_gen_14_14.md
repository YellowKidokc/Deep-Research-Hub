### CHAPTER 14
The Battle of Sodom and Gomorrah
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) 14:1-17
1 And it came to pass in the days of Amraphel king of Shinar, Arioch king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Tidal king of nations;
2 That these made war with Bera king of Sodom, and with Birsha king of Gomorrah, Shinab king of Admah, and Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar.
3 All these were joined together in the vale of Siddim, which is the salt sea.
4 Twelve years they served Chedorlaomer, and in the thirteenth year they rebelled.
5 And in the fourteenth year came Chedorlaomer, and the kings that were with him, and smote the Rephaims in Ashteroth Karnaim, and the Zuzims in Ham, and the Emins in Shaveh Kiriathaim,
6 And the Horites in their mount Seir, unto Elparan, which is by the wilderness.
Soon after Lot moved to Sodom, a war broke out between the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah and some of the other local kingdoms.
The Sodomites were defeated and Lot was taken prisoner.
That's when Abraham got involved. He sent an army of slaves to pursue and smite Lot's captors.
7 And they returned, and came to Enmishpat, which is Kadesh, and smote all the country of the Amalekites, and also the Amorites, that dwelt in Hazezontamar.
8 And there went out the king of Sodom, and the king of Gomorrah, and the king of Admah, and the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela (the same is Zoar;) and they joined battle with them in the vale of Siddim;
9 With Chedorlaomer the king of Elam, and with Tidal king of nations, and Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar; four kings with five.
10 And the vale of Siddim was full of slimepits; and the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled, and fell there; and they that remained fled to the mountain.
11 And they took all the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah, and all their victuals, and went their way.
12 And they took Lot, Abram's brother's son, who dwelt in Sodom, and his goods, and departed.
13 And there came one that had escaped, and told Abram the Hebrew; for he dwelt in the plain of Mamre the Amorite, brother of Eshcol, and brother of Aner: and these were confederate with Abram.
_They ... smote all the country of the Amalekites._ [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/14.html#1n)]
14 And when Abram heard that his brother was taken captive, he armed his trained servants, born in his own house, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued them unto Dan.
15 And he divided himself against them, he and his servants, by night, and smote them, and pursued them unto Hobah, which is on the left hand of Damascus.
16 And he brought back all the goods, and also brought again his brother Lot, and his goods, and the women also, and the people.
17 And the king of Sodom went out to meet him after his return from the slaughter of Chedorlaomer, and of the kings that were with him, at the valley of Shaveh, which is the king's dale.
To free Lot from captivity, Abraham sends an army of 318 slaves ("servants" in the KJV) to pursue and smite his captors. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/14.html#2n)]
_Pursued them into Dan_ [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/14.html#3n)]

Melchizedek [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/14.html#4n)], the king of Salem and "priest of the most high God", brought bread and wine.

21 And the king of Sodom said unto Abram, Give me the persons, and take the goods to thyself.

23 That I will not take from a thread even to a shoelatchet, and that I will not take any thing that is thine, lest thou shouldest say, I have made Abram rich:

### Notes
  1. _They ... smote all the country of the Amalekites._ [[v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/14.html#7)] 
The Amalekites were smitten before Amalek (from whom they descended) was born. Amalek was the grandson of Esau ([Genesis 36:12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/36.html#12)).
  2. _When[Abram](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/topics/abram.html) heard that his brother was taken captive, he armed his trained servants, born in his own house, three hundred and eighteen._ [[v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/14.html#14)] 
[Does God approve of slavery?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/slavery.html)
[Drunk with Blood: Abraham's war to rescue Lot](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/DWB/gen.html#abes-war)
  3. _Pursued them into Dan_ [[v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/14.html#14)] 
"Dan" is an obvious anachronism, since the city was not named "Dan" until the time of the Judges ([Jg 18:29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jg/18.html#29)). In fact, Dan (for whom the city was named) was not even born yet ([Gen 30:6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/30.html#6)).
[When was the city of Dan named?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/dan.html)
  4. _Melchizedek king of Salem_ [[v.18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/14.html#18)] 
Melchizedek, by the way, had no father or mother, no beginning or end, just like the Son of God. ([Heb 7:1-3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/heb/7.html#1))
  5. _The most high God, possessor of heaven and earth_ [[v.19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/14.html#19)] 
[Who owns the earth?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/owns.html)
