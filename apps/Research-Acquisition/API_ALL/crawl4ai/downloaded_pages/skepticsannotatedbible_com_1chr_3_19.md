### Notes
  1. _The sons of David, which were born unto him in Hebron_ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/3.html#1)) 
[The sons of David born in Hebron](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/david_sons_hebron.html)
Daughters, of course, are not mentioned. 
  2. _These were born unto him in Jerusalem._ ([v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/3.html#5)) 
[The sons of David born in Jerusalem](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/david_sons.html)
[Was Solomon David's second or fourth son by Bathsheba?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/solomon.html)
Daughters, of course, are not mentioned. 
  3. _All the sons of David, except for those he had with concubines_ ([v.9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/3.html#9)) 
The list of David's sons doesn't include the sons (or daughters) of his concubines. 
  4. _Solomon's son was Rehoboam._ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/3.html#10)) 
Solomon's son was Rehoboam. 
[Solomon had 700 wives and 300 concubines](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/11.html#3), but only one son. (And no daughter that anyone knows of.) 
  5. _Joash ... Amaziah ... Azariah ... Jehoiakim_ ([vv.11-15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/3.html#11)) 
The gospel of Mathew ([1:6-11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/1.html#6)) gives the same genealogy as is given here, except Matthew, in an attempt to preserve the magical number 14, leaves out four generations (Joash, Amaziah, Azariah, and Jehoiakim). 
[Generations from David to the Babylonian Captivity](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/david_cap.html)
[Who was Uzziah's father?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/uzziah.html)
  6. _And the sons of Josiah were ... Jehoiakim ... And the sons of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son._ ([vv.15-16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/3.html#15)) 
[Was Jeconiah the son or the grandson of Josiah?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jeconiahs_dad.html)
  7. _And the sons of Jeconiah; Assir, Salatheil his son, Malchiram also, and Pedaiah, and Shenazar, Jecamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah._ ([v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/3.html#17)) 
[Did Jeconiah have any sons?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jeconiah.html)
  8. _The sons of Pedaiah were, Zerubbabel..._ ([v.19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/3.html#19)) 
[Who was Zerubbabel's father?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/zerub.html)
  9. _The sons of Zerubbabel; Meshullam, and Hananiah ... And Hashubah, and Ohel, and Berechiah, and Hasadiah, Jushabhesed, five._ ([vv.19-20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/3.html#19)) 
Seven sons of Zerubbabel are listed, not five as is said in verse 20. 
  10. _The sons of Shemaiah; Hattush, and Igeal, and Bariah, and Neariah, and Shaphat, six_ ([v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/3.html#22)) 
Five sons of Shemiah are listed, not six as is said in this verse.
