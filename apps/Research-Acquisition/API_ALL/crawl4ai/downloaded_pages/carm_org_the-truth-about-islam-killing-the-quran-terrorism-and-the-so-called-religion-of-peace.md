# The truth about Islam, killing, the Quran, terrorism, and the so-called religion of peace
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 12, 2016 
5 min read 
In the aftermath of Islamic terrorist activity in America and abroad, the news media has defended Islam by calling it a religion of peace. But there is an unpleasant truth about Islam, the Quran, terrorism, and killing that everyone needs to know. The Quran, which is the source of Islamic theology and a guide to Muslim behavior along with the Hadith _(The Hadith is a record of the deeds and the sayings of Muhammad and is used as a guide in Muslim legal issues and cultural practices) definitely advocates killing non-Muslims. Let’s take a look:_
The Quran is not arranged chronologically or topically. Instead, it is arranged from the largest surah first (a surah is a chapter) to the smallest. So, for example, the last surah written is Surah 110 and the second to last is Surah 9. In addition, there is a concept in Islam called abrogation. This means that the later surah (chapters), are more authoritative than the ones before it. Surah 9 is the second to last surah written in the Quran. Therefore, it is the final word since the last surah in the Quran, 110, contains nothing that relates to Surah 9. Remember, it is Surah 9 that has the admonition to kill non-Muslims until the population of “unbelievers are subdued under sharia (Islamic Law).
The unpleasant truth is that the Quran teaches faithful Muslims to be at war with unbelievers until they are all subdued. This war includes killing to bring about Islamic order worldwide.
