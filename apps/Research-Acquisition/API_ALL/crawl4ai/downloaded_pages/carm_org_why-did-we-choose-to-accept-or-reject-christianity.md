# Why did we choose to accept or reject Christianity?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
June 10, 2016 
8 min read 
On August 29, 2015, I debated Shadid Lewis, a [Muslim](https://carm.org/dictionary/muslim/), on the topic, “Why did we choose to accept or reject [Christianity](https://carm.org/answers-for-seekers/what-is-christianity/)?” The following are my notes from the opening statement. I did not necessarily use all of the information here.
——————————-
The title of our debate now is “Why did we choose to accept or reject Christianity?” As a Christian theologian, and in particular, one who holds to reformed theology, I chose to follow Christ because he first chose me. The Bible tells us in [2 Thessalonians 2:13](https://ref.ly/2%20Thess%202.13;nasb95?t=biblia) that God chooses us for salvation. In [Philippians 1:29](https://ref.ly/Phil%201.29;nasb95?t=biblia) we are told that God grants that we believe. Jesus said in [John 6:28-29](https://ref.ly/John%206.28-29;nasb95?t=biblia) that it is the work of God the Father that we believe.
