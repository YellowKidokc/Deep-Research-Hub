### Notes
  1. _Chapter 37: Jacob loved Joseph more than his other children_ [[vv.1-4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/37.html#jacob)] 
See [Strange Flesh: There's something about Joseph](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/SF/possibly.html#Joseph) for the use of the stories about Joseph in Genesis chapters 37 - 41.
  2. _Joseph brought unto his father their evil report._ [[v.2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/37.html#2)] 
The Bible doesn't say what this "evil report" was about, but apparently Joseph spied on his brothers and reported their misdeeds to his father.
  3. _Israel loved Joseph more than all his children, because he was the son of his old age_ [[v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/37.html#3)] 
This seems like a strange reason to love one of your children more than the others. And it doesn't make much sense either, since Jacob was even older when he had Benjamin. Joseph was Rachel's first son ([Gen 30:22-24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/30.html#22)) and Benjamin was her second and last son ([Gen 35:17-18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#17)).
  4. _Joseph dreamed a dream._ [[v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/37.html#5)] 
[Dreams: Joseph's Dreams](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/topics/dreams.html#joseph)
  5. _The sun and the moon and the eleven stars made obeisance to me._ [[v.9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/37.html#9)] 
The sun, moon, and eleven stars in Joseph's second dream are supposed to represent his father, mother, and brothers. But Joseph's mother [died a couple chapters ago](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#ben).
  6. _Shall I and thy mother and thy brethren indeed come to bow down ourselves to thee to the earth?_ [[v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/37.html#10)] 
This would be especially hard to manage since Joseph's mother (Rachel) was already dead. ([Gen 35:18-20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/35.html#18))
  7. _Joseph went after his brethren, and found them in Dothan._ [[v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/37.html#17)] 
Jacob sent his favorite son (Joseph) to check up on his brothers who were tending flocks in [Shechem](http://www.bible-history.com/geography/ancient-israel/ot/shechem.html), which was about fifty miles north of [Hebron](http://www.bible-history.com/geography/ancient-israel/ot/hebron.html). When he got there, he found out that his brothers weren't there, but were another thirteen miles away in [Dothan](http://www.bible-history.com/geography/ancient-israel/ot/dothan.html). The trip must have taken poor Joseph a week or so.
  8. _The Ishmeelites ... brought Joseph into Egypt._ [[v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/37.html#28)] 
[Who brought Joseph into Egypt?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/soldjoe.html)
  9. _All his daughters rose up to comfort him._ [[v.35](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/37.html#35)] 
All of Jacob's daughters try to comfort him. And yet there Jacob only has one daughter -- Dinah. Were there others that just weren't mentioned?
  10. _The Midianites sold him into Egypt._ [[v.36](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/37.html#36)] 
[Who brought Joseph into Egypt?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/soldjoe.html)
