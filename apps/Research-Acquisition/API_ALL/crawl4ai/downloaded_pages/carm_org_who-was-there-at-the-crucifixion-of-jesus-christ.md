# Who was there at the crucifixion of Jesus Christ?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 20, 2023 
9 min read 
Quite a few people were present at the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, maybe as many as 100 (see below). There was a large crowd of people ([Luke 23:27](https://ref.ly/Luke%2023.27;nasb95?t=biblia)), the women who accompanied Jesus ([Luke 23:49](https://ref.ly/Luke%2023.49;nasb95?t=biblia)), Mary Jesus’ Mother, Mary’s sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene ([John 19:25](https://ref.ly/John%2019.25;nasb95?t=biblia)), the disciple whom Jesus loved ([John 19:26](https://ref.ly/John%2019.26;nasb95?t=biblia)), the centurion ([Matt. 27:54](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2027.54;nasb95?t=biblia)), chief priests, scribes, elders ([Matt. 27:41](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2027.41;nasb95?t=biblia)), along with the people and the rulers ([Luke 23:35](https://ref.ly/Luke%2023.35;nasb95?t=biblia)). A lot of people were there.
However, the gospels do not directly name any of the disciples as being present. Nevertheless, at the crucifixion, there was “the disciple whom He loved” ([John 19:26](https://ref.ly/John%2019.26;nasb95?t=biblia)). Most presume him to be John, the apostle, because it says in [John 13:23](https://ref.ly/John%2013.23;nasb95?t=biblia), “There was reclining on Jesus bosom one of his disciples, whom Jesus loved.” ![](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/crucifixion1.jpg)
In light of this, we have to ask if there’s a contradiction when dealing with [Matt. 26:56](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2026.56;nasb95?t=biblia), where it says that “all the disciples left him and fled.” This does not necessitate a contradiction for the following reasons. First, when it says “all the disciples left Him,” it could be an exaggeration with the use of the word ‘all.’ Such embellishments are frequent in the New Testament. For example, “Now in those days, a decree went out from Caesar Augustus, that a census be taken of all the inhabited earth” ([Luke 2:1](https://ref.ly/Luke%202.1;nasb95?t=biblia)). This did not include the people in Australia and Japan. In [Acts 11:28](https://ref.ly/Acts%2011.28;nasb95?t=biblia) it states that there was a “great famine all over the world.” Was there? Or was Luke conveying an exaggeration for emphasis? You can read more on this idea of the biblical use of the word all by reading the article “[Words mean what they mean in context – All](https://carm.org/tables-and-charts/words-mean-what-they-mean-in-context-all/).”
