## [Matthew](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/outline.html "Matthew Outline")
### CHAPTER 27
Jesus is taken to Pontius Pilate
  
27:1-2, Cf. [Mk 15:2-5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#S2); [Lk 23:1-7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#S1); [Jn 18:28-40](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/18.html#S7), [19:1-16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/19.html#S1)
1 When the morning was come, all the chief priests and elders of the people took counsel against Jesus to put him to death:
The next morning, all the chief priests and elders met to plan Jesus's death.
2 And when they had bound him, they led him away, and delivered him to Pontius Pilate the governor.
They tied him up, led him away, and delivered him to Pontius Pilate the governor.
Judas hangs himself
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/pR1.gif) 27:3-10, Cf. [Acts 1:12-19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/1.html#S3)
3 Then Judas, which had betrayed him, when he saw that he was condemned, repented himself, and brought again the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders,
4 Saying, I have sinned in that I have betrayed the innocent blood. And they said, What is that to us? see thou to that.
Judas felt bad about betraying Jesus, so he returned the thirty silver coins to the chief priests and elders, saying, "I was wrong to betray Jesus." 
They said to him, "What's that to us? That is your deal."
5 And he cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, and departed, and went and hanged himself.
So Judas threw down the silver coins in the temple [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#1n)], and went to hang himself. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#2n)]
6 And the chief priests took the silver pieces, and said, It is not lawful for to put them into the treasury, because it is the price of blood.
The chief priests took the silver coins, and said, "We can't put these in the treasury because they are blood money."
7 And they took counsel, and bought with them the potter's field, to bury strangers in.
So they bought the potter's field to bury strangers in. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#3n)]
8 Wherefore that field was called, The field of blood, unto this day.
So the field was called, the "Field of Blood". And it is still called that today. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#4n)]
9 Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremy the prophet, saying, And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him that was valued, whom they of the children of Israel did value;
10 And gave them for the potter's field, as the Lord appointed me.
This was to fulfill Jeremiah's [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#5n)] prophecy, saying, "They took thirty silver coins, the price of a person, as valued by the Israelites. and spent them on a potter's field, as the Lord commanded me."
Jesus is questioned by Pilate
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 27:11-14, Cf. [Mk 15:2-5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#S2); [Lk 23:2-7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#S2); [Jn 18:29-40](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/18.html#S8), [19:1-16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/19.html#S1)
11 And Jesus stood before the governor: and the governor asked him, saying, Art thou the King of the Jews? And Jesus said unto him, Thou sayest.
Jesus stood in front of the governor, who asked him, "Are you the King of the Jews?" 
Jesus answered, "You say that I am."
12 And when he was accused of the chief priests and elders, he answered nothing.
When Jesus was accused by the chief priests and elders, he didn't say anything. [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#6n)]
13 Then said Pilate unto him, Hearest thou not how many things they witness against thee?
Pilate said to him, "Do you hear what the witnesses have said against you?"
14 And he answered him to never a word; insomuch that the governor marvelled greatly.
Jesus didn't say a single word. [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#7n)] Pilate wondered why.
The people prefer Barabbas to Jesus
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/int.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) 27:15-26, Cf. [Mk 16:6-15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#S3), [Lk 23:18-25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#S5), [Jn 18:39-40](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/18.html#S9)
15 Now at that feast the governor was wont to release unto the people a prisoner, whom they would.
At the feast of passover, the governor liked to release a prisoner to please the people.
16 And they had then a notable prisoner, called Barabbas.
Barabbas was a well-known prisoner.
17 Therefore when they were gathered together, Pilate said unto them, Whom will ye that I release unto you? Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?
So Pilate asked the people, which should I release? Barabbas or Jesus who is called Christ?
18 For he knew that for envy they had delivered him.
Because he knew Jesus was accused out of envy.
19 When he was set down on the judgment seat, his wife sent unto him, saying, Have thou nothing to do with that just man: for I have suffered many things this day in a dream because of him.
Pilate's wife said to him, "Don't have anything to do with this just man. I had a bad dream about him."
20 But the chief priests and elders persuaded the multitude that they should ask Barabbas, and destroy Jesus.
But the chief priests and elders convinced the crowd to ask for Barabbas instead of Jesus.
21 The governor answered and said unto them, Whether of the twain will ye that I release unto you? They said, Barabbas.
Pilate asked the crowd, "Which should I release for you?" 
The crowd answered, "Barabbas."
22 Pilate saith unto them, What shall I do then with Jesus which is called Christ? They all say unto him, Let him be crucified.
Pilate asked them, "What should I do with Jesus who is called Christ?" 
The crowd all said to him, "Crucify him!"
23 And the governor said, Why, what evil hath he done? But they cried out the more, saying, Let him be crucified.
Pilate said, "Why? What did he do that was wrong?" 
But the crowd shouted even louder, "Crucify him!"
24 When Pilate saw that he could prevail nothing, but that rather a tumult was made, he took water, and washed his hands before the multitude, saying, I am innocent of the blood of this just person: see ye to it.
When Pilate saw that he couldn't change their minds, he washed his hands in front of the crowd, saying, "I am not responsible for this just man's death. It's your fault."
25 Then answered all the people, and said, **His blood be on us, and on our children.**
Then all the people said, "We are responsible for his death, we and our children." [[8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#8n)]
26 Then released he Barabbas unto them: and when he had scourged Jesus, he delivered him to be crucified.
Pilate then released Barabbas to the crowd, had Jesus whipped and delivered him to be crucified.
The soldiers make fun of Jesus
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 27:27-30, Cf. [Mk 15:16-20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#S4), [Jn 19:1-3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/19.html#S1)
27 Then the soldiers of the governor took Jesus into the common hall, and gathered unto him the whole band of soldiers.
28 And they stripped him, and put on him a scarlet robe.
The soldiers stripped Jesus and put a scarlet robe on him. [[9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#9n)]
29 And when they had platted a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand: and they bowed the knee before him, and mocked him, saying, Hail, King of the Jews!
Then they put a crown of thorns on his head and a stick in his hand. And they bowed down to him, saying, "Hail, King of the Jews!"
30 And they spit upon him, and took the reed, and smote him on the head.
And they spit on him, and hit him on the head with the reed.
Jesus is crucified
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/pR1.gif) 27:31-44, Cf. [Mk 15:24-26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#S5), [Lk 23:38](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#38), [Jn 19:29-30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/19.html#29)
31 And after that they had mocked him, they took the robe off from him, and put his own raiment on him, and led him away to crucify him.
After they made fun of him, they took off the robe, put his own clothes back on him, and led him away to be crucified.
32 And as they came out, they found a man of Cyrene, Simon by name: him they compelled to bear his cross.
As they went out, they found a man from Cyrene, named Simon, and they forced him to carry Jesus's cross. [[10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#10n)]
33 And when they were come unto a place called Golgotha, that is to say, a place of a skull,
34 They gave him vinegar to drink mingled with gall: and when he had tasted thereof, he would not drink.
When they came to a place called Golgotha, meaning "a place of a skull", they gave Jesus vinegar, mixed with gall [[11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#11n)], to drink. When Jesus tasted it, he refused to drink.
35 And they crucified him, and parted his garments, casting lots: that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, They parted my garments among them, and upon my vesture did they cast lots. 
36 And sitting down they watched him there;
And they crucified him, dividing his clothes among them. 
This was done to fulfill the prophecy, "They divided my clothes, and cast lots on them." [[12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#12n)]
37 And set up over his head his accusation written, THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS.
They put up a sign over his head, saying, 
> THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS. [[13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#13n)]
38 Then were there two thieves crucified with him, one on the right hand, and another on the left.
Two thieves were crucified next to Jesus, one on the right and one on the left.
39 And they that passed by reviled him, wagging their heads,
40 And saying, Thou that destroyest the temple, and buildest it in three days, save thyself. If thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross.
Everyone that passed by mocked Jesus, saying, 
> You said you could destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days. 
> If you're the Son of God, come down from the cross.
41 Likewise also the chief priests mocking him, with the scribes and elders, said,
42 He saved others; himself he cannot save. If he be the King of Israel, let him now come down from the cross, and we will believe him.
43 He trusted in God; let him deliver him now, if he will have him: for he said, I am the Son of God.
The priests, scribes, and elders also mocked him, saying, 
> He saved others but can't save himself. 
> We will believe he is the King of Israel if he comes down from the cross.
> He said, "I am the Son of God". So he should ask God to save him now.
44 The thieves also, which were crucified with him, cast the same in his teeth.
The two thieves also mocked him. [[14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#14n)]

### Notes
  1. _Judas ... cast down the pieces of silver in the temple._ ([v.3-5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#3)) 
[What did Judas do with the silver?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/silver.html)
  2. _Judas ... went and hanged himself._ ([vv.3-5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#3)) 
[How did Judas die?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/judas.html)
  3. _The chief priests took the silver pieces ... and bought with them the potter's field._ ([vv.6-7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#6)) 
[Who bought the Potter's field?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/field.html)
  4. _Unto this day_ ([v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#8)) 
The phrase "unto this day" shows that the gospel of Matthew was written long after the events it describes.
  5. _Spoken by Jeremy the prophet, saying, And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him that was valued, whom they of the children of Israel did value; And gave them for the potter's field, as the Lord appointed me._ ([vv.9-10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#9)) 
This is not a quote from Jeremiah, but a misquote of Zechariah ([11:12-13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/zech/11.html#12)).
  6. _When he was accused of the chief priests and elders, he answered nothing._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#12)) 
[Was Jesus silent during his trial?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/speech.html)
  7. _He answered him to never a word._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#14)) 
[Was Jesus silent during his trial?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/speech.html)
  8. _His blood be on us, and on our children._ ([v.25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#25)) 
This verse blames the Jews for the death of Jesus and has been used to justify their persecution for twenty centuries.
  9. _Then the soldiers of the governor took Jesus ... and they stripped him, and put on him a scarlet robe._ ([v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#28)) 
[Who put the robe on Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/dressed.html)
[What color was Jesus's robe?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/color.html)
  10. _They found a man of Cyrene, Simon by name: him they compelled to bear his cross._ ([v.32](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#32)) 
[Who carried Jesus's cross?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/cyrenian.html)
  11. _They gave him vinegar to drink mingled with gall._ ([v.34](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#34)) 
[What did the soldiers give Jesus to drink?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/vinegar.html)
  12. _That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, They parted my garments among them, and upon my vesture did they cast lots._ ([v.35](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#35)) 
> They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture. [Psalm 22:18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/22.html#18)
  13. _THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS._ ([v.37](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#37)) 
None of the gospels agree on the wording of the sign placed over Jesus' head.
[What did the sign say?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sign.html)
  14. _The thieves also, which were crucified with him, cast the same in his teeth._ ([v.44](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#44)) 
[Did both thieves revile Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/reviled.html)
  15. _From the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land unto the ninth hour._ ([v.45](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#45)) 
When Jesus was crucified, there was three hours of complete darkness "over all the land."
[When (at what hour) was Jesus crucified?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/hour.html)
  16. _My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?_ ([v.46](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#46)) 
[What were the last words of Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/lastwords.html)
[Is Jesus God?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/Jesus_God.html)
  17. _The veil of the temple was rent._ ([v.51](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#51)) 
[When did the Temple curtain rip?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/curtain.html)
  18. _The graves were opened; and many bodies ... came out of the graves after his resurrection, and went into the holy city._ ([vv.52-53](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#52)) 
And when Jesus died, there was a great earthquake with many corpses walking the streets of Jerusalem.
It is strange that there is no record of any of these extraordinary events outside of the gospels.
[Was Jesus the first to rise from the dead?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/first.html)
  19. _Truly this was the Son of God._ ([v.54](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#54)) 
[What did the centurion call Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/centurion_call.html)
  20. _Many women were there beholding afar off._ ([v.55](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#55)) 
[From where did the women watch?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/women_watching.html)
  21. _There came a rich man of Arimathaea, named Joseph ... went to Pilate, and begged the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded the body to be delivered._ ([v..57-58](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#57)) 
[Who buried Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/buried.html)
  22. _So they went, and made the sepulchre sure, sealing the stone, and setting a watch._ ([v.66](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#66)) 
Only Matthew's gospel mentions a guard at the tomb.
