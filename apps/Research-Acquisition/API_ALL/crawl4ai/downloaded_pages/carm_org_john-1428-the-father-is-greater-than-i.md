# John 14:28, “The Father is greater than I.”
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 05, 2008 
3 min read 
“You heard that I said to you, ‘I go away, and I will come to you.’ If you loved Me, you would have rejoiced, because I go to the Father; for the Father is greater than I.'” ([John 14:28](https://ref.ly/John%2014.28;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Jesus said that the Father was greater than He not because Jesus is not God but because [Jesus was also a man](https://carm.org/doctrine-and-theology/jesus-two-natures-god-and-man/), and as a man, He was in a lower position. He was “. . . made for a little while lower than the angels . . .” ([Heb. 2:9](https://ref.ly/Heb.%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia)). Also in [Phil. 2:5-8](https://ref.ly/Phil.%202.5-8;nasb95?t=biblia), it says that Jesus “emptied Himself, taking the form of a bond-servant, and being made in the likeness of men . . .” ![The Father is greater than I](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/jesus-christ-gbcd10a5dd_640-300x199.jpg)
Jesus has two natures. Jesus was not denying that He was God. He was merely acknowledging the fact that He was also a man. Jesus is both God and man. As a man, He was in a lesser position than the Father. He had added to Himself human nature ([Col. 2:9](https://ref.ly/Col.%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia)). He became a man to die for people.
