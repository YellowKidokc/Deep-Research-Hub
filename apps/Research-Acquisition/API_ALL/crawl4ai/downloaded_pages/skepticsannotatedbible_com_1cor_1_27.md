1 Paul called to be an apostle of Jesus Christ through the will of God, and Sosthenes our brother,
A letter from Paul and Sosthenes [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#1n)]
2 Unto the church of God which is at Corinth, to them that are sanctified in Christ Jesus, called to be saints, with all that in every place call upon the name of Jesus Christ our Lord, both their's and our's:
3 Grace be unto you, and peace, from God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.
4 I thank my God always on your behalf, for the grace of God which is given you by Jesus Christ;
5 That in every thing ye are enriched by him, in all utterance, and in all knowledge;
6 Even as the testimony of Christ was confirmed in you:
to the church in Corinth.
7 So that ye come behind in no gift; waiting for the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ:
8 Who shall also confirm you unto the end, that ye may be blameless in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.
9 God is faithful, by whom ye were called unto the fellowship of his Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
So that you will be ready when Jesus comes again. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#2n)]
Believe the same things (agree on everything)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/interp.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 1:10-13
10 Now I beseech you, brethren, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye all speak the same thing, and that there be no divisions among you; but that ye be perfectly joined together in the same mind and in the same judgment.
I beg you all to say the same thing
and agree on everything. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#3n)]
11 For it hath been declared unto me of you, my brethren, by them which are of the house of Chloe, that there are contentions among you.
I've heard from people in Chloe's house
that some of you are arguing with each other.
12 Now this I say, that every one of you saith, I am of Paul; and I of Apollos; and I of Cephas; and I of Christ.
13 Is Christ divided? was Paul crucified for you? or were ye baptized in the name of Paul?
You're all saying, "I belong to Paul,"
or "I belong to Apollo,"
or "I belong to Cephas."
or "I belong to Christ." [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#4n)]
Thank God I didn't baptize any of you
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 1:14-18
14 I **thank God that I baptized none of you** , but Crispus and Gaius;
Thank God I didn't baptize any of you,
except for Crispus and Gaius.
15 Lest any should say that I had baptized in mine own name.
Or some may say that I baptized in my own name.
16 And I baptized also the household of Stephanas: besides, **I know not whether I baptized any other**.
I also baptized Stephanas's family.
I can't remember whether I've baptized any others. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#5n)]
17 For Christ sent me not to baptize, but to preach the gospel: not with wisdom of words, lest the cross of Christ should be made of none effect.
18 For the preaching of the cross is to them that perish foolishness; but unto us which are saved it is the power of God.
Because Christ didn't send me to baptize, but to preach.
God will destroy the wisdom of the wise
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 1:19-31
19 For it is written, **I will destroy the wisdom of the wise** , and will bring to nothing the understanding of the prudent.
20 Where is the wise? where is the scribe? where is the disputer of this world? hath not God made foolish the wisdom of this world?
It is written,
> I'll destroy the wisdom of the wise
> and the understanding of the prudent. [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#6n)]
21 For after that in the wisdom of God the world by wisdom knew not God, **it pleased God by the foolishness of preaching to save them that believe.**
22 For the Jews require a sign, and the Greeks seek after wisdom:
23 But we preach Christ crucified, unto the Jews a stumblingblock, and unto the Greeks foolishness;
24 But unto them which are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God, and the wisdom of God.
25 Because the foolishness of God is wiser than men; and the weakness of God is stronger than men.
26 For ye see your calling, brethren, how that not many wise men after the flesh, not many mighty, not many noble, are called:
God saved believers by the foolishness of preaching. [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#7n)]
27 But **God hath chosen the foolish things of the world to confound the wise** ; and God hath chosen the weak things of the world to confound the things which are mighty;
28 And base things of the world, and things which are despised, hath God chosen, yea, and things which are not, to bring to nought things that are:
29 That no flesh should glory in his presence.
30 But of him are ye in Christ Jesus, who of God is made unto us wisdom, and righteousness, and sanctification, and redemption:
31 That, according as it is written, He that glorieth, let him glory in the Lord.
God chose the foolishness of the world
to confound the wise. [[8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#8n)]

### Notes
  1. _Sosthenes_ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#1)) 
Sosthenes is the co-author of 1 Corinthians. No one knows who he was (although there is a Sosthenes mentioned in [Acts 18:17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/18.html#17)). Whoever he might have been, he doesn't get much credit for his efforts.
  2. _So that ye come behind in no gift; waiting for the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#7)) 
Paul expected Jesus to return within the lifetime of his followers.
[What the Bible says about the end of the world](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/end.html)
  3. _Now I beseech you ... that ye all speak the same thing, and that there be no divisions among you._ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#10)) 
Jehovah's Witnesses: "Now I exhort you, brothers ... that you should all speak in agreement, and that there should not be divisions among you, but that you may be fitly united in the same mind and in the same line of thought." (New World Translation)
The Governing Body uses this verse to control the thoughts and beliefs of Jehovah's Witnesses. They are instructed not to accept or read the religious literature of others, not to listen to criticism of the Watchtower Society's teachings, and to fight against independent thinking.
  4. _I am of Paul; and I of Apollos; and I of Cephas; and I of Christ._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#12)) 
Christians started out divided [into followers of Paul, Apollos, Cephas (Peter), or Christ], and it has remained so ever since.
  5. _I baptized none of you, but Crispus and Gaius ... And I baptized also the household of Stephanas: besides, I know not whether I baptized any other._ ([vv.14-16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#14)) 
Poor Paul is confused. First he says that he baptized no one. Well, except for Crispus and Gaius. And maybe Stephanus and his household. He can't remember if he did it to anyone else.
  6. _I will destroy the wisdom of the wise._ ([v.19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#19)) 
God: the destroyer of wisdom (See also [Isaiah 29:14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/29.html#14))
[Is wisdom a good thing?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/wisdom_good.html)
  7. _It pleased God by the foolishness of preaching to save them that believe._ ([v.21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#21)) 
God saves fools and is pleased with their foolishness.
[Is it good to be foolish?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/foolish.html)
  8. _God hath chosen the foolish things of the world to confound the wise._ ([v.27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/1.html#27)) 
[Is God the author of confusion?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/confusion.html)
