# Did Jesus experience everything that normal people experienced?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
May 23, 2024 
3 min read 
No, Jesus did not experience everything that normal people experience. He did not sin ([1 Pet. 2:22](https://ref.ly/1%20Pet.%202.22;nasb95?t=biblia)). He did not seek His own will the way we do ([John 5:30](https://ref.ly/John%205.30;nasb95?t=biblia)). He did not lie, steal, or covet. He did not get married. He did not experience sexual intercourse because He was never married. He did not have children. And, since He had no sin, we can say He did not get sick either since sickness is part of the Fall, and Jesus did not have a fallen nature.[![Jesus experience normal people](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/Jesus-19.jpg)](https://carm.org/evidence-and-answers/it-is-improbable-that-jesus-rose-from-the-dead/attachment/jesus-19/)
Jesus did things “normal” people don’t do.
But Jesus experienced what we experienced in many ways. He was baptized ([Matthew 3:13–17](https://ref.ly/Matt%203.13%E2%80%9317;nasb95?t=biblia)), tempted ([Matt. 4:1](https://ref.ly/Matt.%204.1;nasb95?t=biblia)), ate and drank ([Luke 7:34](https://ref.ly/Luke%207.34;nasb95?t=biblia)), slept ([Mark 4:38](https://ref.ly/Mark%204.38;nasb95?t=biblia)), walked ([Mark 10:32](https://ref.ly/Mark%2010.32;nasb95?t=biblia)), talked ([John 3:16](https://ref.ly/John%203.16;nasb95?t=biblia)), grew ([Luke 2:52](https://ref.ly/Luke%202.52;nasb95?t=biblia)), got thirsty ([John 19:28](https://ref.ly/John%2019.28;nasb95?t=biblia)), and died ([1 Cor. 15:3](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2015.3;nasb95?t=biblia)). This is because He was, and still is, a man ([1 Tim. 2:5](https://ref.ly/1%20Tim.%202.5;nasb95?t=biblia); [Col. 2:9](https://ref.ly/Col.%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia)).
