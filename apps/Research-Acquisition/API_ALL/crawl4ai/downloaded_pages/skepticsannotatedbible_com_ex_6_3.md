1 Then the LORD said unto Moses, **Now shalt thou see what I will do to Pharaoh** : for with a strong hand shall he let them go, and with a strong hand shall he drive them out of his land.
2 And God spake unto Moses, and said unto him, I am the LORD:
3 And I appeared unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob, but by the name of God Almighty, but by my name JEHOVAH was I not known to them.
4 And I have also established my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land of their pilgrimage, wherein they were strangers.
5 And I have also heard the groaning of the children of Israel, whom the Egyptians keep in bondage; and I have remembered my covenant.
6 Wherefore say unto the children of Israel, I am the LORD, and I will bring you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians, and I will rid you out of their bondage, and I will redeem you with a stretched out arm, and with great judgments:
7 And I will take you to me for a people, and I will be to you a God: and ye shall know that I am the LORD your God, which bringeth you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians.
8 And I will bring you in unto the land, concerning the which I did swear to give it to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob; and I will give it you for an heritage: I am the LORD.
God said to Moses,
> Now you'll see what I will do to Pharaoh.
> I am God.
> I appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/6.html#1n)]
> But they didn't know my name was Jehovah. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/6.html#2n)]
> I gave the land of Canaan to them when they were strangers.
> I heard the Israelites groan in Egypt.
> So I said to them,
>> I am God. I'll bring you out of Egypt, to the land that I gave to your fathers.
9 And Moses spake so unto the children of Israel: but they hearkened not unto Moses for anguish of spirit, and for cruel bondage.
Moses speaks to the people but they don't listen to him.
10 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,
11 Go in, speak unto Pharaoh king of Egypt, that he let the children of Israel go out of his land.
God said to Moses, 
> Tell the Pharaoh to let my people go.
12 And Moses spake before the LORD, saying, Behold, the children of Israel have not hearkened unto me; how then shall Pharaoh hear me, who am of **uncircumcised lips**?
Moses said to God,
> The people won't listen to me.
> I have uncircumcised lips. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/6.html#3n)]

God said to Moses and Aaron, 
> Bring my people out of Egypt.

### Notes
  1. _I appeared unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/6.html#3)) 
[Has anyone ever seen God?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/seen.html)
  2. _But by the name of God Almighty, but by my name JEHOVAH was I not known to them._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/6.html#3)) 
[Did Abraham know God's name?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jehovah.html)
[What is God's name?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/name.html)
  3. _Uncircumcised lips_ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/6.html#12)) 
[Was Moses a good speaker?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/moses_speaker.html)
  4. _The years of the life of Levi ... Kohath ... Amram_ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/6.html#16), [18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/6.html#18), [20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/6.html#20)) 
Levi, Kohath, and Amram join the [long list](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/topics/lifespans.html) of biblical characters with ridiculously long lives (137, 133, and 137 years, respectively).
[How many generations from Abraham to Moses?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/abe-moses.html)
[How many generations were between Levi and Moses?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/levi-moses.html)
[Was Mahli the son of Levi?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/mahli.html)
  5. _Amram took him Jochebed his father's sister to wife; and she bare him Aaron and Moses._ ([v.20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/6.html#20)) 
Moses and Aaron were the products of an incestuous marriage.
Notice that there were four generations from Jacob to Moses (Jacob, Levi, Kohath, Amram, Moses), while eleven generations are listed for Joshua, a contemporary of Moses, in [1 Chr 7:20-27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/7.html#20).
[Is incest forbidden?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/incest.html)
[How many generations from Abraham to Moses?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/abe-moses.html)
