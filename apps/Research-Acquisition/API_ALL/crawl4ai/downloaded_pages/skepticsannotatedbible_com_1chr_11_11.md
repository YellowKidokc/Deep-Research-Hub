David becomes king of Israel 
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) 11:1-9, Cf. [2 Sam 5:1-3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2sam/5.html#S1)
1 Then all Israel gathered themselves to David unto Hebron, saying, Behold, we are thy bone and thy flesh. 
Everyone in Israel came to David and said to him, 
> We are your flesh and blood. 
2 And moreover in time past, even when Saul was king, thou wast he that leddest out and broughtest in Israel: and the LORD thy God said unto thee, Thou shalt feed my people Israel, and thou shalt be ruler over my people Israel. 
> When Saul was king, you led his army. 
> God said to you, "You'll feed my people and be their king." 
3 Therefore came all the elders of Israel to the king to Hebron; and David made a covenant with them in Hebron before the LORD; and they anointed David king over Israel, according to the word of the LORD by Samuel. 
So they made David their king, according to God's word as spoken by Samuel. 
4 And David and all Israel went to Jerusalem, which is Jebus; where the Jebusites were, the inhabitants of the land. 
David, along with everyone in Israel, went to Jerusalem (which is Jebus). 
5 And the inhabitants of Jebus said to David, Thou shalt not come hither. Nevertheless David took the castle of Zion, which is the city of David. 
When they arrived, the people of Jebus said to David, 
> You won't come here. 
But David took Jerusalem anyway. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/11.html#1n)] 
6 And David said, Whosoever smiteth the Jebusites first shall be chief and captain. So Joab the son of Zeruiah went first up, and was chief.
7 And David dwelt in the castle; therefore they called it the city of David. 
8 And he built the city round about, even from Millo round about: and Joab repaired the rest of the city. 
David said, 
> Whoever is first to smite the Jebusites will be my chief and captain. 
And Joab was the first to smite them, so he was chief. 
9 So David waxed greater and greater: for the LORD of hosts was with him.
David lived in the castle and became greater and greater, because the God of war was with him. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/11.html#2n)]

10 These also are the chief of the mighty men whom David had, who strengthened themselves with him in his kingdom, and with all Israel, to make him king, according to the word of the LORD concerning Israel. 
This is a list of David's mighty men. 
11 And this is the number of the mighty men whom David had; **Jashobeam** , an Hachmonite, **the chief of the captains: he lifted up his spear against three hundred slain by him at one time.**
Jashobeam, the chief of the captains, killed 300 men with his spear. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/11.html#3n)] 
12 And after him was Eleazar the son of Dodo, the Ahohite, who was one of the three mighties.
13 He was with David at Pasdammim, and there the Philistines were gathered together to battle, where was a parcel of ground full of barley; and the people fled from before the Philistines. 
Eleazar, the son of Dodo, was one of the three mighties. 
14 And they set themselves in the midst of that parcel, and delivered it, and slew the Philistines; and the LORD saved them by a great deliverance.
15 Now three of the thirty captains went down to the rock to David, into the cave of Adullam; and the host of the Philistines encamped in the valley of Rephaim. 
16 And David was then in the hold, and the Philistines' garrison was then at Bethlehem. 
God saved Eleazar and David by helping them kill Philistines. 
17 And David longed, and said, Oh that one would give me drink of the water of the well of Bethlehem, that is at the gate!
18 And the three brake through the host of the Philistines, and drew water out of the well of Bethlehem, that was by the gate, and took it, and brought it to David: but David would not drink of it, but poured it out to the LORD.
19 And said, My God forbid it me, that I should do this thing: shall I drink the blood of these men that have put their lives in jeopardy? for with the jeopardy of their lives they brought it. Therefore he would not drink it.
These things did these three mightiest. 
Once David was thirsty and said to his three warriors, 
> I'd like someone to get me a drink of water from the well in Bethlehem. 
So the three warriors snuck through the Philistine soldiers to the well in Bethlehem and brought back some water for David to drink. 
But David poured it out to God and said, 
> God forbid that I drink the blood of the men who risked their lives for me. 
And he wouldn't drink it.

Abishai got a name for himself by killing 300 with his spear. 
21 Of the three, he was more honourable than the two; for he was their captain: howbeit he attained not to the first three. 
Of the three, Abishai was more honorable than the other two, but wasn't as great as the first three. 
22 **Benaiah** the son of Jehoiada, the son of a valiant man of Kabzeel, who had done many acts; he **slew two lionlike men of Moab: also he went down and slew a lion in a pit in a snowy day.**
Benaiah killed two lion-like men of Moab, and he also killed a lion in a pit on a snowy day.

He plucked the spear out of the giant's hand and killed him with it. 
24 These things did Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and had the name among the three mighties. 
He had a name among the three mighties.

### Notes
  1. _The castle of Zion, which is the city of David_ ([v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/11.html#5)) 
The people of Jerusalem didn't want David in their city, but he took it anyway. 
The chronicles calls it "the castle of Zion" and "the city of David." 
  2. _So David waxed greater and greater: for the LORD of hosts was with him._ ([v.9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/11.html#9)) 
It would be hard to find a more disgusting character in all fiction than David, but "the Lord of hosts was with him." 
  3. _Three hundred slain by him at one time_ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/11.html#11)) 
The chief of David's captains killed with his own spear 300 guys at one time -- or was it 800 like it says in [2 Samuel 23:8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2sam/23.html#8)? 
[How many men did the chief of David's captains kill?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/300or800.html)
