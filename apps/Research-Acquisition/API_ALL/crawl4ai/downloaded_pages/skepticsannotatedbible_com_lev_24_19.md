### CHAPTER 24
Twelve cakes for the Lord
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 24:1-9
1 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,
2 Command the children of Israel, that they bring unto thee pure oil olive beaten for the light, to cause the lamps to burn continually.
3 Without the vail of the testimony, in the tabernacle of the congregation, shall Aaron order it from the evening unto the morning before the LORD continually: it shall be a statute for ever in your generations.
4 He shall order the lamps upon the pure candlestick before the LORD continually.
God said to Moses, 
> Command the Israelites to bring pure olive oil to cause lamps to burn before me continually.
> It is a statute forever.
5 And thou shalt take fine flour, and **bake twelve cakes** thereof: two tenth deals shall be in one cake.
> Take some fine flour and bake twelve cakes, with one-fifth deal in each cake.
6 And thou shalt set them in two rows, six on a row, upon the pure table before the LORD.
> Set them in two rows, six on a row, on the table before me.
7 And thou shalt put pure frankincense upon each row, that it may be on the bread for a memorial, even an offering made by fire unto the LORD.
> Put frankincense on each row as an offering of fire to me.
8 Every sabbath he shall set it in order before the LORD continually, being taken from the children of Israel by an everlasting covenant. 
> Every sabbath set it before me continually by an everlasting covenant.
9 And it shall be Aaron's and his sons'; and they shall eat it in the holy place: for it is most holy unto him of the offerings of the LORD made by fire by a perpetual statute.
> Aaron and his sons will eat it in the holy place.
> It is most holy.
God orders a blasphemer to be stoned to death
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/int.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/interp.gif) 24:10-16
10 And the son of an Israelitish woman, whose father was an Egyptian, went out among the children of Israel: and this son of the Israelitish woman and a man of Israel strove together in the camp;
There was a fight between an Israelite man and a man whose mother was an Israelite but whose father was an Egyptian.
11 And the Israelitish woman's son blasphemed the name of the Lord, and cursed. And they brought him unto Moses: (and his mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan:)
12 And they put him in ward, that the mind of the LORD might be shewed them.
During the fight, the son of the Egyptian man cursed and blasphemed the name of the Lord.
So they brought him to Moses to find out what to do about it.
13 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,
14 **Bring forth him that hath cursed** without the camp; and let all that heard him lay their hands upon his head, **and let all the congregation stone him.**
God said to Moses,
> Bring the man who cursed and let all the congregation stone him.
15 And thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel, saying, **Whosoever curseth his God shall bear his sin.**
> Say this to the Israelites: 
>> Whoever curses me will bear his sin.
16 And **he that blasphemeth the name of the LORD, he shall surely be put to death, and all the congregation shall certainly stone him** : as well the stranger, as he that is born in the land, when he blasphemeth the name of the Lord, shall be put to death.
> > He that blasphemes my name shall be killed.
>> All the congregation shall stone him. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/24.html#1n)]
Miscellaneous laws
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/good1.gif) 22:17-22
17 And he that killeth any man shall surely be put to death.
> > Whoever kills a man shall be killed.
18 And he that killeth a beast shall make it good; beast for beast.
> > Whoever kills a beast shall make it good(?); beast for beast. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/24.html#2n)]
19 And if a man cause a blemish in his neighbour; as he hath done, so shall it be done to him;
20 Breach for breach, eye for eye, tooth for tooth: as he hath caused a blemish in a man, so shall it be done to him again.
> > If a man causes a blemish in his neighbor, the same blemish will be made on him.
>> Eye for eye, tooth for tooth.
21 And he that killeth a beast, he shall restore it: and he that killeth a man, he shall be put to death.
> > He that kills a beast must restore it.
>> He that kills a man must be killed.
22 Ye shall have one manner of law, as well for the stranger, as for one of your own country: for I am the LORD your God.
> > The same laws apply to residents of a country and for aliens.
>> I am the Lord your God.
The blasphemer is stoned to death
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) 24:23
23 And Moses spake to the children of Israel, that they should bring forth him that had cursed out of the camp, and **stone him with stones**. And the children of Israel did as the LORD commanded Moses.
Moses said to the Israelites,
> Stone to death the man who cursed.
And the Israelites did as God commanded Moses (and stoned him to death).

[DWB: ](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html)[A blasphemer is stoned to death](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/lev.html#stoned)
[To kill or not to kill](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/kill.html)
[Does God approve of capital punishment?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/capital.html)
What the Bible says about [blasphemy](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/blasphemy.html), [stoning](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/stoning.html), and [capital punishment](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/capital.html)
  2. _He that killeth a beast shall make it good; beast for beast._ ([v.18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/24.html#18)) 
I don't know what this means.
How do you "make it good, beast for beast"?
Does it mean that if you kill someone's animal, one of your animals must be killed?
Or that you must replace the animal that was killed?
