# Bible Difficulties
by [CARM](https://carm.org/carm)
June 23, 2020 
1 min read 
Because the Bible was written over 1400 years, on three different continents, and in three different languages, it will have some cultural and contextual difficulties. Responding to this broad topic means we need to look at logic, evidence, history, and cultural context. You will find that upon closer examination, the Bible is consistent.
