## [Joshua](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/outline.html "Joshua Outline")
### CHAPTER 24
Joshua's final speech (continued)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/int.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/pol.gif) 24:1-28
1 And Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem, and called for the elders of Israel, and for their heads, and for their judges, and for their officers; and they presented themselves before God.
Joshua gathered all the Israelites (again) and said to them, [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#1n)]
2 And Joshua said unto all the people, Thus saith the LORD God of Israel, Your fathers dwelt on the other side of the flood in old time, even Terah, the father of Abraham, and the father of Nachor: and they served other gods.
> God has this to say to you. 
>> Terah and his sons, Abraham and Nachor, served other gods. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#2n)]
3 And I took your father Abraham from the other side of the flood, and led him throughout all the land of Canaan, and multiplied his seed, and gave him Isaac.
> > I took Abraham from the other side of the flood, led him through Canaan, multiplied his seed, and gave him Isaac. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#3n)]
4 And I gave unto Isaac Jacob and Esau: and I gave unto Esau mount Seir, to possess it; but Jacob and his children went down into Egypt.
> > I gave Isaac Jacob and Esau.
5 I sent Moses also and Aaron, and **I plagued Egypt** , according to that which I did among them: and afterward I brought you out.
> > I plagued Egypt. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#4n)]
6 And I brought your fathers out of Egypt: and ye came unto the sea; and the Egyptians pursued after your fathers with chariots and horsemen unto the Red sea.
> > I brought your fathers out of Egypt.
7 And when they cried unto **the LORD** , he put darkness between you and the Egyptians, and **brought the sea upon them** , and covered them; and your eyes have seen what I have done in Egypt: and ye dwelt in the wilderness a long season.
> > I drowned the Egyptians. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#5n)]
8 And I brought you into the land of the Amorites, which dwelt on the other side Jordan; and they fought with you: and **I gave them into your hand, that ye might possess their land; and I destroyed them from before you.**
> > I killed (and helped you kill) the Amorites. [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#6n)]
9 Then Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab, arose and warred against Israel, and sent and called Balaam the son of Beor to curse you:
> > Balak warred against Israel [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#7n)] and called Balaam to curse you. [[8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#8n)]
10 But I would not hearken unto Balaam; therefore he blessed you still: so I delivered you out of his hand.
> > But I wouldn't listen to Balaam. So he blessed you and I delivered you out of his hand.
11 And you went over Jordan, and came unto Jericho: and the men of Jericho fought against you, the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Girgashites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites; and **I delivered them into your hand.**
> > I helped you massacre Jericho, and kill the Amorites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hittites, Girgashites, Hivites, and the Jebusites. [[9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#9n)]
12 And **I sent the hornet before you** , which drave them out from before you, even the two kings of the Amorites; but not with thy sword, nor with thy bow.
> > I sent hornets in front of you to drive out the Amorites. [[10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#10n)] 
>> You didn't even have to use a sword or a bow.
13 And I have given you a land for which ye did not labour, and cities which ye built not, and ye dwell in them; of the vineyards and oliveyards which ye planted not do ye eat.
> > I gave you land that you didn't work for, cities you didn't build, and vineyards and olive trees that you didn't plant.
14 Now therefore fear the LORD, and serve him in sincerity and in truth: and put away the gods which your fathers served on the other side of the flood, and in Egypt; and serve ye the LORD.
> So fear and serve God, and reject the gods of your fathers. [[11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#11n)]
15 And if it seem evil unto you to serve the LORD, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the flood, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell: but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD.
> If it seems wrong to serve the Lord, choose whom you will serve [[12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#12n)], the gods of your fathers or the gods of the Amorites. 
> But I and my family will serve the Lord.
16 And the people answered and said, God forbid that we should forsake the LORD, to serve other gods;
All the people answered simultaneously, saying, 
> God forbid that we should reject the Lord to serve other gods.
17 For the LORD our God, he it is that brought us up and our fathers out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage, and which did those great signs in our sight, and preserved us in all the way wherein we went, and among all the people through whom we passed:
> Because he brought us out of Egypt, did all those great things in our sight, and protected us wherever we went.
18 And the LORD drave out from before us all the people, even the Amorites which dwelt in the land: therefore will we also serve the LORD; for he is our God.
> He drove out everyone, even the Amorites. 
> So we'll seve him as our God.
19 And Joshua said unto the people, Ye cannot serve the LORD: for he is an holy God; **he is a jealous God; he will not forgive your transgressions nor your sins.**
Joshua said to the people, 
> You can't serve the Lord, because he is a holy and jealous God. 
> He won't forgive you sins. [[13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#13n)]
20 **If ye forsake the LORD, and serve strange gods, then he will turn and do you hurt, and consume you** , after that he hath done you good.
> If you forsake the Lord, he'll turn on you, hurt you, and consume you, even though he was good to you before.
21 And the people said unto Joshua, Nay; but we will serve the LORD.
The people said to Joshua, 
> Okay, we'll serve the Lord.
22 And Joshua said unto the people, Ye are witnesses against yourselves that ye have chosen you the LORD, to serve him. And they said, We are witnesses.
Joshua said to the people, 
> You are witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen to serve the Lord.
The people said, 
> We are witnesses.
23 Now therefore put away, said he, the strange gods which are among you, and incline your heart unto the LORD God of Israel.
Joshua said to the people, 
> Okay then, put away your strange gods and turn your hearts to the God of Israel.
24 And the people said unto Joshua, The LORD our God will we serve, and his voice will we obey.
The people said to Joshua, 
> We'll serve the Lord and obey his voice.
25 So Joshua made a covenant with the people that day, and set them a statute and an ordinance in Shechem.
So Joshua made an agreement with the people and established laws in Shechem.
26 And Joshua wrote these words in the book of the law of God, and took a great stone, and set it up there under an oak, that was by the sanctuary of the LORD.
Joshua wrote these words in the book of the law of God, and took a big stone and set it under an oak, near the sanctuary of the Lord.
27 And Joshua said unto all the people, **Behold, this stone shall be a witness unto us** ; for it hath heard all the words of the LORD which he spake unto us: it shall be therefore a witness unto you, lest ye deny your God.
Joshua said to all the people, 
> This stone is a witness to us. [[14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#14n)] 
> It will be a witness to you, if you ever reject your God.
28 So Joshua let the people depart, every man unto his inheritance.
Then Joshua let the people go home.
Joshua dies
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/boring.63b5b35a30388.jpg) 24:29-33, Cf. [Jg 2:8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jg/2.html#dies)
29 And it came to pass after these things, that Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died, being an hundred and ten years old.
Joshua died at the age of 110.
30 And they buried him in the border of his inheritance in Timnathserah, which is in mount Ephraim, on the north side of the hill of Gaash.
They buried him north of the hill of Gaash.
31 And Israel served the LORD all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders that overlived Joshua, and which had known all the works of the LORD, that he had done for Israel.
Everyone in Israel served God while Joshua was alive.
32 And the bones of Joseph, which the children of Israel brought up out of Egypt, buried they in Shechem, in a parcel of ground which Jacob bought of the sons of Hamor the father of Shechem for an hundred pieces of silver: and it became the inheritance of the children of Joseph.
They also buried the bones of Joseph [[15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#15n)] in Shechem, in a parcel of land bought by Joseph from the father of Shechem for 100 pieces of silver. [[16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#16n)]
33 And Eleazar the son of Aaron died; and they buried him in a hill that pertained to Phinehas his son, which was given him in mount Ephraim.
Eleazar the son of Aaron also died. They buried him on a hill that belonged to his son Phinehas.

### Notes
  1. _Joshua said unto all the people...._ [v.2a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#2)
Joshua finishes his speech by recounting [all the people that God killed](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html) and warning them that God would do the same to them if they disobey him.
  2. _They served other gods._ [v.2b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#2)
[How many gods are there?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gods.html)
  3. _I took your father Abraham ... and gave him Isaac._ [v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#3)
God only cared about one of Abraham's sons. But [Abraham had at least seven.](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/abes_sons.html)
  4. _I plagued Egypt._ [v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#5)
[DWB](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html): [11. The Lord smote all the firstborn in the land of Egypt](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/ex.html#firstborn)
  5. _The LORD ... brought the sea upon them._ [v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#7)
[DWB](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html): [12. God drowned the Egyptian army](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/ex.html#drown)
  6. _I gave them [the Amorites] into your hand, that ye might possess their land; and I destroyed them from before you._ [v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#8)
[DWB](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html): [31. God hardens king Sihon's heart so that all his people can be killed](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/ex.html#hardens)
  7. _Balak ... warred against Israel._ [v.9a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#9)
[Did Balak fight with Israel?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/balak.html)
  8. _Balak ... called Balaam the son of Beor to curse you._ [v.9b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#9)
[Did Balaam curse the Israelites?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/balaam.html)
  9. _Jericho, ... the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Girgashites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites; and I delivered them into your hand._ [v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#11)
[Drunk With Blood](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html)  
- [33. The Jericho Massacre](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/jos.html#33)  
- [39. The genocide of twenty kingdoms](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/jos.html#39)
  10. _I sent the hornet before you._ [v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#12)
When did God send hornets?
He promised to do so in [Ex 23:28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/23.html#28) and [Dt 7:20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/7.html#20), but there is nothing in the Bible (outside of this verse) that says that he actually did.
  11. _Fear the Lord ... and put away the gods which your fathers served._ [v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#14)
[Should we fear God?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/feargod.html)
[How many gods are there?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gods.html)
  12. _Choose you this day whom ye will serve_ [v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#15)
Scott Pruit, President Trump's former Environmental Protection Agency Administrator, [quoted this verse](http://www.motherjones.com/environment/2017/10/scott-pruitt-is-using-the-bible-as-his-guide-for-reorganizing-epas-science-boards/) to justify preventing EPA scientists from serving as scientific advisors if they have previously obtained EPA funding for their research.
[Do humans have free will?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/free.html)
  13. _He will not forgive your transgressions nor your sins._ [v.19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#19)
[Does God forgive sins?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/forgive.html)
  14. _This stone shall be a witness unto us._ [v.27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#27)
Just before he died, Joshua wrote "these words" (which words?) in "the book of the law." Then he put a big stone under an oak tree, saying "this stone shall be a witness unto us."
Good idea.
  15. _The bones of Joseph_ [v.32a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#32)
[Jacob's favorite son](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/37.html#3).
  16. _A parcel of ground which Jacob bought of the sons of Hamor_ [v.32b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/24.html#32)
[Who bought the sepulcher?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jacob_bought.html)
