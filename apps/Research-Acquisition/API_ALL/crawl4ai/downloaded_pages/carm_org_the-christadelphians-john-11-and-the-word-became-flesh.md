# The Christadelphians, John 1:1, and “The Word Became Flesh”
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
January 12, 2002 
6 min read 
An examination of John 1 provides an important contrast between the Christadelphian Jesus and Jesus of Scripture.
The Bible bears witness of Jesus ([John 5:39](https://ref.ly/John%205.39;nasb95?t=biblia)). He is our Savior, our King, and Lord. But not all who call themselves Christian agree on who [ Jesus](https://carm.org/doctrine-and-theology/jesus-two-natures-god-and-man/) is. Some say He is God in flesh, others that He is an angel who became a man, and still others teach He only came into existence at His birth. The latter is the position of the Christadelphians. To them, Jesus did not exist as God. To them, He was just a man who first existed at His birth.
If you are a Christian who knows His Bible, then you will immediately recognize the error of the Christadelphians. The Bible says that whoever denies that Jesus has come in the flesh is of the spirit of the Antichrist ([1 John 4:1-2](https://ref.ly/1%20John%204.1-2;nasb95?t=biblia)). Of course, the Christadelphians agree that Jesus came in the flesh. But they will not agree that He is God in flesh.
