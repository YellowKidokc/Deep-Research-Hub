# What does the Bible say about motherhood?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
June 13, 2016 
7 min read 
The Bible has a great deal to say about motherhood. It holds mothers in high regard. In fact, there are even scriptures where God himself is compared to motherhood. But first, we need to understand that motherhood, like fatherhood, is an expression of God’s character and nature.
Gen: 1:27, “God created man in His own image, in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them.”
In this verse, we see that the full image of God revealed in both male and female genders. This expression is for several purposes. Let’s look at four of them, though this list is not exhaustive.
