### Notes
  1. _Hear, O Israel, the statutes and judgments which I speak in your ears this day._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#1)) 
The famous "Ten Commandments" are listed in this chapter (and in [Ex 20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/20.html#word)).
Catholics, Protestants, and Jews all agree there are ten (to match the number given in [Ex 34:28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/34.html#28); [Dt 4:13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/4.html#13), [10:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/10.html#4)), but disagree about their numbering.
The Bible never provides a numbered list.
  2. _The LORD talked with you face to face._ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#4)) 
[Can God be seen?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/seen.html)
[Did the Israelites see God face to face on Mount Horeb?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/horeb.html)
  3. _Thou shalt have none other gods before me._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#7)) 
The first commandment condemns those who worship any other than the biblical god.
[How many gods are there?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gods.html)
  4. _Thou shalt not make thee any graven image._ ([v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#8)) 
[Is it OK to make images?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/images.html)
  5. _I the LORD thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation._ ([v.9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#9)) 
[Are we punished for the sins of others?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/iniquity.html)
  6. __Keep the sabbath day.__ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#12)) 
[Is it necessary to keep the sabbath?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sabbath.html)
  7. _Honour thy father and thy mother_ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#16)) 
[How should parents be treated?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/parents.html)
[Is it OK to call your father (or anyone else) father?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/father.html)
  8. _Thou shalt not kill._ ([v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#17)) 
[To kill or not to kill](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/kill.html)
  9. _Neither shalt thou commit adultery._ ([v.18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#18)) 
[Is it wrong to commit adultery?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/adultery.html)
  10. _Neither shalt thou steal._ ([v.19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#19)) 
[Is it wrong to steal?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/steal.html)
  11. _Neither shalt thou bear false witness._ ([v.20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#20)) 
[Is it wrong to lie?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/lie.html)
  12. _Neither shalt thou desire thy neighbour's wife ... his ox, or his ass, or any thing that is thy neighbour's._ ([v.21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#21)) 
You see, in the eyes of God, women are the possessions of men.
_Neither shalt thou covet they neighbor's ... manservant, or his maidservant._ ([v.21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#21))
It's OK to have slaves. (But don't covet your neighbor's.)
[Is it OK to covet?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/covet.html)
[Does God approve of slavery?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/slavery.html)
  13. _The LORD spake ... in the mount out of the midst of the fire, of the cloud, and of the thick darkness, with a great voice._ ([v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/5.html#2)) 
Just like the Great and Powerful Wizard of Oz, God spoke with a great voice in the midst of fire, cloud, and thick darkness.
