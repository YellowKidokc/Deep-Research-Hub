# Was Jesus circumcised?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 02, 2009 
1 min read 
Yes, Jesus was circumcised because He was like any other Jewish male. Remember, Jesus was a Jew and under Old Testament law ([Gal. 4:4](https://ref.ly/Gal.%204.4;nasb95?t=biblia)). He was to be circumcised on the eighth day after His birth. “And every male among you who is eight days old shall be circumcised throughout your generations, a servant who is born in the house or who is bought with money from any foreigner, who is not of your descendants,” ([Gen. 17:12](https://ref.ly/Gen.%2017.12;nasb95?t=biblia)).
