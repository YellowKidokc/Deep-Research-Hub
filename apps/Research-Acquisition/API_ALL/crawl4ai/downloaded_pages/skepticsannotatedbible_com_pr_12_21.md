### CHAPTER 12
Lying lips 
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/good1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/wom1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 12:1-28 
1 Whoso loveth instruction loveth knowledge: but he that hateth reproof is brutish. 
2 A good man obtaineth favour of the LORD: but a man of wicked devices will he condemn. 
Whoever loves instruction loves knowledge. 
3 A man shall not be established by wickedness: but the root of the righteous shall not be moved.
The root of righteous people can't be moved. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#1n)] 
4 A virtuous woman is a crown to her husband: but she that maketh ashamed is as rottenness in his bones.
5 The thoughts of the righteous are right: but the counsels of the wicked are deceit. 
6 The words of the wicked are to lie in wait for blood: but the mouth of the upright shall deliver them. 
7 The wicked are overthrown, and are not: but the house of the righteous shall stand. 
8 A man shall be commended according to his wisdom: but he that is of a perverse heart shall be despised. 
9 He that is despised, and hath a servant, is better than he that honoureth himself, and lacketh bread. 
A virtuous woman is a crown to her husband, 
but a shameful wife is a rottenness in his bones. 
10 A righteous man regardeth the life of his beast: but the tender mercies of the wicked are cruel.
11 He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread: but he that followeth vain persons is void of understanding. 
12 The wicked desireth the net of evil men: but the root of the righteous yieldeth fruit. 
Righteous people are kind to animals, 
but wicked people are cruel. 
13 The wicked is snared by the transgression of his lips: but the just shall come out of trouble.
14 A man shall be satisfied with good by the fruit of his mouth: and the recompence of a man's hands shall be rendered unto him. 
Just people stay out of trouble. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#2n)] 
15 The way of a fool is right in his own eyes: but he that hearkeneth unto counsel is wise.
16 A fool's wrath is presently known: but a prudent man covereth shame. 
17 He that speaketh truth sheweth forth righteousness: but a false witness deceit. 
18 There is that speaketh like the piercings of a sword: but the tongue of the wise is health. 
Fools are right in their own eyes; 
The wise listen to counsel. 
19 The lip of truth shall be established for ever: but a lying tongue is but for a moment.
20 Deceit is in the heart of them that imagine evil: but to the counsellors of peace is joy. 
Truth lasts forever, but lies last only a moment. 
21 There shall no evil happen to the just: but the wicked shall be filled with mischief.
Bad things only happen to bad people. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#3n)] 
22 **Lying lips** are abomination to the LORD: but they that deal truly are his delight. 
23 A prudent man concealeth knowledge: but the heart of fools proclaimeth foolishness. 
24 The hand of the diligent shall bear rule: but the slothful shall be under tribute. 
Lying lips are an abomination to God. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#4n)] 
25 Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it stoop: but a good word maketh it glad.
26 The righteous is more excellent than his neighbour: but the way of the wicked seduceth them. 
27 The slothful man roasteth not that which he took in hunting: but the substance of a diligent man is precious. 
When people are sad, a kind word can cheer them up. 
28 In the way of righteousness is life: and in the pathway thereof there is no death.
Righteous people never die. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#5n)]

### Notes
  1. _The root of the righteous shall not be moved._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#3)) 
[Has there ever been a righteous person?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/righteous.html) (See also vv. [5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#5), [7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#7), [10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#10), [12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#12), [26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#26)) 
  2. _The just shall come out of trouble._ ([v.13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#13)) 
[Has there ever been a just person?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/just.html) (See aslo [v. 21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#21)) 
  3. _There shall no evil happen to the just._ ([v.21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#21)) 
[Do bad things happen to good people?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/shithappens.html)
[Has there ever been a just person?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/just.html)
  4. _Lying lips are an abomination to the Lord._ ([v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#22)) 
[Is it wrong to lie?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/lie.html)
  5. _The way of righteousness is life: and in the pathway thereof there is no death._ ([v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/pr/12.html#28)) 
They just smell that way.
