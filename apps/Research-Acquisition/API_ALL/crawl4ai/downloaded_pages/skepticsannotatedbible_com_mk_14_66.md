### Notes
  1. _There came a woman having an alabaster box of ointment of spikenard very precious; and she brake the box, and poured it on his head._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#3)) 
Ointment for Jesus's head is more important than helping the poor.
[Is it OK to use perfume?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/perfume.html)
  2. _The first day of unleavened bread, When they killed the passover, his disciples said unto him, Where wilt thou that we go and prepare that thou mayest eat the passover?_ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#12)) 
[Was Jesus crucified the day before or the day after the Passover meal?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/passover_meal.html)
  3. _Take, eat: this is my body ... This is my blood._ ([vv.22-24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#22)) 
Jesus tells his disciples to eat his body and drink his blood.
  4. _It is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered._ ([v.27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#27)) 
Jesus seems to be (mis)quoting Zechariah here. The actual passage reads:
> Awake, O sword, against my shepherd, and against the man that is my fellow, saith the LORD of hosts: smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered: and I will turn mine hand upon the little ones. [Zechariah 13:7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/zech/13.html#7)
If so and Jesus is the shepherd, then God is going to kill Jesus and then kill his flock with a sword.
  5. _After that I am risen, I will go before you into Galilee._ ([v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#28)) 
[Did Jesus forewarn the apostles about his death and resurrection?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/comingdeath.html)
  6. _Before the cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice._ ([v.30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#30)) 
[Did Jesus say before the cock crow or before the cock crow twice?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/crow.html)
  7. _Take away this cup from me._ ([v.36](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#36)) 
[Did Jesus ask God to save him from crucifixion?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/crucifixion.html)
  8. _Whomsoever I shall kiss, that same is he._ ([v.44](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#44)) 
[Did Judas identify Jesus with a kiss?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/judas_kiss.html)
  9. _And he left the linen cloth, and fled from them naked._ ([v.52](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#52)) 
One of the followers of Jesus was a young, nearly naked man who dropped his linen cloth and "fled from them naked" when the priests came to arrest Jesus.
[What the Bible says about nudism](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/nudism.html)
[Strange Flesh](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/SF/intro.html): [The mysterious naked follower of Jesus](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/SF/jesus.html#naked)
  10. _They led Jesus away to the high priest._ ([v.53](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#53)) 
[Was Jesus taken to Caiaphas or Annas first?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/caiaphas.html)
  11. _We heard him say, I will destroy this temple that is made with hands, and within three days I will build another made without hands._ ([v.58](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#58)) 
Mark says that those who quoted Jesus were "false witnesses," yet according to [John 2:19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/2.html#19), Jesus said what these witnesses said that he said. So how are they false witnesses?
[Did Jesus say, "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up"?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/temple.html)
  12. _I am._ ([v.62](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#62)) 
Jesus, when asked by the high priest if he is the Christ, answers "I am" in this gospel. But in Matthew ([26:64](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/26.html#64)) he equivocates by saying, "Thou hast said it." In both gospels he falsely prophesies that the high priest would see his second coming.
[How did Jesus respond when questioned by the high priest?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/christ.html)
[What the Bible says about the end of the world](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/end.html)
  13. _As Peter was beneath in the palace, there cometh one of the maids of the high priest._ ([v.66](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#66)) 
[To whom did Peter deny knowing Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/deny.html)
  14. _And the cock crew._ ([v.68](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#68)) 
[Did the cock crow before or after Peter's denial?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/cock.html)
