## 💡 **How to Use These Searches**

### **1. Google Scholar** (scholar.google.com)

- Use the exact phrases in quotes
- Add "filetype:pdf" to get full papers
- Use date range filters for recent research

### **2. Academic Databases**

- **JSTOR** - jstor.org
- **Academia.edu** - academia.edu
- **ResearchGate** - researchgate.net
- **PhilPapers** - philpapers.org (philosophy of religion)

### **3. University Repositories**

- Add "site:edu" to any search
- Look for thesis/dissertation repositories
- Check seminary libraries (Princeton, Yale, Oxford, Cambridge)

### **4. Specific Journals**

- **Journal of the Evangelical Theological Society**
- **Philosophia Christi**
- **Faith and Philosophy**
- **Religious Studies**
- **Biblica**
