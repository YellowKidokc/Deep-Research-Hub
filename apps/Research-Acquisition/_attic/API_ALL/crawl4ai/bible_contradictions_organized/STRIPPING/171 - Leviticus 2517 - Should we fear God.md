## Should we fear God?

### No.
> God has given believers the spirit of love, not of fear. 
>> For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind. [2 Timothy 1:7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2tim/1.html#7)
> Not as fearful slaves, but as the children of a loving God. 
>> For ye have not received the spirit of bondage again to fear; but ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father. [Romans 8:15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/rom/8.html#15)
> God is love, and there is no fear in love. 
>> God is Love. [1 John 4:8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1jn/4.html#8)
>> There is no fear in love; but perfect love casteth out fear: because fear hath torment. He that feareth is not made perfect in love. [1 John 4:18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1jn/4.html#18)

The word 'fear' is manipulated to help give God a better image. When asked about whether people should really be 'fearing' God, a pastor on a particular Christian television show said that the word 'fear' should be interpreted as "intense respect" (for God). So, by his definition, a "god-fearing" person simply has utmost respect for God. This seems to be in contradiction with the Book of John, which uses 'fear' in the traditional sense of the word.
