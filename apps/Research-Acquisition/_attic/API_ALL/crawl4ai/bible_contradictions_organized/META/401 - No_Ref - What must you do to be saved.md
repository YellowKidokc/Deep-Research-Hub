## [1 Corinthians](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/outline.html "1 Corinthians Outline")
### CHAPTER 7
On marriage (it's better than burning)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/wom1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/interp.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/good1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sex1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/fv.gif) 7:1-9
1 Now concerning the things whereof ye wrote unto me: **It is good for a man not to touch a woman.**
2 Nevertheless, to avoid fornication, let every man have his own wife, and let every woman have her own husband.
It's good for a man not to touch a woman. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#1n)]
But to avoid fornication,
let every man have a wife
and let every woman have a husband.
3 Let the husband render unto the wife due benevolence: and likewise also the wife unto the husband.
Husbands and wives should be kind to each other.
4 The wife hath not power of her own body, but the husband: and likewise also the husband hath not power of his own body, but the wife.
The husband has power over his wife's body,
and the wife has power over her husband's. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#2n)]
5 Defraud ye not one the other, except it be with consent for a time, that ye may give yourselves to fasting and prayer; and come together again, that Satan tempt you not for your incontinency.
6 But I speak this by permission, and not of commandment.
To avoid being tempted by Satan,
don't deprive your spouse of sex.
7 For **I would that all men were even as I myself.** But every man hath his proper gift of God, one after this manner, and another after that.
I'd prefer that everyone be like me and not marry. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#3n)]
8 I say therefore to the unmarried and widows, It is good for them if they abide even as I.
9 But if they cannot contain, let them marry: for **it is better to marry than to burn**.
If you are a widow or are unmarried, don't get married.
Unless you can't control yourself, that is.
It's better to marry than to burn.
On divorcing an unbelieving spouse
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/interp.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/fv.gif) 7:10-16
10 And unto the married I command, yet not I, but the Lord, Let not the wife depart from her husband:
11 But and if she depart, let her remain unmarried or be reconciled to her husband: and let not the husband put away his wife.
But if you're married, stay married.
12 **But to the rest speak I, not the Lord** : If any brother hath a wife that believeth not, and she be pleased to dwell with him, let him not put her away.
13 And the woman which hath an husband that believeth not, and if he be pleased to dwell with her, let her not leave him.
I'm speaking for myself, not for the Lord. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#4n)]
If you have an unbelieving spouse,
who is willing to stay with you, don't divorce. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#5n)]
14 For the unbelieving husband is sanctified by the wife, and the unbelieving wife is sanctified by the husband: else were your children unclean; but now are they holy.
The unbelieving spouse is made holy by the believing one. [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#6n)]
Otherwise, your children would be unclean,
but now they're holy. [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#7n)]
15 But if the unbelieving depart, let him depart. A brother or a sister is not under bondage in such cases: but God hath called us to peace.
But if an unbelieving spouse leaves, let them leave.
It's okay to divorce them. [[8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#8n)]
16 For what knowest thou, O wife, whether thou shalt save thy husband? or how knowest thou, O man, whether thou shalt save thy wife?
Who knows? Maybe a wife can save her husband.
Or a man can save his wife.
On circumcision (don't get uncircumcised)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 7:17-20
17 But as God hath distributed to every man, as the Lord hath called every one, so let him walk. And so ordain I in all churches.
Stay the way you were when you became a Christian.
18 **Is any man called being circumcised? let him not become uncircumcised.** is any called in uncircumcision? let him not be circumcised.
If you were circumcised, don't get uncircumcised. [[9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#9n)]
If you were uncircumcised, don't get circumcised. [[10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#10n)]
19 Circumcision is nothing, and uncircumcision is nothing, but the keeping of the commandments of God.
20 Let every man abide in the same calling wherein he was called.
Circumcision is nothing. It just doesn't matter.
On slavery (If you are a slave, stay a slave)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 7:21-24
21 **Art thou called being a servant? care not for it** : but if thou mayest be made free, use it rather.
22 For he that is called in the Lord, being a servant, is the Lord's freeman: likewise also he that is called, being free, is Christ's servant.
If you were you a slave when you became a Christian,
stay a slave. [[10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#10n)]
23 Ye are bought with a price; be not ye the servants of men.
Don't be a slave of men.
24 Brethren, let every man, wherein he is called, therein abide with God.
Whatever you were when you became a Christian,
that's what you should be.
On virgins, marriage, and the end of the world  
(The end is coming. There's no time for sex)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/fv.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/pR1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif)7:25-40
25 Now concerning virgins I have no commandment of the Lord: yet I give my judgment, as one that hath obtained mercy of the Lord to be faithful.
I have no commandment from God about virgins, [[12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#12n)]
but here's what I think about them.
26 I suppose therefore that this is good for the present distress, I say, that it is good for a man so to be.
It's good for a man to be a virgin.
27 Art thou bound unto a wife? seek not to be loosed. **Art thou loosed from a wife? seek not a wife.**
If you're married, stay married.
If you're unmarried, don't get married. [[13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#13n)]
28 But and if thou marry, thou hast not sinned; and if a virgin marry, she hath not sinned. Nevertheless such shall have trouble in the flesh: but I spare you.
It's not a sin to get married.
If a virgin gets married, she hasn't sinned.
You'll have trouble in the flesh. But I'll spare you the details.
29 But this I say, brethren, **the time is short: it remaineth, that both they that have wives be as though they had none** ;
30 And they that weep, as though they wept not; and they that rejoice, as though they rejoiced not; and they that buy, as though they possessed not;
31 And they that use this world, as not abusing it: for the fashion of this world passeth away.
But the end is near, so there's no time for sex. [[14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#14n)]
So if you have a wife, don't have sex with her.
32 But I would have you without carefulness. He that is unmarried careth for the things that belong to the Lord, how he may please the Lord:
An unmarried man cares about God and God's things.
33 But he that is married careth for the things that are of the world, how he may please his wife.
A married man cares for the things of this world, and for his wife.
34 There is difference also between a wife and a virgin. The unmarried woman careth for the things of the Lord, that she may be holy both in body and in spirit: but she that is married careth for the things of the world, how she may please her husband.
35 And this I speak for your own profit; not that I may cast a snare upon you, but for that which is comely, and that ye may attend upon the Lord without distraction.
36 But if any man think that he behaveth himself uncomely toward his virgin, if she pass the flower of her age, and need so require, let him do what he will, he sinneth not: let them marry.
37 Nevertheless he that standeth stedfast in his heart, having no necessity, but hath power over his own will, and hath so decreed in his heart that he will keep his virgin, doeth well.
The same is true for women.
38 So then he that giveth her in marriage doeth well; but he that giveth her not in marriage doeth better.
39 The wife is bound by the law as long as her husband liveth; but if her husband be dead, she is at liberty to be married to whom she will; only in the Lord.
40 But she is happier if she so abide, after my judgment: and I think also that I have the Spirit of God.
So it's okay to get married, but it's better not to.

### Notes
  1. _It is good for a man not to touch a woman._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#1)) 
Paul would prefer that no one marry. But he says "to avoid fornication, let every man have his own wife."
[Is marriage a good thing?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/marriage.html)
[Is it wrong to have sex outside of marriage?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/adultery.html)
[Is polygamy OK?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/polygamy.html)
[What the Bible says about marriage](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/marriage.html)
  2. _The wife hath not power of her own body, but the husband: and likewise also the husband hath not power of his own body, but the wife._ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#4)) 
Married people should have sex whenever and however their spouse wants.
[What the Bible says about Oral Sex](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/oral-sex.html)
  3. _I would that all men were even as I myself._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#7)) 
Paul would prefer that all men and woman would be celibate and abstain from sex completely. But those who can't control themselves should get married, for "it is better to marry than to burn."
[Is marriage a good thing?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/marriage.html)
[What the Bible says about marriage](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/marriage.html)
  4. _But to the rest speak I, not the Lord._ ([v.12a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#12)) 
Really? So this stuff is in scripture but is not inspired by God?
[Was all scripture inspired by God?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/scripture.html)
  5. _If any brother hath a wife that believeth not, and she be pleased to dwell with him, let him not put her away._ ([v.12b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#12)) 
The Pauline Privilege: A believer may either keep or divorce an unbelieving spouse.
[Is it OK to marry unbelievers?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/unclean.html)
  6. _The unbelieving husband is sanctified by the wife, and the unbelieving wife is sanctified by the husband._ ([v.14a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#14)) 
You can get saved by marrying a believer!
[If a husband believes, is his wife saved also?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/husband.html)
  7. _Else were your children unclean; but now are they holy._ ([v.14b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#14)) 
The children of nonbelievers are unclean. (But believers' kids are holy.)
[Is only God holy?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/holy.html)
  8. _But if the unbelieving depart, let him depart. A brother or a sister is not under bondage in such cases._ ([v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#15)) 
[Is divorce ever permissible?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/divorce.html)
  9. _Is any man called being circumcised? let him not become uncircumcised._ ([v.18a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#18)) 
Good advice. It's kind of hard to undo, isn't it?
  10. _Is any called in uncircumcision? let him not be circumcised._ ([v.18b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#18)) 
[Is circumcision required?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/circum.html)
  11. _Art thou called being a servant? care not for it ... be not ye the servants of men._ ([vv.21-23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#21)) 
Slaves ("servants" in the KJV) should not desire their freedom. Yet they should not be the slaves of men.
[Does God approve of slavery?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/slavery.html)
  12. _Now concerning virgins I have no commandment of the Lord: yet I give my judgment._ ([v.25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#25)) 
So this is just Paul's opinion and is not divinely inspired scripture?
[Was all scripture inspired by God?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/scripture.html)
  13. _Art thou loosed from a wife? seek not a wife._ ([v.27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#27)) 
Those who are unmarried should never marry.
[What the Bible says about marriage](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/marriage.html)
[Is marriage a good thing?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/marriage.html)
  14. _The time is short: it remaineth, that both they that have wives be as though they had none._ ([v.29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1cor/7.html#29)) 
Paul, like Jesus and the other New Testament writers, expects the end to come soon. "The time is short." So there's no time for sex and marriage since the world will be ending soon.
