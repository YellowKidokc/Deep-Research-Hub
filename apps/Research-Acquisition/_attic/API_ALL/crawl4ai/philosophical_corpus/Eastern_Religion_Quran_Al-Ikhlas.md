# Sūra CXII.: I _kh_ lāṣ, or Purity (of Faith).
### Section 1
![](https://sacred-texts.com/isl/gif/112/112_001.gif)
1. Qul huwa All _a_ hu a _h_ ad**un**
1. Say: He is God,  
The One and Only;
* * *
![](https://sacred-texts.com/isl/gif/112/112_002.gif)
2. All _a_ hu a**l** _ss_ amad**u**
2. God, the Eternal, Absolute;
* * *
![](https://sacred-texts.com/isl/gif/112/112_003.gif)
3. Lam yalid walam yoolad**u**
3. He begetteth not,  
Nor is He begotten;
* * *
![](https://sacred-texts.com/isl/gif/112/112_004.gif)
4. Walam yakun lahu kufuwan a _h_ ad**un**
4. And there is none  
Like unto Him.
