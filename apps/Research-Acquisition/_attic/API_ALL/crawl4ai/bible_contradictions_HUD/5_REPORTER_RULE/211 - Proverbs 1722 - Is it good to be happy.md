### Notes
  1. _And he said, Men, brethren, and fathers, hearken_ ([v.2a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#2)) 
Stephen, filled with the Holy Ghost, delivers a long, boring, anti-Semitic speech in a synagogue.
  2. _The God of glory appeared unto our father Abraham, when he was in Mesopotamia, before he dwelt in Haran._ ([v.2b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#2)) 
[Did God call Abraham before or after he moved to Haran?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/haran.html)
  3. _When his father was dead_ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#4)) 
Abraham's father, Terah, was 70 when Abraham was born ([Gen 11:26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#26)), died at 205 years of age ([11:32](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#32)). According to this verse, Abraham didn't leave home until after Terah died. If so, then Abraham must have been at least 135 years old when he left Haran. Yet according to [12:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/12.html#4), Abraham was 75 years old when he left home.
[How old was Abraham when he left Haran?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/oldabe.html)
[How old was Abraham when Ishmael was born?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/abram_ishmael.html)
[How old was Terah when he died?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/terah.html)
  4. _Four hundred years_ ([v.6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#6)) 
[How long was the Egyptian captivity?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/captivity.html)
  5. _Jacob ... and all his kindred, threescore and fifteen souls._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#14)) 
[How many were in Jacob's family when they came into Egypt?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jacobs_clan.html)
  6. _Jacob went down into Egypt, and died._ ([v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#15)) 
[Where was Jacob buried?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jacob_buried.html)
  7. _And were carried over into Shechem, and laid in the sepulchre that Abraham bought_ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#16)) 
[Who bought the sepulchre?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jacob_bought.html)
  8. _Moses was ... mighty in words._ ([v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#22)) 
[Was Moses a good speaker?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/moses_speaker.html)
  9. _The angel which appeared to him in the bush_ ([v.35](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#35)) 
[Who appeared to Moses in the burning bush?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/bush.html)
  10. _The most High dwelleth not in temples._ ([v.48](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#48)) 
[Does God dwell in temples?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/temples.html)
  11. _The Just One; of whom ye have been now the betrayers and murderers_ ([v.52](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#52)) 
Stephen blames the Jews for persecuting the prophets and murdering Jesus.
  12. _Lord Jesus, receive my spirit._ ([v.59](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#59)) 
Jehovah's Witnesses: Although in [verse 59](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/7.html#59), Stephen was clearly praying to Jesus, the NWT tries to give the (false) impression that he was really praying to Jehovah by translating "Lord" (kyrios) as "Jehovah". [Then, bending his knees, he cried out with a strong voice: "Jehovah, do not charge this sin against them." NWT]
