# Does 2 Peter 2:1 teach that we can lose our salvation?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
May 18, 2014 
7 min read 
If Christians cannot lose their salvation, then why does it say there were false teachers who denied the Lord who bought them? Is eternal security true in light of this? Doesn’t that imply that they lost their salvation since they were bought with the blood of Christ? Let’s take a look.
“But false prophets also arose among the people, just as there will also be false teachers among you, who will secretly introduce destructive heresies, even denying the Master who bought them, bringing swift destruction upon themselves,” ([2 Peter 2:1](https://ref.ly/2%20Pet%202.1;nasb95?t=biblia)).
They are called false teachers. It is difficult to maintain the position that the false teachers are also true Christians or _were_ true Christians, who would then lose their salvation by denying Jesus. It makes more sense to say that these false teachers appeared as Christians but were not really saved, to begin with. But if that is the case, then how could they had been bought by the Master?
