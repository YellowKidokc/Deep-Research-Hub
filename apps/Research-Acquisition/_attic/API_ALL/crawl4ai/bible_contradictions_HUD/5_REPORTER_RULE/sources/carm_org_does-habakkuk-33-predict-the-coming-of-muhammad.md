# Does Habakkuk 3:3 predict the coming of Muhammad?
by [Ryan Turner](https://carm.org/ryan-turner)
January 06, 2010 
1 min read 
[Habakkuk 3:3](https://ref.ly/Hab%203.3;nasb95?t=biblia) does not predict the coming of Muhammad.
“God comes from Teman, and the Holy One from Mount Paran Selah His splendor covers the heavens, and the earth is full of His praise,” ([Habakkuk 3:3](https://ref.ly/Hab%203.3;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Some Muslims argue that this passage refers to Muhammad coming from Paran (Arabia) in light of [Deuteronomy 33:2](https://ref.ly/Deut%2033.2;nasb95?t=biblia). (See: [Does Deuteronomy 33:2 predict the coming of Muhammad?](https://carm.org/islam/does-deuteronomy-332-predict-the-coming-of-muhammad/))
