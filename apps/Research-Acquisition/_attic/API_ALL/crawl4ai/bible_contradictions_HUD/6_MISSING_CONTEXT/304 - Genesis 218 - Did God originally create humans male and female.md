## Did God originally create humans male and female?
* * *
### Yes.
> He ... made them at the beginning made them male and female. [Matthew 19:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/19.html#4)
> God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them. [Genesis 1:27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#27)
### No, God created a man first, then animals, and then a woman.
> And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground. [Genesis 2:7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/2.html#7)
> And the LORD God said, It is not good that the man should be alone; I will make him an help meet for him. And out of the ground the LORD God formed every beast of the field, and every fowl of the air; and brought them unto Adam ... but for Adam there was not found an help meet for him. [Genesis 2:18-20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/2.html#18)
> And the LORD God caused a deep sleep to fall upon Adam, and he slept: and he took one of his ribs, and closed up the flesh instead thereof; And the rib, which the LORD God had taken from man, made he a woman, and brought her unto the man. [Genesis 2:21-22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/2.html#21)
