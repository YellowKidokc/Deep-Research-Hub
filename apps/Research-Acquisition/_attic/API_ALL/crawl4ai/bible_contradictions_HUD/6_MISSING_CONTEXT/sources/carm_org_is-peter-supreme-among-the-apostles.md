# Is Peter supreme among the Apostles?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
March 24, 2010 
7 min read 
The Roman Catholic Church considers Peter to be supreme among Christ’s apostles. This position is known as the Primacy of Simon Peter. It says that Peter is the Vicar of Christ upon whom the Church is built ([Matt. 16:18](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2016.18;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Is the Catholic Church’s assertion about Peter true? If it is, then we should see such supremacy in the person of Peter as revealed in the New Testament–the place where spiritual truth must be verified. Unfortunately, we find no such supremacy of Peter in the Scriptures. Let’s examine what the New Testament says in reference to Peter.
**[Matt. 16:18-19](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2016.18-19;nasb95?t=biblia)** , “And I also say to you that you are Peter, and upon this rock I will build My church; and the gates of Hades shall not overpower it. 19 “I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven; and whatever you shall bind on earth shall be bound in heaven, and whatever you shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.”
