# What does the Eastern Orthodox Church say about the eucharist?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 02, 2025 
8 min read 
The Eastern Orthodox Church promotes the Eucharist as the actual body and blood of Jesus. It is not just symbolic. It holds to transubstantiation, where the elements are changed in the divine liturgy by the work of the Holy Spirit through the EO priest. To them it is propitiatory, helps the person be saved, is where Christ dwells in you, restores spiritual health, etc. It is said to unite the EO followers with Christ and each other. In the ceremony, people receive forgiveness and salvation. It is often preceded by fasting, prayer, and confession of sins.
