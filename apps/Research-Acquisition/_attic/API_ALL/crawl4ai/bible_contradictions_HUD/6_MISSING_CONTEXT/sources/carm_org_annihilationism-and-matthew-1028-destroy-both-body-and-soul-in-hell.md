# Annihilationism and Matthew 10:28, destroy both body and soul in hell
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 11, 2018 
10 min read 
There are two points worth examining. First, notice what the first part of the verse says. “Do not fear those who kill the body, but are unable to kill the soul.” Jesus said don’t fear those who can kill the body but NOT the soul. If the soul and the body are related such that when the body dies, the soul ceases to exist – as some annihilationists affirm – then why does Jesus say that those who can kill the body cannot kill the soul? The body and soul must be different.
Matthew 10:28, “Do not fear those who kill the body, but are unable to kill the soul; but rather fear Him who is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.” 
Second, the conditionalist assumes that the word “destroy” here means nonexistence. But the verse does not say that. Strictly speaking, we have two possibilities for what the word means in its context. One is nonexistence, and the other is continued, conscious punishment. Both are possible since the text does not explicitly specify either one.
