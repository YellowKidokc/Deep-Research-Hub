# What does it mean to be saved by grace?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 26, 2013 
3 min read 
To be saved by grace means to be delivered from the righteous judgment of [God](https://carm.org/dictionary/god/) through no act of your own but by His unmerited favor. God gave us the [law](https://carm.org/dictionary/the-law/) ([Exodus 20:1-17](https://ref.ly/Exod%2020.1-17;nasb95?t=biblia)). The law is comprised of what we should and should not do regarding loving God and loving man ([Matthew 22:37-40](https://ref.ly/Matt%2022.37-40;nasb95?t=biblia)). However, to break the law of God is to sin ([1 John 3:4](https://ref.ly/1%20John%203.4;nasb95?t=biblia)). Unfortunately, we have all sinned against God ([Romans 3:23](https://ref.ly/Rom%203.23;nasb95?t=biblia)), so we all deserve to be judged by God according to the Law. The Bible tells us that the wages of sin is death ([Romans 6:23](https://ref.ly/Rom%206.23;nasb95?t=biblia)) and separation from him ([Isaiah 59:2](https://ref.ly/Isa%2059.2;nasb95?t=biblia)). So, we are all under the righteous judgment of God. Because we are sinners, we are by nature children of wrath ([Ephesians 2:3](https://ref.ly/Eph%202.3;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Grace, on the other hand, is unmerited favor. Let me illustrate with three scenarios.
Grace is getting what we do not deserve from God. We do not deserve forgiveness of our sins, but he gives it to us through Jesus. We do not deserve to go to heaven, but we are able to do that through Jesus.
