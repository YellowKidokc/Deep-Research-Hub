# 2 Peter 3:17-18, Growing in Grace
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 01, 2008 
8 min read 
A sermon on 2 Peter 3:17-18 about a warning to be wary of godless people and how we are, instead, to grow in the grace of Jesus Christ. ￼ ![Truths about Jesus](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/100-truths-about-jesus-300x110.png)
“Therefore, dear friends, since you already know this, be on your guard so that you may not be carried away by the error of lawless men and fall from your secure position. 18 But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To him be glory both now and forever! Amen,” (2 Pet. 3:17-18).
God wants you to grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior. But how? How do you who are in Christ grow in a proper and healthy manner? Like a vine growing on a wall, without a solid root and good light, it will die. You too, without a solid root in Christ and without His light of the Word, would shrivel up spiritually.
