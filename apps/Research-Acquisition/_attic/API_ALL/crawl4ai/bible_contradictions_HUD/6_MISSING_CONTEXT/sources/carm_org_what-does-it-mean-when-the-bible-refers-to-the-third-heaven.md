# What does it mean when the Bible refers to the “third heaven”?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 22, 2008 
3 min read 
At the time of ancient Israel, they did not have as complete an understanding of the universe as we do today. So they wrote in terms with which they were familiar. The Jews spoke of three heavens. The first heaven consisted of the atmosphere where the clouds and birds were. The second heaven was where the sun, stars, and moon was. The third heaven was the dwelling place of God. When Paul said he was caught up to the third heaven (2 Cor. 12:2), he was referring to the very dwelling place of God. ![the third heaven](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/heaven-clouds.jpg)
As a note, the Mormons erringly teach that the three heavens consist of telestial, terrestrial, and celestial. They divide them into compartments dwelt by people after they die.
