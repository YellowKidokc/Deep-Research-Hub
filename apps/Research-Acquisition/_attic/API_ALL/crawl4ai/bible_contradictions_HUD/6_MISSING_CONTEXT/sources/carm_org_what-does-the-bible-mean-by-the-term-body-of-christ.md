# What does the Bible mean by the term body of Christ?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
February 20, 2018 
5 min read 
The term “body of Christ” occurs only in three verses in the Bible: [Rom. 7:4](https://ref.ly/Rom.%207.4;nasb95?t=biblia), [1 Cor. 10:16](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2010.16;nasb95?t=biblia), and [Eph. 4:12](https://ref.ly/Eph.%204.12;nasb95?t=biblia). It is used in three different senses. The first refers to the literal physical body of Jesus ([Rom. 7:4](https://ref.ly/Rom.%207.4;nasb95?t=biblia)). The second concerns the Last Supper ([1 Cor. 10:16](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2010.16;nasb95?t=biblia)). The third concerns the Church ([Eph. 4:12](https://ref.ly/Eph.%204.12;nasb95?t=biblia)).[![term body of Christ](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/Jesus-19.jpg)](https://carm.org/evidence-and-answers/it-is-improbable-that-jesus-rose-from-the-dead/attachment/jesus-19/)
[Romans 7:4](https://ref.ly/Rom%207.4;nasb95?t=biblia) specifically deals with the issue of the physical body of Christ because it speaks of him being raised from the dead. He prophesied His own resurrection in [John 2:19-21](https://ref.ly/John%202.19-21;nasb95?t=biblia) and accomplished it as is demonstrated by [John 20:25-28](https://ref.ly/John%2020.25-28;nasb95?t=biblia).
[1 Cor. 16:16](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2016.16;nasb95?t=biblia) refers to the communion supper that was instituted by Jesus in [Matthew 26:26](https://ref.ly/Matt%2026.26;nasb95?t=biblia), where he said, “Take, eat; this is my body.” Of course, it was not his literal body that he was asking the disciples to eat because he would have been asking them to violate Levitical law about not eating blood ([Lev. 17:14](https://ref.ly/Lev.%2017.14;nasb95?t=biblia)). Jesus still referred to the wine as wine ([Matt. 26:26-29](https://ref.ly/Matt.%2026.26-29;nasb95?t=biblia)).
