# Why did God not clearly reveal the Trinity in the Old Testament?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
February 02, 2022 
5 min read 
Why did God wait so long to reveal the doctrine of the [Trinity](https://carm.org/cut-and-paste/cut-trinity/)? Why is the Trinity hidden in the Old Testament? We can’t know precisely why God chose to do things the way He did regarding His self-revelation because He didn’t specifically tell us, but we can speculate. So, here goes. ![Trinity and divine simplicity](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/trinitygraphic.jpg)
God chose not to slowly reveal Himself as a Trinity because that was simply how He decided to do things. God has a reason for everything, and He works all things after the counsel of His will ([Ephesians 1:11](https://ref.ly/Eph%201.11;nasb95?t=biblia)). This answer should be sufficient in itself, but it isn’t very satisfying. So let me continue.
[Isaiah 28:13](https://ref.ly/Isa%2028.13;nasb95?t=biblia), “So the word of the LORD to them will be, “Order on order, order on order, Line on line, line on line, A little here, a little there,” That they may go and stumble backward, be broken, snared and taken captive.”
