# Was Mark 11:26 removed from modern Bibles?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
October 31, 2018 
8 min read 
Proponents of King James Onlyism attack modern translations for “deleting” or “removing” Mark 11:26. Modern translators, however, argue that they have not _deleted_ the verse at all. On the contrary, the verse was _added_ by later scribes. It was not originally a part of what Mark wrote. If we are to meaningfully settle the matter, we cannot simply side with our favorite translation. We must go to the manuscripts and examine the evidence.
The verse under discussion here traditionally reads in older English translations:
“But if ye do not forgive, neither will your Father which is in heaven forgive your trespasses,” (Mark 11:26, KJV).
