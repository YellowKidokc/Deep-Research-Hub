# Do Ancient Coptic manuscripts vindicate Jehovah’s Witnesses at John 1:1?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
August 21, 2019 
13 min read 
One of the most commonly discussed (and easily refuted) changes that Jehovah’s Witnesses made to the Bible when they produced their New World Translation (NWT) is their rendering of [John 1:1](https://ref.ly/John%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia). While virtually every reputable English translator from any religious background and in any era has rendered the verse:
“In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God,” ([John 1:1](https://ref.ly/John%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia)).
The NWT changes the phrase “the Word was God” to “the word was a god.” Though the Jehovah’s Witnesses literature loves to publish baseless, self-congratulatory statements like “Greek grammar and the context strongly indicate that the New World Translation rendering is correct,” _(https://www.jw.org/en/publications/magazines/wp20081101/was-the-word-god, (accessed 8/20/2019))_ this is simply untrue. The fact is that the [grammar and context](https://carm.org/uncategorized/the-word-and-the-watchtower-an-exegesis-of-john-11/) of the passage itself as well as the [biblical and cultural background](https://carm.org/uncategorized/why-is-jesus-called-the-word/) both plainly affirm the traditional rendering that “the Word was God.” Indeed, even a [ close reading of the NWT](https://carm.org/uncategorized/using-the-new-world-translation-to-refute-the-new-world-translation-john-11/) itself proves this. At best, the NWT’s rendering represents the kind of simplistic error a student might make in first-year Greek before they have learned any of the rules of grammar and syntax. At worst, it is a willful alteration of the text to prop up their denial of the deity of Christ. Either way, the NWT is simply incorrect here.
