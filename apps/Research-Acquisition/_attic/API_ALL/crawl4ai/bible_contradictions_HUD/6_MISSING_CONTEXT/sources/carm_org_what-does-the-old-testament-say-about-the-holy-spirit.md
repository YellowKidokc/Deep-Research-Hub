# What does the Old Testament say about the Holy Spirit?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
May 13, 2024 
53 min read 
The Holy Spirit is throughout the Old Testament. He is referred to by various titles and performs various actions. He is called His Holy Spirit, The Spirit of God, the Spirit of the Lord, etc. He abides among people, fills people, indwells people, moves people, etc. ![Old Testament Holy Spirit](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/hs.jpg) In the first table, there are two columns dealing with the titles of the Holy Spirit, and the second column deals with His actions and His works. The second table is the source list of the Scriptures in the Old Testament referring to the Holy Spirit. They are arranged as they appear in the Bible.
