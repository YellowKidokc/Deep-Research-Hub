# What does it mean for a Christian to be born again?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 31, 2013 
2 min read 
To be born again means to be changed by God into a new creature. “Therefore if any man is in Christ, he is a new creature; the old things passed away; behold, new things have come,” ([2 Cor. 5:17](https://ref.ly/2%20Cor.%205.17;nasb95?t=biblia)). He is different. He is saved from God’s righteous judgment. He is enlightened spiritually to what is right and what is good, and he accepts the truths found in the Bible, which is God’s word, where before he could not ([1 Cor. 2:14](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%202.14;nasb95?t=biblia)). He then seeks to please God and resist sin because this born again change in him makes it possible. Being born again is also known as regeneration. Along with this regeneration, God indwells him ([John 14:23](https://ref.ly/John%2014.23;nasb95?t=biblia)), and he receives guidance from God’s Spirit through the Scriptures.
Literally, the English phrase “born again” is the Greek “to be born from above.”
