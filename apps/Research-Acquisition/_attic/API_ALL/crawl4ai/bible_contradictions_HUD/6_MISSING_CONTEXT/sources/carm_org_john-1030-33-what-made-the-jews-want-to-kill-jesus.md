# John 10:30-33, What made the Jews want to kill Jesus?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 05, 2008 
12 min read 
“I and the Father are one.” 31The Jews took up stones again to stone Him.32Jesus answered them, “I showed you many good works from the Father; for which of them are you stoning Me?” 33The Jews answered Him, “For a good work we do not stone You, but for blasphemy; and because You, being a man, make Yourself out to be God,” ([John 10:30-33](https://ref.ly/John%2010.30-33;nasb95?t=biblia))
What was it that Jesus had said or done that caused the Jews to say that He was claiming to be God? Was it “I and the Father are one”? If so, why would that cause the Jews to want to kill Jesus? Perhaps it was something else. Maybe it was something Jesus said elsewhere that made them so angry. ![jews kill Jesus John 10](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/525a-300x169.jpg)
There are only two places in John where the Jews wanted to kill Jesus with stones. Both of these occur after Jesus spoke and made a claim about Himself. The first was in [John 8:58-59](https://ref.ly/John%208.58-59;nasb95?t=biblia), and the second was in [John 10:30-33](https://ref.ly/John%2010.30-33;nasb95?t=biblia).
