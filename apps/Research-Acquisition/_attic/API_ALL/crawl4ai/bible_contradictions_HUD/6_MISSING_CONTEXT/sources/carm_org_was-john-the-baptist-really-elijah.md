# Was John the Baptist really Elijah?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
4 min read 
Some verses in the gospels seem to say that John the Baptist ![John the Baptist](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/john-the-baptist.png) was Elijah, but others say he was not. Is this a contradiction? Let’s take a look:
The teaching of reincarnation is against the Old Testament. Therefore, Jesus was not teaching that John the Baptist was Elijah reincarnated. So, what did Jesus mean when He said that John the Baptist was Elijah? We see in [Malachi 4:5](https://ref.ly/Mal%204.5;nasb95?t=biblia) this prophecy, “Behold, I am going to send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and terrible day of the Lord.” Jesus is referring to the prophecy concerning Elijah. We see that the coming of Elijah was in the spirit of Elijah, which is so stated in [Luke 1:13-17](https://ref.ly/Luke%201.13-17;nasb95?t=biblia). The context is when Zecharias, John’s father-to-be, was performing his priestly duties in the temple ([Luke 1:8ff](https://ref.ly/Luke%201.8ff;nasb95?t=biblia)). An angel of the Lord appeared to Zacharias and said,
“Do not be afraid, Zacharias, for your petition has been heard, and your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you will give him the name [John. 14](https://ref.ly/John.%2014;nasb95?t=biblia) “And you will have joy and gladness, and many will rejoice at his birth. 15 “For he will be great in the sight of the Lord, and he will drink no wine or liquor; and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit, while yet in his mother’s womb. 16 “And he will turn back many of the sons of Israel to the Lord their God. 17 “And it is he who will go as a forerunner before Him in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of the fathers back to the children, and the disobedient to the attitude of the righteous; so as to make ready a people prepared for the Lord,” ([Luke 1:13-17](https://ref.ly/Luke%201.13-17;nasb95?t=biblia)).
