# Why did God have the Israelites kill the Canaanites? Is this genocide?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
February 08, 2024 
4 min read 
God had the Israelites wipe out the Canaanites because they were exceedingly evil people full of vile practices like child sacrifice and sexual immorality. They were in the promised land, and so God had them wiped out. This is also to keep Israel, as a nation, morally pure ([Exo. 23:23-33](https://ref.ly/Exod.%2023.23-33;nasb95?t=biblia); [Lev. 18:24–28](https://ref.ly/Lev.%2018.24%E2%80%9328;nasb95?t=biblia)). Still, Israel would fall into idolatry ([Num. 25:3](https://ref.ly/Num.%2025.3;nasb95?t=biblia); [Judges 2:11-12](https://ref.ly/Judg%202.11-12;nasb95?t=biblia); [Psalm 106:28](https://ref.ly/Ps%20106.28;nasb95?t=biblia)). So that from it, the fulfillment of the promise to bring the Messiah through the nation of Israel would manifest ([Gen. 12:3](https://ref.ly/Gen.%2012.3;nasb95?t=biblia)). It is only for the Messiah, Jesus, who died for our sins can we be redeemed. Though the destruction of the people of Canaan is difficult for us to comprehend, it pales in comparison to the severity of eternal damnation. God, in Christ, delivers us from that damnation through our faith and trust in Jesus ([Rom. 3:28](https://ref.ly/Rom.%203.28;nasb95?t=biblia); [10: 9-10](https://ref.ly/Rom%2010.%209-10;nasb95?t=biblia); [Eph. 2:8-9](https://ref.ly/Eph.%202.8-9;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Canaan was the son of Ham ([Genesis 9:22](https://ref.ly/Gen%209.22;nasb95?t=biblia)). It appears that the Canaanites were the descendants of Canaan. In the Table of Nations ([Genesis 10:15-19](https://ref.ly/Gen%2010.15-19;nasb95?t=biblia)), Canaan was the father of 11 groups that spread out in that area.[ ![Why did God have the Israelites kill the Canaanites? Is this genocide?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/moloch-01.jpg) ](https://carm.org/questions/why-did-god-have-the-israelites-kill-the-canaanites-genocide/attachment/moloch-01/)
“One of the earliest known references to the people of Canaan is in a tablet from Mari (15th century BC) in which a military officer reported his surveillance of “thieves and Canaanites.” The Canaanites were listed as a group on the Memphis stele (inscribed column) of the Egyptian pharaoh Amenophis II (c. 1440 BC). The land of Canaan was mentioned in a 15th-century inscription of Idrimi, king of Aleppo (west of Ugarit), who fled to the Canaanite seaport of Ammiya and then became ruler of Alalakh (north of Ugarit).” (Elwell, Walter A., and Barry J. Beitzel. 1988. In Baker Encyclopedia of the Bible. Grand Rapids, MI: Baker Book House.)
