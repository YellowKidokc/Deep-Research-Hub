# What does the Eastern Orthodox Church say about Icons?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 02, 2025 
4 min read 
Icons in Eastern Orthodoxy are sacred images that are usually made by painting on wood. They are renditions of various people such as Jesus, Mary, the saints, and angels. They play an important role in Orthodox theology, and are used in their daily devotions. The EOC considers them to be windows to heaven. The icons are venerated and honored, but they say, not worshipped. According to the Eastern Orthodox Church, icons are paintings, usually on wood, kept in homes and churches, blessed by a priest, represent saints who are intercessors, can be conduits of blessings, and are not idolatrous. ![What does the Eastern Orthodox Church say about Icons?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/icon-03.jpg)
