# Deuteronomy 23:15, Do not return an escaped slave
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 12, 2019 
5 min read 
[Deut. 23:15](https://ref.ly/Deut.%2023.15;nasb95?t=biblia), “You shall not hand over to his master a slave who has escaped from his master to you.”
The status of the slave in biblical context as property ([Exodus 21:21](https://ref.ly/Exod%2021.21;nasb95?t=biblia)) is not the same kind as [chattel slavery](https://carm.org/slavery/what-are-the-different-kinds-of-slavery-in-the-world/) where an individual was considered property as was a goat, or a garment. In biblical times, mere ‘property’ was to be returned.
But, escaped slaves were not to be returned ([Deut. 23:15](https://ref.ly/Deut.%2023.15;nasb95?t=biblia)). If the slave was property in the chattel sense, then it would make no sense for the Scriptures to say not to return an escaped slave to his master. Therefore, the sense in which a person was considered property in the biblical context is not the same thing as property in chattel slavery.
