# What does the Eastern Orthodox Church say about Mary?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 02, 2025 
9 min read 
The Eastern Orthodox Church elevates Mary to a level of deep reverence – far more than is revealed in Scripture. She is called the theotokos (God-bearer), that she remained a virgin her entire life, that she never sinned, that she died and her body was assumed into heaven; that she can be prayed to, that she intercedes for us, and more. The EOC has raised her to the level of a functioning goddess. Now, they do not affirm that she is a goddess nor do they affirm that she functions as a goddess. But whether or not they affirm it, Mary plays the role of a goddess to them. After all, she can hear millions of prayers, spoken and thought, from millions of people simultaneously, know the intention of the hearts, and answer them. If that isn’t a functioning goddess, I don’t know what is. ![What does the Eastern Orthodox Church say about Mary?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/icon-mary-02.jpg)
