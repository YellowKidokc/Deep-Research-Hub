# What does the Bible mean when it says that Jesus was made Lord and Christ?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
March 22, 2016 
3 min read 
How could Jesus be made Lord if He was already the Lord? In other words, Jesus is [God](https://carm.org/dictionary/god/) in flesh ([John 1:1](https://ref.ly/John%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia), [14](https://ref.ly/John%201.14;nasb95?t=biblia); [Colossians 2:9](https://ref.ly/Col%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia)). Therefore, He already is Lord and Christ.
[Acts 2:36](https://ref.ly/Acts%202.36;nasb95?t=biblia), “Therefore let all the house of Israel know for certain that God has made Him both Lord and Christ—this Jesus whom you crucified.”
Jesus was not made Lord in the sense of He was made into something He was not already. Instead, it is dealing with His status as a man made under the law ([Gal. 4:4](https://ref.ly/Gal.%204.4;nasb95?t=biblia)), in a lower position ([Luke 14:28](https://ref.ly/Luke%2014.28;nasb95?t=biblia)). In that sense, He was made Lord and Christ by God the Father.
