# Does 1 Corinthians 11:4-5 mean a woman should never ever cut her hair?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 30, 2009 
3 min read 
In ancient Greek society, it was the custom of men to keep their heads uncovered during [worship](https://carm.org/dictionary/worship/). It was the custom of women to keep their heads covered to show reverence to God. But that custom is no longer observed.
“Every man who has something on his head while praying or prophesying, disgraces his head. 5 But every woman who has her head uncovered while praying or prophesying disgraces her head; for she is one and the same with her whose head is shaved,” ([1 Cor. 11:4-5](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2011.4-5;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Therefore, this verse does not say that a woman is not to cut her hair.
