# Annihilationism and Psalm 9:17, The wicked will return to Sheol
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 11, 2018 
4 min read 
“The wicked will return to Sheol, even all the nations who forget God” ([Psalm 9:17](https://ref.ly/Ps%209.17;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Sometimes annihilationists will quote [Psalm 9:17](https://ref.ly/Ps%209.17;nasb95?t=biblia) in their attempt to support their doctrine of the wicked’s final extinction and how the dead cease to exist upon death or enter soul sleep. But [Psalm 9:17](https://ref.ly/Ps%209.17;nasb95?t=biblia) says “the wicked will return to Sheol.” This is problematic if it’s to be taken literally. Sheol is generically the abode of the dead. Even annihilationists affirm this.
“Scripture’s overall usage of Sheol and Hades reveals that, upon death, it is a place where all men, righteous and unrighteous, go to.” (http://www.rethinkinghell.com/2017/05/hypocrisy-not-hell-the-polemic-parable-of-lazarus-and-the-rich-man)
