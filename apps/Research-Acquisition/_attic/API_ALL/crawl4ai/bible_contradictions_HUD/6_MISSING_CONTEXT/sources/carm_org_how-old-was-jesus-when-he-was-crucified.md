# How old was Jesus when He was crucified?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 11, 2008 
2 min read 
We do not know for sure the exact age of Jesus when He was crucified, but He was probably 33 years old. Here is the argument.
[Jesus was baptized.](https://carm.org/doctrine-and-theology/why-was-jesus-baptized/) But the reason He was baptized was to “fulfill all righteousness,” ([Matt. 3:15](https://ref.ly/Matt.%203.15;nasb95?t=biblia)). He had to fulfill the legal requirements for entering into the priesthood after the order of Melchizedek ([Psalm 110:4](https://ref.ly/Ps%20110.4;nasb95?t=biblia); [Heb. 5:8-10](https://ref.ly/Heb.%205.8-10;nasb95?t=biblia); [6:20](https://ref.ly/Heb%206.20;nasb95?t=biblia)). Priests offered sacrifice to God on behalf of the people. Jesus became a sacrifice for our sin ([1 Pet. 2:24](https://ref.ly/1%20Pet.%202.24;nasb95?t=biblia); [2 Cor. 5:21](https://ref.ly/2%20Cor.%205.21;nasb95?t=biblia)) in His role as priest.
To be consecrated as a priest, Jesus had to be: 1) washed with water – baptism – ([Lev. 8:6](https://ref.ly/Lev.%208.6;nasb95?t=biblia); [Exodus 29:4](https://ref.ly/Exod%2029.4;nasb95?t=biblia), [Matt. 3:16](https://ref.ly/Matt.%203.16;nasb95?t=biblia)). 2) Anointed with oil – the Holy Spirit – ([Lev. 8:12](https://ref.ly/Lev.%208.12;nasb95?t=biblia); [Exodus 29:7](https://ref.ly/Exod%2029.7;nasb95?t=biblia); [Matt. 3:16](https://ref.ly/Matt.%203.16;nasb95?t=biblia)). Additionally, He may have needed to be 30 years old, [Num. 4:3](https://ref.ly/Num.%204.3;nasb95?t=biblia), “fro ![crucify](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/crucify.gif) m thirty years and upward, even to fifty years old, all who enter the service to do the work in the tent of meeting.”
