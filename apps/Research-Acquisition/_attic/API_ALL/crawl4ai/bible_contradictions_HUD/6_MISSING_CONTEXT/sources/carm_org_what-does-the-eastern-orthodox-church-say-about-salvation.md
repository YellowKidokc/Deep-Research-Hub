# What does the Eastern Orthodox Church say about salvation?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 10, 2025 
11 min read 
According to the Eastern Orthodox Church, salvation is a long process that begins with baptism, where a person’s sins are washed away. Afterward, the person is anointed with oil (Chrismation), which bestows the Holy Spirit. At that point, the person is now a member of the Church and begins his walk as a Christian. In the church, he must partake of the Eucharist (Communion Supper) where his sins are regularly cleansed. He must practice confession (Penance) before a priest to be absolved of his sins. In addition, he must cooperate with the energies of God (divine grace) that are administered through sacraments. He is obligated to keep commandments and retain a right relationship with God, without which, he cannot be saved. The whole process leads to theosis – being godlike in character, by which salvation is achieved.
