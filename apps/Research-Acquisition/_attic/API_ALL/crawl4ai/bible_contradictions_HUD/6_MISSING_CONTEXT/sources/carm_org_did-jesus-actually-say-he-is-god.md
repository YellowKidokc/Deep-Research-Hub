# Did Jesus actually say He is God?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
July 12, 2017 
22 min read 
A common challenge offered by Muslims (and others who deny the deity of Christ) is that Jesus never claimed to be God. While there are numerous places that the Bible explicitly refers to Jesus as “God” and identifies Him as Yahweh, they accuse the biblical writers of forcing these titles on Jesus. Though the Bible is, in fact, the inerrant word of God and everything it says about Jesus can be trusted, it can be helpful in reaching our Muslim friends to be able to show them in Jesus’ own words that He did, in fact, claim to be God.[ ![Jesus claim be God](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Jesus-09sqs.jpg) ](https://carm.org/attachment/jesus-09sqs/)
This is further complicated by the fact that many Muslim’s today have been taught to distrust the Gospel of John, thus making them less open to accepting Jesus’ clear self-identification as the “I AM” of [Exodus 3:14](https://ref.ly/Exod%203.14;nasb95?t=biblia) or Jesus’ affirmation of Thomas calling Him “my God” ([John 20:28-29](https://ref.ly/John%2020.28-29;nasb95?t=biblia)) in that Gospel. While we should in no way acquiesce to their dismissal of John, (Especially since many of the same Muslims will appeal to John as an accurate record of Jesus’ words when attempting to twist those words to argue that Jesus prophesied the coming of Muhammad.) there are several other places to which we can point in the other three Gospels to avoid unnecessary side debates.
