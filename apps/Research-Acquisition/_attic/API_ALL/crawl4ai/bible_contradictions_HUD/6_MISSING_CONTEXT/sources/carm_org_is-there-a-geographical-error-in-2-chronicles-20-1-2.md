# Is there a geographical error in 2 Chronicles 20:1-2? The problem of “Aram”
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
May 19, 2020 
12 min read 
Is there a geographical error in [2 Chronicles 20:1-2](https://ref.ly/2%20Chron%2020.1-2;nasb95?t=biblia)? The problem of “Aram” 2 Chronicles 20 provides an account of a coalition of enemies who gather against King Jehoshaphat of Judah and how God delivers His people from these invaders. Some critics, however, claim that [2 Chronicles 20:1-2](https://ref.ly/2%20Chron%2020.1-2;nasb95?t=biblia) makes a geographical error when describing where the invading party comes from. While the passage does present an apparent difficulty, the critics are incorrect. The author of 2 Chronicles is not in error.
The key verses in question are:
“Now it came about after this that the sons of Moab and the sons of Ammon, together with some of the Meunites, came to make war against Jehoshaphat. Then some came and reported to Jehoshaphat, saying, ‘A great multitude is coming against you from beyond the sea, out of Aram and behold, they are in Hazazon-tamar (that is Engedi),'” ([2 Chronicles 20:1-2](https://ref.ly/2%20Chron%2020.1-2;nasb95?t=biblia)).
