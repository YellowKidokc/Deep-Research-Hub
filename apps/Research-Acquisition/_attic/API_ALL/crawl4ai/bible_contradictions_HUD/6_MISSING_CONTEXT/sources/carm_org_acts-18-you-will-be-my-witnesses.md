# Acts 1:8, You will be My Witnesses
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 01, 2008 
8 min read 
“And so when they had come together, they were asking Him, saying, Lord, is it at this time You are restoring the kingdom to Israel?” 7 He said to them, “It is not for you to know times or epochs which the Father has fixed by His own authority; 8 but you shall receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you; and you shall be My witnesses both in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and even to the remotest part of the earth,” ([Acts 1:6-8](https://ref.ly/Acts%201.6-8;nasb95?t=biblia)). ![Evangelism](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/evangelism-02.jpg)
Is this verse for you now? Yes. Same Spirit, same Lord, same need. Do you need power when witnessing to unbelievers? [Acts 1:8](https://ref.ly/Acts%201.8;nasb95?t=biblia) is a promise from Jesus that you will not be left empty-handed when sharing the truth of Christ’s love and sacrifice for sinners.
