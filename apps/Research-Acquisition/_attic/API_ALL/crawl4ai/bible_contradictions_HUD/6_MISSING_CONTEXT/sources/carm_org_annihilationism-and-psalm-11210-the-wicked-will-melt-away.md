# Annihilationism and Psalm 112:10, the wicked will melt away
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 11, 2018 
8 min read 
“The wicked will see it and be vexed, He will gnash his teeth and melt away. The desire of the wicked will perish,” ([Psalm 112:10](https://ref.ly/Ps%20112.10;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Psalm 112 is about praising the Lord as well as the blessedness of fearing him. The godly who trust in him will find that the Lord is gracious and compassionate (v. 4) and that in his hands are truth and justice (v. 7). Also, it is an imprecatory Psalm. This means it contains curses against enemies. There are many imprecatory Psalms. Some of the references are as follows: [Psalm 55:15](https://ref.ly/Ps%2055.15;nasb95?t=biblia); [59:5](https://ref.ly/Psalm%2059.5;nasb95?t=biblia); [71:13](https://ref.ly/Psalm%2071.13;nasb95?t=biblia); [79:6](https://ref.ly/Psalm%2079.6;nasb95?t=biblia); [94:1-2](https://ref.ly/Psalm%2094.1-2;nasb95?t=biblia); [109:6-9](https://ref.ly/Psalm%20109.6-9;nasb95?t=biblia); [129:5-6](https://ref.ly/Psalm%20129.5-6;nasb95?t=biblia); [137:9](https://ref.ly/Psalm%20137.9;nasb95?t=biblia); [139:19](https://ref.ly/Psalm%20139.19;nasb95?t=biblia); [140:10-11](https://ref.ly/Psalm%20140.10-11;nasb95?t=biblia).
