# How long was Jesus dead in the tomb?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
14 min read 
How long was Jesus’ body, dead in the tomb?  ![Jesus empty tomb](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/empty-tomb-04.jpg) Was it more or less than three days and nights? [Matthew 12:40](https://ref.ly/Matt%2012.40;nasb95?t=biblia) and [Matthew 28:1](https://ref.ly/Matt%2028.1;nasb95?t=biblia); [Mark 16:2](https://ref.ly/Mark%2016.2;nasb95?t=biblia); [Luke 24:1](https://ref.ly/Luke%2024.1;nasb95?t=biblia); [John 20:1](https://ref.ly/John%2020.1;nasb95?t=biblia)
The Jewish day was measured from sundown to sundown. If Jesus was in the grave for three 24 hour periods, then He could not have been raised on the third day because the third day had not yet been completed. He would have to be raised on the fourth day for three 24 hour periods to have been completed, and that wouldn’t make sense to then say He was raised on the third day. So, what is going on?
