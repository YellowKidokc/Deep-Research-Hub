# Mark 1:1-3, Did Mark quote from Isaiah or Isaiah and Malachi?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 27, 2025 
3 min read 
Is there a contradiction between Mark 1:1-3, Mal. 3:1, and Isaiah 40:3? No, there is not. In Mark 1:1-3, Mark quotes the Old Testament from two different passages and attributes both to the prophet Isaiah. It is not a contradiction or a mistake, because Jewish writers often blended ideas and referenced a major Bible book to summarize citations in minor books.
These are actually quotes from two different places. The first is from Malachi 3:1, "Behold, I am going to send My messenger, and he will clear the way before Me." The second quote, in verse 3, is from Isaiah 40:3, "A voice is calling, 'Clear the way for the LORD in the wilderness. Make smooth in the desert a highway for our God." In light of this, do we have a contradiction? No.
Mark is blending two verses from two places and covers them with Isaiah. Sometimes a person could reference several books by the major book. It was a kind of shorthand approach to referencing Scripture.
