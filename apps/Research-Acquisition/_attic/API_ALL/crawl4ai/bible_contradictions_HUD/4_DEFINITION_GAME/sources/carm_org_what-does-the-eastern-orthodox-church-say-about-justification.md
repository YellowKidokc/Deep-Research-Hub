# What does the Eastern Orthodox Church say about justification?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 09, 2025 
5 min read 
From the Eastern Orthodox perspective, justification is a process maintained through good works and keeping commandments. It involves a change in the believer that is enabled and perpetuated by participation in the life of Christ ([Recapitulation Theory of the Atonement](https://carm.org/doctrine-and-theology/what-is-the-recapitulation-theory-of-the-atonement/)), which leads towards theosis – Christlikeness. The EO teaching is contrary to Scripture. I’ve underlined essential parts of the quotes.
