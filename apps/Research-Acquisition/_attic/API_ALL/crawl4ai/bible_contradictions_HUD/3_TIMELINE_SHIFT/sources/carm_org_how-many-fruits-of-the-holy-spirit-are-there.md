# How many fruits of the Holy Spirit are there?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 01, 2022 
3 min read 
A lot of people don’t know that when the Bible lists the fruit of the Holy Spirit in [Gal. 5:22-23](https://ref.ly/Gal.%205.22-23;nasb95?t=biblia), it’s only one fruit, not fruits. So, when we ask how many fruits of the Holy Spirit there are, the answer is one. This is because in Greek, the word fruit is in the singular. The word for ‘fruit’ in Greek is καρπός karpós, and it is the nominative, masculine, singular. Nominative means it is the subject of the sentence. Masculine deals with gender; many languages have gender related to nouns. And “singular” deals with number. So, [karpos](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2590/nasb95/mgnt/0-1/) is in the singular form. If it were plural, the form would be karpoi. So, we can then interpret it thus: “but the one fruit of the Spirit is….”
[Galatians 5:22-23](https://ref.ly/Gal%205.22-23;nasb95?t=biblia), “But the **fruit** of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, 23 gentleness, self-control; against such things, there is no law.”
So why does Paul the apostle refer to the fruit of the Holy Spirit in the singular and not plural? Some commentators think it’s because the word love, which is the first word in the list, encompasses all the rest of them. After all, the Holy Spirit is the third person of the Trinity. And because God is love ([1 John 4:8](https://ref.ly/1%20John%204.8;nasb95?t=biblia)) we can say the Holy Spirit is also love. Hence, the listing with love preceding all the rest might encompass the ones that follow.
