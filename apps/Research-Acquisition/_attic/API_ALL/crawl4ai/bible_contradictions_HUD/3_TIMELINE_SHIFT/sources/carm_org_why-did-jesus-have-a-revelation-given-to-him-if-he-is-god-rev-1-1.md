# Why did Jesus have a revelation given to him in Rev. 1:1, if He is God?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
March 19, 2022 
3 min read 
Why did God (the Father) give a revelation to Jesus ([Rev. 1:1](https://ref.ly/Rev.%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia)) if Jesus is God? Wouldn’t Jesus already have the revelation?
“The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave Him to show to His bond-servants, the things which must soon take place; and He sent and communicated it by His angel to His bond-servant John,” ([Rev. 1:1](https://ref.ly/Rev.%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia)).
First of all, the Bible teaches that Jesus is God in the flesh. We see this taught in [John 1:1](https://ref.ly/John%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia), [14](https://ref.ly/John%201.14;nasb95?t=biblia); [Colossians 2:9](https://ref.ly/Col%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia); [Hebrews 1:8](https://ref.ly/Heb%201.8;nasb95?t=biblia), and other verses. From that fact, some might conclude that Jesus would not need anything given to Him by God the Father. So, if that is the case, then why did Jesus have a revelation given to Him by God in [Revelation 1:1](https://ref.ly/Rev%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia)?
