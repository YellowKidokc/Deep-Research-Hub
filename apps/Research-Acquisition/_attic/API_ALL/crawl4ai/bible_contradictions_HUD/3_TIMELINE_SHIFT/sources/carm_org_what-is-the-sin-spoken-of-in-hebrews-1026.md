# What is the sin spoken of in Hebrews 10:26?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
August 01, 2009 
2 min read 
**What is the sin spoken of in[Hebrews 10:26](https://ref.ly/Heb%2010.26;nasb95?t=biblia)?**
The book of Hebrews was, of course, written to the Hebrew people. Though the author of Hebrews is unknown (many suspect it was Paul), the writer obviously had an exhaustive knowledge of the Old Testament since a great many Old Testament themes and citations are used. Therefore, it seems that the writer of Hebrews is addressing those Jews who had knowledge not only the Old Testament but also of the work of Christ.
[Heb. 10:26](https://ref.ly/Heb.%2010.26;nasb95?t=biblia) says, “For if we go on sinning willfully after receiving the knowledge of the truth, there no longer remains a sacrifice for sins.”
