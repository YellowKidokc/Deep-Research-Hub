# Crisis Period (2023)

Major crisis hits the community. Leadership contradicts itself, destroying trust. Members abandon core values, pursuing selfish interests. Impatience leads to hasty, destructive decisions.

No hope remains for recovery under current trajectory. Faithfulness is rare. Peace is gone, replaced by constant conflict. Unity completely shattered. Pride and arrogance dominate. Goodness is abandoned. Grace is denied to those who fail. Discipline collapses. Joy is absent.

**Crisis Indicators**:
- Contradictory statements from leaders
- Selfish pursuit of individual gain
- Impatient, reckless decisions
- Hopelessness pervades
- Broken commitments everywhere
- Constant conflict and fighting
- Complete fragmentation
- Arrogance in leadership
- Evil tolerated
- Harsh judgment, no forgiveness
- Chaos and disorder
- Despair and fear
