# Founding Principles (2020)

Our community is built on cooperation, love, and truth. We work together patiently to solve problems and maintain our core values. There is hope for the future and grace for mistakes.

Everyone is faithful to their commitments, showing self-control and goodness in daily life. We promote unity and peace among all members, with humility guiding our actions. Joy comes from shared purpose.

**Core Values**:
- Mutual support and love
- Truthful communication
- Patient problem-solving
- Hopeful outlook
- Faithful commitments
- Peaceful resolution
- Unified action
- Humble service
- Goodness in practice
- Graceful forgiveness
- Self-control and discipline
- Joyful community
