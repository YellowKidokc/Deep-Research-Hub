# Unitarian Universalism
Fast Facts on Unitarian Universalism   
---  
[Adherents](https://religionfacts.com/unitarian-universalism/adherents) |  800,000   
[History](https://religionfacts.com/unitarian-universalism/history) |  Formal merger of Unitarians and Universalists in 1961, USA.   
[Beliefs](https://religionfacts.com/unitarian-universalism/beliefs) |  Has no set beliefs, which is its defining characteristic.   
[Practices](https://religionfacts.com/unitarian-universalism/practices) |  Ceremonies for marriages, funerals, etc. Church services have elements from various religions. Emphasis on civil rights, social justice, equality and environment. Most UUs are anti-death penalty and pro-gay rights.   
[Texts](https://religionfacts.com/unitarian-universalism/texts) |  Many sacred texts are revered by various members; some none at all. The Bible is the most commonly used text.
