About Patheos Recreation, Outdoors and Religion 
The outdoors is a great opportunity to connect with one’s faith via nature, recreation, and other activities. Seeking the latest information about outdoor recreation and how it fits with religion? Patheos writers include some of the top faith leaders and scholars in the world exploring outdoor recreation and religion. Our hope is by providing religious outdoor information, stories and conversation, it leads to understanding, empathy and acceptance of all faiths.
