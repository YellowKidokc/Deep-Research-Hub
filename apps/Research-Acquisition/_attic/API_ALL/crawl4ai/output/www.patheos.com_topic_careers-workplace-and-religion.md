About Patheos Careers, Workplace and Religion 
Everyone hits roadblocks in their career. Sometimes its workplace related and sometimes you need a different perspective to continue on. Whether you’re in a good spot or not, this is the place to learn how to navigate the workplace as a person of faith. Patheos writers include some of the top faith leaders and scholars in the world exploring the workplace and religion. Our hope is by providing religious business information, stories and conversation, it leads to understanding, empathy and acceptance of all faiths.
