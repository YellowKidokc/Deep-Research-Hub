Islamic Rituals and Practices 
# Islamic Rituals and Practices
Islamic **religious rituals** are relatively few in number but great in importance. They enable Muslims to remember history, express conviction, and grow in devotion.
The [Five Pillars of Islam](https://religionfacts.com/five-pillars-islam) are core practices regarded by all sects of Islam as essential to the Muslim faith.
##  [](https://religionfacts.com/islam/practices#five-pillars-of-islam "link to this section")Five Pillars of Islam
The Five Pillars of Islam (Arabic _arkan ud-Din_ , "pillars of the faith") are the five religious duties expected of every Muslim. The five pillars are mentioned individually throughout the [Qur'an](https://religionfacts.com/quran) and Muhammad listed them together in the [Hadith](https://religionfacts.com/hadith) when he was asked to define Islam.
###  [](https://religionfacts.com/islam/practices#confession-of-faith-shahada "link to this section")Confession of faith (shahada)
The first of the Five Pillars of Islam is the shahada. Shahada is the Muslim profession of faith, expressing the two simple, fundamental beliefs that make one a Muslim:
> La ilaha illa Allah wa-Muhammad rasul Allah. There is no god but God and Muhammad is the prophet of God.
###  [](https://religionfacts.com/islam/practices#ritual-prayer-salat "link to this section")Ritual prayer (salat)
Perhaps the most well-known Muslim practices among non-Muslims is ritual prayer, or salat, which is performed five times a day: at dawn (al-fajr), midday (al-zuhr), afternoon (al-'asr), sunset (al-maghrib) and evening (al-'isha).
###  [](https://religionfacts.com/islam/practices#alms-tax-zakat "link to this section")Alms tax (zakat)
Almsgiving is a central activity in Islam. The Quran explicitly requires it (9:60) and often places it alongside prayer when discussing a Muslim's duties. ("Perform the prayer and give the alms." 2:43, 110, 277)
For those who are greedy and use their money outside of the will of Allah, the Quran has harsh words: the fires of hell will heat up the coins and the greedy will be branded with it (9:34-35).
###  [](https://religionfacts.com/islam/practices#fasting-during-the-month-of-ramadan-sawm "link to this section")Fasting during the month of Ramadan (sawm)
Sawm (also siyam), fasting, commemorates the revelation of the Quran to humanity during Ramadan, the ninth month of the Islamic year.
###  [](https://religionfacts.com/islam/practices#pilgrimage-to-mecca-hajj "link to this section")Pilgrimage to Mecca (hajj)
At least once in his or her lifetime, each Muslim is expected to undertake a pilgrimage to Mecca, the sacred city of Islam. This holy journey is called the hajj in Arabic.
