The **Eye of Providence** or **All-Seeing Eye** is a symbol depicting an [eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye "Eye"), often enclosed in a triangle and surrounded by rays of light or a halo, intended to represent [Providence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divine_providence "Divine providence"), as the eye watches over the workers of mankind.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-1)[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-3) A well-known example of the Eye of Providence appears on the reverse of the [Great Seal of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Seal_of_the_United_States "Great Seal of the United States"), which is depicted on the [United States one-dollar bill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_one-dollar_bill "United States one-dollar bill"). It also features prominently atop the original publication of France's [Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Declaration_of_the_Rights_of_Man_and_of_the_Citizen "Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen").[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-Wilson-4)

### Christianity
The association of an eye with the concept of [Divine Providence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divine_Providence "Divine Providence") is found in [Christianity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity "Christianity"). In late [Renaissance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renaissance "Renaissance") European [iconography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iconography "Iconography"), the Eye, surrounded by a triangle, was an explicit symbol of the Christian [Holy Trinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Trinity "Holy Trinity").[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-5) The Eye of Providence was later painted above an image of three faces in [Pontormo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontormo "Pontormo")'s 1525 _Supper at Emmaus_.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-Wilson-4) Seventeenth-century depictions of the Eye sometimes show it surrounded by clouds or [sunbursts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunburst "Sunburst").[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-6) The Eye of God in a triangle is still used in [church architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_architecture "Church architecture") and [Christian art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_art "Christian art") to symbolize the [Trinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity "Trinity") and God's [omnipresence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnipresence "Omnipresence") and divine providence. 
The Eye of Providence is notably featured on the following buildings of the [Roman Catholic Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church "Roman Catholic Church"), [Eastern Orthodoxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Orthodoxy "Eastern Orthodoxy"), and [The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Church_of_Jesus_Christ_of_Latter-day_Saints "The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints"): 
  * The [Mannheim Jesuit Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuit_Church,_Mannheim "Jesuit Church, Mannheim") in [Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mannheim "Mannheim"), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany "Germany")
  * The [Kazan Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan_Cathedral,_St._Petersburg "Kazan Cathedral, St. Petersburg") in [Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg "Saint Petersburg"), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia "Russia")
  * The [Shio-Mgvime Monastery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shio-Mgvime_Monastery "Shio-Mgvime Monastery") in [Mtskheta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mtskheta "Mtskheta"), [Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_\(country\) "Georgia \(country\)")
  * The [Salt Lake Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Lake_Temple "Salt Lake Temple") in [Salt Lake City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Lake_City "Salt Lake City"), [Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah "Utah")


### Freemasonry
Commonly, the Eye of Providence is often associated with [Freemasonry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freemasonry "Freemasonry"), first appearing as part of the standard Freemason [iconography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iconography "Iconography") in 1797 with the publication of [Thomas Smith Webb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Smith_Webb "Thomas Smith Webb")'s _The Freemason's Monitor_.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-7)[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-8) In this use, the Eye, representing the [all-seeing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omniscience "Omniscience") eye of [God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/God "God"), serves as a reminder that humanity's thoughts and deeds are always observed by God—who is referred to in Masonry as the [Great Architect of the Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Architect_of_the_Universe "Great Architect of the Universe"). Typically, the Masonic Eye of Providence has a [semicircular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semicircle "Semicircle") [glory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glory_\(religion\) "Glory \(religion\)") below it, and is sometimes enclosed by a [triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle "Triangle"). Popular among [conspiracy theorists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conspiracy_theory "Conspiracy theory") is the claim that the Eye of Providence shown atop an unfinished [pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramid "Pyramid") on the [Great Seal of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Seal_of_the_United_States "Great Seal of the United States") indicates the influence of Freemasonry in the [founding of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Founding_Fathers_of_the_United_States "Founding Fathers of the United States"). However, common Masonic use of the Eye dates to 14 years after the creation of the Great Seal. Furthermore, the only [Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freemasonry_in_the_Americas "Freemasonry in the Americas") among the members of the various design committees for the Great Seal was [Benjamin Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Franklin "Benjamin Franklin"), whose ideas for the seal were not adopted. Likewise, various Masonic organizations have explicitly denied any connection to the creation of the Seal.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-9)[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-10)
## Governmental use

Decreed early in the [French Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Revolution "French Revolution") by the Assembly in France (1789), the document was illustrated by French artist, [Jean-Jacques-François Le Barbier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Jacques-Fran%C3%A7ois_Le_Barbier "Jean-Jacques-François Le Barbier") (1738-1826).
### United States
In 1782, the Eye of Providence was adopted as part of the [symbolism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbolism_\(arts\) "Symbolism \(arts\)") featured on the reverse side of the [Great Seal of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Seal_of_the_United_States "Great Seal of the United States"). It was first proposed as an element of the Great Seal by the first of three design committees in 1776, and it is thought to be the suggestion of the artistic consultant [Pierre Eugene du Simitiere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Eugene_du_Simitiere "Pierre Eugene du Simitiere").[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-GSUSbooklet-11) At the time, it was a conventional symbol for God’s benevolent oversight.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-Wilson-4)
In his original proposal to the committee, du Simitiere placed the Eye over [shields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coat_of_arms "Coat of arms") so as to symbolize each of the original [thirteen states of the Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteen_Colonies "Thirteen Colonies"). On the version of the seal that would eventually be approved, the Eye is positioned above an unfinished [pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramid "Pyramid") of thirteen steps (again symbolizing the original States, but also incorporating the nation's potential for future growth). Such symbolism is explained through the motto that appears above the Eye, _[annuit cœptis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annuit_c%C5%93ptis "Annuit cœptis")_ , meaning "He approves [our] undertakings" (or "has approved").[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-12)[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-13)
Perhaps due to its use in the design of the Great Seal, the Eye has made its way into other American seals and logos, such as, for example: 
  * The [Seal of Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seal_of_Colorado "Seal of Colorado")
  * The [city seal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_United_States_county_and_city_insignia "List of United States county and city insignia") of [Kenosha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenosha,_Wisconsin "Kenosha, Wisconsin"), [Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisconsin "Wisconsin")
  * [DARPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DARPA "DARPA")'s [Information Awareness Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_Awareness_Office "Information Awareness Office")


#### U.S. currency
The Eye of Providence has been used among various forms of currency throughout U.S. history, including: 
  * the [United States one-dollar bill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_one-dollar_bill "United States one-dollar bill"), as part of the Great Seal (since 1935)
  * the [Vermont Copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vermont_copper "Vermont copper")
  * [Nova Constellatio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nova_Constellatio "Nova Constellatio") patterns of 1783[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-14)
  * Nova Constellatio coppers of 1783 and 1785[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-15)
  * some Immune Columbia issues[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-16)


### Other countries
The Eye of Providence appears in France's [Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Declaration_of_the_Rights_of_Man_and_of_the_Citizen "Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen"),[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_of_Providence#cite_note-Wilson-4) and it appears on the front page of the [Constitution of Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Serbia "Constitution of Serbia") (from 1835). The Eye of Providence appears prominently on several [Lithuanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuania "Lithuania") coats of arms, including those of [Alovė](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Alov%C4%97&action=edit&redlink=1 "Alovė \(page does not exist\)"), [Baisogala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baisogala "Baisogala"), [Kalvarija](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalvarija,_Lithuania "Kalvarija, Lithuania"), [Plungė](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plung%C4%97 "Plungė") and [Šiauliai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%A0iauliai "Šiauliai") (for the latter two, in Eyes appear in their flags as well). 
The Eye of Providence is also a feature in the [coats of arms of Victoria, Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coat_of_arms_of_Victoria,_British_Columbia "Coat of arms of Victoria, British Columbia"); [Brasłaŭ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braslaw "Braslaw"), [Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus "Belarus"); [Neman, Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neman,_Russia "Neman, Russia"); [Radekhiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radekhiv "Radekhiv"), [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine "Ukraine"); and [Radzymin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radzymin "Radzymin") and [Wilamowice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilamowice "Wilamowice"), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland "Poland"). The Eye was also part of the flag and coat of arms adopted by the [Confederation of the Equator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederation_of_the_Equator "Confederation of the Equator"), a short-lived 1824 [secessionist revolt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secession "Secession") in the [northeastern provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_Region,_Brazil "Northeast Region, Brazil") of [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil "Brazil"). In the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom"), the symbol was part of the [Guards Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guards_Division_\(United_Kingdom\) "Guards Division \(United Kingdom\)") insignia, created in 1915. In [Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria "Nigeria"), the eye symbol is part of the [Nigeria Customs Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria_Customs_Service "Nigeria Customs Service") logo. 
In [Estonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonia "Estonia"), the [50 krooni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/50_krooni "50 krooni") note shows the Eye as part of a depiction of the [pipe organ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pipe_organ "Pipe organ") of the [Käina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%A4ina "Käina") church. Likewise, the (old) 500 [Ukrainian hryvnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_hryvnia "Ukrainian hryvnia") note also depicts the Eye.
