**Operation Himmler** , also called **Operation Konserve** , consisted of a group of 1939 [false flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False_flag "False flag") undertakings planned by [Nazi Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Germany "Nazi Germany") to give the appearance of [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Polish_Republic "Second Polish Republic") aggression against Germany. The Germans then used propaganda reports of the events to justify their [invasion of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion_of_Poland "Invasion of Poland"), which started on 1 September 1939. Operation Himmler included the Germans staging false attacks on themselves—directed at innocent people, such as civilians and [concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_concentration_camps "Nazi concentration camps") prisoners. The operation arguably became the first act of the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War "Second World War") in [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe "Europe").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Himmler-1)
## Planning
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Operation_Himmler&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Planning")]
Prior to the 1939 invasion, German newspapers and politicians like [Adolf Hitler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Hitler "Adolf Hitler") carried out a national and international [propaganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propaganda "Propaganda") campaign accusing Polish authorities of organizing or tolerating violent [ethnic cleansing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_cleansing "Ethnic cleansing") of [ethnic Germans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_minority_in_Poland "German minority in Poland") living in Poland.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Hitler-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-German_Editor-3)
The plan, named after its originator, [Heinrich Himmler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_Himmler "Heinrich Himmler"),[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Himmler-1) was supervised by [Reinhard Heydrich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinhard_Heydrich "Reinhard Heydrich")[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Nuremberg-4) and managed[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-5) by [Heinrich Müller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_M%C3%BCller_\(Gestapo\) "Heinrich Müller \(Gestapo\)").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Himmler-1)[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Nuremberg-4) The goal of this [false flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False_flag "False flag") project was to create the appearance of [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Polish_Republic "Second Polish Republic") aggression against Germany, which could be used to [justify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casus_belli "Casus belli") the [German invasion of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion_of_Poland "Invasion of Poland"). Hitler also might have hoped to confuse Poland's allies, the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom") and [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France "France"), into delaying or stopping their declaration of war on Germany.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Zaloga-6)
## Implementation
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Operation_Himmler&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Implementation")]
The operations were mostly carried out on 31 August.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-WirtzGordon-7) The operation, as well as the main German offensive, was originally scheduled for 26 August; the shifting diplomatic situation resulted in delay until 31 August and 1 September. The operations were carried out by agents of the [SS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schutzstaffel "Schutzstaffel")[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-WirtzGordon-7) and the [SD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sicherheitsdienst "Sicherheitsdienst").[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Himmler2-8) The German troops, dressed in Polish uniforms, would storm various border buildings, scare the locals with inaccurate shots, carry out acts of vandalism, retreat and leave behind dead bodies in Polish uniforms.[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Himmler2-8) The bodies were really prisoners from [concentration camps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concentration_camps "Concentration camps") who were dressed in Polish uniforms, killed by [lethal injection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethal_injection "Lethal injection"), shot for appearances and left behind. They were described in plans as _Konserve_ : canned goods, which also led to the informal name of the operation, _Operation Konserve_.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Himmler-1)[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-WirtzGordon-7)[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Ailsby-9)[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-10)
There were several separate operations, including staged attacks on the following: 
  * The strategic railway at Jablunka Pass ([Jabłonków Incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jab%C5%82onk%C3%B3w_Incident "Jabłonków Incident")), on the border between Poland and Czechoslovakia[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-EspMachine-11)
  * The German radio station Sender Gleiwitz ([Gliwice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gliwice "Gliwice")), the [Gleiwitz incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gleiwitz_incident "Gleiwitz incident") being arguably the most notable of the Operation Himmler operations[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Ailsby-9)
  * The German customs station at Hochlinden (now part of [Rybnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rybnik "Rybnik")-[Stodoły](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stodo%C5%82y,_Rybnik "Stodoły, Rybnik"))[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Himmler2-8)[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Ailsby-9)
  * The forest service station in Pitschen, now [Byczyna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byczyna "Byczyna")[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Himmler2-8)

### Gleiwitz incident
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Operation_Himmler&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Gleiwitz incident")]
[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e9/Alfred_Naujocks.jpg/250px-Alfred_Naujocks.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Alfred_Naujocks.jpg)Alfred Naujocks [![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0c/Sender_gliwice.jpg/250px-Sender_gliwice.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Sender_gliwice.jpg)[Gliwice Radio Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gliwice_Radio_Tower "Gliwice Radio Tower") today. It is the highest wooden structure in Europe.
Further information: [Gleiwitz incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gleiwitz_incident "Gleiwitz incident")
On the night of 31 August a small group of German operatives, dressed in Polish uniforms and led by [Alfred Naujocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Naujocks "Alfred Naujocks"), seized the Gleiwitz radio station and broadcast a short [anti-German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-German_sentiment "Anti-German sentiment") message in [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language "Polish language") (sources vary on the content of the message). Several prisoners (most likely from the [Dachau concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dachau_concentration_camp "Dachau concentration camp")) and a local Polish-[Silesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silesia "Silesia") activist (arrested a day earlier) were left dead on the scene in Polish uniforms.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Ailsby-9)[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Franciszek_Honiok-12)
## Aftermath
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Operation_Himmler&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Aftermath")]
In his [1 September speech to the Reichstag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_September_1939_Reichstag_speech "1 September 1939 Reichstag speech") announcing war, Hitler cited the 21 border incidents as justification for Germany's "defensive" action against Poland: 
> I can no longer find any willingness on the part of the Polish Government to conduct serious negotiations with us. These proposals for mediation have failed because in the meanwhile there, first of all, came as an answer to the sudden Polish general mobilization, followed by more Polish atrocities. These were again repeated last night. Recently in one night, there were as many as twenty-one frontier incidents: last night there were fourteen, of which three were quite serious. I have, therefore, resolved to speak to Poland in the same language that Poland for months past has used toward us... This night for the first time Polish regular soldiers fired on our own territory. Since 5:45 a. m., we have been returning the fire... I will continue this struggle, no matter against whom, until the safety of the Reich and its rights are secured[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Hitler-2)
By mid-1939, thousands of Polish [Volksdeutsche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volksdeutsche "Volksdeutsche") had been secretly prepared for sabotage and guerrilla warfare by the [Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breslau "Breslau") (Wrocław) office of the [Abwehr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abwehr "Abwehr"). Their activities were meant to provoke anti-German reprisals that could be claimed as provocations.[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-W-13)
The German agents indeed co-operated with the German forces during the invasion of Poland, which led to some reprisals that were highly exaggerated by the German propaganda.[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-W-13)[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Naziprop-14)[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Blanke580-582-15) One of the most notable cases of such a scenario was reportedly carried out during [Bydgoszcz Bloody Sunday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloody_Sunday_\(1939\) "Bloody Sunday \(1939\)"). An instruction issued by the Ministry of Propaganda stated that the press 
> must show news on the barbarism of Poles in Bromberg. The expression "bloody sunday" must enter as a permanent term in the dictionary and circumnavigate the globe. For that reason, this term must be continuously underlined.[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Kunert-16)
The operation convinced very little international opinion about the German claims.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler#cite_note-Zaloga-6)
