Geography  
[![Kaimanawa Mountains is located in New Zealand](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f7/New_Zealand_%28relief_map%29.png/330px-New_Zealand_%28relief_map%29.png)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:New_Zealand_\(relief_map\).png "Kaimanawa Mountains is located in New Zealand") ![Kaimanawa Mountains](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/3/39/Red_triangle_with_thick_white_border.svg/20px-Red_triangle_with_thick_white_border.svg.png) Kaimanawa Mountains  
The **Kaimanawa Range** , officially called the **Kaimanawa Mountains** since 16 July 2020,[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-2) is a range of mountains in the central [North Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Island "North Island") of [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand "New Zealand").[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-linz-3) They extend for 50 kilometres in a northeast–southwest direction through largely uninhabited country to the south of [Lake Taupō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Taup%C5%8D "Lake Taupō"), east of the "Desert Road". Their slopes form part of the [North Island Volcanic Plateau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Island_Volcanic_Plateau "North Island Volcanic Plateau"). 
The New Zealand [Ministry for Culture and Heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_for_Culture_and_Heritage "Ministry for Culture and Heritage") gives a translation of "breath for food" for _Kaimanawa_.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-4)
The lands around the mountains are scrubby. To the west, where the [Rangipo Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rangipo_Desert "Rangipo Desert") is located, the soils are poor quality. To the east, the soils are more fertile, but the land is very rough. A population of feral horses, the [Kaimanawa horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_horse "Kaimanawa horse"), roam free on the ranges. 
Unlike the majority of mountain ranges in New Zealand, the Kaimanawa Range is divided into private land. Considerable areas of the Rangipo Desert are used by the [New Zealand Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Army "New Zealand Army") for training. 
## Demographics
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Kaimanawa_Range&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Demographics")]
Kaimanawa covers 1,342.26 km2 (518.25 sq mi)[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-Area-5) and had an estimated population of 310 as of June 2025,[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-NZ_population_data_2023_SA2-6) with a population density of 0.23 people per km2.

Source: [[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-Census_2023-7)[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-Census_2018-8)  
Kaimanawa had a population of 312 in the [2023 New Zealand census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_New_Zealand_census "2023 New Zealand census"), an increase of 132 people (73.3%) since the [2018 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_New_Zealand_census "2018 New Zealand census"), and an increase of 306 people (5100.0%) since the [2013 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_New_Zealand_census "2013 New Zealand census"). There were 303 males and 9 females in 9 dwellings.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-9) 5.8% of people identified as [LGBTIQ+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ "LGBTQ"). The median age was 48.3 years (compared with 38.1 years nationally). There were 33 people (10.6%) aged 15 to 29, 240 (76.9%) aged 30 to 64, and 42 (13.5%) aged 65 or older.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-Census_2023-7)
People could identify as more than one ethnicity. The results were 53.8% [European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_New_Zealanders "European New Zealanders") ([Pākehā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C4%81keh%C4%81 "Pākehā")), 57.7% [Māori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C4%81ori_people "Māori people"), 5.8% [Pasifika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasifika_New_Zealanders "Pasifika New Zealanders"), 4.8% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_New_Zealanders "Asian New Zealanders"), and 2.9% other, which includes people giving their ethnicity as "New Zealander". English was spoken by 98.1%, Māori by 19.2%, Samoan by 1.0%, and other languages by 3.8%. [New Zealand Sign Language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Sign_Language "New Zealand Sign Language") was known by 1.9%. The percentage of people born overseas was 8.7, compared with 28.8% nationally.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-Census_2023-7)
Religious affiliations were 34.6% [Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity_in_New_Zealand "Christianity in New Zealand"), 1.0% [Hindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism_in_New_Zealand "Hinduism in New Zealand"), 7.7% [Māori religious beliefs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C4%81ori_religious_beliefs "Māori religious beliefs"), 1.0% [Buddhist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism_in_New_Zealand "Buddhism in New Zealand"), 1.0% [New Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Age "New Age"), and 3.8% other religions. People who answered that they had [no religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irreligion_in_New_Zealand "Irreligion in New Zealand") were 49.0%, and 3.8% of people did not answer the census question.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-Census_2023-7)
Of those at least 15 years old, 18 (5.8%) people had a bachelor's or higher degree, 183 (58.7%) had a post-high school certificate or diploma, and 111 (35.6%) people exclusively held high school qualifications. 3 people (1.0%) earned over $100,000 compared to 12.1% nationally. The employment status of those at least 15 was 15 (4.8%) full-time, 39 (12.5%) part-time, and 15 (4.8%) unemployed.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-Census_2023-7)
## Kaimanawa Wall
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Kaimanawa_Range&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Kaimanawa Wall")]
[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b0/Kaimanawa_wall.jpg/250px-Kaimanawa_wall.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Kaimanawa_wall.jpg)Kaimanawa wall
The Kaimanawa Wall is a geological feature in the Kaimanawa State Forest.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-wall-10)[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-11) The [Tūwharetoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ng%C4%81ti_T%C5%ABwharetoa "Ngāti Tūwharetoa") _[tangata whenua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangata_whenua "Tangata whenua")_ (people) claim an "oral tradition" of the place as a _kōhatu_ (rock).[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-wall-10) A popular theory is that the wall is a [pre-Māori civilization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-M%C4%81ori_settlement_of_New_Zealand_theories "Pre-Māori settlement of New Zealand theories") artifact.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-wall-10)
The wall formation was inspected by an archaeologist and a geologist; neither saw evidence of a human origin. In a preliminary investigation, archaeologist [Neville Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neville_Ritchie "Neville Ritchie") of the New Zealand [Department of Conservation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Conservation_\(New_Zealand\) "Department of Conservation \(New Zealand\)") observed "matching micro-irregularities along the joints." This indicated that the blocks in the wall were too perfectly matched. He also observed the joints were neither straight nor truly horizontal nor perpendicular, indicating the joint alignments were too poorly constructed. Ritchie concluded the blocks are a natural formation based on the presence of matching micro-irregularities in blocks and imperfect joint alignment.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-wall-10)
Peter Wood, a geologist of the [Institute of Geological and Nuclear Sciences at Wairakei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNS_Science "GNS Science"), inspected the blocks for an afternoon and concluded they are natural fractures in "[jointed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_\(geology\) "Joint \(geology\)") Rangitaiki [ignimbrite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ignimbrite "Ignimbrite"), a 330,000 year old volcanic rock that is common in the [Taupō Volcanic Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taup%C5%8D_Volcanic_Zone "Taupō Volcanic Zone")."[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-wall-10) Both vertical and horizontal joints are common. Fractures in the Rangitaiki ignimbrite formed when it cooled and contracted after flowing into place during an eruption.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimanawa_Range#cite_note-wall-10)
