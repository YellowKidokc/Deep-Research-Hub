# How could Adam sin if he was good?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
June 20, 2012 
2 min read 
When God made Adam and Eve he said that what he made was good. So, Adam was good.
“And God created man in His own image, in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them…And God saw all that He had made, and behold, it was very good. And there was evening and there was morning, the sixth day” ([Gen. 1:27](https://ref.ly/Gen.%201.27;nasb95?t=biblia),[31](https://ref.ly/Gen%201.31;nasb95?t=biblia)).
However, if Adam was good how could he choose to sin? Logically speaking we can see two truths. First, Adam was good. Second, he chose to sin. We must conclude then that being good, at least in Adam’s case, does not necessitate the inability to sin. God did not say that Adam was perfect or that he could not sin but only that he was good.
