### Notes
  1. _And there shall come forth a rod out of the stem of Jesse, and a Branch shall grow out of his roots._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/11.html#1)) 
It's not at all clear (to me, anyway) that the root or rod of Jesse refers to the messiah, but Christians believe that it does.
If so, then since Jesse was David's father ([1 Sam 16:11-13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1sam/16.html#11)), the messiah will be a descendant of David.
[Will the Messiah be a descendant of David?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/messiah.html)
  2. _He shall smite the earth: with the rod of his mouth, and with the breath of his lips shall he slay the wicked._ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/11.html#4)) 
Is this passage describing Jesus, the Prince of Peace?
  3. _The wolf also shall dwell with the lamb, and the leopard shall lie down with the kid; and the calf and the young lion ... And the lion shall eat straw like the ox._ ([vv.6-7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/11.html#6)) 
I wonder what will become of the spiders. Will they be friendlier toward flies? And will the parasitic wasps find another way to feed their larvae? Or will they continue to feed off the living bodies of caterpillars?
  4. _The weaned child shall put his hand in the cockatrice' den._ ([v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/11.html#8)) 
A [cockatrice](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cockatrice) is a serpent, hatched from a cock's egg, that can kill with a glance. They are rare nowadays.
  5. _The four corners of the earth._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/11.html#12)) 
In the Bible's view, the earth is flat with four corners.
  6. _They shall fly upon the shoulders of the Philistines._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/11.html#14)) 
So if this chapter refers to Jesus, then Jesus would have lead an army against the Philistines, Edomites, Moabites, and Ammonites.
  7. _The LORD shall utterly destroy the tongue of the Egyptian sea; and with his mighty wind shall he shake his hand over the river._ ([v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/11.html#15)) 
The note for this verse in the HarperCollins Study Bible: _Sea of Egypt_ , the Red Sea; _the River_ , the Euphrates.
