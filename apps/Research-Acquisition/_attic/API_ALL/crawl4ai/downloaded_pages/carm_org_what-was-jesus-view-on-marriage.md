# What was Jesus’ view on marriage?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 30, 2016 
4 min read 
Jesus’ view on marriage is that it is to be between one man and one woman. He refers to the created order that is revealed in the book of Genesis, particularly [Genesis 2:24](https://ref.ly/Gen%202.24;nasb95?t=biblia). In this verse, we see the creation of Adam and Eve and the admonition for the man and woman to join and become one flesh. Jesus referenced them.
[Matthew 19:4-6](https://ref.ly/Matt%2019.4-6;nasb95?t=biblia), “And He answered and said, ‘Have you not read that He who created them from the beginning made them male and female, 5 and said, ‘For this reason a man shall leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh’? 6 “So they are no longer two, but one flesh. What therefore God has joined together, let no man separate.’” 
So Jesus is telling us that marriage is based on the work of God. It is he who ordained the union and established it. The original form of marriage was monogamy, not polygamy, permanence, not divorce, and it was between a man and a woman. This excludes the possibility of allowing [homosexual marriage](https://carm.org/homosexuality-secular-movements/are-homosexuality-and-homosexual-marriage-moral-issues/). Nevertheless, Jesus made a qualification for divorce.
