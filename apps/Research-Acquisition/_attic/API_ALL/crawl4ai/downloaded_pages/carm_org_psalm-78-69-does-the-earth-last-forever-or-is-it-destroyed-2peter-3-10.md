# Does the earth last forever (Psalm 78:69), or is it destroyed (2 Peter 3:10)?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 22, 2022 
4 min read 
Does the earth last forever, or is it destroyed? ￼ ![](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Earth-on-Fire-300x228.jpg) [Psalm 78:69](https://ref.ly/Ps%2078.69;nasb95?t=biblia) says, “And He built His sanctuary like the heights, Like the earth which He has founded forever.” But [2 Peter 3:10](https://ref.ly/2%20Pet%203.10;nasb95?t=biblia) says, “But the day of the Lord will come like a thief, in which the heavens will pass away with a roar and the elements will be destroyed with intense heat, and the earth and its works will be burned up.” So which one is correct? Does the earth last forever, or is it destroyed? The answer is simple. They are both correct.
Scriptures teach that the earth will last forever.
When the Bible talks about the earth lasting forever, it is not saying that the earth cannot be changed or remade. This is what is talking about when it says the earth and its works are burned up ([2 Pet. 3:10](https://ref.ly/2%20Pet.%203.10;nasb95?t=biblia)). It is so the new heavens, and new earth can be made. God is going to clean up the universe and all that is in it.
