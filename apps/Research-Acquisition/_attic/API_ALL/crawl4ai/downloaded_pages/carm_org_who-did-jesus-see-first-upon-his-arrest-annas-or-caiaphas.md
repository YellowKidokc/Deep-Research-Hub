# Who did Jesus see first upon His arrest, Annas or Caiaphas?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
4 min read 
Who did Jesus see first upon His arrest, Annas or Caiaphas? It was Annas. Let’s take a look at [Matthew 26:57](https://ref.ly/Matt%2026.57;nasb95?t=biblia) and [John 18:19](https://ref.ly/John%2018.19;nasb95?t=biblia), [24](https://ref.ly/John%2018.24;nasb95?t=biblia).
At the time of Jesus, there were two high priests: Annas and Ciaphas. [Luke 3:2](https://ref.ly/Luke%203.2;nasb95?t=biblia) says, “In the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas, the word of God came to John, the son of Zacharias, in the wilderness.” Also, [Acts 4:5-6](https://ref.ly/Acts%204.5-6;nasb95?t=biblia) says, “And it came about on the next day, that their rulers and elders and scribes were gathered together in Jerusalem; 6 and Annas the high priest was there, and Caiaphas and John and Alexander, and all who were of high-priestly descent.” [John 18:19](https://ref.ly/John%2018.19;nasb95?t=biblia) says that the high priest questioned Jesus, and then in verse 24, it says that Annas (supposedly the high priest of verse 19) sent Jesus to Caiaphas, who was, as we have seen, also the High Priest. Furthermore, when [Matthew 26:57](https://ref.ly/Matt%2026.57;nasb95?t=biblia) says that Jesus was led to Caiaphas, it is not denying that He first saw Annas. ![Who Jesus saw first](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/Jesus-23.jpg)
Therefore, Annas saw Jesus first, and then He was sent to see Caiaphas. Caiaphas was the high priest at the time of Jesus’ trial. Annas initially questioned Jesus, and then the proceedings followed, which led to Jesus being crucified.
