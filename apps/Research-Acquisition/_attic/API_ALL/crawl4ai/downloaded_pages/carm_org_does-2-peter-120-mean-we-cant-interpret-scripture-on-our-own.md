# Does 2 Peter 1:20 mean we can’t interpret scripture on our own?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
January 31, 2018 
25 min read 
Roman Catholics routinely appeal to [2 Peter 1:20](https://ref.ly/2%20Pet%201.20;nasb95?t=biblia) for justification of their position that Protestants are not allowed to properly interpret Scripture because they don’t have authority. ￼ ![plain](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/RCcoursestudent-300x112.jpg) Their approach is interesting because they are appealing to Scripture to justify why we Protestants can’t do the same thing; namely, appeal to Scripture for our position. Interesting.
Let’s take a look at the verse. I’ll quote the verse from several versions, including one from the Vatican website.
Notice that the verse says, “prophecy OF scripture.” It does not say, “no scripture” is of any personal interpretation. Prophecy is a subset of Scripture. It is not Scripture as a whole. So, what is prophecy of Scripture? Well, its prophecy. Let’s look at the context.
