### Notes
  1. _In the beginning_ ([1:1-2:3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#1)) 
Compare with [Gen 2:4-25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/2.html#4) in which the order of events is entirely different.
The Genesis 1 account conflicts with the order of events that are known to science.
The earth and "heaven" are created together "in the beginning," whereas according to current estimates, the earth and universe are about 4.6 and 13.8 billion years old, respectively.
Also in the first creation account, the earth is created before light, sun and stars; birds and whales before reptiles and insects; and flowering plants before any animals. The order of events known from science is in each case just the opposite.
[The two creations](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/accounts.html)
[When was heaven created?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/heaven.html)
[Who created heaven and earth?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/who_created.html)
[When was the universe created?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/interp/universe.html)
[The Gap Theory](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/interp/gap.html)
  2. _Let there be light._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#3)) 
These are God's first spoken words. It's not clear who he was talking to.
God continues to talk (to himself?) throughout the creation process. (See verses [6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#6), [9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#9), [11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#11), [14-15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#14), [20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#20), [22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#22), [24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#24), and [26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#26).)
But after creating animals, God stops talking to himself and begins talking to them. (See [verse 22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#22).)
  3. _God divided the light from the darkness._ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#4)) 
Yet he didn't make the light producing objects (the sun and the stars) until the fourth day ([1:14-19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#14)).
And how could there be "the evening and the morning" on the first day if there was no sun to mark them?
[When did God divide light from darkness?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/light.html)
[How long did creation take?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/days.html)
  4. _The first day_ ([v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#5)) 
When did the first day begin? Did God create heaven and earth before or during the first day? And when did his spirit move on the waters? Did day 1 begin with the creation of light?
It's impossible to tell from the text, but it seems more likely that God began his workweek with with the creation of light. So I'm calling everything before that "Day Zero".
  5. _A firmament_ ([v.6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#6)) 
God spends one-sixth of his entire creative effort (the second day) working on a solid firmament. This strange structure, which God calls heaven, is intended to separate the higher waters from the lower waters.
  6. _Let them be for signs._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#14)) 
God placed the sun, moon, and stars in the firmament so that they can be used "for signs". This, of course, is exactly what astrologers do: read "the signs" in the Zodiac to predict what will happen on Earth.
[Does the bible condemn astrology?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/astrology.html)
[What the Bible says about astrology](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/astrology.html)
  7. _God made two great lights._ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#16)) 
But the moon is not a light; it only reflects light from the sun. And why, if God made the moon to "rule the night", does it spend half of its time moving through the daytime sky?
  8. _He made the stars also._ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#16)) 
God spends a day making light (before making the sun and stars) and separating light from darkness; then, at the end of a hard day's work, and almost as an afterthought, he makes [1024 stars](https://www.space.com/26078-how-many-stars-are-there.html).
[When were the stars made?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/stars_made.html)
  9. _To give light upon the earth_ ([v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#17)) 
Then why is only a tiny fraction of stars visible from earth? Under the best conditions, no more than a few thousand stars are visible with the unaided eye, yet there are hundreds of billions of stars in our galaxy and a hundred billion or so galaxies. Were they all created "to give light upon the earth"?
  10. _Let the waters bring forth abundantly the moving creature that hath life, and fowl._ ([v.20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#20)) 
Notice that the waters brought forth the living creatures -- God didn't create them directly.
The same language is used to describe the origin of land animals [verse 11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#11) in and plants in [verse 24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#24).

  17. _To every beast ... I have given every green herb for meat._ ([v.30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#30)) 
All animals were originally herbivores. Tapeworms, vampire bats, mosquitoes, and barracudas -- all were strict vegetarians, as created by God.
  18. _Behold, it was very good._ ([v.31](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/1.html#31)) 
God purposefully designed a system that ensures the suffering and death of all his creatures, parasite and host, predator and prey.
[Was God satisfied with his creation?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/satisfied.html)
