# Did Jesus know the future?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 19, 2017 
3 min read 
Jesus definately knew the future. Being God in flesh ([John 1:1](https://ref.ly/John%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia), [14](https://ref.ly/John%201.14;nasb95?t=biblia); [Colossians 2:9](https://ref.ly/Col%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia)) because he had ordained it ([Ephesians 1:11](https://ref.ly/Eph%201.11;nasb95?t=biblia)). Please consider the following verses that show that Jesus prophesied about the future.
So, we can see that Jesus prophesied the future. Some of the prophecies are yet to be fulfilled but others were fulfilled in his time. Nevertheless, what we do with statements where, for example, [Jesus did not know the time of his return?](https://carm.org/bible-difficulties/if-the-holy-spirit-is-god-why-didnt-he-know-the-time-of-christs-return-man/) Basically, we have to understand that Jesus was cooperating with the limitations of being a man ([Philippians 2: 5-8](https://ref.ly/Phil%202.%205-8;nasb95?t=biblia)) because he was made under the law ([Galatians 4:4](https://ref.ly/Gal%204.4;nasb95?t=biblia)). There is a paradox in that he was both divine and human in one person and we cannot exhaustively determine the relationship between his two natures and his knowledge. Some things we just have to take on faith. Nevertheless, Jesus prophesied the future, yet he also grew in wisdom and stature ([Luke 2:52](https://ref.ly/Luke%202.52;nasb95?t=biblia)).
