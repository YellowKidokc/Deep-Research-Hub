# If Jesus is God, then why did He say that the Father was greater than He?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 24, 2008 
4 min read 
Jesus said the Father was greater than Him. But, it was not because Jesus is not God. Instead, it was because Jesus was in a lower position than God the Father. He was “…made for a little while lower than the angels…” ([Heb. 2:9](https://ref.ly/Heb.%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia)). In [Gal. 4:4](https://ref.ly/Gal.%204.4;nasb95?t=biblia), it says He was made under the Law. So, as someone under the Law, he had to pray to the Father and do the Father’s will. Also, in [Phil. 2:5-8](https://ref.ly/Phil.%202.5-8;nasb95?t=biblia), it says that Jesus “emptied Himself, taking the form of a bond-servant, and being made in the likeness of men…” ![Father was greater](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/father-is-greater-300x300.jpg)
“You heard that I said to you, ‘I go away, and I will come to you.’ If you loved Me, you would have rejoiced, because I go to the Father; for the Father is greater than I,” ([John 14:28](https://ref.ly/John%2014.28;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Jesus has two natures: divine and human. Jesus was not denying that He was God. He was merely acknowledging the fact that He was also a man. Jesus is both God and man. As a man, he was in a lesser position than the Father because he had added to Himself human nature ([Col. 2:9](https://ref.ly/Col.%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia)) and was made under the Law ([Gal. 4:4](https://ref.ly/Gal.%204.4;nasb95?t=biblia)). He became a man to die for people ([1 Cor. 15:1-4](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor.%2015.1-4;nasb95?t=biblia)).
