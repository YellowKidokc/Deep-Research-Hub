# Annihilationism, Ecclesiastes 9:5, and the dead know nothing
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 11, 2018 
5 min read 
“For the living know they will die; but the dead do not know anything, nor have they any longer a reward, for their memory is forgotten. Indeed their love, their hate, and their zeal have already perished, and they will no longer have a share in all that is done under the sun,” ([Ecclesiastes 9:5](https://ref.ly/Eccles%209.5;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Conditionalists often use [Ecclesiastes 9:5](https://ref.ly/Eccles%209.5;nasb95?t=biblia) to demonstrate that the human soul is not conscious after death. The reason they do this is that if the soul is immortal, then annihilationism is not true. Therefore, they look for verses that they can interpret in a manner consistent with their conclusion.
Nevertheless, Ecclesiastes is speaking relative to the human perspective and how things are seen “under the sun,” a phrase that is repeated throughout the book ([Ecc. 1:3](https://ref.ly/Eccles.%201.3;nasb95?t=biblia), [9](https://ref.ly/Ecc%201.9;nasb95?t=biblia), [14](https://ref.ly/Ecc%201.14;nasb95?t=biblia); [2:11](https://ref.ly/Ecc%202.11;nasb95?t=biblia), [17](https://ref.ly/Ecc%202.17;nasb95?t=biblia), [18](https://ref.ly/Ecc%202.18;nasb95?t=biblia), [19](https://ref.ly/Ecc%202.19;nasb95?t=biblia), [20](https://ref.ly/Ecc%202.20;nasb95?t=biblia), [22](https://ref.ly/Ecc%202.22;nasb95?t=biblia); [3:16](https://ref.ly/Ecc%203.16;nasb95?t=biblia); [4:1](https://ref.ly/Ecc%204.1;nasb95?t=biblia), [3](https://ref.ly/Ecc%204.3;nasb95?t=biblia), [7](https://ref.ly/Ecc%204.7;nasb95?t=biblia), [15](https://ref.ly/Ecc%204.15;nasb95?t=biblia); [5:13](https://ref.ly/Ecc%205.13;nasb95?t=biblia), [18](https://ref.ly/Ecc%205.18;nasb95?t=biblia); [6:1](https://ref.ly/Ecc%206.1;nasb95?t=biblia), [12](https://ref.ly/Ecc%206.12;nasb95?t=biblia); [8:9](https://ref.ly/Ecc%208.9;nasb95?t=biblia), [15](https://ref.ly/Ecc%208.15;nasb95?t=biblia), [17](https://ref.ly/Ecc%208.17;nasb95?t=biblia); [9:3](https://ref.ly/Ecc%209.3;nasb95?t=biblia), [6](https://ref.ly/Ecc%209.6;nasb95?t=biblia), [9](https://ref.ly/Ecc%209.9;nasb95?t=biblia), [11](https://ref.ly/Ecc%209.11;nasb95?t=biblia), [13](https://ref.ly/Ecc%209.13;nasb95?t=biblia); [10:5](https://ref.ly/Ecc%2010.5;nasb95?t=biblia) ). Solomon laments that the “fate of the sons of men and the fate of beasts is the same,” (3:19), that they go to the same place (3:20), and that “the day of one’s death is better than the day of one’s birth,” (7:1). (See the article [Annihilationism, Ecclesiastes 12:7, and the soul returning to God](https://carm.org/annihilationism/annihilationism-ecclesiastes-127-and-the-soul-returning-to-god/))
