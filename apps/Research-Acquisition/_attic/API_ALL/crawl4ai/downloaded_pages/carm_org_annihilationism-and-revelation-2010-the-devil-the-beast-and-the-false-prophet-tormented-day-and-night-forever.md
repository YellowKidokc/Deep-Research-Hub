# Annihilationism and Revelation 20:10, the devil, the beast, and the false prophet, tormented day and night forever
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 11, 2018 
10 min read 
“And the devil who deceived them was thrown into the Lake of Fire and brimstone, where the beast and the false prophet are also; and they will be tormented day and night forever and ever,” ([Revelation 20:10](https://ref.ly/Rev%2020.10;nasb95?t=biblia)).
This is a very significant verse in support of eternal conscious torment. It clearly says at three beings are thrown into the Lake of Fire to be tormented without end. But, how do the conditionalists respond? In summary, here are some of the basic issues the annihilationists raise concerning [Revelation 20:10](https://ref.ly/Rev%2020.10;nasb95?t=biblia). I can’t list every question and objection they’ve raised. So, I’ve stuck with the main ones and responded to each.
In reading conditionalist literature, I’ve constantly read that the Lake of Fire is symbolic. But, who’s to say it isn’t a literal lake? Now, of course, normally, a lake is usually surrounded by a landmass. But the concept of a large body of fire into which people are thrown would be well described in the phrase “Lake of Fire.” When the conditionalist “says” it isn’t an actual Lake of Fire, what makes them right? Why can’t it be an area where fire is constantly burning? I asked the question because it needs to be asked.
