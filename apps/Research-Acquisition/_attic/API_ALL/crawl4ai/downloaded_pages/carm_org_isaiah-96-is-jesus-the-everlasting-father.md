# Isaiah 9:6, Is Jesus the Everlasting Father?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 08, 2008 
3 min read 
Oneness Pentecostal believers deny the [Trinity](https://carm.org/doctrine-and-theology/trinity-chart/) and teach that Jesus, the Father, and the Holy Spirit are all one person. They sometimes quote [Isaiah 9:6](https://ref.ly/Isa%209.6;nasb95?t=biblia) in their attempt to prove their position. However, [Isaiah 9:6](https://ref.ly/Isa%209.6;nasb95?t=biblia) cannot be used to disprove the Trinity nor bolster their Oneness doctrine.
“For a child will be born to us, a son will be given to us, and the government will rest on His shoulders; And His name will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Eternal Father, Prince of Peace” ([Isaiah 9:6](https://ref.ly/Isa%209.6;nasb95?t=biblia)).
When [Isaiah 9:6](https://ref.ly/Isa%209.6;nasb95?t=biblia) says that Jesus’ name will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Eternal Father, etc., it is not saying that Jesus is the eternal Father, but that he has the characteristics of God. In other words, Jesus has all the attributes of God–including eternality.
