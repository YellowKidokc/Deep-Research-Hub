### Notes
  1. _Jesus Christ, the Son of God_ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/1.html#1)) 
The first verse of the Gospel of Matthew introduces Jesus as the son of David. Mark does the same, except he calls Jesus the Son of God.
[Was Jesus the son of David?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/Son_of_David.html)
  2. _As it is written in the prophets, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, which shall prepare thy way before thee._ ([v.2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/1.html#2)) 
Mark claims that John the Baptist fulfilled the prophecy given in Malachi ([3:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mal/3.html#1), [4:1, 5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mal/4.html#1)). But the Malachi prophecy says that God will send Elijah before "the great and dreadful day of the LORD" in which the world will be consumed by fire. John the Baptist flatly denied that he was Elijah (Elias) in [John 1:21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/1.html#21) and the earth was not destroyed after John's appearance.
The phrase "as it is written in the prophets" is not found in the oldest and best Greek manuscripts which say, rather, "as it is written in Isaiah." Scribes made the change to correct the mistake of attributing the quotation to Isaiah, since the first part of the quote (v.2) is not from Isaiah, but from [Ex 23:20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/23.html#20) and [Mal 3:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mal/3.html#1).  
See Bart D. Ehrman, [Misquoting Jesus](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0060738170/skepticsannotate), (2005), pp.94-5
  3. _John did baptize ... for the remission of sins._ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/1.html#4)) 
[Did Jesus sin?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jesus_sin.html)
  4. _All the land of Judea, and they of Jerusalem, and were all baptized of him in the river of Jordan._ ([v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/1.html#5)) 
[Where did John baptize?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jordan.html)
  5. _Thou art my beloved son._ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/1.html#11)) 
[How did God address Jesus at his baptism?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/this_or_thou.html)
  6. _Immediately the spirit driveth him into the wilderness._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/1.html#12)) 
[What did Jesus do after his baptism?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/postbaptism.html)
  7. _As he walked by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew his brother casting a net into the sea._ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/1.html#16)) 
[Which came first: the calling of Peter and Andrew or the imprisonment of John the Baptist?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/imprisonment.html)
[Did Jesus heal Peter's mother in law before or after calling Peter?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/peters-mil.html)
[How did Peter asnd Andrew become Jesus's disciples?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/calling.html)
  8. _They went into Capernaum; and straightway on the sabbath day he entered into the synagogue ... when they were come out of the synagogue, they entered into the house of Simon and Andrew_ ([v.21, 29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/1.html#21)) 
[Where was the home of Peter and Andrew?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/peters_home.html)
  9. _A man with an unclean spirit ... cried out, Saying ... I know thee who thou art, the Holy One of God._ ([vv.23-26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/1.html#23)) 
Jesus casts out a godly unclean spirit.
[Are those who believe that Jesus is the Christ "of God"?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/of_god.html)
  10. _Simon's wife's mother_ ([v.30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/1.html#30)) 
Simon's mother-in-law was sick with a fever. Jesus healed her by touching her hand, so she could get back to serving them.
(Simon was later [renamed "Peter" by Jesus](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/16.html#S3). So, yeah, the first pope was married.)
[Did Jesus heal Peter's mother in law before or after calling Peter?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/peters-mil.html)
  11. _Jesus, moved with compassion_ ([v.41](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/1.html#41)) 
Some manuscripts say that Jesus was moved with anger, not compassion. Bart Ehrman in [Misquoting Jesus](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0060738170/skepticsannotate) (p.133-138) argues that the original text probably said that Jesus reacted with wrath rather than compassion, and was later changed by scribes to make the verse less problematic.
