# Who was Jesus?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 05, 2016 
5 min read 
Jesus, the founder of Christianity, lived approximately 2000 years ago and is the most influential person in history. The [Bible](https://carm.org/bible/) claims that Jesus is God in flesh ([John 1:1](https://ref.ly/John%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia), [14](https://ref.ly/John%201.14;nasb95?t=biblia); [Colossians 2:9](https://ref.ly/Col%202.9;nasb95?t=biblia)). What we know about him is found mainly in the New Testament, particularly in the four Gospels: Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John. Of course, there are critics who would say that Jesus never existed and there are others who say that the gospel accounts are exaggerations and myth. However, the New Testament documents are extremely reliable and are dated very early. For example, the book of Acts is a book about the history of the early church. It does not contain the historical markers of the death of Paul and Peter, which occurred no later than 67 A.D., some say 64 A.D. But Luke, who wrote the book of Acts and was a companion of Paul, also wrote the gospel named after him. The latest it was written was 64 A.D., but possibly around 61 A.D. The gospel of Mark was written a few years before that, in the ’50s. This would put the gospel writings at a very early date which would mean that they were written at the time that the apostles were alive. In other words, what was written about Jesus was written early and did not have enough time for legend and myth to creep into the accounts.
Jesus taught many things and is widely considered to be one of the wisest people who ever lived. Of course, within Christianity, it would say he was the greatest teacher. His parables, the sermon on the Mount ([Matthew 5:1-7: 27](https://ref.ly/Matt%205.1-7.%2027;nasb95?t=biblia)), and [his miracles](https://carm.org/about-jesus/what-are-the-miracles-of-jesus/) set him apart from all other religious leaders.
According to Scripture, Jesus Christ is the Messiah, the second person of the [Trinity](https://carm.org/doctrine-and-theology/trinity-chart/), God in flesh ([John 1:1](https://ref.ly/John%201.1;nasb95?t=biblia), [14](https://ref.ly/John%201.14;nasb95?t=biblia)), who came to earth, was born under the law ([Galatians 4:4](https://ref.ly/Gal%204.4;nasb95?t=biblia)), and was the proper sacrifice for our sins ([1 Pet. 2:24](https://ref.ly/1%20Pet.%202.24;nasb95?t=biblia)). He was crucified, buried, and rose from the dead ([1 Corinthians 15:1-4](https://ref.ly/1%20Cor%2015.1-4;nasb95?t=biblia)).
