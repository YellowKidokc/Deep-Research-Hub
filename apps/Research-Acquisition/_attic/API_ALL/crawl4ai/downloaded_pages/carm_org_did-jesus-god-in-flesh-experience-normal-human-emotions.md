# Did Jesus, God in flesh, experience normal human emotions?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 31, 2024 
3 min read 
Yes, Jesus experienced normal human emotions. This is because Jesus is both God and man, i.e., the hypostatic union. Being a man, He would have experienced the normal emotions that a man would have experienced – otherwise, how is He a man? The range of emotions attributed to Jesus are as follows: Anger (Matt. 21:12-13), Compassion (Matt. 9:36; 14:14; Mark 1:41), Dread (Luke 22:44), Distress (Mark 14:33), Fear (Matt. 26:38), Grief/Sadness (Matt. 23:37; 26:38), Love (John 11:5, 36), Rejoicing/Joy (Luke 10:21), and Sadness (John 11:35).[ ![Jesus experience human emotions](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Jesus-09sqs.jpg) ](https://carm.org/attachment/jesus-09sqs/)
In addition to the hypostatic union, the attributes of both natures are ascribed to the single person. This is called the communication idiomatum. It means that Jesus experiences the attributes of both God and man. Since He was a man (and still is, 1 Tim. 2:5; Col. 2:9), He would have experienced normal human emotions. He did.
