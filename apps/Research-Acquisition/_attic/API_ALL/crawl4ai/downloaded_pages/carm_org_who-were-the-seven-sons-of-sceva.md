# Who were the seven sons of Sceva?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 30, 2017 
3 min read 
The seven sons of Sceva were the sons of a Jewish chief priest ([Acts 19:14](https://ref.ly/Acts%2019.14;nasb95?t=biblia)). When Paul was on his third missionary journey in Ephesus ([Acts 19:1](https://ref.ly/Acts%2019.1;nasb95?t=biblia)), he was performing many miracles ([Acts 19:11](https://ref.ly/Acts%2019.11;nasb95?t=biblia)). There was a chief priest there by the name of Sceva whose sons were apparently either exorcists by practice or were capitalizing on the work Paul was doing. While there handkerchiefs and aprons were carried from Paul to the sick and people were being healed, and evil spirits were being exercised ([Acts 19:12](https://ref.ly/Acts%2019.12;nasb95?t=biblia)). But there were Jewish exorcists who traveled the area, and they were casting out demons in the name of Jesus ([Acts 19:13](https://ref.ly/Acts%2019.13;nasb95?t=biblia)). The seven sons of the high priest Sceva were also doing this, but it didn’t go well for them.
[Acts 19:15–16](https://ref.ly/Acts%2019.15%E2%80%9316;nasb95?t=biblia), “And the evil spirit answered and said to them, “I recognize Jesus, and I know about Paul, but who are you?” 16 And the man, in whom was the evil spirit, leaped on them and subdued all of them and overpowered them, so that they fled out of that house naked and wounded.”
The reason there were assaulted by the evil spirit was that they were not believers in Christ and were not Christians. Therefore they did not have the proper authority ([John 1:12](https://ref.ly/John%201.12;nasb95?t=biblia)). They were merely using the name of Jesus as a formula, as a kind of magic spell by which they might cast out demons.
