# What did Jesus say to Satan when He was tempted?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
February 28, 2015 
4 min read 
In the Bible, when the devil tempted Jesus, he quoted Scripture. Jesus likewise quoted Scripture in each of His responses. Let’s take a look at the temptations and responses. Jesus’ words are in Red. Quotes from the Old Testament are in small caps. The devil’s words are underlined. Notice that Satan quotes scripture, too. But his intent is to use it to deceive.
