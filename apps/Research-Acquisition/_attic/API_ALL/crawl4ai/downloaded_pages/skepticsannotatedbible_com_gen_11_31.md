### CHAPTER 11
The Tower of Babel
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif)![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 11:1-9
1 And **the whole earth was of one language** , and of one speech.
Before the Tower of Babel was constructed, all humans spoke the same language. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#1n)]
2 And it came to pass, as they journeyed from the east, that they found a plain in the land of Shinar; and they dwelt there.
At this time, everyone on earth traveled to the plain of Shinar and lived there.
3 And they said one to another, Go to, let us make brick, and burn them thoroughly. And they had brick for stone, and slime had they for morter.
4 And they said, Go to, **let us build us a city and a tower, whose top may reach unto heaven** ; and let us make us a name, lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth.
They said to each other,
> Let's get some brick and slime, and build a tower that reaches to heaven.
> We need to make ourselves famous, so we aren't scattered all over the face of the earth.
5 **And the LORD came down to see the city and the tower** , which the children of men builded.
God came down to see the tower that the people had built. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#2n)]
6 And the LORD said, **Behold, the people is one, and** **they have all one language** ; and this they begin to do: and **now nothing will be restrained from them, which they have imagined to do**.
7 Go to, **let us go down** , **and** there **confound their language** , that they may not understand one another's speech.
And he said to himself (themselves?):
> These people are unified and have one language. If they can build a tower, they can do anything.
> Let us go down and mess up their speech so they can't understand each other. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#3n)]
8 So the LORD scattered them abroad from thence upon the face of all the earth: and they left off to build the city.
9 Therefore is the name of it called Babel; because **the LORD did there confound the language of all the earth** : and from thence did the LORD scatter them abroad upon the face of all the earth.
So God confounded the language of the people who were building the tower, and scattered them over the face of the earth.
From Shem to Abraham

![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/wom1.gif) ![Family Values](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/fv.gif) 11:27-32
27 Now these are the generations of Terah: Terah begat Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran begat Lot.
Terah was Abraham's father and Lot was Abraham's nephew.
28 And Haran died before his father Terah in the land of his nativity, in Ur of the Chaldees.
Haran (Abraham's brother and Lot's father) died in Ur of the Chaldees. [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#7n)]

Abraham married Sarah, who could not have children. [[8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#8n)]

Terah, Abraham, Sarah, and Lot traveled from Ur of the Chaldees to Haran. [[10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#10n)]
32 And the days of Terah were two hundred and five years: and Terah died in Haran.
Terah died in Haran when he was 205 years old.

### Notes
  1. _The whole earth was of one language._ [[v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#1)] 
This could not be true, of course, since by this time (supposedly around 2400 BCE) there were already many languages, each unintelligible to the others.
This is even admitted earlier in Genesis ([10:5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/10.html#15), [20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/10.html#20), [31](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/10.html#31)) where other languages are mentioned before the tower of Babel was supposedly constructed.
[How many languages before Babel?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/babel.html)
  2. _The LORD came down to see the city and the tower._ [[v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#5)] 
Couldn't God see the tower from where he was sitting?
[Does God know everything?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/knows.html)
  3. _Let us go down, and there confound their language._ [[v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#7)] 
Maybe God hasn't been talking to himself; maybe there is more than one of them up there.
Well, however many there may be, they all decide to come down to confuse the builders by confounding human language and scattering them [humans] abroad.
[Is God the author of confusion?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/confusion.html)
[How many gods are there?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gods.html)
  4. _These are the generations of Shem._ [[v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#10)] 
Another boring genealogy that we are told to avoid in [1 Tim 1:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1tim/1.html#4) and [Titus 3:9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/tit/3.html#9). ("Avoid foolish questions and genealogies.")
Also note the [ridiculously long lives](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/abs/lifespans.html "Incredibly long life spans") of the patriarchs.
[How long is the human life span?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/lifespan.html)
  5. _Arphaxad ... and begat Salah._ [[v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#12)] 
[Who was Salah's father?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/salah.html)
  6. _Terah lived seventy years, and begat[Abram](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/topics/abram.html)._ [[v.26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#26)] 
Who was Abraham's father? Azar or Terah ([Quran 6:74](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/quran/6/index.htm#74))? Which is right, the Bible or the Quran?
[How old was Terah when he died?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/terah.html)
[How old was Abraham when he left Haran?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/oldabe.html)
[How old was Abraham when Ishmael was born?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/abram_ishmael.html)
  7. _Ur of the Chaldees_ [[v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#28)] 
"Ur of the Chaldees" is probably a reference to the city of [Ur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ur#Identification_with_Biblical_Ur) in southern Mesopotamia, in present-day Iraq.
  8. _Sarai was barren; she had no child._ [[vv.29-30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#29)] 
[Barren women in the Bible](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/topics/barren-women-in-the-bible.html)
  9. _Nahor's wife, Milcah, the daughter of Haran_ [[v.29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#29)] 
More Genesis family values: Abraham's brother, Nahaor, married his niece.
  10. _They came unto Haran._ [[v.31](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/gen/11.html#31)] 
[Did God call Abraham before or after he moved to Haran?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/haran.html)
[Haran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haran_\(biblical_place\)) is usually identified with a town in modern-day Turkey.
