## [Exodus](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/outline.html "Exodus Outline")
### CHAPTER 31
Bezaleel and Aholiab
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 31:1-11, Cf. [Ex 35:30-36:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/35.html#S4)
1 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,
2 See, I have called by name Bezaleel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah:
3 And I have filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship,
God said to Moses, 
> See, I have filled Bezaleel with my spirit, in wisdom, understanding, knowledge and all manner of workmanship skills.
4 To devise cunning works, to work in gold, and in silver, and in brass,
5 And in cutting of stones, to set them, and in carving of timber, to work in all manner of workmanship.
> He will devise cunning works for me out of gold, silver, brass, stones, and wood. 
6 And I, behold, I have given with him Aholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan: and in the hearts of all that are wise hearted I have put wisdom, that they may make all that I have commanded thee;
7 The tabernacle of the congregation, and the ark of the testimony, and the mercy seat that is thereupon, and all the furniture of the tabernacle,
8 And the table and his furniture, and the pure candlestick with all his furniture, and the altar of incense,
9 And the altar of burnt offering with all his furniture, and the laver and his foot,
10 And the cloths of service, and the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons, to minister in the priest's office,
11 And the anointing oil, and sweet incense for the holy place: according to all that I have commanded thee shall they do.
> And behold, I have given Aholiab to Bezaleel to help him make all that I have commanded, including: the tabernacle, the ark of the testimony, mercy seat, tabernacle furniture, table, candlestick, incense altar, burnt offering altar, laver and his foot, service cloths, holy garments, anointing oil, and incense.
The Sabbath
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/cr1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/int.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif)31: 12-18  
Cf. [Ex 35:2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/35.html#sabbath), [Num 15:32-36](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/15.html#32)
12 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,
13 Speak thou also unto the children of Israel, saying, Verily my sabbaths ye shall keep: for it is a sign between me and you throughout your generations; that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you.
God said to Moses, 
> Keep my sabbaths.
> It's a sign between me and you throughout your generations, so that you will know that I'm God.
14 **Ye shall keep the sabbath** therefore; for it is holy unto you: **every one that defileth it shall surely be put to death** : for whosoever doeth any work therein, that soul shall be cut off from among his people.
> You will keep the sabbath, because it is holy to you.
> Everyone who defiles it will be killed. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/31.html#1n)]
15 Six days may work be done; but in the seventh is the sabbath of rest, holy to the LORD: **whosoever doeth any work in the sabbath day, he shall surely be put to death.**
> You can work on six days, but the seventh is the sabbath of rest. It's holy to me.
> Whoever does any work on the sabbath day will be killed.
16 Wherefore the children of Israel shall keep the sabbath, to observe the sabbath throughout their generations, for a perpetual covenant.
> The sabbath must be observed forever as a perpetual covenant.
17 It is a sign between me and the children of Israel for ever: for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, and on the seventh day he rested, and was refreshed.
> It's a sign between me and the children of Israel forever, because I made heaven and earth in six days, and on the seventh I rested and was refreshed. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/31.html#2n)]
18 And he gave unto Moses, when he had made an end of communing with him upon mount Sinai, two tables of testimony, tables of stone, written with **the finger of God**.
When God finally finished his instructions to Moses on Mount Sinai, he gave him two stone tablets written with his finger. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/31.html#3n)]

### Notes
  1. _Ye shall keep the sabbath ... every one that defileth it shall surely be put to death._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/31.html#14)) 
[Is it necessary to keep the sabbath?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sabbath.html)
[To kill or not to kill](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/kill.html)
[How should Sabbath-breakers be punished?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sabbath_breakers.html)
[Does God approve of capital punishment?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/capital.html)
[What the Bible says about capital punishment](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/capital-punishment.html)
  2. _On the seventh day he rested._ ([v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/31.html#17)) 
Even God gets tired now and then.
[Does God ever tire?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/weary.html)
  3. _He gave unto Moses, when he had made an end of communing with him upon mount Sinai, two tables of testimony, tables of stone, written with the finger of God._ ([v.18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/31.html#18)) 
God finally stops talking to Moses. In the last seven chapters, God has done all the talking. He described in detail all the gifts that he wanted the people to give to him; how he wanted the tabernacle built; how to make priests and priestly garments; how to do animal sacrifices; how make altars, oils, and perfume; and how to observe the sabbath (and kill those who don't).
[Where did Moses receive the ten commandments?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sinai.html)  
[What the Bible says about God's finger](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/finger-of-god.html)
