# Does Jeremiah 29:11 apply to Christians today?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
September 04, 2024 
4 min read 
[Jeremiah 29:11](https://ref.ly/Jer%2029.11;nasb95?t=biblia) says, “‘For I know the plans that I have for you,’ declares the LORD, ‘plans for welfare and not for calamity to give you a future and a hope.” A lot of Christians take this verse and apply it to themselves. But the verse is not addressed to Christians, nor is it about Christians. It’s about the Jewish exiles in Babylon.So, does [Jeremiah 29:11](https://ref.ly/Jer%2029.11;nasb95?t=biblia) apply to Christians today? Yes and no.[ ![Jeremiah 29:11](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/jeremiah29-11.jpg) ](https://carm.org/attachment/jeremiah29-11/) Let’s take a look.
The context of [Jer. 29:11](https://ref.ly/Jer.%2029.11;nasb95?t=biblia) is the first thing we must go to to so we can understand what it is saying, who it is addressing, and why is it being said.
So, the context is not about [Christians](https://carm.org/questions/about-christianity/). It is addressing the Jewish exiles at the time of Nebuchadnezzar because they were in exile. God’s promise was to them, not Christians. So, no, the verse does not apply to Christians today.
