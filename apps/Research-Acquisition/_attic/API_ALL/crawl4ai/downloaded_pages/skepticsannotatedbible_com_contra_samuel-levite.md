## Was Samuel a Levite or an Ephraimite?
### He was a Levite
> The sons of _Levi_ ; Gershon, _Kohath_ , and Merari. [1 Chronicles 6:16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/6.html#16)
> The sons of Kohath; Amminadab his son, Korah his son, Assir his son, [1 Chronicles 6:22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/6.html#22)
> _Elkanah_ his son, and Ebiasaph his son, and Assir his son, [1 Chronicles 6:23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/6.html#23)
> Elkanah knew Hannah his wife; and the LORD remembered her. [1 Samuel 1:19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1sam/1.html#19)
> Hannah had conceived, that she bare a son, and called his name _Samuel_. [1 Samuel 1:21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1sam/1.html#21)
### He was an Ephraimite
(Since his father Elkanah was an Ephraimite.)
> Now there was a certain man of Ramathaimzophim, of mount Ephraim, and his name was _Elkanah_ , the son of Jeroham, the son of Elihu, the son of Tohu, the son of Zuph, _an Ephrathite_. [1 Samuel 1:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1sam/1.html#1)
