### Notes
  1. _The children of Israel gave by lot unto the Levites these cities with their suburbs, as the LORD commanded by the hand of Moses._ ([v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/21.html#8)) 
[Is gambling condemned by the bible?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gambling.html)
  2. _Out of the tribe of Dan ... Aijalon with her suburbs_ ([vv.23-24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/21.html#23)) 
[What tribe was Aijalon from?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/aijalon.html)
  3. _The LORD delivered all their enemies into their hand._ ([v.44](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jos/21.html#44)) 
According to these verses, God fulfilled his promise to give the Israelites all of the lands that they encountered. But in several places the Bible tells us that [these promises were not kept](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/topics/destroy-nations.html).
[Did God destroy the nations?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/driveout.html)
