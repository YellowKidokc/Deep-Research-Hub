### CHAPTER 34
Moses dies
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 34:1-12
1 And Moses went up from the plains of Moab unto the mountain of Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, that is over against Jericho. And the LORD shewed him all the land of Gilead, unto Dan,
2 And all Naphtali, and the land of Ephraim, and Manasseh, and all the land of Judah, unto the utmost sea,
3 And the south, and the plain of the valley of Jericho, the city of palm trees, unto Zoar.
Moses climbed the mountain of Nebo, where God showed him the promised land.
4 And the LORD said unto him, This is the land which I sware unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob, saying, I will give it unto thy seed: I have caused thee to see it with thine eyes, but thou shalt not go over thither.
God said to him, 
> This is the land I swore to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 
> I've let you see it with your own eyes, but you won't go there.
5 So Moses the servant of the LORD died there in the land of Moab, according to the word of the LORD.
Moses died there in the land of Moab, according to the word of the Lord. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/34.html#1n)]
6 And he buried him in a valley in the land of Moab, over against Bethpeor: but no man knoweth of his sepulchre unto this day.
7 And Moses was an hundred and twenty years old when he died: his eye was not dim, nor his natural force abated.
8 And the children of Israel wept for Moses in the plains of Moab thirty days: so the days of weeping and mourning for Moses were ended.
Moses died when he was 120. His sight was still good and he was as strong as ever. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/34.html#2n)]
9 And Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom; for Moses had laid his hands upon him: and the children of Israel hearkened unto him, and did as the LORD commanded Moses.
Joshua was full of wisdom, because Moses had laid his hands on him.
10 And there arose not a prophet since in Israel like unto Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face,
11 In all the signs and the wonders, which the LORD sent him to do in the land of Egypt to Pharaoh, and to all his servants, and to all his land,
There was never another prophet like Moses, whom God knew face to face. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/34.html#3n)]
12 And in all that mighty hand, and in all the great terror which Moses shewed in the sight of all Israel.
No one could match his mighty hand or great terror.

### Notes
  1. _Moses ... died there ... according to the word of the LORD._ ([v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/34.html#5)) 
Did God kill Moses?
If so, I guess I should add it to [Drunk With Blood](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html).
Note that Moses, the alleged author of the Pentateuch, describes his own death and burial.
[Who wrote the Pentateuch?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/pentateuch.html)
  2. _His eye was not dim, nor his natural force abated._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/34.html#7)) 
At the age of 120, Moses is still as spry as ever. Although according to [Deuteronomy 31:2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/31.html#2) he could no longer "go out and come in," which seems like a significant handicap.
[Was Moses in good mental and physical health at age 120?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/moses120.html)
  3. _There arose not a prophet since in Israel like unto Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face._ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dt/34.html#10)) 
Not even Jesus, John the Baptist, Muhammad, or Joseph Smith?
[Can God be seen?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/seen.html)
[Did Moses see God face to face?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/face.html)
[Who wrote the Pentateuch?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/pentateuch.html)
[Who was the greatest prophet?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/interp/greatest.html)
