# The Fig Tree, Luke 13:1-9
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 04, 2008 
13 min read 
A verse-by-verse breakdown of the “parable of the fig tree” in [Luke 13:1-9](https://ref.ly/Luke%2013.1-9;nasb95?t=biblia):
Perhaps the people reporting to Jesus were seeking to get Him to comment politically on Pilate and thereby use Jesus as a means of rallying support for their cause. Remember, the Jews were under the rule of the Roman government and resented it. Ungodly gentiles were ruling over the house of Israel. Obviously, the people doing the reporting are interested in deliverance as well as justice. They want what is right; at least, right the way _**they**_ see it.
