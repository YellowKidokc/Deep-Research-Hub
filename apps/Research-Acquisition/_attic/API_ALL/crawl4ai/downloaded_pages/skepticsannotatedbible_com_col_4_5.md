### Notes
  1. _"Masters, give unto your servants that which is just and equal._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/col/4.html#1)) 
Slave owners that did this would set their slaves free.
[Does God approve of slavery?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/slavery.html)
  2. _Let your speech be always with grace, seasoned with salt, that ye may know how ye ought to answer every man._ ([v.6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/col/4.html#6)) 
[Should believers discuss their faith with non-believers?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/discuss.html)
  3. _Read the epistle from Laodicea._ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/col/4.html#16)) 
Apparently God intended to include [the epistle from Laodicea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistle_to_the_Laodiceans "Wikipedia: Epistle to the Ladoceans") in the Bible, but it was lost somewhere along the way. There were several letters that claimed to be the lost epistle (and one of these was often included in Latin medieval bibles), but they are considered forgeries today (as is Colossians, itself, by many scholars).
