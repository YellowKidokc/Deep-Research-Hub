### Notes
  1. _See thou tell no man._ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#4)) 
It is strange that Jesus told the man not to tell anyone, since "great multitudes" were present who must have witnessed the event.
  2. _When Jesus was entered into Capernaum, there came unto him a centurion._ ([v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#5)) 
Jesus is approached by a centurion who asks him to heal his servant (slave). But when the same story is told in Luke ([7:1-7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/7.html#2)), the centurion doesn't come himself but sends some "elders of the Jews."
[Did the Centurion ask Jesus directly to help his servant?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/centurion.html)
  3. _Lord, my servant lieth at home sick._ ([v.6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#6)) 
This was the perfect opportunity for Jesus to condemn slavery. All he'd have to do is say, "OK, I'll heal him. But then you must set your slave free, because slavery is an abomination to God."
[Does God approve of slavery?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/slavery.html)
What was the relationship between the centurion and his slave? Were they a gay couple? If so, Jesus didn't seem to mind.
[![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/mt8-5.jpg)](http://www.wouldjesusdiscriminate.org/biblical_evidence/gay_couple.html "WouldJesusDiscriminate.org")
[Strange Flesh: The Centurion and his slave](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/SF/possibly.html#centurion)
  4. _When Jesus heard it, he marvelled._ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#10)) 
[Did Jesus know everything?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jesus_knew.html)
  5. _The children of the kingdom shall be cast out into outer darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#12)) 
When Jesus refers to "the children of heaven" here he means the Jews.
[What will happen to Jews when they die?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jews.hmtl)
  6. _When Jesus was come into Peter's house, he saw his wife's mother laid, and sick of a fever._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#14)) 
Peter's mother-in-law was sick with a fever. Jesus healed her by touching her hand, so she could get back to serving them.
(Peter's mother-in-law? The first pope was married?)
[Did Jesus heal Peter's mother in law before or after calling Peter?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/peters-mil.html)
  7. _That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by Isaiah the prophet._ ([v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#17)) 
This is from [Isaiah 53:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/53.html#4). But the context shows that the quote from Isaiah refers to the nation of Israel as God's servant, not Jesus. See [Isaiah 41:8-10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/41.html#8); [44:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/44.html#1), [21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/44.html#21); [49:3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/49.html#3) in which the servant is explicitly called Israel.
  8. _The foxes have holes, and the birds of the air have nests; but the Son of man hath not where to lay his head._ ([v.20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#20)) 
In this verse Jesus claims to be homeless, but according to Mark ([2:15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/2.html#15)) he had his own house in Capernaum -- which is where he supposedly is at the time. So why didn't he just go there?
[Was Jesus homeless?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/homeless.html)
  9. _Follow me; and let the dead bury their dead._ ([v.22](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#22)) 
Jesus shows his family values by telling a man who had just lost his father and would like to bury him: "Follow me, and let the dead bury the dead."
  10. _What manner of man is this, that even the winds and the sea obey him!_ ([v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#28)) 
The disciples are surprised that Jesus can calm a storm, even though they just saw him cast out devils and heal the sick.
Is it harder to calm a storm than cast out devils?
  11. _There met him two possessed with devils._ ([v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#28)) 
[How many men were possessed with devils?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/possessed.html)
  12. _They cried out, saying, What have we to do with thee, Jesus, thou Son of God?_ ([v.29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#29)) 
The devils confess that Jesus is the Son of God.
So according to [1 John 4:15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1jn/4.html#15), God dwells in the devils and the devils in God.
  13. _The whole herd of swine ran violently down a steep place into the sea, and perished in the waters._ ([v.32](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#32)) 
Why did Jesus listen to and obey the devils? Couldn't he have found a way to cast out the devils without causing a whole herd of pigs to drown?
  14. _The whole city came out to meet Jesus: and when they saw him, they besought him that he would depart out of their coasts._ ([v.34](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/8.html#34)) 
Can you blame them? Jesus had just killed a herd of pigs, without compensating their owner. This would have destroyed the livelihood of the owner and caused a loss of revenue for the entire region.
