# Colossians Chapter 4
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 23, 2008 
8 min read 
_1 “Masters, grant to your slaves justice and fairness, knowing that you too have a Master in heaven.”_
This is a continuation of the previous chapter, which concluded that God is a fair and impartial judge and that we should work at our jobs with honesty and integrity.
Paul turns his attention briefly to Masters and employers and tells them to treat those under their charge fairly because they, too, have a master in heaven, the ultimate and impartial judge, who treats them fairly. ![Colossians Chapter 4 Bible Study](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/bible-study-02.jpg)
