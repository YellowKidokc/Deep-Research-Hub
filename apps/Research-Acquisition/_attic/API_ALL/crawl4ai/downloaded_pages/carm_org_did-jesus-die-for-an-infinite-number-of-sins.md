# Did Jesus die for an infinite number of sins?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 05, 2025 
3 min read 
No, it does not make sense to say that Jesus died for an infinite number of sins. This is because there does not seem to be an infinite number of sins to begin with. In order to have an actual number of sins, there would need to be either an infinite number of people who would sin or an infinite length of time for them to sin. But neither condition is true. So there isn’t an infinite number of sins.
Mankind is not infinitely old. God created Adam, the first human, several thousand years ago. So there’s a beginning – not an infinite amount of time in the past by which sin could occur. This would include fallen angels since they were created as good angels who then chose to rebel. They are not infinitely old, so there is no infinite time in the past in which they sinned. ![Did Jesus die for an infinite number of sins?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Jesus-09sqs.jpg)
There is a coming judgment where people will be damned and people will be saved. At that time, it appears that the act of sinning will stop, at least for the believers, since we will have resurrected bodies. But someone might ask if the people in hell, where they will be forever and ever, continue to sin. While it is possible that those in eternal hell _may_ continue to sin, we can’t say for sure. Perhaps they will be cursing God forever. I don’t know. Or, perhaps their anguish is such that they don’t have time or the ability to sin. I don’t know what the case would be.
