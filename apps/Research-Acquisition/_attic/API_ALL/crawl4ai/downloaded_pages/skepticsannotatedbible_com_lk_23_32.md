### CHAPTER 23
Jesus is questioned by Pontius Pilate
  
23:1-7, Cf. [Mt 27:1-14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#S1), [Mk 15:1-5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#S1), [Jn 18:28-40](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/18.html#S7)
1 And the whole multitude of them arose, and led him unto Pilate.
The whole crowd took Jesus to Pilate.
2 And they began to accuse him, saying, We found this fellow perverting the nation, and forbidding to give tribute to Caesar, saying that he himself is Christ a King.
They all accused him, saying,
> This man perverted the nation.
> He refused to pay taxes to Caesar,
> and claimed to be Christ the king.
3 And Pilate asked him, saying, Art thou the King of the Jews? And he answered him and said, Thou sayest it.
Pilate said, "Are you the king of the Jews?"
Jesus said, "You say that I am."
4 Then said Pilate to the chief priests and to the people, I find no fault in this man.
Pilate said to the chief priests and the people,
> I find no fault with this man.
5 And they were the more fierce, saying, He stirreth up the people, teaching throughout all Jewry, beginning from Galilee to this place.
They said,
> He's inciting Jews from Galilee to Jerusalem.
6 When Pilate heard of Galilee, he asked whether the man were a Galilaean.
7 And as soon as he knew that he belonged unto Herod's jurisdiction, he sent him to Herod, who himself also was at Jerusalem at that time.
When Pilate heard that Jesus was a Galilean,
he sent him to Herod, who was in Jerusalem at the time.
Jesus is taken to Herod
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 23:8-12
8 And when Herod saw Jesus, he was exceeding glad: for he was desirous to see him of a long season, because he had heard many things of him; and he hoped to have seen some miracle done by him.
Herod was glad to see Jesus,
since he'd heard so much about him. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#1n)]
9 Then he questioned with him in many words; but he answered him nothing.
He asked him many things,
but Jesus didn't answer.
10 And the chief priests and scribes stood and vehemently accused him.
The chief priests and scribes accuse him of things.
11 And Herod with his men of war set him at nought, and mocked him, and arrayed him in a gorgeous robe, and sent him again to Pilate.
And Herod's soldiers mocked him,
put a gorgeous robe on him,
and sent him to Pilate.
12 And the same day Pilate and Herod were made friends together: for before they were at enmity between themselves.
On that day Pilate and Herod became friends.
Before then they were enemies.
Pilate tries to release Jesus
  
23:13-17, Cf. [Jn 19:4-16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/19.html#S2)

Pilate called the chief priests and said to them,
14 Said unto them, Ye have brought this man unto me, as one that perverteth the people: and, behold, I, having examined him before you, have found no fault in this man touching those things whereof ye accuse him:
> You accused this man of perverting the people,
> but I've found no fault with him.

> And neither did Herod.

> So I'll just beat him and let him go.
17 (For of necessity he must release one unto them at the feast.)
(But first Pilate had to release one prisoner on the feast.)
The people prefer Barabbas to Jesus

19 (Who for a certain sedition made in the city, and for murder, was cast into prison.)
The people said,
> Release Barabbas for us.
(Barabbas was in prison for murder and sedition.)
20 Pilate therefore, willing to release Jesus, spake again to them.
Pilate said he'd like to release Jesus.
21 But they cried, saying, Crucify him, crucify him.
But the people cried out,
> Crucify him, crucify him!

Pilate said for a third time,
> Why, what has he done?
23 And they were instant with loud voices, requiring that he might be crucified. And the voices of them and of the chief priests prevailed.
But the people cried out for him to be crucified.
24 And Pilate gave sentence that it should be as they required.
So Pilate gave the order for him to be crucified.
25 And he released unto them him that for sedition and murder was cast into prison, whom they had desired; but he delivered Jesus to their will.
So Barabbas was released and Jesus was sent to be crucified.
Jesus is crucified

As they led Jesus away, a man named Simon from Cyrene was forced to carry the cross for Jesus. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#3n)]
27 And there followed him a great company of people, and of women, which also bewailed and lamented him.
There were many people who followed Jesus,
including women who wept for him.

But Jesus turned and said to them,

Jesus was crucified at a place called Calvary
between two criminals.

Jesus said,
> Father forgive them,

> If you're Christ, save yourself and us.

But the other criminal rebuked him saying,
> Don't you fear God?

### Notes
  1. _When Herod saw Jesus, he was exceeding glad_ ([v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#8)) 
This trial before Herod is found only in Luke.
  2. _Herod with his men of war ... arrayed him in a gorgeous robe._ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#11)) 
["Who put the robe on Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/dressed.html)
  3. _They laid hold upon one Simon, a Cyrenian ... and on him they laid the cross._ ([v.26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#26)) 
[Who carried Jesus' cross?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/cyrenian.html)
  4. _The soldiers also mocked him ... offering him vinegar._ ([v.36](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#36)) 
[What did the soldiers give Jesus to drink?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/vinegar.html)
  5. _A superscription also was written over him in letters of Greek, and Latin, and Hebrew, THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS._ ([v.38](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#38)) 
[What did the sign over Jesus say?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sign.html)
  6. _Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom._ ([v.42](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#42)) 
[Did both thieves revile Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/reviled.html)
  7. _Verily I say unto thee, to day shalt thou be with me in paradise._ ([v.43](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#43)) 
The phrase "Truly I say unto you" is used by Jesus more than 50 times in the New Testament. In all verses except this one, the Watchtower places the comma after the word "you". But the New World Translation of the Jehovah's Witnesses translates this verse as follows: "Truly I tell you today, You will be with me in paradise."
Why would they do that? Well, the Governing Body doesn't believe in a soul, so they say the good thief died like everyone else. Only later, nearly 2000 years later, after Armageddon, would he be resurrected. So they couldn't let Jesus tell him that he'd be with him that very day in paradise. Instead, they have Jesus say that he is telling the truth today (Not yesterday or tomorrow, but today. Or maybe "today" here is emphasizing that he's telling the truth today, but he was lying yesterday or something) -- and that sometime in the far distant future they'd be together in paradise.
But then maybe the JWs have a point here. Jesus tells the "good thief" that they'll both be in heaven "today." But how can that be since it's only Friday and, according to the gospels, Jesus lay dead in the tomb Friday night and all day Saturday.
[Can thieves go to heaven?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/thieves.html)
[Did Jesus go to heaven after he died but before his ascension?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/visit.html)
  8. _It was about the sixth hour, and there was a darkness over all the earth until the ninth hour._ ([v.44](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#44)) 
When Jesus was crucified, there was three hours of complete darkness "over all the earth." It is strange that there is no record of this extraordinary event outside of the gospels.
  9. _The veil of the temple was rent._ ([v.45](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#45)) 
[When did the Temple curtain rip?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/curtain.html)
  10. _Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit: and having said thus, he gave up the ghost._ ([v.46](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#46)) 
[What were the last words of Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/lastwords.html)
  11. _Certainly this was a righteous man._ ([v.47](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#47)) 
[What did the Centurion call Jesus when he died?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/centurion_call.html)
  12. _The women ... stood afar off._ ([v.49](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#49)) 
[From where were the women watching?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/women_watching.html)
  13. _He was a good man, and a just._ ([v.50](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#50)) 
[Has there ever been a good person?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/good.html)
[Has there ever been a just person?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/just.html)
  14. _Joseph ... of Arimathaea ... took it down, and wrapped it in linen, and laid it in a sepulchre._ ([vv.50-53](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#50)) 
[Who buried Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/buried.html)
