### Notes
  1. _These are the words which the LORD hath commanded._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/35.html#1)) 
Moses just received the second set of commandments, the ones that were called "the ten commandments", yet he only mentions one of them: the one about the sabbath. Then he launches into a list of things God wants: gold, silver, goats' hair, ram's skins, etc.
  2. _Whosoever doeth work therein shall be put to death._ ([v.2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/35.html#2)) 
"Exodus 35:2 clearly states that anyone working on the Sabbath should be put to death. You really going to feel good about enforcing that one?" -- [Penn and Teller on the Bible](http://youtube.com/watch?v=Y_sb2fSRByI)
[Is it necessary to keep the sabbath?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sabbath.html)
[How should Sabbath-breakers be punished?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sabbath_breakers.html)
[Does God approve of capital punishment?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/capital.html)
[What the Bible says about capital punishment](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/capital.html)
  3. _Take ye from among you an offering unto the LORD: whosoever is of a willing heart, let him bring it, an offering of the LORD._ ([v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/35.html#5)) 
The same list of silly stuff is repeated in Exodus [25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/25.html) and [39](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/39.html).
