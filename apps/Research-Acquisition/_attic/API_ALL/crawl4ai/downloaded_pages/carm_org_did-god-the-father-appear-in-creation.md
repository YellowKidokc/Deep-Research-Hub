# Did God the Father appear in creation?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
April 27, 2024 
2 min read 
Did God the Father appear physically in creation at any time? We can’t say definitely yes or no since we can’t say how such a manifestation of the Father might occur. However, biblically speaking, we don’t find any record of the physical appearance of the Father. Instead, physical manifestations of God (Gen. 17:1; 18:1; Exodus 6:2-3; 24:1-11) are the pre-incarnate Christ because Jesus said no one had ever seen God the Father (John 6:46). Paul likewise revealed that the Father has never been seen (1 Tim. 6:16).[ ![Father in creation](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/God-03.jpg) ](https://carm.org/questions/did-god-the-father-appear-in-creation/attachment/god-03/)
So, the Father has never been seen. But, we do see the presence of the Father recorded in the Bible – though it was not a physical form. Consider the following verses.
As for the question Did God the Father appear physically in creation at any time? Scripture shows that God the Father never appeared in physical form anywhere. But since He is manifested in speech, we can say people experienced His presence.
