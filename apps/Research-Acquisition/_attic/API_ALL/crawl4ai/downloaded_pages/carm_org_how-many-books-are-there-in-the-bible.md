The Jewish Scriptures are organized differently but consist of the same books. The Jewish Bible lists only 24 books, rather than the 39 we list in the Old Testament, because some of the books Christians list separately are combined into one book in the Jewish Canon. Samuel, Kings, and Chronicles are each one book rather than two. Ezra and Nehemiah are also combined into one book.
