# 1 Samuel 3:14 and Universalism
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
February 12, 2015 
7 min read 
There are a variety of views within universalism. Some hold that all humans will be saved but not all angels. Other Universalists maintain that all of the angelic realms will be saved while some say all will be saved except the devil. But, for those who say that all people will eventually be saved because the atoning sacrifice of Christ paid for every individual, the following verse in [1 Samuel 3:14](https://ref.ly/1%20Sam%203.14;nasb95?t=biblia) is relevant. Let’s check it out.
“Therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli that the iniquity of Eli’s house shall not be atoned for by sacrifice or offering forever,” ([1 Sam. 3:14](https://ref.ly/1%20Sam.%203.14;nasb95?t=biblia)).
[1 Samuel 3:14](https://ref.ly/1%20Sam%203.14;nasb95?t=biblia) says the iniquity Eli’s house will never be atoned for. If this is true, it would mean that universalism is proven false since Eli’s house could not be saved without a sin sacrifice. But, Universalists believe everyone will be atoned for and thus saved. So this verse cannot mean that Eli’s house will _never_ be atoned for. It must mean something else. Let’s examine some of the universalist responses to this verse.
