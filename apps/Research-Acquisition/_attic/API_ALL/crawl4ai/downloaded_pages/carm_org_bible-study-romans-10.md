# Romans Bible Study, Chapter 10
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
August 17, 2023 
22 min read 
In this Romans 10 Bible Study, Paul continues his admonition to the Jews that salvation is not by the Law but by Christ. Furthermore, he emphasizes the need to preach the gospel. And he states that the Jews have already heard via the Old Testament revelation that God will reach out to all nations. Salvation is not only for the Jews.
