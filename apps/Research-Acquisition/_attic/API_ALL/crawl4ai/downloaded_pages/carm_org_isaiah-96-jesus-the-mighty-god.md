# Isaiah 9:6, Jesus the Mighty God
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
September 13, 2016 
4 min read 
The words of [Isaiah 9:6](https://ref.ly/Isa%209.6;nasb95?t=biblia) are one of the most powerful [prophecies](https://carm.org/bible/prophecy-the-bible-and-jesus/) testifying plainly and clearly that the promised Messiah to come would, in fact, be God Himself born in human flesh. The child that is born, the son that is given, is to be called “Mighty God.” Jehovah’s Witnesses, however, contend that the Messiah is not _really_ called God here, at least not in the sense that biblical Christians mean because He is called _mighty_ God rather than _almighty_ God. They will concede that Jesus is a “god” in the sense of being a very powerful, spiritual being, but He is not Jehovah, the one and only _almighty_ God. This is a very important distinction for Jehovah’s Witnesses. It does not, however, hold up very well under scrutiny.
