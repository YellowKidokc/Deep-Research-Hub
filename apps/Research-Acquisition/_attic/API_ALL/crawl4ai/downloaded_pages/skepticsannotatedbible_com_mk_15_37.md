## [Mark](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/outline.html "Mark Outline")
### CHAPTER 15
Jesus is taken to Pontius Pilate
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 15:1, Cf. [Mt 27:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#S1), [Lk 23:1-5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#S1), [Jn 18:28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/18.html#S7)
1 And straightway in the morning the chief priests held a consultation with the elders and scribes and the whole council, and bound Jesus, and carried him away, and delivered him to Pilate.
In the morning, the chief priests took Jesus to Pilate.
Jesus is questioned by Pilate
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 15:2-5, Cf. [Mt 27:11-14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#S3); [Lk 23:2-7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#S2); [Jn 18:29-40](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/18.html#S8), [19:1-16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/19.html#S1)
2 And Pilate asked him, Art thou the King of the Jews? And he answering said unto them, Thou sayest it.
Pilate asked Jesus, "Are you the king of the Jews?"
Jesus said, "You said it."
3 And the chief priests accused him of many things: but he answered nothing. 
The chief priests asked him many questions,
but he didn't answer. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#1n)]
4 And Pilate asked him again, saying, Answerest thou nothing? behold how many things they witness against thee.
Pilate him again, saying,
> Aren't you going to answer?
> Look how many witnesses there are against you.
5 But Jesus yet answered nothing; so that Pilate marvelled.
But Jesus didn't answer.
The people prefer Barabbas to Jesus
  
15:6-15, Cf. [Mt 27:15-26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#S4), [Lk 23:13-26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#S5), [Jn 18:39](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/18.html#33)-[19:16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/19.html#16)
6 Now at that feast he released unto them one prisoner, whomsoever they desired.
At that feast, Pilate released one prisoner whom the crowd desired.
7 And there was one named Barabbas, which lay bound with them that had made insurrection with him, who had committed murder in the insurrection.
8 And the multitude crying aloud began to desire him to do as he had ever done unto them.
There was a prisoner named Barabbas,
who killed someone in an uprising.
9 But Pilate answered them, saying, Will ye that I release unto you the King of the Jews?
10 For he knew that the chief priests had delivered him for envy.
11 But the chief priests moved the people, that he should rather release Barabbas unto them.
12 And Pilate answered and said again unto them, What will ye then that I shall do unto him whom ye call the King of the Jews?
Pilate asked the crowd,
> Should I release the king of the Jews?
13 And they cried out again, Crucify him.
14 Then Pilate said unto them, Why, what evil hath he done? And they cried out the more exceedingly, Crucify him.
But the crowd cried out,
> Crucify him.
15 And so Pilate, willing to content the people, released Barabbas unto them, and delivered Jesus, when he had scourged him, to be crucified.
So Pilate released Barabbas
and delivered Jesus to be crucified.
The soldiers make fun of Jesus
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 15:16-20, Cf. [Mt 27:27-30](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#S5), [Jn 19:1-3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/19.html#S1)
16 And  led him away into the hall, called Praetorium; and they call together the whole band.
17 And they clothed him with purple, and platted a crown of thorns, and put it about his head,
The soldiers clothed him in a purple robe, [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#2n)]
and put a crown of thorns on his head.
18 And began to salute him, Hail, King of the Jews!
And saluted him, saying, "Hail, King of the Jews!"
19 And they smote him on the head with a reed, and did spit upon him, and bowing their knees worshipped him.
They hit and spat upon him,
bowing their knees to worship him.
20 And when they had mocked him, they took off the purple from him, and put his own clothes on him, and led him out to crucify him.
After they were finished mocking him,
they took off the purple robe, put his own clothes back on him,
and led him off to be crucified.
Jesus is crucified
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 15:21-32, Cf. [Mt 27:31-44](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#S6), [Lk 23:36](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#36), [Jn 19:29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/19.html#29)
21 And they compel one Simon a Cyrenian, who passed by, coming out of the country, the father of Alexander and Rufus, to bear his cross.
22 And they bring him unto the place Golgotha, which is, being interpreted, The place of a skull.
They forced a man from Cyrene named Simon [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#3n)] to carry his cross to a place called Golgotha.
23 And they gave him to drink wine mingled with myrrh: but he received it not.
They gave him some wine mingled with myrrh to drink,
but he didn't drink it. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#4n)]
24 And when they had crucified him, they parted his garments, casting lots upon them, what every man should take.
When they crucified him, the soldiers cast lots for his clothes.
25 And it was the third hour, and they crucified him.
It was the third hour when he was crucified. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#5n)]
26 And the superscription of his accusation was written over, THE KING OF THE JEWS.
They put a sign up over him saying,
> THE KING OF THE JEWS [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#6n)]
27 And with him they crucify two thieves; the one on his right hand, and the other on his left.
28 And the scripture was fulfilled, which saith, And he was numbered with the transgressors.
Jesus was crucified between two thieves.
29 And they that passed by railed on him, wagging their heads, and saying, Ah, thou that destroyest the temple, and buildest it in three days,
30 Save thyself, and come down from the cross.
Those who passed by wagged their heads and mocked him, saying,
> You said you'd destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days. So why don't you save yourself, and come down from the cross?
31 Likewise also the chief priests mocking said among themselves with the scribes, He saved others; himself he cannot save.
The chief priests and scribes also mocked him, saying,
> He saved others, but he can't save himself.
32 Let Christ the King of Israel descend now from the cross, that we may see and believe. And they that were crucified with him reviled him.
> Come down from the cross, so we can see and believe.
The thieves that were crucified with Jesus also mocked him. [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#7n)]
Jesus dies
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 15:33-41, Cf. [Mt 27:45-51](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#S17), [Lk 23:44](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#38)
33 And when the sixth hour was come, there was darkness over the whole land until the ninth hour.
There was darkness over the whole land from the sixth to the ninth hour. [[8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#8n)]
34 And at the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani? which is, being interpreted, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? 
At the ninth hour, Jesus cried out with a loud voice,
> My God, my God, why have you forsaken me? [[9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#9n)]
35 And some of them that stood by, when they heard it, said, Behold, he calleth Elijah.
Some that heard him said,
> He's calling Elijah.
36 And one ran and filled a spunge full of vinegar, and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink, saying, Let alone; let us see whether Elijah will come to take him down.
Someone soaked a sponge with vinegar, put it on a stick, gave it to him to drink, and said,
> Let's see if Elijah will come to take him down.
37 And Jesus cried with a loud voice, and gave up the ghost.
Then Jesus cried out with a loud voice and died.
38 And the veil of the temple was rent in twain from the top to the bottom.
And the veil of the temple was torn from top to bottom. [[10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#10n)]
39 And when the centurion, which stood over against him, saw that he so cried out, and gave up the ghost, he said, Truly this man was the Son of God.
A centurion at the site of the crucifixion, said,
> This was the son of God. [[11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#11n)]
40 There were also women looking on afar off: among whom was Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the less and of Joses, and Salome;
41 (Who also, when he was in Galilee, followed him, and ministered unto him;) and many other women which came up with him unto Jerusalem.
There was a group of women watching from far away, among them were Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the less and of Joses, and Salome. [[12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#12n)]
Jesus's burial
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 15:42-47, Cf. [Mt 27:51-66](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/27.html#S11), [Lk 23:47-56](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/23.html#S5), [Jn 19:31-47](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/19.html#31)
42 And now when the even was come, because it was the preparation, that is, the day before the sabbath,
43 Joseph of Arimathea, an honourable counsellor, which also waited for the kingdom of God, came, and went in boldly unto Pilate, and craved the body of Jesus.
That evening, on the preparation for the sabbath,
Joseph of Arimathea asked Pilate for permission to remove Jesus's body.
44 And Pilate marvelled if he were already dead: and calling unto him the centurion, he asked him whether he had been any while dead.
45 And when he knew it of the centurion, he gave the body to Joseph.
Pilate was surprised that Jesus was already dead, [[13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#13n)]
granted his request.
46 And he bought fine linen, and took him down, and wrapped him in the linen, and laid him in a sepulchre which was hewn out of a rock, and rolled a stone unto the door of the sepulchre.
47 And Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses beheld where he was laid.
He took the body down, wrapped it in linen, and laid it in a sepulchre, [[14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#14n)]
which was cut out of rock, with a stone rolled to close the entrance.

### Notes
  1. _The chief priests accused him of many things: but he answered nothing._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#3)) 
[Was Jesus silent during his trial?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/speech.html)
  2. _The soldiers ... clothed him with purple._ ([vv.16-17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#11)) 
[Who put the robe on Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/dressed.html)
[What color was Jesus' robe?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/color.html)
  3. _They compel one Simon a Cyrenian ... to bear his cross._ ([v.21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#21)) 
[Who carried Jesus' cross?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/cyrenian.html)
  4. _They gave him to drink wine mingled with myrrh: but he received it not._ ([v.23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#23)) 
[What did the soldiers give Jesus to drink?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/vinegar.html)
[Did Jesus drink from the cross?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/drink.html)
  5. _It was the third hour, and they crucified him._ ([v.25](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#25)) 
[When (at what hour) was Jesus crucified?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/hour.html)
[Was Jesus crucified the day before or the day after the Passover meal?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/passover_meal.html)
  6. _The superscription of his accusation was written over, THE KING OF THE JEWS._ ([v.26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#26)) 
All of the gospels give the exact words that were written over the cross, but none of them agree about the words that were used.
[What did the sign over Jesus say?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sign.html)
  7. _They that were crucified with him reviled him._ ([v.32](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#32)) 
[Did both thieves revile Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/reviled.html)
  8. _When the sixth hour was come, there was darkness over the whole land until the ninth hour._ ([v.33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#33)) 
When Jesus was crucified, there was three hours of complete darkness "over the whole land." It is strange that there is no record of this extraordinary event outside of the gospels.
  9. _My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?_ ([v.34](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#34)) 
[Is Jesus God?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/Jesus_God.html)
  10. _Jesus cried with a loud voice, and gave up the ghost. And the veil of the temple was rent._ ([v.37](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#37)) 
[When did the Temple curtain rip?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/curtain.html)
  11. _The centurion ... said, Truly this man was the Son of God._ ([v.39](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#39)) 
[What did the Centurion call Jesus when he died?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/centurion_call.html)
  12. _There were also women looking on afar off._ ([v.40](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#40)) 
[From where were the women watching?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/women_watching.html)
  13. _And Pilate marvelled if he were already dead._ ([v.44](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#44)) 
Maybe he wasn't!
  14. _Joseph of Arimathaea ... took him down, and wrapped him in the linen, and laid him in a sepulchre._ ([vv.43-46](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/15.html#43)) 
[Who buried Jesus?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/buried.html)
