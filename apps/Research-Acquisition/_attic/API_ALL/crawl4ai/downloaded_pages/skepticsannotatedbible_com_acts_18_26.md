### Notes
  1. _Paul ... having shorn his head in Cenchrea: for he had a vow._ ([v.18b](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/18.html#18)) 
Apparently, Paul is becoming a Nazarite here by shaving his head.
But if so, he's doing it backwards. After making the vow, he's supposed to stop cutting his hair (and stop drinking alcohol, not touch dead bodies, etc.). Then after fulfilling the vow, he cuts his hair. (See [Numbers 6:1-21) ](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/6.html#S1)
  2. _Priscilla and Aquila_ ([v.18a](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/acts/18.html#18)) 
Women are not often mentioned in the Bible, and when they are they are usually nameless. But here, the wife's name is mentioned before her husband's, which almost never occurs in the Bible. Priscilla must have been an especially important woman!
