# What Does Mormonism Teach?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 30, 2008 
14 min read 
The doctrines of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (Mormons) are very interesting. Most ‘odd’ ones are not initially taught to potential converts, but they should be. Instead, “they are revealed later as one matures and gains the ability to accept them.” The LDS Church tries to make its official doctrines appear Christian, but what underlies those Christian-sounding terms is far from Christian in meaning. The following are the teachings of its officials throughout the years. Please note that these teachings are documented by Mormon writers – not anti-Mormon writers. These statements come from within Mormonism, not from outside it. All the quotes are from Latter-day Saints.
Finally, many Latter-day Saints respond that most of the citations below are not from official Mormon writings, as if that disproves the doctrines they teach. If they are not official, fine. But if not, why have the Mormon apostles and high officials taught and written them, and why are their books sold in Mormon bookstores? The truth is that the following is what Latter-day Saints are taught. ![What Does Mormonism Teach](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/book-of-mormon-s.jpg)
Some Latter-day Saints may disagree with a few of the points listed on this page, but all of what is stated here is from Mormon authors in good standing of the Mormon church.
