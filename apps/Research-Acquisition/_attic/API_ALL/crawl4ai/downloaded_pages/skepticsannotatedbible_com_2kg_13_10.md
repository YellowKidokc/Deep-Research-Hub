## [2 Kings](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/outline.html "2 Kings Outline")
### CHAPTER 13
Jehoahaz and Jehoash
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/boring.63b5b35a30388.jpg) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif)13:1-13
1 In the three and twentieth year of Joash the son of Ahaziah king of Judah Jehoahaz the son of Jehu began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and reigned seventeen years.
Jehu's son, Jehoahaz, became king of Israel in the 23rd year of Joash's reign in Judah. 
He was king for 17 years. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/13.html#1n)]
2 And he did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD, and followed the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, which made Israel to sin; he departed not therefrom.
3 And the anger of the LORD was kindled against Israel, and he delivered them into the hand of Hazael king of Syria, and into the hand of Benhadad the son of Hazael, all their days.
Jehoahaz did evil in God's sight, so God delivered Israel into the hands of the Syrian kings, Hazael and Benhadad.
4 And Jehoahaz besought the LORD, and the LORD hearkened unto him: for he saw the oppression of Israel, because the king of Syria oppressed them.
Jehoahaz asked God to stop making Syria oppress Israel.
5 (And the LORD gave Israel a saviour, so that they went out from under the hand of the Syrians: and the children of Israel dwelt in their tents, as beforetime.
7 Neither did he leave of the people to Jehoahaz but fifty horsemen, and ten chariots, and ten thousand footmen; for the king of Syria had destroyed them, and had made them like the dust by threshing.
So God sent Israel a savior.
6 Nevertheless they departed not from the sins of the house of Jeroboam, who made Israel sin, but walked therein: and there remained the grove also in Samaria.)
But Israel kept on sinning 
(like Jeroboam who made Israel sin [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/13.html#2n)]).
And there was still a grove in Samaria.
8 Now the rest of the acts of Jehoahaz, and all that he did, and his might, are they not written in [the chronicles of the kings of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chronicles_of_the_Kings_of_Israel)?
All the rest of Jehoahaz's acts can be found in [the chronicles of the kings of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chronicles_of_the_Kings_of_Israel).
9 And Jehoahaz slept with his fathers; and they buried him in Samaria: and Joash his son reigned in his stead.
10 In the thirty and seventh year of Joash king of Judah began Jehoash the son of Jehoahaz to reign over Israel in Samaria, and reigned sixteen years.
Jehoahaz died and his son, Joash, became king of Israel in the 37th year of Joash (Jehoash) the king of Judah. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/13.html#1n)]
11 And he did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD; he departed not from all the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who made Israel sin: but he walked therein.
Joash did evil in the sight of God 
(like Jeroboam who made Israel sin [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/13.html#2n)]).
12 And the rest of the acts of Joash, and all that he did, and his might wherewith he fought against Amaziah king of Judah, are they not written in [ the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chronicles_of_the_Kings_of_Israel)?
The rest of Joash's acts can be found in [the chronicles of the kings of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chronicles_of_the_Kings_of_Israel).
13 And Joash slept with his fathers; and Jeroboam sat upon his throne: and Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.
Joash died and Jeroboam became king of Israel.
Elisha dies
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 13:14-25
14 Now Elisha was fallen sick of his sickness whereof he died. And Joash the king of Israel came down unto him, and wept over his face, and said, O my father, my father, the chariot of Israel, and the horsemen thereof.
15 And Elisha said unto him, Take bow and arrows. And he took unto him bow and arrows.
16 And he said to the king of Israel, Put thine hand upon the bow. And he put his hand upon it: and Elisha put his hands upon the king's hands.
Elisha was sick and was about to die. 
When King Joash visited him, Elisha said,
> Open the window and shoot an arrow out of it.
And Joash shot an arrow through the window.
17 And he said, Open the window eastward. And he opened it. Then Elisha said, Shoot. And he shot. And he said, The arrow of the LORD's deliverance, and the arrow of deliverance from Syria: for thou shalt smite the Syrians in Aphek, till thou have consumed them.
Elisha said to Joash,
> The arrow is a sign from God that you will smite the Syrians.
18 And he said, Take the arrows. And he took them. And he said unto the king of Israel, Smite upon the ground. And he smote thrice, and stayed.
19 And the man of God was wroth with him, and said, Thou shouldest have smitten five or six times; then hadst thou smitten Syria till thou hadst consumed it: whereas now thou shalt smite Syria but thrice.
20 And Elisha died, and they buried him. And the bands of the Moabites invaded the land at the coming in of the year.
Elisha said to Joash,
> Smite the ground with your arrows.
And Joash smacked the ground three times with his arrows.
Elisha was mad at him and said,
> You should have hit the ground five or six times. 
> But now you'll only smite Syria three times.
Then Elisha died and they buried him.
21 And it came to pass, **as they were burying a man** , that, behold, they spied a band of men; and they cast the man into the sepulchre of Elisha: and **when the man was let down, and touched the bones of Elisha, he revived, and stood up on his feet.**
22 But Hazael king of Syria oppressed Israel all the days of Jehoahaz.
Later, as they were burying another man, the body touched Elisha's bones and the dead man came to life and stood on his feet. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/13.html#3n)]
23 And the LORD was gracious unto them, and had compassion on them, and had respect unto them, because of his covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and would not destroy them, neither cast he them from his presence as yet.
24 So Hazael king of Syria died; and Benhadad his son reigned in his stead.
25 And Jehoash the son of Jehoahaz took again out of the hand of Benhadad the son of Hazael the cities, which he had taken out of the hand of Jehoahaz his father by war. Three times did Joash beat him, and recovered the cities of Israel.
_The Lord ... had respect unto them._ [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/13.html#4n)]

### Notes
  1. _In the three and twentieth year of Joash the son of Ahaziah king of Judah Jehoahaz the son of Jehu began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and reigned seventeen years._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/13.html#1)) 
[When did Jehoash become king of Judah?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jehoash.html)
  2. _Jeroboam, who made Israel sin_ ([v.6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/13.html#6)) 
[Jeroboam made two gold calves](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1kg/12.html#calves) and presented them to Israel as the gods that brought them out of Egypt.
God later [killed his son](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/1kg.html#child) and [wall-pissing family](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/1kg.html#family) to punish him for it.

[Was Jesus the first to rise from the dead?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/first.html)
  4. _The Lord ... had respect unto them._ ([v.23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/2kg/13.html#23)) 
[Does God respect anyone?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/respect.html)
