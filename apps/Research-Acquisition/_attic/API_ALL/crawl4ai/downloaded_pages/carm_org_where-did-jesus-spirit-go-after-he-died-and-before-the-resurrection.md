# Where did Jesus’ spirit go after He died and before the resurrection?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 31, 2009 
3 min read 
Where did Jesus’ spirit go after He died and before the resurrection? This is a common question raised. There is debate on what happened to Jesus during the period after His death and before His resurrection. Generally speaking, the two sections of scripture that deal with this are found in Ephesians 4 and 1 Peter 3.
![Where did Jesus' spirit go after He died and before the resurrection?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/Jesus-23.jpg)
It appears that after Jesus atoned for our sins by dying on the cross, He may have gone into the spiritual world and presented the gospel to those who had died beforehand. This is not to say that people had a second chance to receive the gospel ([Heb. 9:27](https://ref.ly/Heb.%209.27;nasb95?t=biblia)). Rather, there are two groups of people who had died before the time of Christ’s sacrifice. The first group was those who were believers in God and had hoped for the Messiah. The second were those who had rejected God. It is possible that the presentation of the gospel was then for the purpose of informing both groups about the work of the cross and why one group would be taken into the heavens ([Eph. 4:8](https://ref.ly/Eph.%204.8;nasb95?t=biblia)) and the other would receive judgment ([1 Pet. 3:18-19](https://ref.ly/1%20Pet.%203.18-19;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Whichever possibility is real, we need to understand that the resurrection of Christ is proof that whatever he did on the cross, as well as whatever he did before his resurrection, was accepted by God the Father. He was the perfect sacrifice That was acceptable to God the Father, which is why he was raised from the dead.
