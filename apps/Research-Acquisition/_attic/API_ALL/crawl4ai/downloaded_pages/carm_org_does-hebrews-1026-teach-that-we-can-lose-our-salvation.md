# Does Hebrews 10:26 teach that we can lose our salvation?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
May 21, 2014 
6 min read 
When we look at [Hebrews 10:26](https://ref.ly/Heb%2010.26;nasb95?t=biblia) and whether or not we can lose our salvation, we must examine its context. The book of Hebrews was written to Jewish Christians since it begins by referring to “the fathers and the prophets” (v. 1) and then it jumps into quoting Psalm 2. In fact, there are more than thirty Old Testament quotes in the epistle with many more allusions ([Hebrews 3:16](https://ref.ly/Heb%203.16;nasb95?t=biblia); [5:10](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%205.10;nasb95?t=biblia); [8:5](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%208.5;nasb95?t=biblia); [9:11-22](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%209.11-22;nasb95?t=biblia); [12:16-18](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%2012.16-18;nasb95?t=biblia), etc.,) which included references to the Old Testament sacrificial system ([Hebrews 8:3](https://ref.ly/Heb%208.3;nasb95?t=biblia); [9:9](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%209.9;nasb95?t=biblia); [10:4](https://ref.ly/Hebrews%2010.4;nasb95?t=biblia)). Also, it appears that those to whom this epistle is addressed have been Christians for some time ([Hebrews 5:12](https://ref.ly/Heb%205.12;nasb95?t=biblia)) and had suffered persecution ([Hebrews 10:32-34](https://ref.ly/Heb%2010.32-34;nasb95?t=biblia)). Therefore, we can confidently say that the book of Hebrews was written to the Hebrew-Christian community as a whole, which would consist of a mix of true believers and those still leaning on the Old Testament sacrificial system. In Hebrews, the author is demonstrating the superiority of Christianity over Judaism. This is important because it sheds light on [Hebrews 10:26](https://ref.ly/Heb%2010.26;nasb95?t=biblia). Let’s take a look at the verse.
[Hebrews 10:26](https://ref.ly/Heb%2010.26;nasb95?t=biblia), “For if we go on sinning willfully after receiving the knowledge of the truth, there no longer remains a sacrifice for sins.”
Now, let’s take a look at the context of the verse in chapter 10 of Hebrews.
