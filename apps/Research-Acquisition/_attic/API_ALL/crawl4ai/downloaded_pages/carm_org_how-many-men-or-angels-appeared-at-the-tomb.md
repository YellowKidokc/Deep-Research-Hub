# How many men or angels appeared at the tomb?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
3 min read 
How many men or angels ![men, angels, tomb](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/angels-07.jpg) appeared at the tomb after Jesus rose from the dead? [Matt 28:2](https://ref.ly/Matt%2028.2;nasb95?t=biblia); [Mark 16:5](https://ref.ly/Mark%2016.5;nasb95?t=biblia); [Luke 24:4](https://ref.ly/Luke%2024.4;nasb95?t=biblia); [John 20:1-2](https://ref.ly/John%2020.1-2;nasb95?t=biblia), [12](https://ref.ly/John%2020.12;nasb95?t=biblia)
There is no discrepancy at all. An angel of the Lord moved the stone and was sitting upon it outside ([Matthew 28:2](https://ref.ly/Matt%2028.2;nasb95?t=biblia)). The two men ([Luke 24:4](https://ref.ly/Luke%2024.4;nasb95?t=biblia)) were angels ([John 20:12](https://ref.ly/John%2020.12;nasb95?t=biblia)). [Mark 16:5](https://ref.ly/Mark%2016.5;nasb95?t=biblia) presents the only potential issue, and it isn’t the only one at all. If there were two angels in the tomb, then there was at least one. This one was on the right. Therefore, we see that there was one angel outside and two on the inside of the tomb.
