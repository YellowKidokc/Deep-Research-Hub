# The Joseph Smith Translation and Hebrews 6:1
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
November 14, 2019 
8 min read 
Most people know that Joseph Smith, the founder of Mormonism, claimed to miraculously translate the [Book of Mormon](https://carm.org/2008/12/16/a-quick-look-at-the-book-of-mormon/) from a set of ancient plates which were afterward taken up to heaven. What some may not know is that Joseph Smith also claimed to prophetically translate [other documents](https://carm.org/2008/12/16/the-book-of-abraham-papyri-and-joseph-smith/) by the gift and power of God, including a [new “translation” of the Bible](https://carm.org/2016/06/10/the-joseph-smith-translation-of-the-bible-and-his-false-claim-to-prophethood/) itself! Since the Bible is the most thoroughly studied and accurately transmitted book from antiquity in all of human history, and since the languages in which the Bible was written are so widely known, this provides the perfect opportunity to evaluate Joseph Smith’s claims. Was he _really_ able to accurately translate the Bible? When we examine his work, the clear answer is no, he could not. One good sample verse is in Hebrew 6:1.
If we were to open the old KJV and read the verse the way Joseph Smith would have read it in English Bibles of his own day, it would say:
“Therefore leaving the principles of the doctrine of Christ, let us go on unto perfection; not laying again the foundation of repentance from dead works, and of faith toward God,” ([Hebrews 6:1, KJV](https://ref.ly/Heb%206.1;kjv1900?t=biblia)).
