# Mark 3:28-29 and Universalism
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 15, 2008 
8 min read 
The universalist states that there is no unforgivable sin because all people who have ever lived will ultimately be reconciled to God; in other words, all sins from all people who have ever lived will be forgiven. However, if there were a sin that will never be forgiven, then Universalism would be proven wrong. [Mark 3:28-29](https://ref.ly/Mark%203.28-29;nasb95?t=biblia) are important verses in showing that there is an unforgivable sin.[ ![Mark 3:28-29 and Universalism](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/universalism-01.jpg) ](https://carm.org/universalism/mark-328-29-and-universalist/attachment/universalism-01/)
