### Notes
  1. _All this was done, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying, Tell ye the daughter of Sion, Behold, thy King cometh unto thee, meek, and sitting upon an ass, and a colt the foal of an ass._ ([vv.4-5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/21.html#4)) 
These verses claim that Jesus fulfilled the prophecy in [Zechariah 9:9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/zech/9.html#9). But this cannot be since the person referred to in Zechariah (see verses [10-13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/zech/9.html#10)) was both a military leader and the king of an earthly kingdom.
This "prophecy" is a composite quote from Isaiah and Zechariah.
> Behold, the LORD hath proclaimed unto the end of the world, _Say ye to the daughter of Zion_ , Behold, thy salvation cometh; behold, his reward is with him, and his work before him. [Is 62:11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/62.html#11)
> Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion; shout, O daughter of Jerusalem: _behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass._ [Zech 9:9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/zech/9.html#9)
  2. _The disciples ... brought the ass, and the colt, and put on them their clothes, and they set him thereon._ ([v.6-7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/21.html#6)) 
Matthew has Jesus ride into Jerusalem sitting on _both_ an ass and a colt (must have taken some practice!).
It also looks like Jesus and his disciples stole the animals.
[On what did Jesus ride into Jerusalem?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/ass_colt.html)
[On the day that Jesus rode into Jerusalem, did Jesus ask his disciples to get one animal or two?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/one-or-two.html)
[Did Jesus sin?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/interp/jesus_sin.html)
  3. _Jesus went into the temple of God, and cast out all them that sold and bought in the temple._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/21.html#12)) 
[When did Jesus's temple tantrum occur?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/tantrum.html)
  4. _My house shall be called the house of prayer; but ye have made it a den of thieves._ ([v.13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/21.html#13)) 
> Mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all people. [Is 56:7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/56.html#7)
> Is this house, which is called by my name, become a den of robbers in your eyes? [Jer 7:11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jer/7.html#11)
  5. _Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings thou hast perfected praise._ ([v.16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/21.html#16)) 
Jesus misquotes Psalm 8:2.
> Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength because of thine enemies, that thou mightest still the enemy and the avenger. [Psalm 8:2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/8.html#2)
  6. _And presently the fig tree withered away._ ([v.19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/21.html#19)) 
Jesus curses a fig tree and the tree dies immediately (showing the world how much [God Hates Figs](https://www.ucc.org/daily_devotional_god_hates_figs_satire_alert)). But in Mark's gospel ([11:14, 20-21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/11.html#14)) the cursed fig tree doesn't die until the next morning.
[When did the fig tree die?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/fig.html)
[When did Jesus curse the fig tree?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/fig_temple.html)
[What the Bible says about figs](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/figs.html)
  7. _The lord of the vineyard ... will miserably destroy those wicked men, and will let out his vineyard unto other husbandmen, which shall render him the fruits in their seasons._ ([vv.40-41](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/21.html#40)) 
God is like a rich man who owns a vineyard and rents it to poor farmers.
When he sends servants to collect the rent, the tenants beat or kill them.
So he sent his son to collect the rent, and they kill him too.
Then the owner comes and kills the farmers and rents the vineyard to others who will give him the fruits from the vineyard.
  8. _Whosoever shall fall on this stone shall be broken: but on whomsoever it shall fall, it will grind him to powder._ ([v.44](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/21.html#44)) 
Whoever falls on "this stone" (Jesus) will be broken, and whomever the stone falls on will be ground into powder.
