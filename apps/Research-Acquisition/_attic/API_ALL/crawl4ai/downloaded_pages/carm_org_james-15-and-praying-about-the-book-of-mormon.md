by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
May 21, 2012 
6 min read

“But if any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God, who gives to all men generously and without reproach, and it will be given to him” ([James 1:5](https://ref.ly/James%201.5;nasb95?t=biblia)).
Mormons typically quote this verse in support of praying about the Book of Mormon as is suggested within its pages.
