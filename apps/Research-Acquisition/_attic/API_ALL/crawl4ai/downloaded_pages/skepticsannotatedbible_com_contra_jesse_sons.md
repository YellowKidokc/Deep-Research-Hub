## How many sons did Jesse have?
* * *
### Jesse had eight sons.
> Jesse made seven of his sons to pass before Samuel. And Samuel said unto Jesse, Are these all thy children? And he said, There remaineth yet the youngest [David]. [1 Samuel 16:10-11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1sam/16.html#10)
> Now David was the son of that Ephrathite of Bethlehemjudah, whose name was Jesse; and he had eight sons. [1 Samuel 17:12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1sam/17.html#12)
### Jesse had seven sons.
> And Jesse begat his firstborn Eliab, and ... David the seventh. [1 Chronicles 2:13-15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/2.html#13)
