# Does the gospel of Mark teach the deity of Jesus?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 04, 2016 
5 min read 
Yes, the Gospel of Mark teaches the deity of Christ. In fact, it is found in the opening words of the gospel. Let’s take a look.
The words that are in capitals are quotes from the Old Testament from two different locations.
What is significant is [Isaiah 40:3](https://ref.ly/Isa%2040.3;nasb95?t=biblia) where the word “LORD” is the Hebrew word formed by the four letters YHWH. YHWH is the name of God, known as “I AM”, ([Exodus 3:14-15](https://ref.ly/Exod%203.14-15;nasb95?t=biblia)). Since no one knows how to pronounce those four letters, and in order to not take the Lord’s name in vain by mispronouncing it, the custom has been to substitute those four letters for the Hebrew word ‘adonai’ wich is the word ‘lord.’ So, it is translated as LORD in English. Therefore, when it says “clear the way for the LORD in the wilderness” it is like saying “clear the way for GOD in the wilderness.” Mark is using this in the context of Jesus in the wilderness. Therefore, the Gospel of Mark teaches the deity of Jesus.
