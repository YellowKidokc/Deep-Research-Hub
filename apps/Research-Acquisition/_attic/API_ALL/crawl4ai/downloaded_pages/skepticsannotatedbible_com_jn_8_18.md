The woman caught in adultery

1 Jesus went unto the mount of Olives.
2 And early in the morning he came again into the temple, and all the people came unto him; and he sat down, and taught them.
3 And the scribes and Pharisees brought unto him a woman taken in adultery; and when they had set her in the midst,
The next morning, Jesus was teaching at the temple,
when the scribes and Pharisees brought a woman who was accused of adultery. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#1n)]
4 They say unto him, Master, this woman was taken in adultery, in the very act.
They said to him,
> Master, this woman was caught in the act of adultery.
5 Now Moses in the law commanded us, that such should be stoned: but what sayest thou?
> The law of Moses commands us to stone her to death.
> What do you say we should do?
6 This they said, tempting him, that they might have to accuse him. But Jesus stooped down, and with his finger wrote on the ground, as though he heard them not.
Jesus stooped down and wrote on the ground, as though he hadn't heard them.

Then he stood up and said,
> Let whoever is without sin throw the first stone. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#2n)]

Then Jesus stooped down again and wrote on the ground.
9 And they which heard it, being convicted by their own conscience, went out one by one, beginning at the eldest, even unto the last: and Jesus was left alone, and the woman standing in the midst.
And all of the woman's accusers left, one by one.
10 When Jesus had lifted up himself, and saw none but the woman, he said unto her, Woman, where are those thine accusers? hath no man condemned thee?
Jesus stood up and said,
> Woman, where are your accusers?
> Has no one condemned you?
11 She said, No man, Lord. And Jesus said unto her, Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more.
The woman said, "No one."
And Jesus said,
> Then I don't condemn you either.
> Go, and don't sin anymore.

> I'm the light of the world. If you follow me, you won't walk in darkness, but will have the light of life.

The Pharisees said,
> You are your own witness.
> Your testimony isn't true.

Jesus said,

> my testimony is true. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#3n)]

16 And yet if I judge, my judgment is true: for I am not alone, but I and the Father that sent me.

> I am my own witness.

Is he going to kill himself?

The Jews said, "Is he going to commit suicide?"

27 They understood not that he spake to them of the Father.
28 Then said Jesus unto them, When ye have lifted up the Son of man, then shall ye know that I am he, and that I do nothing of myself; but as my Father hath taught me, I speak these things.
29 And he that sent me is with me: the Father hath not left me alone; for I do always those things that please him.

### Notes
  1. _The scribes and Pharisees brought unto him a woman taken in adultery._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#3)) 
This is one of the best-known and most-loved of all Bible stories, but it probably shouldn't be in the Bible. For although some manuscripts put it here, others after John 7:36, 21:35, or Luke 21:38, it is not found anywhere in the oldest and best manuscripts.
  2. _He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#7)) 
Jesus tells those who'd like to stone to death an adulteress that whoever among them that is without sin should "cast the first stone."
Good advice -- but it directly contradicts the teachings of the Old Testament ([Leviticus 20:10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lev/20.html#10)).
If that wasn't a good law then, why did God make it? Has he since changed his mind? If so, shouldn't it then be removed (along with most of the OT) from the bible?
[How should adulterers be punished?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/adultery_punishment.html)
[Does God approve of capital punishment?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/capital.html)
  3. _Though I bear record of myself, yet my record is true._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#14)) 
_I am one that bear witness of myself._ ([v.18](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#18))
[Did Jesus bears witness of himself?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/jesus_witness.html)
[Was Jesus's witness of himself true?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/true.html)
  4. _I judge no man._ ([v.15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#15)) 
[Does Jesus judge people?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/Judge_Jesus.html)
  5. _If ye believe not that I am he, ye shall die in your sins._ ([v.24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#24)) 
If you don't believe in Jesus, you will "die in your sins" (and then go to hell).
  6. _We be not born of fornication._ ([v.41](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#41)) 
Implying that Jesus was.
  7. _If a man keep my saying, he shall never see death._ ([v.51](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#51)) 
[Must everyone die?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/die.html)
  8. _Before Abraham was, I am._ ([v.58](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/8.html#58)) 
By saying "before Abraham was, I am," Jesus claims to be God.
[Was he?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/Jesus_God.html)
But it may be a bit more complicated than that. Here is what Dan McClellan has to say about it.
