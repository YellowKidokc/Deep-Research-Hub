# How many charioteers were killed, 700 or 7,000?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 28, 2008 
1 min read 
How many charioteers were killed, 700 or 7,000? 2 Samuel 10:18 and 1 Chronicles 19:18
This is most probably a copyist error. Notice how the number is off by a single zero; that is, by a single notation of a digit. According to “Alleged Discrepancies of the Bible,” page 382, regarding the characters used to designate numbers, “Nun final  ![Final Nun](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/Nun_final.gif) , was mistaken for dotted Zayin  ![Zayin](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/Zayin.gif) ,” would account for the copyist error in the text. Most probably, the correct number is 7,000 charioteers.
