### Notes
  1. _Now Benjamin begat Bela ... Ashbel ... Aharah._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/8.html#11)) 
There are four lists of Benjamin's sons in the Bible. None of them agree and only one name (Bela) is found in all four lists.
[Who were the sons of Benjamin?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/bens_sons.html)
  2. _And at Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon...._ ([v.29](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/8.html#29)) 
You can say that again.
If you happen to miss some of the details in these verses, don't worry. They are repeated for you in the next chapter. ([9:35-44](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/9.html#35))
  3. _Ner begat Kish_ ([v.33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/8.html#33)) 
[Was Ner or Abiel the father of Kish?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/kish_dad.html)
  4. _Saul begat Jonathan, and Malchishua, and Abinadab, and Eshbaal._ ([v.33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/8.html#33)) 
[Who were Saul's sons?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/sauls_sons.html)
