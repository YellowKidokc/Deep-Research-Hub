# What figures of speech did Jesus use?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
October 08, 2025 
8 min read 
Jesus used many different types of figures of speech. He was a master at communication. Jesus often used illustrations, exaggerations, comparisons, and other techniques to communicate profound spiritual truths that would be familiar to the average person of His day. His words were often simple enough to be understood by children and deep enough to be examined by the mature. The fishermen could understand the parable about the nets catching fish, while theologians would understand how such images reflected the kingdom of God. To understand Jesus better, we often need to know what kinds of speech He used. The following is a list of different figures of speech used by Jesus and His teaching.
An aphorism is a memorable statement that expresses a general truth. “I am the light of the world” (John 8:12).
A hyperbole is a figure of speech that uses exaggeration to make a point. “You are the light of the ENTIRE WORLD!”
