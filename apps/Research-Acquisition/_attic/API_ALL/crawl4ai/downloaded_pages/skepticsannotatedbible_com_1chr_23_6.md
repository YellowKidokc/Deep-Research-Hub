David makes Solomon king and counts Levites 
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/boring.63b5b35a30388.jpg) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 23:1-32 
**1** So when David was old and full of days, he made Solomon his son king over Israel. 
**2** And he gathered together all the princes of Israel, with the priests and the Levites. 
When David was old, he made his son, Solomon, king of Israel. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/23.html#1n)] 
**3** Now the Levites were numbered from the age of thirty years and upward: and their number by their polls, man by man, was thirty and eight thousand. 
**4** Of which, twenty and four thousand were to set forward the work of the house of the LORD; and six thousand were officers and judges: 
**5** Moreover four thousand were porters; and four thousand praised the LORD with the instruments which I made, said David, to praise therewith. 
David gathered all the Levites together and counted the men who were at least 30 years old. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/23.html#2n)]

**9** The sons of Shimei; Shelomith, and Haziel, and Haran, three. These were the chief of the fathers of Laadan. 
**10** And the sons of Shimei were, Jahath, Zina, and Jeush, and Beriah. These four were the sons of Shimei. 
**11** And Jahath was the chief, and Zizah the second: but Jeush and Beriah had not many sons; therefore they were in one reckoning, according to their father's house. 
_The sons of Levi, namely, Gershon, Kohath, and Merari_ [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/23.html#3n)]

**13** The sons of Amram; Aaron and Moses: and Aaron was separated, that he should sanctify the most holy things, he and his sons for ever, to burn incense before the LORD, to minister unto him, and to bless in his name for ever.

**15** The sons of Moses were, Gershom, and Eliezer.

**25** For David said, The LORD God of Israel hath given rest unto his people, that they may dwell in Jerusalem for ever: 
**26** And also unto the Levites; they shall no more carry the tabernacle, nor any vessels of it for the service thereof.

### Notes
  1. _So when David was old and full of days, he made Solomon his son king over Israel._ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/23.html#1)) 
That's all the Chronicler has to say about the transfer of power from David to Solomon. 
See the last ten chapers of 2 Samuel and the first couple chapters of 1 Kings for messy details. 
  2. _Now the Levites were numbered from the age of thirty years and upward._ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/23.html#3), [24](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/23.html#24), [27](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/23.html#27)) 
This seems a bit reckless on David's part. 
[God had just killed 70,000 men](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/21.html#kills) for a similar census just a couple chapters ago. 
[How old did a Levite have to be to be counted?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/counted.html)
  3. _The sons of Levi, namely, Gershon, Kohath, and Merari_ ([v.6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/23.html#6), [12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/23.html#12), [13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/23.html#13)) 
[Was Mahli the son of Levi?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/mahli.html)
[How many generations were between Levi and Moses?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/levi-moses.html)
  4. _The sons of Merari; Mahli_ ([v.21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/1chr/23.html#21)) 
[Was Mahli the son of Levi?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/mahli.html)
