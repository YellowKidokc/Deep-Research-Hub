# Does Matthew 27:9-10 misquote Jeremiah?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
September 08, 2021 
11 min read 
![Jeremiah 32: is it referenced in Matthew 27:9-10?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Jeremiah-32-300x254.jpg) Critics of Christianity frequently accuse the New Testament of “misquoting” the Old Testament. _(For other examples, see: Does Hebrews 8:9 misquote Jeremiah 31:32, Does Luke 4:18 misquote Isaiah 61:1, Does Hebrews 10:5 misquote Psalm 40:6, and Does Matthew 2:6 misquote Micah 5:2-4?)_ Perhaps the most challenging example of this is the citation which Matthew attributes to Jeremiah in Matthew 27:9-10. The words he quotes are not found in Jeremiah at all, but most closely resemble a citation of Zechariah 11:13 (though with notable differences). Even sincere Christian readers can sometimes find this passage confusing and wonder what Matthew was thinking. How can we reconcile this with a view that this gospel is the inspired, inerrant word of God?
Let’s take a look at the passage in question:
“Then that which was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled: ‘And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of the one whose price had been set by the sons of Israel; and they gave them for the Potter’s Field, as the Lord directed me,” (Matthew 27:9-10).
