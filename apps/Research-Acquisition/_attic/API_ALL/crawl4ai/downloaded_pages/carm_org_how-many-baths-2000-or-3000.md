# How many baths, 2,000 or 3,000?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 29, 2008 
1 min read 
How many baths, 2,000 or 3,000 in 1 Kings 7 and 2 Chronicles 4?
It appears that the difference in numbers is due to a copyist error. The characters used for the numbers 2,000 and 3,000 are very similar.  ![2,000 character in Hebrew](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/2000.gif) = 2,000 and  ![3,000 character in Hebrew](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/3000.gif) = 3,000. A tired copyist could easily mistake one for another. (Haley, Alleged Discrepancies of the Bible, p. 382.)
