### CHAPTER 20
Miriam dies
20:1
1 Then came the children of Israel, even the whole congregation, into the desert of Zin in the first month: and the people abode in Kadesh; and Miriam died there, and was buried there.
Miriam died in the first month in the Desert of Zin. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#1n)]
The people complain (again) about the lack of food and water
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 20:2-5, Cf. [Num 11:4-33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/11.html#S2)
2 And there was no water for the congregation: and they gathered themselves together against Moses and against Aaron.
3 And the people chode with Moses, and spake, saying, Would God that we had died when our brethren died before the LORD!
There was no water for the people to drink, so they said to Moses, 
> We wish to God we had died when God killed our brothers. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#2n)]
4 And why have ye brought up the congregation of the LORD into this wilderness, that we and our cattle should die there?
> Why have you brought us into this wilderness to die?
5 And wherefore have ye made us to come up out of Egypt, to bring us in unto this evil place? it is no place of seed, or of figs, or of vines, or of pomegranates; neither is there any water to drink.
> Why did you force us to leave Egypt to bring us to this evil place, where there is no seed, figs, vines, pomegranates, or water?
Moses smites a rock (twice) and water comes out
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif)![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) 20:6-11, Cf. [Ex 17:1-1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/17.html#S1)
6 And Moses and Aaron went from the presence of the assembly unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and they fell upon their faces: and the glory of the LORD appeared unto them.
When Moses and Aaron heard the people's complaints, [they fell upon their faces](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/topics/FOF.html) and the glory of the LORD appeared to them.
7 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,
8 Take the rod, and gather thou the assembly together, thou, and Aaron thy brother, and **speak ye unto the rock** before their eyes; and it shall give forth his water, **and thou shalt bring forth to them water out of the rock** : so thou shalt give the congregation and their beasts drink.
God said to Moses, 
> Take your rod and talk to the rock where the people can see you. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#3n)] 
> Water will come out of the rock, so the people and animals can drink.
9 And Moses took the rod from before the LORD, as he commanded him.
10 And Moses and Aaron gathered the congregation together before the rock, and he said unto them, Hear now, ye rebels; must we fetch you water out of this rock?
Moses took his rod and gathered the congregation in front of the rock. 
He said to them,
> Hear now, you rebels. 
> Do I have to get water for you out of this rock?
11 And **Moses** lifted up his hand, and with his rod he **smote the rock twice: and the water came out** abundantly, and the congregation drank, and their beasts also.
Moses smote the rock twice, and water came out.
There was enough water for everyone to drink.
God punishes Moses and Aaron for smiting the rock twice
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif)20:12-22, Cf. [Ex 17:6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/17.html#6), [Num 27:12-13](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/27.html#12)

13 This is the water of Meribah; because the children of Israel strove with the LORD, and he was sanctified in them.
God said to Moses and Aaron,
> Because you didn't believe me, you won't enter the promised land. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#4n)] 
> This is the water of Meribah.

### Notes
  1. _The desert of Zin_ ([v.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#1)) 
[Where did Moses get water from a rock?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/waterrock.html)
  2. _Would God that we had died when our brethren died before the LORD!_ ([v.3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#3)) 
It's not clear which killing the people are referring to here.
Here a some of God's previous killings in Numbers:
     * He killed Aaron's sons, Nadab and Abihu. [11:1-4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#S1)
     * God burned people who complained. [11:1-3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/11.html#S1)
     * He killed others in a plague for complaining about the quails. [11:33-35](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/11.html#33)
     * He killed the ten honest spies. [14:36-45](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/14.html#S5)
     * He commanded the people to stone to death a sabbath breaker. [15:32-36](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/15.html#S3)
     * He killed Korah and his companions. [16:23-33](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/16.html#S3)
     * He killed 250 for burning incense. [16:34-40](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/16.html#S4)
     * And he killed 14,700 for complaining about his killings. [16:49-50](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/16.html#S8)
Maybe they were referring to all of these killings.
  3. _Speak ye unto the rock_ ([v.8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#8)) 
[How did God tell Moses to get water from a rock?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/water_rock.html)
  4. _Because ye believed me not._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#12)) 
God told Moses to get water out of a rock [by talking to it](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#8). Moses struck the rock twice with his rod, and water came out. I guess God wanted Moses to get water out by talking to the rock, not by hitting it with his rod. (Although he did say to bring his rod when getting water from the rock.)
In [Exodus 17:6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/17.html#6), God told Moses to smite the rock (once) to get water out of it. Moses did that and it worked.
In any case, God was angry with Moses and Aaron for messing up the magic trick. To punish them God makes sure they never get to the promised land.
  5. _Strip Aaron of his garments, and put them upon Eleazar his son._ ([v.26](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#26)) 
God strips Aaron of his garments and gives them to his son, Eleazar. Then he announces Aaron's death.
God will not permit him to see the promised land, because [Moses struck the rock twice](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#11) -- instead of just [speaking to the rock](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#8). Or something like that.
  6. _And Aaron died there._ ([v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/20.html#28)) 
Did God kill Aaron on mount Hor? The Bible doesn't say, but it sure looks suspicious.
Since the Bible isn't clear on this, I didn't include Aaron's death in [Drunk With Blood: God's killings in the Bible](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/dwb/intro.html)
