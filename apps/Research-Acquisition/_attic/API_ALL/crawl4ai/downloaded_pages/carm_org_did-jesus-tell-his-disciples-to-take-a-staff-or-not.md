# Did Jesus tell His disciples to take a staff or not?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 02, 2008 
2 min read 
Did Jesus tell His disciples to take a staff or not? [Mark 6:8](https://ref.ly/Mark%206.8;nasb95?t=biblia) with [Matthew 10:9-10](https://ref.ly/Matt%2010.9-10;nasb95?t=biblia) and [Luke 9:3](https://ref.ly/Luke%209.3;nasb95?t=biblia). ![Did Jesus tell His disciples to take a staff or not?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/man-with-staff-01.jpg)
There are no textual variants in these verses in the Greek manuscripts that would bear upon this issue regarding the staff. The word for “take” in both [Mark 6:8](https://ref.ly/Mark%206.8;nasb95?t=biblia) and [Luke 9:3](https://ref.ly/Luke%209.3;nasb95?t=biblia) is _airo_. In [Matthew 10:9](https://ref.ly/Matt%2010.9;nasb95?t=biblia), the word for “acquire” is _ktaomai_. Each account is preceded by the raising of the dead girl so the context of each is the same. It is possible that there is a textual variation not yet known. Yet, this is not an acceptable explanation I would take since it is based on a lack of proof. How then can this difficulty be reconciled?
The best explanation I can offer is that Matthew clarifies the issue by telling the disciples to not acquire anything more than what they already had. It is possible that the “take” of Mark and Luke can be interpreted to mean acquire, but I think this is a weak explanation. Nevertheless, each verse is saying that the disciples are to go as they are and to not take provisions – just trust the Lord.
