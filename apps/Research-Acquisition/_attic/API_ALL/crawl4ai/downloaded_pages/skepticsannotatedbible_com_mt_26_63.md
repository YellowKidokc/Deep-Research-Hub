### Notes
  1. _Jesus ... said unto his disciples, Ye know that after two days is the feast of the passover, and the Son of man is betrayed to be crucified._ ([vv.1-2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/26.html#1)) 
This the fourth time that Jesus predicts his death. (See [16:21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/16.html#satan), [17:22-23](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/17.html#death), and [20:17-19](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/20.html#death) for the first three times.)
[Did Jesus forewarn his disciples about his coming death?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/comingdeath.html)
  2. _There came unto him a woman having an alabaster box of very precious ointment, and poured it on his head._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/26.html#7)) 
There are four similar stories about this in the gospels. It's hard to say whether they are supposed to describe the same event.
If so, they contradict one another. If not, Jesus sure likes to get his head and feet anointed with precious oils.
Here's a a summary of the differences between the accounts. 

_When did Jesus get his head and/or feet anointed?_
    Two days before the Passover ([Mt 26:2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/26.html#2), [Mk 14:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#1))     Long before the Passover ([Lk 7:36-50](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/7.html#36))     Six days before the Passover ([Jn 12:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/12.html#1)) 

_Where did it happen?_
    In Bethany, at Simon the leper's house ([Mt 26:6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/26.html#6), [Mk 14:3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#3))     At a Pharisee's house ([Lk 7:36](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/7.html#36))     In Bethany, at Lazarus's house ([Jn 12:1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/12.html#1)) 

_Who did the anointing?_
    A woman ([Mt 26:6-7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/26.html#6), [Mk 14:3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#3))     A sinful woman ([Lk 7:36-37](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/7.html#36))     Mary, the sister of Lazarus ([Jn 12:3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/12.html#3)) 

_What type of oil was used?_
    An alabaster box of very precious ointment ([Mt 26:7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/26.html#7))     An alabaster box of spikenard ([Mk 14:3-9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#3))     An alabaster box of ointment ([Lk 7:37](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/7.html#37))     A pound of spikenard ([Jn 12:3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/12.html#3)) 

_What part of Jesus's body was anointed?_
    Jesus's head ([Mt 26:7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/26.html#7), [Mk 14:3-9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#3)) and body ([Mt 26:12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/26.html#12))     Jesus's feet ([Lk 7:38](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/7.html#38), [Jn 12:3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/12.html#3))

    The disciples ([Mt 26:8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/26.html#8))     Some people ([Mk 14:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/14.html#4))     The Pharisee ([Lk 7:39](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/7.html#39))     Judas Iscariot ([Jn 12:4-5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/jn/12.html#4))
  3. _For ye have the poor always with you; but me ye have not always._ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/26.html#11))
