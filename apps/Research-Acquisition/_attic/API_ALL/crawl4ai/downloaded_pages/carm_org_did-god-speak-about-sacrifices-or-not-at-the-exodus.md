# Did God speak about sacrifices or not at the Exodus?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
November 28, 2008 
3 min read 
Jeremiah says that God did not speak to the early Israelites about sacrifices when He brought them out of Egypt. In Exodus, however, God seems to say a lot about sacrifices. Is this a contradiction? Note the verses:
In [Jeremiah 7:22](https://ref.ly/Jer%207.22;nasb95?t=biblia), God was speaking about “the day” that He brought the Israelites out of Egypt. That day was the day that they took the spoils of Egypt and walked out into the desert finally escaping their bondage. Exodus 20 is the time when Moses received the Ten Commandments from God. Between the day that the Israelites left Egypt and the Ten Commandments at Sinai (Exodus 20) was at least three months. “In the third month after the sons of Israel had gone out of the land of Egypt, on that very day they came into the wilderness of Sinai” ([Exodus 19:1](https://ref.ly/Exod%2019.1;nasb95?t=biblia)). Therefore, we see that it wasn’t on the day that God brought them out of Egypt that He spoke about sacrifices but three months later at mount Sinai.
The following are some verses extracted between Exodus 12 when Israel left Egypt and Exodus 20 when they received the Ten Commandments. They demonstrate that “that day/the day” is in reference to the day of the Exodus–not their arrival three months later at Sinai.
