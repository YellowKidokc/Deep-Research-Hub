# Hebrews 11:1, Faith
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
December 01, 2008 
13 min read 
[Hebrews 11:2](https://ref.ly/Heb%2011.2;nasb95?t=biblia), “Now faith is being sure of what we hope for and certain of what we do not see.” ![Graphics: faith is reckoned as righteousness](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/greekfaithasrighteousness.gif)
**Conclusion**
Faith is vitally important in the life of the Christian. It is by faith that you are saved, justified, cleansed, and look forward to the return of Jesus. Faith is where you please God, admit your dependence upon Him, and continually seek to rely upon Him and His grace. I would like to close by asking you these questions: “What has faith changed in your life? What are faith’s evidences in your life? Is faith growing in your life?
