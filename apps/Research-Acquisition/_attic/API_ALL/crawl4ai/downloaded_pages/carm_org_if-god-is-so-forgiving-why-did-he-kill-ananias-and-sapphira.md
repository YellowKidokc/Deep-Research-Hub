# If God is so forgiving, why did He kill Ananias and Sapphira?
by [Matt Slick](https://carm.org/matt-slick)
July 25, 2009 
3 min read 
Ananias and Sapphira were killed because they lied to the Holy Spirit. In Acts chapter 5:1-11, they specifically lied to the apostles and, therefore, to the Holy Spirit ([Acts 5:3](https://ref.ly/Acts%205.3;nasb95?t=biblia)). What they did was sell their land for a certain price and then keep some of the money. Their lie was that they had given  _all_ the money they had received for the sale of the property when, in reality, they hadn’t. Therefore, their judgment was swift. Also, it would serve as a warning to people about the seriousness of sin, as well as showing that God knows our sins and we’ll be held accountable for them. This does not mean we gain or lose salvation by our goodness, honesty, and integrity. But it does provide us with a representation of the consequences of sin.
God is not obligated to forgive anyone. All deserve God’s judgment, and His mercy is shown repeatedly to all people. He allows them to sin against Him and still enjoy life ([Matt. 5:43-48](https://ref.ly/Matt.%205.43-48;nasb95?t=biblia)). God’s forgiveness is always based on the work of Christ, and those who would openly and directly rebel against God and His goodness, as Ananias and Sapphira did, rightly deserve the judgment of God. ![If God is so forgiving, why did He kill Ananias and Sapphira?](https://carm.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/God-01.jpg)
God is holy ([Isaiah 6:3](https://ref.ly/Isa%206.3;nasb95?t=biblia); [1 Pet. 1:16](https://ref.ly/1%20Pet.%201.16;nasb95?t=biblia); [Rev. 4:8](https://ref.ly/Rev.%204.8;nasb95?t=biblia) ), and He is always just ([Neh. 9:32-33](https://ref.ly/Neh.%209.32-33;nasb95?t=biblia); [2 Thess. 1:6](https://ref.ly/2%20Thess.%201.6;nasb95?t=biblia)) and merciful ([Exodus 34:6](https://ref.ly/Exod%2034.6;nasb95?t=biblia); [Psalm 67:1](https://ref.ly/Ps%2067.1;nasb95?t=biblia); [James 5:11](https://ref.ly/James%205.11;nasb95?t=biblia)).
