### Notes
  1. _The LORD spake unto Moses and unto Eleazar ... saying, Take the sum of all the congregation of the children of Israel._ ([vv.1-2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/26.html#1)) 
[Is it OK to have a census?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/census.html)
  2. _Who left the land of Egypt_ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/26.html#4)) 
What is meant here is (apparently) exactly the opposite of what is said. The census was for those who were more than 20 years old _w_ _ho had not left the land of Egypt_ - because God made sure that none of the Israelites who left Egypt would survive to enter the land of Canaan. (God said he'd make sure none of them lived to enter the promised land to punish them for listening to the ten honest spies after they returned from their scouting mission. See [Numbers 14:20-35](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/14.html#20))
  3. _Notwithstanding the children of Korah died not._ ([v.11](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/26.html#11)) 
I'm not sure why this is included in the story about the census. Maybe God is especially proud of these killings.
[Did God burn to death the children of Korah?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/korahs_children.html)
  4. _The sons of Benjamin_ ([v.38](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/26.html#38)) 
[Who were the sons of Benjamin?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/bens_sons.html)
  5. _The sons of Bela were Ard and Naaman_ ([v.40](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/26.html#40)) 
[Were Ard and Naaman the sons or grandsons of Benjamin?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/bens_sons_grandsons.html)
  6. _Six hundred thousand and a thousand seven hundred and thirty._ ([v.51](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/26.html#51)) 
Population Explosion: The Israelite population went from seventy ([Exodus 1:5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ex/1.html#5)) to several million (over 600,000 adult males) in about 400 hundred years.
  7. _The land shall be divided by lot._ ([v.55](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/26.html#55)) 
God's Lottery: God must be okay with gambling since he divided the land by lots.
[Does the bible condemn gambling?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/gambling.html)
  8. _Nadab and Abihu died, when they offered strange fire before the LORD._ ([v.61](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/26.html#61)) 
It's strange that God mentions this killing here, since it has nothing to do with the story.
See the very brief (one verse) story in [Numbers 3:4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/num/3.html#4).
