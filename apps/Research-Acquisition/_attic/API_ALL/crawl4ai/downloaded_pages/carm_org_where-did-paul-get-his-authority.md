# Where did Paul get his authority?
by [Ryan Turner](https://carm.org/ryan-turner)
April 08, 2011 
3 min read 
If Paul did not know Jesus while He lived on this earth, then how can Paul have any authority to teach and preach the Gospel? Where did Paul get his authority? Though Paul did not walk with Jesus while he was on this earth, he did receive approval from the followers of Jesus. In Galatians chapters 1-2, Paul explains that he took two different trips to Jerusalem to see the Jerusalem apostles. _(No scholars today debate the fact that Paul actually wrote the epistle to the Galatians. It is dated around 49-52 A.D.)_
In his first trip, Paul mentions the most popular apostles by name: Peter and James. In [Galatians 1:18-19](https://ref.ly/Gal%201.18-19;nasb95?t=biblia), Paul says,
“Then three years later [36 A.D.?] I went up to Jerusalem to become acquainted with Cephas [or Peter], and stayed with him fifteen days. 19But I did not see any other of the apostles except James, the Lord’s brother.”
