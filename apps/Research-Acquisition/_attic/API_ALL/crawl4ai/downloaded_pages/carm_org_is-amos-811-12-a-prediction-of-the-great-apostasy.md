# Is Amos 8:11-12 a prediction of the Great Apostasy?
by [Luke Wayne](https://carm.org/luke-wayne)
March 02, 2020 
9 min read 
One of the Mormon religion’s central doctrines is their teaching that the New Testament church fell into absolute, universal apostasy shortly after the death of the [Apostles](https://carm.org/dictionary/apostle/). This is the doctrine that justifies their claim of the need for a “restoration” of the “true church,” the “fullness of the gospel,” the “priesthood,” and so much else. Yet, the proof-texts they use to defend this alleged “great apostasy” all turn out to teach nothing of the sort! A great example is Amos 8:11-12. So, let’s take a look at the issue of Amos 8:11-12 and the Great Apostasy.
When teaching about the supposed “great apostasy,” [Mormon](https://carm.org/mormonism/) Missionaries will often refer to Amos 8:11-12, which says:
“‘Behold, days are coming,’ declares the Lord God, ‘When I will send a famine on the land, Not a famine for bread or a thirst for water, But rather for hearing the words of the Lord. People will stagger from sea to sea And from the north even to the east; They will go to and fro to seek the word of the Lord, But they will not find it,” (Amos 8:11-12).
