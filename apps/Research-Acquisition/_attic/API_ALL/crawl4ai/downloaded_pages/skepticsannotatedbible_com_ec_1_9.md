All is vanity 
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/interp.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/good1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) 1:1-18 
1 The words of the Preacher, the son of David, king in Jerusalem.
The words of the Preacher, the son of David, king in Jerusalem. [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#1n)] 
2 Vanity of vanities, saith the Preacher, vanity of vanities; **all is vanity**.
Vanity of vanities; all is vanity. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#2n)] 
3 What profit hath a man of all his labour which he taketh under the sun? 
In the end, nothing is achieved by all that we do in our lives. 
4 One generation passeth away, and another generation cometh: but the earth abideth for ever.
One generation is replaced by another. 
Only the earth lasts forever. [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#3n)] 
5 The sun also ariseth, and the sun goeth down, and hasteth to his place where he arose. 
6 The wind goeth toward the south, and turneth about unto the north; it whirleth about continually, and the wind returneth again according to his circuits. 
The sun rises, and goes down, 
and returns to the place where it arose. [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#4n)] 
7 All the rivers run into the sea; yet the sea is not full; unto the place from whence the rivers come, thither they return again.
8 All things are full of labour; man cannot utter it: the eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing. 
The rivers flow to the sea, yet the sea isn't full. 
The water returns to the place where the rivers began and then return again. [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#5n)] 
9 The thing that hath been, it is that which shall be; and that which is done is that which shall be done: and there is no new thing under the sun.
10 Is there any thing whereof it may be said, See, this is new? it hath been already of old time, which was before us. 
Things that have happened in the past will happen again. 
There's nothing new on earth. [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#6n)] 
11 There is no remembrance of former things; neither shall there be any remembrance of things that are to come with those that shall come after.
12 I the Preacher was king over Israel in Jerusalem.
13 And I gave my heart to seek and search out by wisdom concerning all things that are done under heaven: this sore travail hath God given to the sons of man to be exercised therewith. 
14 I have seen all the works that are done under the sun; and, behold, all is vanity and vexation of spirit. 
No one remembers what happened long ago, 
and no one will remember what happens now or in the future. 
15 That which is crooked cannot be made straight: and that which is wanting cannot be numbered. 
The imperfect can't be corrected, 
and what is missing can't be counted. 
16 I communed with mine own heart, saying, Lo, I am come to great estate, and have gotten more wisdom than all they that have been before me in Jerusalem: yea, my heart had great experience of wisdom and knowledge. 
I am the wisest person who has ever existed. 
17 And I gave my heart to know wisdom, and to know madness and folly: I perceived that this also is vexation of spirit. 
18 For in much wisdom is much grief: and he that increaseth knowledge increaseth sorrow.
I have sought wisdom, and have come to know madness and folly. 
With wisdom comes grief, and increasing knowledge increases sorrow. [[7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#7n)]

### Notes
  1. _The words of the Preacher, the son of David, king in Jerusalem_ ([vv.1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#1), [12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#12), [16](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#16)) 
The author of Ecclesiastes claims to be the wise, rich son of King David, which is to say, Solomon. He isn't. Ecclesiastes was written (forged) about six hundred years after Solomon died (if he ever lived, that is) 
  2. _Vanity of vanities; all is vanity._ ([vv.2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#2), [14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#14)) 
This idea is repeated throughout Ecclesiastes. It seems to mean, more or less, that everything that exists is absurd and meaningless. 
  3. _The earth abideth for ever._ ([v.4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#4)) 
[Will the earth last forever?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/earth.html)
  4. _The sun also ariseth, and the sun goeth down, and hasteth to his place where he arose._ ([v.5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#5)) 
Although this verse is interpreted figuratively today, it was taken literally by virtually all Christians until the Copernican revolution, and was used by the Church to condemn Galileo for teaching the heliocentric heresy. 
  5. _The place from whence the rivers come, thither they return again._ ([v.7](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#7)) 
This is a good description of the water cycle, though the details are missing. 
See [Index to Creationist Claims](http://www.talkorigins.org/indexcc/CH/CH133.html)
  6. _There is nothing new under the sun._ ([v.9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#9)) 
[What's new?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/new.html)
  7. _For in much wisdom is much grief: and he that increaseth knowledge increaseth sorrow._ ([v.](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ec/1.html#)) 
[Does wisdom make people happy?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/wisdom_happy.html)
