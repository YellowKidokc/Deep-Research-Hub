### CHAPTER 13
The parable of the sower
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/boring.63b5b35a30388.jpg)13:1-9, Cf. [Mk 4:1-9](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/4.html#S1), [Lk 8:4-8](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/8.html#S2)
1 The same day went Jesus out of the house, and sat by the sea side.
The same day Jesus went out of the house and sat by the seaside.
2 And great multitudes were gathered together unto him, so that he went into a ship, and sat; and the whole multitude stood on the shore.
A large crowd gathered to see him, so he sat in a boat, leaving the crowd on the shore.
3 And he spake many things unto them in parables, saying, Behold, a sower went forth to sow;
4 And when he sowed, some seeds fell by the way side, and the fowls came and devoured them up:
He said many things to the crowd, such as this parable, 
> A sower sowed some seeds that fell on the wayside, and the birds ate them up.
5 Some fell upon stony places, where they had not much earth: and forthwith they sprung up, because they had no deepness of earth:
6 And when the sun was up, they were scorched; and because they had no root, they withered away.
> Some seeds fell on rocky soil, were burnt by the sun, and withered away because they had no roots.
7 And some fell among thorns; and the thorns sprung up, and choked them:
> Some fell among thorns, and the thorns sprang up and choked them.
8 But other fell into good ground, and brought forth fruit, some an hundredfold, some sixtyfold, some thirtyfold.
> But others fell onto good ground, producing 100, 60, or 30 times as much seed as was scattered.
9 Who hath ears to hear, let him hear.
> If you have ears, hear.
The purpose of the parables (to confuse people)
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/abs1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/inj1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/contra1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/pol.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/pR1.gif) 13:10-17, Cf. [Mk 4:10-12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/4.html#S2), [Lk 8:9-10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/8.html#S3)
10 And the disciples came, and said unto him, Why speakest thou unto them in parables?
The disciples came to Jesus, and said to him, 
> Why do you speak in parables? [[1](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#1n)]
11 He answered and said unto them, Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given.
12 **For whosoever hath, to him shall be given, and he shall have more abundance: but whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken away even that he hath.**
Jesus answered, 
> Because you have been given the power to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven. 
> To them it isn't given.
> Whoever has, will be given even more; whoever has little, will have even that taken away. [[2](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#2n)]
13 Therefore speak I to them in parables: because they seeing see not; and hearing they hear not, neither do they understand.
> So I speak to them in parables, because they see, but don't see, hear, but don't hear, and don't understand anything.
14 And in them is fulfilled the prophecy of Isaiah, which saith, By hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand; and seeing ye shall see, and shall not perceive:
> This is to fulfill the prophecy of Isaiah, which says, "You will hear, but not understand, and see but not perceive." [[3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#3n)]
15 For this people's heart is waxed gross, and their ears are dull of hearing, and their eyes they have closed; lest at any time they should see with their eyes and hear with their ears, and should understand with their heart, and should be converted, and I should heal them.
> These people have had their hearts, ears, and eyes closed to make sure that they never see, hear, or understand.
> So that they will not be converted and I won't heal them.
16 But blessed are your eyes, for they see: and your ears, for they hear.
> Blessed are your eyes, for they see; and your ears, for they hear.
17 For verily I say unto you, That many prophets and righteous men have desired to see those things which ye see, and have not seen them; and to hear those things which ye hear, and have not heard them.
> Many prophets and righteous men [[4](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#4n)] have wanted to see what you see and hear what you hear, but did not.
Jesus explains the parable of the sower
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/boring.63b5b35a30388.jpg)13:18-23, Cf. [Mk 4:13-20](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/4.html#S3), [Lk 8:11-15](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/8.html#S4)
18 Hear ye therefore the parable of the sower.
19 When any one heareth the word of the kingdom, and understandeth it not, then cometh the wicked one, and catcheth away that which was sown in his heart. This is he which received seed by the way side. 
20 But he that received the seed into stony places, the same is he that heareth the word, and anon with joy receiveth it;
21 Yet hath he not root in himself, but dureth for a while: for when tribulation or persecution ariseth because of the word, by and by he is offended.
So here's what the parable of the sower means:
> The one who received the seed in stony places hears the word and receives it with joy, but he didn't have roots. So when troubles and persecution comes, he is offended.
22 He also that received seed among the thorns is he that heareth the word; and the care of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, choke the word, and he becometh unfruitful.
> The one who received the seed in the thorns, is the one who hears the word, but the cares of this world and the pursuit of wealth choke out the word. And he becomes unfruitful.
23 But he that received seed into the good ground is he that heareth the word, and understandeth it; which also beareth fruit, and bringeth forth, some an hundredfold, some sixty, some thirty.
> But the one who received the seed in good ground, hears the word, understands it, and produces 100, 60, or 30 times as much seed as was scattered.
Weeds and wheat
![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/sci1.gif) ![](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/images/interp.gif) 13:24-30
24 Another parable put he forth unto them, saying, The kingdom of heaven is likened unto a man which sowed good seed in his field:
25 But while men slept, his enemy came and sowed tares among the wheat, and went his way.
26 But when the blade was sprung up, and brought forth fruit, then appeared the tares also.
Jesus told them another parable, saying, 
> The kingdom of heaven is like a man who sowed good seed in his field. 
> But while everyone slept, his enemy sowed weeds among the wheat and then went away.
> So when the crop began to grow, weeds grew along with the crop.
27 So the servants of the householder came and said unto him, Sir, didst not thou sow good seed in thy field? from whence then hath it tares?
> The servants said to him, "Didn't you sow good seed in the field? If so, why are there weeds?"
28 He said unto them, An enemy hath done this. The servants said unto him, Wilt thou then that we go and gather them up?
> The farmer said, "An enemy has done this." [[5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#5n)] 
> His servants said, "Should we remove the weeds?"
29 But he said, Nay; lest while ye gather up the tares, ye root up also the wheat with them.
30 Let both grow together until the harvest: and in the time of harvest I will say to the reapers, Gather ye together first the tares, and bind them in bundles to burn them: but gather the wheat into my barn.
> He said, "No, you might destroy the wheat with the weeds. Let them grow together until harvest time. 
> I'll tell the reapers to pull the weeds first and burn them. Then gather the wheat and put it in my barn."
A mustard seed

31 Another parable put he forth unto them, saying, The kingdom of heaven is like to a grain of **mustard seed** , which a man took, and sowed in his field:
32 Which indeed is **the least of all seeds** : but when it is grown, it is the greatest among herbs, and **becometh a tree** , so that the birds of the air come and lodge in the branches thereof.
Jesus told another parable, saying, 
> The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed that a man sowed in his field. 
> The mustard seed is the smallest of all seeds [[6](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#6n)] but it becomes a tree large enough for birds to nest in.
Heaven is like yeast
13:33, Cf. [Lk 13:20-21](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/lk/13.html#20)

> The kingdom of heaven is like yeast that a woman puts into flour to make bread.

### Notes
  1. _Why speakest thou unto them in parables?_ ([v.10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#10)) 
Jesus reveals to his disciples the reason speaks in parables: so that others (those who are not his disciples) won't understand him, "lest ... they ... should be converted, and I should heal them."
[Did Jesus have secret teachings?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/secret.html)
  2. _Whosoever hath, to him shall be given, and he shall have more abundance: but whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken away even that he hath._ ([v.12](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#12)) 
Isn't this from the Republican Party platform?
  3. _In them is fulfilled the prophecy of Isaiah, which saith, By hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand; and seeing ye shall see, and shall not perceive._ ([v.14](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#14)) 
See [Isaiah 6:10](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/is/6.html#10).
  4. _Many ... righteous men have desired to see those things which ye see._ ([v.17](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#17)) 
[Has there ever been a righteous person?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/righteous.html)
  5. _An enemy has done this._ ([v.28](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#28)) 
[Some Christians](https://reknew.org/2015/03/natural-evil-7-arguments-implicating-satan/) believe that the natural evil in the world (predators, parasites, pain, death) is due to Satan, not God.
  6. _A ... mustard seed ... is the least of all seeds._ ([vv.31-32](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#31)) 
Jesus is incorrect when he says that the mustard seed is the smallest seed. ([Orchids have the smallest seeds.](https://www.kew.org/read-and-watch/orchid-seeds-natures-tiny-treasures))
And there are no trees in the mustard family ([Brassicaceae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brassicaceae)).
  7. _He never spoke to them without using parables._ ([v.34](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#34)) 
[Did Jesus always speak in parables?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/parables.html)
  8. _"I will open my mouth in parables; I will utter things which have been kept secret from the foundation of the world._ ([v.35](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#35)) 
Misquote of [Psalm 78:2-3](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/ps/78.html#2) which says: "I will open my mouth in a parable: I will utter dark sayings of old: Which we have heard and known, and our fathers have told us."
According to Psalm 78, the parables were to reveal truths that were already known; not things that had been kept secret, as stated in the quote in Matthew.
  9. _A furnace of fire_ ([v.42](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#42)) 
Jesus will send his angels to gather up "all that offend" and they "shall cast them into a furnace of fire: there shall be wailing and gnashing of teeth."

[What the Bible says about Hell](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/says_about/hell.html)
  10. _Then shall the righteous shine forth as the sun in the kingdom of their Father._ ([v.43](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#43)) 
[Has there ever been a righteous person?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/righteous.html)
  11. _The pearl of great price_ ([v.46](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#46)) 
This phrase was adopted by the Mormon (LDS) church as the title of one of its [standard works](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Works). [The Pearl of Great Price](https://skepticsannotatedbible.com/page.php?type=mainintro&book=m&id=) is a part of Mormon scripture.
  12. _The good ... and ... the just_ ([vv.48-49](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#48)) 
[Is anyone good?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/good.html)
[Has there ever been a just person?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/just.html)
  13. _When he was come into his own country_ ([v.54](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#54)) 
[When was Jesus rejected by the people in his hometown of Nazareth?](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/contra/rejected.html)
  14. _His brethren, James, and Joses, and Simon, and Judas_ ([v.55](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#55)) 
Jesus's brothers and sisters are mentioned, so apparently, contrary to Catholic belief, Mary was not always a virgin. ([Catholic Response](http://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02767a.htm "Catholic Response"))
  15. _And they were offended in him._ ([v.57](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#57)) 
Jesus is rejected by those who know him the best -- the people of his home town of Nazareth.
  16. _He did not many mighty works there because of their unbelief._ ([v.58](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mt/13.html#58)) 
Mark ([6:5](https://www.skepticsannotatedbible.com/mk/6.html#5)) says he couldn't do _any_ mighty works there (besides heal a few sick folk).
