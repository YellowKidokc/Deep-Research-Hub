# DEEP RESEARCH: Conspiracy Theories by Decade (1900s–2020s)

## Instructions for AI Deep Research Mode

You are conducting an exhaustive, decade-by-decade survey of conspiracy theories from 1900 to the present. For each decade, you must be **thorough** — this means capturing not just the famous ones, but the obscure, regional, debunked, and still-active theories.

## For EACH DECADE, provide:

### 1. Major Conspiracy Theories
For each theory, document:
- **Name/Title** — What it's commonly called
- **Core Claim** — What believers assert happened
- **Origin** — When and where it emerged, who started it
- **Key Figures** — Proponents and debunkers
- **Evidence Cited** — What believers point to
- **Debunking** — What the mainstream explanation is
- **Current Status** — Dead, dormant, or still active
- **Connected Theories** — What other conspiracies does it link to

### 2. Categories to Cover
- **Government/Political** — Cover-ups, false flags, assassinations, coups
- **Scientific/Medical** — Suppressed cures, manufactured diseases, hidden technology
- **Financial/Economic** — Banking cabals, market manipulation, hidden wealth
- **Religious/Occult** — Secret societies, end-times scenarios, hidden knowledge
- **Military/Intelligence** — Black ops, weapons programs, surveillance
- **Media/Cultural** — Propaganda campaigns, celebrity conspiracies, psychological operations
- **Extraterrestrial/Paranormal** — UFOs, secret contact, hidden beings
- **Technology** — Suppressed inventions, surveillance tech, mind control

### 3. Pattern Analysis
At the end of each decade, note:
- Which institutions were most distrusted that decade
- What real events gave conspiracy theories fertile ground
- How information technology of the era shaped conspiracy spread
- Which theories from this decade turned out to be TRUE (confirmed conspiracies)

## Decades to Cover
Run this prompt once per decade:
1. **1900–1909** — The Progressive Era
2. **1910–1919** — WWI and the Russian Revolution
3. **1920–1929** — Roaring Twenties and Prohibition
4. **1930–1939** — Great Depression and Rise of Fascism
5. **1940–1949** — WWII and the Atomic Age
6. **1950–1959** — Cold War and McCarthyism
7. **1960–1969** — Assassinations, Vietnam, and the Space Race
8. **1970–1979** — Watergate, COINTELPRO, and Church Committee
9. **1980–1989** — Iran-Contra, AIDS, and the Reagan Era
10. **1990–1999** — Oklahoma City, Waco, Y2K, and the Internet
11. **2000–2009** — 9/11, Iraq War, and the Financial Crisis
12. **2010–2019** — Social Media, QAnon, and the Post-Truth Era
13. **2020–2025** — COVID, Elections, and AI

## Critical Rule
**DO NOT dismiss theories without examining them.** Some "conspiracy theories" turned out to be true — MKUltra, COINTELPRO, Gulf of Tonkin, Tuskegee, NSA mass surveillance. The point is to document them all, then let the evidence speak.

## Output Format
For each decade, produce a structured document that could be imported into an Obsidian vault as a standalone note.
