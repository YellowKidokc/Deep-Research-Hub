[15:49] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orxUXa5ypFw&t=949s) You open the door and enter the house, letting in a gust of cold air. Then, the heater kicks on, and the house comes back to 20. [16:39] For that, you need the 2nd pillar of resilience: Adaptability. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orxUXa5ypFw&t=1017s) Adaptability is a system's ability to change its own baseline. [17:21] Trains use 3-4 times less energy per ton, not because their engines are more efficient, but because they barely have any rolling resistance. You cannot engineer that into a truck. [17:57] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orxUXa5ypFw&t=1077s) See, the goal isn't local everything. The goal is a system that can switch, local, regional, global, depending on our needs. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orxUXa5ypFw&t=1095s) Now, none of these solutions alone is enough. And that's the point. Adaptability isn't about finding the one right answer.
