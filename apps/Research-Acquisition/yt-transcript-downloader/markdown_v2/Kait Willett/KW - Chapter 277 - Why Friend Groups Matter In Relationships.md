Do what makes you happy. It's not cheating if you're finding yourself. Your loyalty shouldn't be larger than your self-love. But all we're doing is enabling Edenism. From an evolutionary perspective, women evolved to form tight in-group alliances. And these group help women raise their children, secure resources, and share social knowledge. But today, that same group think tends to normalize behaviors that were once taboo. If all her friends are cheating, exploring options, or rejecting male leadership, she may not view her own disloyalty as wrong. She may see it as liberating. And you might already know things like, "Her friends are wild." But you tell yourself she's not like them. Or, "She's an individual. She makes her own choices. I trust her even if I don't trust them." And these assumptions ignore the social nature of female bonding. If her tribe validates betrayal, your values are now in the minority.

Even if she starts loyal, repeated exposure to toxic circles will erode her moral resistance over time. So, here's the hard truth. A woman's friends are the emotional echo chamber of her relationship. If they glorify cheating, minimize guilt, and cheer her on for living her truth, sooner or later she might follow along.
