If trust is declining, if loneliness is rising, if relationships are forming way less frequently, why does the broader ecosystem surrounding modern dating continue to operate exactly as it is? When negative outcomes persist across large populations of people and nothing meaningful corrects course, the instability itself might be serving some other means. Economic or structural incentives, not as a cartoon conspiracy, but as alignment of incentive. Modern economies nowadays, in 2026, they run on attention, consumption, and psychological activation. And few psychological states drive engagement more reliably than dissatisfaction and uncertainty. Stable, fulfilled, happy people, well, they consume less content. They scroll less, they search less, and they are much harder to monetize emotionally. Meanwhile, uncertain individuals keep searching. They keep swiping, they keep consuming advice, more validation, or identity reinforcement.

The attention economy, it rewards activation. Outrage, fear, and moral conflict perform much better than calm stability. Gender war dialogue and content, well, that continues to perform because it paints a picture of one clear villain, the opponent. These are simple villains and simple narratives.
