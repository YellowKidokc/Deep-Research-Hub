### [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cZIAXbNrbBE&t=0s)

If you can, move through life aiming to not be perfect, but to make an imperfect choice and take the lesson from that imperfect choice to make a better choice. Next time you are faced with that same obstacle, then you will continue to get better and better and improve. And start evolving into the person that you want to be outside of what society or other people have labeled you or what box they've tried to put you into.
