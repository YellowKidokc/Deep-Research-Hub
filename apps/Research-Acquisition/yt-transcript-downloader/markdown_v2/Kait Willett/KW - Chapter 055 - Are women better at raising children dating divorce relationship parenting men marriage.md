Despite the efforts to promote equality across the genders, there's still a prevalent assumption that women are much better caretakers than men when it comes to raising children. However, when we look at the evidence of outcomes for children based on this, well, that's not exactly what we find. Children from single mother households are more likely to experience lower academic achievement and higher dropout rates. Approximately 71% of high school dropouts come from fatherless homes. On top of this, a father's absence leads to further behavioral problems. Children from fatherless homes account for 85% of youths in prison and 90% of homeless and runaway children. And the absence of a father can lead to emotional challenges, including higher rates of depression and anxiety.
