The difficulty in losing weight has nothing to do with knowing what to do. Most people know they need to eat more protein, they need to track their calories. That's not knowledge, it's bridging the gap between intention and action. We have to focus on the psychology around why you behave the way that you do and start to break some of those beliefs. We need to look at what is the largest lever that we can pull relative to you so that we can get you moving in the right direction without trying to do everything all at once. You are indeed limited by the human experience that we all have, which means our time and our capabilities are restricted. Surface level This is going to look like protein, calories and fiber, but this is likely not news to you. We need to start at a baseline. Do you have protein, Can we focus on starting your meals with what you need and then adding on top of that, what you like?

Aim to hit 1 g per pound of ideal body weight or just look to hit 100 G, and if you can start to slowly build that. Over time, you will be able to incorporate a habit of building your meals with protein to hit your ideal protein targets intuitively.
