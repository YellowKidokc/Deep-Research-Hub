Even with the best workout split ever, optimal is not always practical. It doesn't matter how perfect or wonderful it is. If you're not going to do it, doesn't. matter. So meet yourself where you are and then do that you enjoy do things that you can stick with. Whether that's Pilates, a group class crossfit, marathon training endurance. I don't care what gets you started, I don't care what keeps you going, you just need something that you can stick to.
