No, because you don't always have to lose weight in order to be healthier. You don't only have to focus on doing more cardio, tracking your macros, removing foods that you love and cutting carbs when you're trying to lose weight. The actions that you take to improve your overall health are important because they improve your health, not just because you lose weight. But this is what most people miss, and it's something that a lot of people have just completely lost sight of through the vanity lens of the health and fitness industry. Pushing you to be smaller, preter, leaner, as if that cultivates to something long term that will actually add to your life. Now I want to be honest. There's nothing wrong with you chasing an aesthetic goal, but don't lose sight of health in the pursuit of aesthetics, there are long-term consequences to short-term stupidity. And what looks healthy for you or makes you feel your best may not look like Kim Kardashian, and that's okay.
