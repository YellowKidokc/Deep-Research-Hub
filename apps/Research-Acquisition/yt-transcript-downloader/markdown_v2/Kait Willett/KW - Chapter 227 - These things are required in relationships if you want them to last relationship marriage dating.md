People need freedom and autonomy in relationships and ultimately people will change when they choose to change, not because you want them to change. You can't impose your will upon people to make decisions with their life. You can only give them information, communicate what you see, what you feel, what you think, challenge what they think, get an understanding of where they're at, and then help them take the steps. If they are willing to be able to grow in a direction that they have chosen, that they want to go. But you can't do that and force that upon somebody else, and if somebody else doesn't want to change, that doesn't make them the problem. You're the problem. If you think you have the right, tell them how to live their lives. There comes a point where you have to recognize that while you might love somebody, love isn't always enough. And if somebody who's been through heartbreak with that realization, it sucks.

And it hurts. But you cannot change people who do not want to change. You can only look at it objectively and either accept them fully for who they are and choose to stand by them regardless. Or say, Hey, I love you, but I can't do. This is not what I want in my life for me.
