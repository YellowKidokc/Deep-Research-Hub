Cross-sex mind reading is guessing what the opposite sex is going to do, what they're thinking or feeling, by using your own playbook and getting it wrong. The gist of all this is that assumptions are like, well, you know the phrase. We assume what it would mean if I did that, if I said that, means the same thing as when they do that or when they say that. But because men and women use different habits for stress, talking, and connection, those guesses often miss. And many women use their own emotional intelligence rulebook to grade men. If you're emotionally intelligent, you'll notice my subtle cues, aka when she's walking around huffing and puffing, doing all these things, assuming you would know something is wrong without being direct. You should name your feelings out loud and you should be able to give me validation first. And when a man doesn't do these things immediately, the snap judgment becomes he's lacking in emotional intelligence.

But ultimately, men are wired to use a totally different strategy, less signaling, more quiet down-regulation. So, between the genders, there's ultimately two flavors. Women use signaling, which is expressive and relational, one of the reasons talk therapy works so well for women and not so well for men, and men use regulation emotional intelligence. So, judging one by the other is simply a mind reading trap.
