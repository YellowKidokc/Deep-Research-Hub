How do you get over the fear of starting something new and failing? The hardest thing for most people is that you expect yourself to be perfect, especially if you're like an adult. We have this preconceived notion that once we're older, we're supposed to be able to figure everything out because we're older, wiser and more capable. To some degree, what you fail to recognize is that everybody sucks when they start something new, literally everybody, including myself. If you don't know how to do it, you only have certain amount of information, a certain amount of data points that you could hypothesize. How you might go about doing something and the only way that you're going to gather further information as to how you can get better at it is to actually follow through and do it. And we also have to look at, well, how are you defining failure falling short or you're going to fail? Everybody does.

You won't ever get to where you actually want to be or could be because you don't give yourself permission to learn. So chill out. Take the best step forward that you can think of relative to what you understand and where your current capabilities are right now. And then look at how that goes, reflect on it, take the lessons without guilt or shame. You don't need to judge yourself for doing something less than ideal and then choose to move forward with new information.
