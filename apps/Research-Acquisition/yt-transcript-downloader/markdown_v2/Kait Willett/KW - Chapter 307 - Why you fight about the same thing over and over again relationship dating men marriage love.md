Understanding how to engage in a really difficult conversation while respecting the other person and talking about the problem instead of attacking that person, is a really foundational, interpersonal skill that most people don't have. Because they walk in guns of blazing with assumptions about why somebody did something, why somebody didn't do something instead of tabling the assumptions that you have about somebody else's behavior and genuinely leading with curiosity or owning your feelings and communicating them. But not making it about the other person. But these are skills, especially with the dynamic of our culture and having so many options on social media and online. And Bumble and Tinder, and whatever the you might use, where people have one semblance of discomfort in a dynamic of a relationship. And they immediately jump ship. And the sad part about all of that is if that person did that to, you're the one that's wishing they would have fought for it.

And you also tend to be that person for somebody else, because we don't have the skills to navigate these things together. And we don't have the patience to lead with Curiosity and let go of us having to be right.
