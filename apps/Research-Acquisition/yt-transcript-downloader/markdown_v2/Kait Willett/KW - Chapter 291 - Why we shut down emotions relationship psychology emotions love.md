If you believe your feelings are a burden, you'll create a fourth wall and call that independence. This stems from the anxiety that your feelings are negative for other people to experience, and that displacing them onto other people isn't a form of love. And you likely learn this at a young age. Especially if your parents weren't capable of navigating your negative emotional states, but only praised you and were present and excited when you were happy. This drives you to build an emotional vault of all of the negative emotions that you don't want to feel. So you lock them up inside to keep yourself safe. And you do this because you believe that if somebody truly experienced all of you, they would run. And what I want you to understand is every single human has a core belief that if somebody were to see all of our parts, all of our flaws, all of our shadows, that they would run, too.
