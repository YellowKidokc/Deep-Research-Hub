With new clients, I track a lot of their data and their trends. We'll see that their strength is going up, their waste measurements are going down, they're feeling better, their energy is better, their clothes fit differently, but the scale that they're focused on doing harder, why is the scale not moving? You're missing the improvements that you made, the major variables that you should be focused on versus the one thing that's going to drive you. Batshit Crazy. Because it's never going to move the way that you want it to, because it's not actually the most importantly you should be focused on. You're conditioned to believe that you are only seeing progress or doing the right things if that scale moves in the direction of which you want it to.
