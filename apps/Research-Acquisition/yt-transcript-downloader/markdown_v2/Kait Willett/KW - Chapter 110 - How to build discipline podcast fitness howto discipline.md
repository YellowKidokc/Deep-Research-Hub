Discipline is a skill. It is not some inherent character trait that we all have, it's the ability to separate thoughts and feelings from our behaviors. Every feeling that we have is valid and two things can be true at the same time because we try to emotionally reason. The feeling that we have to mean something needs to change as far as the decisions that we've made. But that's where we go wrong. I can feel sad about the breakup that happened while also understanding that that was the best decision I could have made for myself. That was the right choice.
