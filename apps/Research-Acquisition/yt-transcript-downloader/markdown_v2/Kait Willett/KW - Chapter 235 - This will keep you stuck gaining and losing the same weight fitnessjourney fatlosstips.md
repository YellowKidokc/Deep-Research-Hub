You will be consistent and the scale won't move. It's not always going to, and that's okay. You weren't gaining fat just because it went up just like you're not always losing fat just because it went down. The narrative that you're creating around a scale number doesn't exist, you aren't failing, and this doesn't mean that nothing is happening. You will be more disappointed by sabotaging your efforts or are throwing in the towel, so stop getting mental about this. It's not an overnight process. Stop allowing a number on the scale to dictate what the happens next. You're better than that, love you.
