If I was starting my finish journey all over again, here are three things I wouldn't do. The first one is going to surprise you. Number one, I would not go right into a calorie deficit. Many people just need to dial in their macronutrient distribution, and you will see wild body composition changes. Number two, I would not try to go too quick. Most people are in a rush to get there, but what happens is you cut out all the things that you enjoy about your life. And once you lose the weight, you try to reincorporate those things, and inevitably, the pendulum will swing. And number three, I would not get caught up in what the everybody else is doing. You have no idea what their life is like, you have no idea how much time they've committed, you have no idea what they may or may not be taking. And so misleading yourself and beating yourself up about not achieving something that somebody else has is just a waste of time and energy.
