Now, the downstream consequence of this. Women are almost twice as likely as men to receive a mental health treatment. Only 36% of referrals to NHS talking therapies are for men. A field built predominantly on the female psychological experience will naturally develop a vocabulary that mirrors female psychological patterns. The way women process emotion, describe distress, experience relationships, and make sense of conflict became the implicit standard for psychological health. And when that vocabulary moves from research into training, into therapy rooms, into pop psychology, into social media, it carries that standard with it.
