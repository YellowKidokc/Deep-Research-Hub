Any helpful suggestions on unsupportive family members who mock and make Snide comments about fitness and nutrition goals? Well, the hardest thing about that is that you have a goal and you want to change your life. But the people around, you don't necessarily want to do the same. And it can be really difficult when you're around people whose opinions that you value to change your behavior in relationship to them. Most people don't actually talk about the changes that they want to make and why they want to make them. It's not easy to sit there and say, I'm actually really insecure about the way that I look, and it would really like to do something about that. That might make you feel really uncomfortable to address or put a voice to an insecurity that you have. So the first responsibility that you have is to speak up about it so that everybody's on the same page. And there isn't an elephant in the room that's not being addressed.

That also eliminates a lot of assumptions that people can make about why you're doing what you're doing. The other thing that you have to accept is that people have their own perceptions around what you're doing and why you're doing it. And also it might hid an insecurity in them because they aren't ready to make those changes for themselves.
