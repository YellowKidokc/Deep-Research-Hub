Expecting your partner to fulfill every single role and fit every single need that you have is unrealistic. And it also puts a lot of pressure on them to be everything all at once all the time. It's exhausting and insufferable. One person can't possibly be your lover, your best friend, a comedian, and your therapist. Because having this pressure and then the inability to meet the pressure and the standards and expectations that one person should be everything will build resentment over time.
