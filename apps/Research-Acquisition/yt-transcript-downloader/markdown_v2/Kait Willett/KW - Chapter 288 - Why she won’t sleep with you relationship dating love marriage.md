Sexual desire does not always align perfectly between partners. Men often have a higher baseline when it comes to having sex or intimacy, while women's desire is much more influenced by the emotional connectedness of the relationship. And this discrepancy can cause tension between the two. But understanding why it exists can help couples navigate their sexual intimacy much more easily. Men have, from a biological and evolutionary perspective, are hardwired to focus on reproducing, and so they tend to take up the opportunities that come into their space. And this also means that they have a much more consistent and intense sexual appetite. Women, however, often experience desire that is much more context dependent, influenced by emotional intimacy and life circumstances. And this is because they needed a supportive partner who was also resourceful in order to feel confident with the consequences of being physically intimate with somebody.
