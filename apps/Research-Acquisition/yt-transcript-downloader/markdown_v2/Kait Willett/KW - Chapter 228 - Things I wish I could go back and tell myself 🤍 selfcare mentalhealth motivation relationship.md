Few things I wish I knew in my 20s as a 30year-old. If they wanted to, they would. And that goes for you, too. Your health matters more than your weight, and I hope that you figure that out before you lose your health. In the pursuit of aesthetics. Do it regardless of how afraid that you are, because if you wait to be ready, the opportunity will pass you by. You'll always be wrong in somebody else's eyes. You don't need other people's approval to do what it is that you want to do. Confidence won't come from looking a certain way. Work on building trust within yourself and it will. You will get hurt more than you'd like to, but don't let that make you jaded. That's just letting them win over and over again. Your trauma will change you and trying to pretend like it didn't happen to you doesn't make you stronger, but healing does. You'll never hate yourself into somebody that you love, but you can love yourself enough to want better for yourself.

You can be as masculine, bold and aggressive as you want to be, but don't let that take away the beautiful soft parts of you.
