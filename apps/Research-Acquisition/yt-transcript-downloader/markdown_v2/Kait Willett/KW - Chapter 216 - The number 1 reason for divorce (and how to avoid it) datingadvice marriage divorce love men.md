You might think marriage is all about love until money shows up as the uninvited guest. Whether it's overspending or secret, saving lack of financial trust will lead to resentment. And if you didn't know this, financial disagreements are the number one reason for divorces. From the male perspective, men often feel pressured to be the financial provider in the relationship, which can lead to a lot of stress if their partner spends or thinks about money a lot differently. From the female perspective, they might see finances as a shared responsibility and expect a lot of transparency or entitlement. What should you do about this one? Sit down and review your finances together and create a joint plan with how you're going to be able to invest in the things that you guys want to be able to own or experience together in the future. Alternatively, make sure you communicate transparently about your spending habits.

Traditional roles around money are starting to shift, so it's important to discuss who should handle what and what the agreements will be around that together.
