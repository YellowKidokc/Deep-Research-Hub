Here is the difference between low emotional intelligence and different emotional intelligence. People with low levels of emotional intelligence cannot read impact, cannot label the basics, and will not adjust after receiving feedback. Different means he's going to read the room in his own way. He will regulate first and contribute conversationally when he's ready to do so. Because conflict is one thing that can make or break relationships, here are a few very important things to understand from a personal responsibility lens. Number one, ask. Don't assume. Number two, translate your needs upfront. Don't wait for them to guess. Do you want comfort? Do you want them to hear you out? Or do you want solutions? Come into the conversation knowing what you need. Number three, label your behavior. Whether you're the man or the woman, if you need time to chill, you need space, you need to work through what you're going through, talk about it.

Do not compromise the safety of your relationship unless you want the safety to be compromised. Number four, to make sure that you're hearing what they're saying, make sure you reflect back to them. So, you're not mad at me. You're just stressed about work, right? I want to make sure that I'm not missing something. And number five, a simple cheat code in a relationship is just pick words that you both can understand to simplify what's going on in the experience.
