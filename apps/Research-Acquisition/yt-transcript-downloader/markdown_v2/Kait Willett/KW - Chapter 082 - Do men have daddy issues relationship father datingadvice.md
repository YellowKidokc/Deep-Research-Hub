We've heard of women having daddy issues, but we don't necessarily talk about the impact of men growing up without a father and how that can lead to further issues. Without a strong male role model, many men struggle to develop confidence and the social skills that are required for them to navigate a intimate relationship. Think about a young boy growing up without his father around. He might be raised by a super strong, incredibly caregiving and nurturing mother, but there's still a void in his understanding of what it means to be a man in a relationship. And this lack of guidance can manifest itself as insecurity or complete avoidance. Fatherless homes have been linked to a range of social challenges, including the difficulty of forming healthy romantic relationships. Without the tools and examples provided by a healthy father figure, men will find themselves unequipped to engage in meaningful relationships.
