Many men that I work with have a pattern, and this pattern tends to involve jumping from relationship to relationship with never processing what just happened and just trying to move on. And to be fair, it feels really good at first. But soon they realize they're just trying to fill this void. Left empty by an X, aiming to replace the presence of this person with a new face and a new name. Where they never actually took the time to feel this person out and see if they actually liked them. Now, why do people do this well? Rebounding can be a place where you don't actually have to deal with your feelings or emotions relative to the breakup. It's a convenient and easy distraction, but it also prevents you from fully processing the breakup in what you just went through. Which down the road will lead to further emotional complications. Now, unlike the anxious, this is more of an avoidant attachment style matrix.

Individuals with an avoidant attachment are significantly more prone to going into a rebound series post breakup. And because they're so used to suppressing their feelings, jumping into a relationship is a very nice way to distract themselves.
