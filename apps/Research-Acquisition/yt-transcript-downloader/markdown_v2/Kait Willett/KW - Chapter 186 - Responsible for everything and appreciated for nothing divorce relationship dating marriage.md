When one partner feels like they're carrying more of the mental, emotional or financial load, they may begin to feel undervalued or taken advantage of. The Journal of Marriage and Family published a study revealing that 21% of divorces were due to an inequitable division of labor. And this highlights the importance of balanced contributions in marriage. Any imbalancing contribution in a relationship can foster bitterness, especially if one partner feels overburdened with responsibilities or unsupported in their personal or professional goals. Now, the important part about this is couples tend to fall in this dynamic unconsciously, slowly, over time, without clear communication about how these roles impact the other person's feelings of stability, safety and appreciation in the relationship.
