When we talk about security in a relationship, we're talking about a space where your partner feels safe, physically, mentally and emotionally. And for women, this need for security tends to be a bit more pronounced. It's not just about knowing that you won't walk away. It's also about understanding that she's safe to express herself emotionally without the judgment. The protection and security isn't just about protecting her from the outside world, but from emotional harm within the relationship. And this can look like her asking questions about where you guys stand, asking about your personal commitment or needing even more physical affection. Now, here's where men can misunderstand the need for security. If she's asking you for reassurance or wanting more affection, you might think, Why is she being clingy or doesn't she trust me? Sometimes men think, but I've already told her I love her already.

What more does she need? But love and security are things that need to be consistently reinforced. It's not a Oneandone kind of deal, so how should you respond when she's seeking more connection or security in your relationship?
