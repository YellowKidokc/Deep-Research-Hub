Many people think that intimacy is only the physical stuff, but it's not. Research would suggest that couples need to spend 90 minutes a week with one another. Discussing their relationship. How they feel about one another, where they're at, where they're going, what they want to do, really reconnecting on the foundation of what this relationship was built on. Because the truth is, you guys will grow, everybody grows. You're probably not the same person that you were at the beginning of this year that you are right now, and that's okay. That's supposed to happen. But if you don't have these conversations about where you're at, how you feel, what you want, what you love, where you're going, what you aspire to do together, you are going to build a distance within that relationship and it's not ever intentional. But it does happen, and it compounds over enough time. It's so crucial to have emotional intimacy with your partner in order to build the roots that stabilize that relationship so that it can grow.
