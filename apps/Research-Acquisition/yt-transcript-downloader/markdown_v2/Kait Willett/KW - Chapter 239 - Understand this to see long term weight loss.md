When you feel like quitting because you overeat your macros or your calories in a day, I want you to come back to this video. I need you to understand that your metabolism is super dynamic. It is not static, it does not stay the same every single day, it changes on a daily basis. So even the calories or the macros you are tracking are not perfectly reflective of the gravity of the deficit that you may or may not be in. So I need you to zoom out and look at your progress and your consistency over a month. Are you engaging in better habits? Are you walking? MORE? Are You Paying Attention to protein or Eating more fiber? Are you actually learning new habits and behaviors that will sustain long term? Driving youring nuts over numbers is not a way to learn any skills that are actually going to allow you to have a transformation that you can sustain. So while this is a good tool, this is not a codependent relationship that we want to build.

Numbers lie, sometimes stop looking at being perfect and start focusing on making progress.
