Why I'm talking about this? Where? If you are somebody who's trying to really get into a groove or a habit of just moving your body and exercising Cuz, it's good for you. But you don't have the motivation to continue to train like a bodybuilder or a powerlifter or a crossfit athlete or whatever. It is that you might have done into this point. What you want to focus on doing is, How can I make this activity? Have some novelty to it. Maybe that's going to Pilates, maybe that's taking a UFC class, maybe that's doing group classes, maybe that's Peleton, maybe that's training for endurance instead of performance or strength. There's so many different ways that you can engage in exercise and being able to pivot as you notice that your motivation is declining. Or the exercise activity just feels daunting is something that you can leverage to keep yourself actively pursuing the behavior in itself.

So while switching things up all the time is not necessarily helpful. And I don't want you to take this to an extreme of like, you can't stay with a training program for 8 weeks. What you want to notice is if you've been training in the same style for 6 months or a year, and it's just not fun, or it doesn't excite you anymore. Then bring some novelty into that, and you'll be amazed at how much more motivated you are to actually go and do the thing.
