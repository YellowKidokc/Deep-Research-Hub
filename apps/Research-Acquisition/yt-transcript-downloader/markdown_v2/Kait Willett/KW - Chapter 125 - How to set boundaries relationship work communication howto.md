Most women, if you work with women, they will struggle to say no. They will struggle to set boundaries. And so being able to deploy a framework for understanding, okay, what is the purpose of this boundary? What is it not only going to give you, but what is it going to give the other person? We've thought through where we want to be on the other side of this confrontation, where we want to go to with this person. To come together and say, Okay, I can totally respect that boundary. And it makes sense that you're going to show up better, more, energize, less stress. I want that for you, I'm all in. And it empowers them to be able to say, Holy, my life didn't fall apart. It's okay for me to say no to things. Because now they can value their that time. And because they can now experience that time where they can reset and create space for themselves, they see the benefits of doing it.
