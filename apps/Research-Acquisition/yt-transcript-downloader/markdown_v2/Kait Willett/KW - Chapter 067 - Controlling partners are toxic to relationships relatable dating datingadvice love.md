We've all met people like this. It's my way or the highway. But imagine taking a road trip with somebody who never stops when you need to take a pot break. And you as the passenger literally have no idea where you're even going. And no idea if you're even going to make it there safely or take a plunge right off the edge of a cliff. The need for control often stems from anxiety and a desire for certainty. The truth is, people like this don't trust anybody. And at the root of that, it's because they don't trust themselves, especially to navigate terrains that don't have a ton of certainty or predictability within them.
