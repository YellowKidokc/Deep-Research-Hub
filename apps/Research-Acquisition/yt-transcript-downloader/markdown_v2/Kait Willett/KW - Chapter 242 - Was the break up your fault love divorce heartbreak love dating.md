It's common to think I'm the problem, I'm not good enough. It was my fault. And these thoughts can haunt you and impact your self-esteem. This kind of self-deprecation can trap you in a cycle of negative thinking, making it significantly harder to actually move on and heal. Interestingly enough, when it comes to personality, research has found that people that are high in conscientiousness tend to actually struggle. The most post breakup with turning the circumstances that happened inward. And this leads them to completely internalize the blame of their relationship falling apart and question their own sense of Selfworth. And from an attachment perspective, I'm sure you can guess that the anxious attached person is going to be the one who also struggles the most with this.
