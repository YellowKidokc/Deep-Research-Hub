Coinbase creates new crypto lending service geared toward large investors. Coinbase has created a new crypto lending service in the U.S. for institutional clients, helping fill the void left by the blow.ups of firms like Genesis and Block Fi, according to a person familiar with the matter. Clients can lend Coinbase money, predominantly crypto assets, and get collateral exceeding the value of the loan, such over collateralization, acts as a safeguard from disaster. The new service differs from the controversial Lend program that Coinbase canceled in 2021 that was pitched at retail customers, and S.E.C. officials objected with this service. Institutions can choose to lend digital assets to Coinbase under standardized terms in a product that qualifies for a Regulation D exemption, a Coinbase spokesperson said in a statement.
