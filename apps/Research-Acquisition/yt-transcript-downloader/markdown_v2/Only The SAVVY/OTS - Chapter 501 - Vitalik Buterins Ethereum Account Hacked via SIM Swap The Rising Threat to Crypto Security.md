Finally, we're learning more about how hackers took over. Ethereum founder Vitalik Buterin's X Account Vitalik posted on decentralized social app WarpCast that hackers took advantage of a SIM swap. That's when an attacker contacts a mobile phone carrier in this case, Tmobile, and convinces them to switch your phone number to a different SIM card. After gaining access to Vitalik's account, the hackers posted a malicious link that reportedly led to nearly seven hundred thousand dollars in losses from victims.
