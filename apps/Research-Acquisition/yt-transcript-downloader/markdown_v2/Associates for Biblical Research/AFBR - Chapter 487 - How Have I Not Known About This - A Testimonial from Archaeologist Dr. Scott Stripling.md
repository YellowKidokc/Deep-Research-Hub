[00:00] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iNjEI6xamx8&t=0s) First heard about the Associates for Biblical Research in 2004, when I was working at an archaeological excavation in Jordan. I was very impressed by the idea that a Christian organization was actually doing firsthand biblical research and excavating in the land of the Bible. My first question was, How have I not known about this? I think the work that we do at AVR is groundbreaking because it addresses the skeptic. And then I think it's also important for believers who need to be assured in their faith that they can trust the word of God.

[00:34] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iNjEI6xamx8&t=34s) What I have found is that there are hundreds of examples where the Bible and the archaeological data synchronized. And at some point, a fair,minded person has to conclude that. There is a God of the Bible and in my view, that God of the Bible holds a moral claim on our lives.
