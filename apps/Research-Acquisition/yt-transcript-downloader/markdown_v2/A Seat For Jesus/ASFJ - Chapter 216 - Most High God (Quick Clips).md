## Part 1 · 00:04

[00:04] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-fnUDkmLrD4&t=4s) What is he to you? Is he a father, a redeemer, a savior, a provider, a healer? Is he the prince of peace or the king of kings? Now, I wouldn't simply call him God cuz that is definitely not his name. It's more of a title. But there are many gods, so you want to be specific and give him an attribute. For example, you could call him the most high God because that's how he refers to himself to make sure there's a differentiation between the other gods.

[00:34] Psalm 97:9 says, "For (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-fnUDkmLrD4&t=34s) You, oh Lord, are most high over all the earth. You are exalted far above all gods." Psalm 95:3 says, "For the Lord is a great God and a great king above all gods." It was a problem during the Tower of Babel times when God distributed to the nations gods over them. But these gods rebelled and they began to take worship for themselves rather than the most high God. So the most high God had to step in and give some rules.

[01:06] Commandment number one is you shall have no other (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-fnUDkmLrD4&t=66s) Gods before me. 2 Samuel 7:22 says, "Therefore you are great, oh Lord God, for there is none like you and there is no God beside you." Exodus 15:11 says, "Who is like you, oh Lord, among the gods? Who is like you, majestic in holiness, awesome and glorious deeds, doing wonders?" When you pray or speak to God, acknowledge who he is to you. He cares more about that than any particular name.
